## Introduction

黑木香 is one of the most legendary actresses in the history of the Japanese AV industry, celebrated as the "queen of AV". She debuted in 1986 with the promotional slogan 「横浜国立大出のAV嬢」 and caused a huge sensation at the time with her distinctive air and image as an intellectual woman.

She is one of the representative figures of the golden age of the Japanese AV industry; her appearance changed the social image of AV actresses and gradually brought the profession social acceptance. Her romantic relationship with 村西とおる is also widely known.

## Personal notes

- Born in Yokohama, Kanagawa Prefecture; graduated from a girls' junior and senior high school.
- Studied Italian art in the art department of the Faculty of Education at Yokohama National University.
- Entered the AV industry to fund her study-abroad in Italy.
- Her first-person pronoun is 「わたくし」, known for polite diction and a fast, proactive way of speaking.
- Boldly showed her armpit hair; she has never shaved since the age of 16.
- She and 村西とおる were once romantically involved, and she served as a director of a ダイヤモンド映像 group company.
- In 1994 she had a hotel accident (a fall from a building), later reported by weekly magazines as a failed suicide attempt.
- In 2004 she sued several publishers over privacy and portrait rights.
- In the 2019 Netflix series 《全裸監督》 her character was played by 森田望智.

## Work features

- Her debut title 《SMぽいの好き》, with her new style of provoking the director and the male performers, set an abnormal record of success.
- She starred in only three titles, but their influence was profound.
- With her distinctive personality and intellectual image she broke the stereotype of AV actresses at the time.
