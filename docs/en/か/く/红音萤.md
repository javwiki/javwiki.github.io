## Introduction

红音萤 is a former Japanese AV actress, actress and talent. She made her AV debut in 2004 as "秋月杏奈", later renamed 紅音ほたる, and was known in the industry as the "squirting queen". After retiring in 2008 she threw herself into HIV-prevention education and founded the 「つけなアカンプロジェクト」. In August 2016 she died at home of an acute asthma attack, at only 32.

## Personal notes

- Famous for squirting — almost every one of her titles has a squirting scene, and the industry called her the "squirting queen".
- Her squirting power was extreme: more than 20 cameras were once damaged by it, and a 2-litre Pocari Sweat was standing by at every shoot to prevent dehydration.
- She entered the AV industry because she admired Ai Iijima, and after Iijima's death carried on her ideas, devoting herself to sex-education outreach.
- In 2010 she founded the general incorporated association 「つけなアカンプロジェクト」 to promote HIV prevention and condom use.
- In 2011 she spoke at a press conference for HIV testing week at the Taiwanese Legislative Yuan and was appointed a certified HIV education ambassador.
- After retiring she worked as a talent and performed pole dance.
- Her cause of death was sudden death caused by an acute asthma attack.

## Work features

- Squirting is her signature; squirting scenes run through her titles.
- Early on a gyaru style, later a lolicon style, then back to gyaru — a varied style.
- Active as a kikaku-tantai actress with a large catalogue covering many themes.
