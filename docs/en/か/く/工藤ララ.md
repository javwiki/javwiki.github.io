## Introduction

工藤ララ is a Japanese AV actress who debuted in June 2020 as an exclusive of the SODクリエイト streaming-first label 「エモい女の子。」 as 「伊藤はる」. Her status as a current Tokyo University of the Arts student became a topic of conversation. After a temporary hiatus she restarted her activity in 2021 as 「工藤ララ」 as a non-exclusive (kikaku) actress. She took 1st place in the actress category of Monthly FANZA's 「このAV女優がすごい!2022夏」 and is one of the representative lolicon-type performers.

## Personal notes

- From Osaka Prefecture. She started piano at age 3; the grand piano her parents gave her in the first year of middle school was "the best gift of my life".
- She majored in music at university but dropped out in frustration when her small stature and small hands limited the pieces she could play. She was scouted by an AV agency and debuted.
- In the AV industry the small stature she once felt inferior about has instead become a weapon, and she is happy about it.
- Her first time was in the third year of high school in a karaoke booth.
- She grew up in a strict household, shuttling between school, cram school, ballet and rhythmic gymnastics every day with no time to play.
