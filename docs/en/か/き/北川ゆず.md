## Introduction

北川ゆず is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive in February 2016. From June to November of the same year she also worked as a 本中 exclusive. She announced her retirement at the end of March 2018, and re-debuted as 「上田紗奈」 in May 2023. Her interests are cooking and darts; her speciality is flexibility.
