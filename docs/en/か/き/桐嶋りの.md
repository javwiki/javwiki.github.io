## Introduction

桐嶋りの is a former Japanese AV actress. From Tokushima, she worked as a plasterer's office clerk locally before moving to Tokyo, and applied on her own initiative. She debuted in March 2015 as a Prestige exclusive with 《極エロ専属デビュー》 and became known for teasing acting and superb technique, earning the name "carnivorous type". From April to November 2016 she was an IdeaPocket exclusive (IPZ-746 was the first title of that series). In March 2019 she won the on-demand award at the SkyperfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards, announced her retirement that November, and her December title was her last appearance. At her retirement she ran a crowdfunding campaign for a graduation photobook (86 people, ¥2,816,000). She appeared in about 73 solo titles (2022 survey) and close to 400 titles in total over her career.

## Personal notes
- From Tokushima; before moving to Tokyo she worked as a plasterer's office clerk; while out playing in Tokyo she was repeatedly approached by scouts, decided she "could be an AV actress too", moved to Tokyo and applied — a shoot was set within a month of her first interview.
- She describes herself as naturally M, with the teasing image being acting; her first time was at 14 (a classmate).
- In her debut interview she said her motive was curiosity ("you don't know until you try"); living in the countryside there were no scout encounters, she was approached while out in Tokyo and applied herself, and at the first company a shoot was set within a month of the interview; she prefers the cowgirl position and looking at her partner's face, and had been squirting since her teens (May 2015 menscyzo debut interview, first-hand).
- From 2018 she was on the roster of the upscale shop CLUB 虎の穴 Aoyama (diamond grade); records of her roster status remain after her retirement.
- Official blog 《桐嶋りの Official BLOG》 (livedoor, archived 2015–2018); Instagram rino_kirishima.
