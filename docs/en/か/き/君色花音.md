## Introduction

君色花音 is a Japanese AV actress born in 1998 in Kanagawa Prefecture. She debuted as an Idea Pocket exclusive in February 2017 and afterwards went through name changes and agency moves, active under stage names such as "君色華奈" and "涼美ほのか". In 2018 she joined the idol group "un panopticon girl", later left it and suspended her AV activity, and made her comeback in 2019 under a new name.

## Personal notes

- From Kanagawa Prefecture; interest: sauna; speciality: swimming.
- The trigger for becoming an AV actress was seeing 明日花キララ on Twitter, finding her cute, wanting to become an AV actress herself, and then being scouted.
- Moved to the ARROWS agency in February 2018 and changed her name to "君色華奈".
- In August 2018 she joined the idol group "un panopticon girl" with 橋本ありな and 桐谷まつり; left the group in November of the same year and suspended her AV activity.
- In May 2019 she changed her name to "涼美ほのか", moved to バンビプロモーション and restarted her AV activity.
- Her Twitter stopped updating after December 2021; by March 2022 she was no longer on Bambi Promotion's roster.

## Work features
