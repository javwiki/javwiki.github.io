## Introduction

希岛爱理 is one of the most representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry, and is also a singer, actress and voice actress. She debuted as an IdeaPocket exclusive in August 2013 and quickly rose to the top of the industry with her pure looks and excellent acting. Her "classic beauty" image struck deep, and her releases are known for their high quality and much loved by fans.

Airi Kijima is not only an AV actress but also an active singer: she debuted as a singer in 2015 and released several singles and albums. She was a 9th-generation member of 惠比寿麝香葡萄 and is also a member of 恵比寿マスカッツ1.5. She retired as an AV actress in August 2025 and formally left the public stage in May 2026.

## Personal notes

- From Tokyo; before her AV debut she worked as a beautician.
- Interests: reading and cooking; specialities: dance and basketball.
- Favourite author: Keigo Higashino; favourite manga: 《我们存在的时间》 and 《北斗神拳》.
- In her singing career she wrote the lyrics for 10 of her 16 original songs, and also composes.
- From 2015 she admired 中島美雪 and learned to play the acoustic guitar.
- Her signature techniques are the "sushi" cowgirl (close-contact sliding-piston cowgirl) and the rhythmic rotating cowgirl.
- Over 12 years she appeared in 201 AV titles.

## Work features

- Early on centred on an innocent look, later gradually trying more varied roles.
- Her work is known for high quality, with story-driven titles particularly strong.
- Strong at cowgirl techniques, celebrated as the "ローリング希島スペシャル".
