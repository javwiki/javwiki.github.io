## Introduction

北川绘里香 (Japanese: 北川エリカ, born 9 December 1986) is a former Japanese AV actress and model. From Tokyo, she is 153cm with measurements 90-58-86. She began activity in 2009 as 「园田ユリア」 with the 名東 (DINO) agency, and changed her name to 「北川エリカ」 after moving agencies in 2011. Her catalogue of titles is enormous, spanning censored, uncensored, VR, games and other media, making her one of the representative kikaku-tantai actresses of the 2010s. She formally retired from the AV world in April 2019.
