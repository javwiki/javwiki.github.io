## Introduction

五条恋 is a Japanese AV actress, former gravure idol and magician. She was originally active under the stage name 「結城ちか」, later renamed 「髙橋央」 and 「雛田真依羽」. In September 2023 she debuted as an AV actress under the name 「五条恋」. In October 2025 she moved to MOODYZ and became an exclusive actress.

## Personal notes

- From Yamanashi Prefecture, a multi-talented entertainer spanning gravure idol, idol group member and magician (手品師).
- Debuted in 2014 as 「結城ちか」, a high-schoolgirl gravure idol still in school, releasing a photobook the same year — an extremely rare bookstore-distributed photobook debut.
- A member of the idol groups 「GracoRex」「gra-Doll」「NEOMARY」 and of 「17代目ミニスカポリス」.
- Founded 株式会社CKCエンターテイメント and served as its representative director.
- Her speciality is holding a frying pan with her underbust; she once showed this trick to 矢口真里.
- At her AV debut she took 4th and 7th place on the FANZA mail-order floor ranking and 2nd on the monthly AV actress chart for October.
- In 2026 she won "BEST BEAUTIFUL BUST" at Taiwan's 大人國成人展 (ADULTOPIA).
- In 2026 she successfully lost 15 kilograms (in about 10 months) through diet adjustment and pilates.
