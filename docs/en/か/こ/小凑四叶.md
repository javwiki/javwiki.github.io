## Introduction

小凑四叶 is a Japanese AV actress, singer and lyricist, and a former member of the female dance & vocal unit "フェアリーズ" (stage name 井上理香子). She left the entertainment world when the unit disbanded in June 2020 and worked at a beauty clinic for two years; in March 2022 she changed her name to "小湊よつ葉" and made a comeback, debuting in AV that year as an SODクリエイト exclusive. Her debut title took #1 on the FANZA mail-order chart two weeks running and became SOD's biggest hit of the year.

## Personal notes

- From Nagasaki Prefecture; she began training at a local dance school in the sixth year of elementary school, and debuted after three years of practice after moving to Tokyo.
- During her Fairy days she practised hard out of a lack of confidence, and a senior told her: "I knew you were a maniac back then."
- While working at the beauty clinic she had full-body hair removal, going bare below.
- Her introduction to AV was an 18-rated video a friend showed her (which she later realised was gay porn); afterwards she searched for, bought and watched titles herself.
- Her first time was with her high-school boyfriend, but she found it uncomfortable and only painful; before her debut she had three partners and never had oral sex.
- At 18 she worked as a hostess in Ginza, but quit because she could not adjust to the glamorous lifestyle; after switching to a meal-delivery centre the desire to "become famous" led her into the industry.

## Work features

- Sells her former-idol status and an innocent image; most of her titles are lovey-dovey or pure-love.
- Her debut title became SOD's best-selling work of the year, with later titles continuously occupying the FANZA charts.
- Also active in music, holding several solo concerts and releasing original songs.
