## Introduction

小島みなみ is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as an アリスJAPAN exclusive in July 2011 with 《真★美少女》. She moved to S1 NO.1 STYLE in March 2014, graduated from S1 in September 2024 and became a Madonna exclusive from November, releasing a creampie-debut title. She is also active as a singer and is a former member of the idol groups 「SEXY-J」「恵比寿★マスカッツ」「恵比寿マスカッツ1.5」「おとといフライデー」 (disbanded 9 March 2024). Her YouTube channel 「こじみな放送局」 (opened 4 April 2017) has 82,200 subscribers (as of August 2026). She won the DMM.R18 Adult Award 2015 media award and the SkyperfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards 2016 Tokyo Sports Award.
