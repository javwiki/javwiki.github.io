## Introduction

小向美奈子 is a Japanese AV actress, former gravure idol and YouTuber. Scouted in Shibuya at 15, she debuted as a gravure idol in 2000 and was selected for Fuji TV's Visual Queen in 2001. She voiced the anime 《ホイッスル!》 and sang its theme song. She was first arrested over a stimulant-drug incident in 2009, made her AV debut in 2011 with debut-title sales of 200,000 copies, was arrested for the third time in 2015 and imprisoned, and made her comeback after being released in 2016. Despite these twists she has remained active in the AV world.
