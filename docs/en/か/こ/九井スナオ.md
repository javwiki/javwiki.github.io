## Introduction

九井スナオ is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive on 15 August 2023. She became popular for her H-cup figure and cute looks, and belongs to the HANAYA PROJECT agency under the SOD Group. After her debut she actively runs her Fantia fan club (with about 6,500 fans) and is also active in offline events such as cosplay and photo sessions.
