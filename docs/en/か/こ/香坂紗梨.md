## Introduction

香坂紗梨 is a Japanese AV actress born on 1 August 1998 in Tokyo, affiliated with the カプセルエージェンシー agency. In November 2017 she debuted as an exclusive of SODクリエイト's VR label "SODVR" — the first AV actress in history who could only be seen in VR titles and not in ordinary DVDs. From March 2018 she began appearing in ordinary DVD titles and left the exclusive roster; in January 2021 she announced her restart as an unfinished exclusive.
