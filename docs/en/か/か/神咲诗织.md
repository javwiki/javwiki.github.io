## Introduction

神咲诗织 is a former Japanese AV actress from Kyoto Prefecture, raised in Osaka. She debuted as a Million exclusive in March 2011 and quickly became a star with her G-cup bust and elegant air. A music-university graduate and former dental assistant, she joined the idol groups me-me* and 惠比寿麝香葡萄. She formally retired as an AV actress in December 2019, and afterwards changed her name to "こあらちゃん" and continued her activity as a singer.

## Personal notes

- Born in Kyoto, the second of three sisters, raised in Sakai, Osaka from age 3 to 21.
- Her mother is a piano teacher; the family has three grand pianos and two uprights — a well-off household.
- In middle and high school she served as student council president; her bust had already reached an E cup at the time.
- Keeps three dachshunds (Kaninchen dachshunds) named ラム, てん and しのぶ, named after her then-boyfriend's favourite 《福星小子》.
- A music-university graduate, but when she joined me-me* she knew nothing about pop music and chord progressions, and went through a hard learning process.
- After retiring she does not hide her time as an AV actress, saying: "Because there was then, there is me now — I don't deny it or regret it at all."

## Work features

- She moved between studios many times — Million, MOODYZ, OPPAI, IdeaPocket and Attackers — giving her a varied style.
- Strong at story-driven titles; her character work in the Attackers period in particular was praised.
- She has also worked in VR titles and photobooks.
