## Introduction

河北彩花 is one of the most representative active actresses in the Japanese AV industry. Debuting as an S1 exclusive actress in 2018, she quickly rose to the top of the industry with her pure looks and perfect figure. Her "innocent" image struck deep; long legs, a slim build and a baby face with a big bust are her traits. Her interests are watching films and musicals, and she is good at remembering routes. She retired one year after her 2018 debut and made her comeback in 2021. In 2024 her stage name changed to 河北彩伽, and in 2026 she reverted to her original stage name 河北彩花. From 2025 she has also been active in music as 「萌名」.
