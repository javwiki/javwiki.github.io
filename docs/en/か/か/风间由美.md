## Introduction

风间由美 is one of the longest-serving actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She debuted in October 1997 as "鈴川ちか" with 《美乳・豊乳挟み打ち 母性本能》 (TANK), re-debuted as "風間ゆみ" in May 1998, and has been active for more than 25 years since. Counts of her titles differ by source: Japanese Wikipedia counts 1,200 including editorials, Chinese Wikipedia says over 2,000, and a YouTube project title from her agency claims over 3,300 appearances.

She is known for her 98cm bust and is a representative actress of the mature-woman and big-breast genres. She was once a stripper (one performance at age 20) and is the former wife of AV actor 島袋浩 (they lived together from around 2003, married in 2008, divorced in 2013). In 2022 she received the Asahi Entertainment AV Awards legend actress award.
