## Introduction

香水纯 is a Japanese AV actress and singer who debuted as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive in July 2021. She entered the industry after seeing cute, sexy actresses on Instagram. At the end of 2023, with her pre-existing condition SLE (systemic lupus erythematosus) worsening, she announced an indefinite hiatus; in September 2024 she moved to 本中 and announced her comeback. She is also a member of the music unit "ドーパミン" (with Mio Ishikawa) and actively holds solo concerts.
