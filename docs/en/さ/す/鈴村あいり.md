## Introduction

鈴村あいり is a dependable actress who has long been active in the Japanese AV industry as a Prestige exclusive. She debuted as a Prestige exclusive in February 2013 and quickly became popular, reaching 20th on DMM's monthly AV actress chart and 1st on the 1-yen animated-image chart in her debut year, among other rankings. In 2014 she won the best work award in the streaming category at the DMM Adult Awards. By 2015 her cumulative DVD sales stood at 500,000 copies and about ¥500 million in revenue — both the highest records of any Prestige actress in history. She is known for keeping a low profile and basically never appearing on TV, in magazines or other media.
