## Introduction

椎名空 is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as a ナンパJAPAN exclusive in October 2015. She quickly drew attention with her distinctive personality and excellent acting. Known for a "lesbian" and "teasing" image, she is one of the few actresses in the AV industry to publicly identify as bisexual.

Sora Shiina is not only an AV actress but also a designer and painter. From 2019 she has taken part in designing the clothing brand "ceu." under the name "natsuki asano". In 2023 she began painting activity as 椎名そら.
