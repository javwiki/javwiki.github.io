## Introduction

篠田优 (Japanese: 篠田 ゆう, born 21 July 1991) is a former Japanese AV actress, former gravure idol and YouTuber. From Tokyo, 155cm, F cup (measurements 88-60-88). She made her AV debut in 2010 as a member of マークスジャパン. In 2013 she joined the idol group BRW108, and in 2015 the gravure idol unit 「PINKEY」 produced by 篠山纪信. In 2019 she won the Tokyo Sports Award at the スカパー!アダルト放送大賞2019. In March 2023 she became a Madonna exclusive, and that October her agency formally confirmed her retirement. She was a creator of the YouTube channels 「だーしのch」 and 「オヤジ女子だーしの」.
