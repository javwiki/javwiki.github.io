## Introduction

白峰美羽 is a Japanese AV actress and former gravure idol. She debuted as a gravure idol in November 2020 and made her AV debut as an IdeaPocket exclusive in January 2021. Known for a tall 170cm figure, F cups and eight-head proportions, she is called a "本物グラビアアイドル (real gravure idol)". She moved to Attackers in 2022, took about half a year off, and made her comeback in May 2023 while also beginning to release titles under PREMIUM. Often mistaken for mixed-race, she is actually fully Japanese.

## Personal notes

- From Fukuoka Prefecture. Interests: strength training, watching films, cooking; she loves Miffy rabbit very much and buys a new doll every month.
- She considers herself shy (人見知り), but in fact her emotions run high and she becomes talkative after drinking. She likes Islay malt whisky, white wine and sake.
- Invited by entertainment scouts several times in elementary school, but gave up because her parents objected. At 18 she moved to Tokyo and worked as a hostess in Ginza, but quit because she could not adjust to the glamorous life and switched to a meal-delivery centre. Later the desire to "become famous" flared up again and she quit that too.
- In high school she joined the brass band, but also ran in the prefectural track meet.
- She admires Kirara Asuka and Yua Mikami.

## Work features

- Known for a tall 170cm figure, F cups and an extreme waist-to-hip ratio; strong at leg-focused titles.
- From IdeaPocket to Attackers to PREMIUM, her style gradually shifted from innocent to story-driven.
- Steady output, continuously active on the FANZA charts.
