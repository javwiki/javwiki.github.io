## Introduction

城星凜 (also 星セリ) is a Japanese transgender AV actress born on 12 December 1994. Her father is Japanese and her mother of Korean and Russian descent. She signed with DAS and made her AV debut in September 2016, and released a retirement title in September 2017. In August 2018 she made her comeback with the stage name 「星セリ」, and retired again that November.

## Personal notes
