## Introduction

白石茉莉奈 is a Japanese AV actress, singer, DJ and entertainer. She debuted as an SOD exclusive in June 2013 and is known for a voluptuous figure and a maternal air, called the "AV界の聖母 (Holy Mother of AV)". From 2017 she moved from Bambi Promotion to its affiliate agency Bstar.

She was a second-generation member of 惠比寿★マスカッツ (2015–2019) and is also an active singer and YouTube creator. She graduated from SODstar in December 2019, and on 10 February 2020 announced via official X her move to a Madonna exclusive (25 March, 《衝撃移籍 Madonna専属デビュー》 JUL-166).

## Personal notes

- Born in Tokyo; blood type O; the second of four siblings (an older brother, a younger brother and a younger sister).
- **Entertainment career**: from elementary school she appeared in commercials and teen fashion magazines as a junior talent; in high school she appeared in the 『牛乳に相談だ』 commercial, and at her AV debut appeared with that commercial's catch copy (「牛乳に相談だ的美少女」), debuting as a "current mama idol". After high school she took a break to live an ordinary girl's life, and later married and had a child (all of the above per her own account in Smart FLASH 2020-06-27, sourced from 寺井広樹『AV女優の家族』, Kobunsha Shinsho).
- Married with a son; her first photobook was hidden under her bed and discovered by her mother, prompting a family meeting (father a salaryman, mother a full-time housewife); her younger brother was the first to discover his sister's debut title because he worked at an AV store.
- Called the "AV界の聖母 (Holy Mother of AV)" for her medieval-painting-like fleshy figure and maternal way with people; the director of her debut title, 宝瀬博教, commented that her "reassuring sex" is her charm.
- Won Best Newcomer at the DMM Adult Awards in 2014; placed 9th with 107,058 votes in the 2014 《龍が如く》 popularity poll and appeared in 《龍が如く0 誓いの場所》.
- Won the Excellence Award at the DMM Adult Awards in 2015; joined the second generation of 惠比寿★マスカッツ in September 2015 (graduated April 2019 — she says she was actually fired).
- YouTube channel "まりりんといっしょ"; as a singer she appeared at TOKYO IDOL FESTIVAL (2018), ミルジェネ10周年 (2022) and the LADY MADONNA series of lives (2020–2025, as the RWM unit).
- 3rd in FLASH's active strongest reader poll in December 2020; in 2024 a titular talk live, an 11th-anniversary joint event and an OnemanLive (9 August, with a drum solo revealed at the encore); the same year she received the Asahi Entertainment mature-women award (comment: she transformed by losing about 20kg in a year).
