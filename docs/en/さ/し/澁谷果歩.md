## Introduction

澁谷果歩 is a former Japanese AV actress, now writer, Twitch streamer and cosplayer. She debuted as an アリスJAPAN exclusive in November 2014. Her background — born in Tokyo, a graduate of Aoyama Gakuin University, and a former Tokyo Sports newspaper baseball reporter — was a topic of conversation at her debut. Because of her K-cup bust she is called 「かほパイ」. After retiring from AV in May 2018 she reinvented herself as a YouTuber, radio host, voice actress, actor and other roles.

In 2023 she filed a damages lawsuit against the production company and others over online leaks of uncensored versions of her early titles, drawing industry attention.

## Personal notes

- Born in Tokyo. Her father is a doctor; she has an older brother.
- Graduate of Aoyama Gakuin University, majoring in English. Scored a perfect 990 on the TOEIC in 2014.
- After university she worked as a baseball reporter for the Tokyo Sports newspaper (2012-2014), covering the Pacific League.
- Holds qualifications as an English teacher and a childcare worker.
- In 2020 she published 《AVについて女子が知っておくべきすべてのこと》; in 2023 the English edition 《The Japanese Porn Industry Unmasked》 was released.
- In 2025 she appeared as a character of the same name in the game 《如龙8 外传 夏威夷海盗》.
