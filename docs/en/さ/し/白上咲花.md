## Introduction

白上咲花 is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive in April 2024 and was called an "ultra-large newcomer". The charm of the contrast between a baby face and a tall 168cm figure has won her a large fan base, and the media rated her a "天才的AVアイドル (genius AV idol)". Her debut title reached the FANZA chart, and in July 2025 she ranked 3rd on the FANZA rental monthly AV actress chart.
