## Introduction

篠田步美 (Japanese: 篠田あゆみ, born 25 December 1980) is a former Japanese AV actress from Kanagawa Prefecture. She debuted in the AV world as 「池田美和子」 on 18 March 2010 and retired that 1 July. She made her comeback as 「篠田あゆみ」 on 25 April 2014, and was active in mature-woman titles with an I-cup bust and skilled acting. On 3 March 2015 she won the "mature actress award" and the "Fuji Yūkan award" at the 「スカパー!アダルト放送大賞2015」. On 16 June 2016 she posted a retirement statement on her personal Twitter. After retiring she reinvented herself as a YouTuber and TikToker under the name 「ひろみ (Hiromi)」, keeping up with beauty, daily life and behind-the-scenes Shorts, and runs the paid fan club 「ヒロミ」 on the Mi-muse platform.
