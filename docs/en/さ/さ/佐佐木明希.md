## Introduction

佐佐木明希 is one of the most representative mature-woman actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She debuted in October 2015 with the slogan "SOD人妻レーベル史上最高の30代" and quickly rose to the top of the mature-woman field with her refined looks and mature charm. Her "清楚で美しい人妻 (refined and beautiful married woman)" image struck deep, and her releases are known for their high quality and much loved by fans.

After retiring in 2019, she made her comeback as a MOODYZ exclusive in December 2025.

## Personal notes

- From Saitama Prefecture. At her debut she claimed to be married with two children (SOD promoted it as "going into AV to fund her children's tuition").
- Her real birthday is 17 March 1983, but for years she publicly claimed 24 December 1979; she revealed her real birthday on X in December 2025.
- She divorced in 2018 after her husband discovered her AV work, becoming a single mother.
- After retiring in 2019 she worked at a hotel.
- When she retired in 2018 she said "I won't come back unless I'm chased by debt", but on her 2025 comeback she said "after becoming ordinary I suddenly wanted to see my fans".
- In January 2026 she disclosed that she had been stalked and harassed by a man about 30 years her senior for some 20 years.
- AV critic 東風克智 said of her comeback title: "Those five years weren't a retirement, they were a charging period" and "a transformation from married woman to beautiful onee-san image".

## Work features

- Mainly mature-woman and married-woman titles.
- Known for high quality and natural, realistic acting.
- Strong at themes such as pure married women and affairs.
- In the first half of 2016 she appeared in over 30 titles, called the "綺麗すぎる人妻女優 (too-beautiful married-woman actress)".
