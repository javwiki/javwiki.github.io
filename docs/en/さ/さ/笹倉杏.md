## Introduction

笹倉杏 (Japanese: 笹倉 杏, born 1 April 1987) is a Japanese AV actress whose former stage name was 「緒川はる」. She debuted as an OPPAI exclusive in February 2016, and an H-cup (100cm) bust is her trademark. From a well-off family, she studied piano, horn, jazz dance, ballet and other arts as a child. Originally extremely prone to nervousness, during a Weekly Playboy shoot she was called by number, and using the self-reference 「杏ちゃん」 from that point on became a way of training herself. She left her agency at the end of August 2025 and restarted as "笹倉杏".
