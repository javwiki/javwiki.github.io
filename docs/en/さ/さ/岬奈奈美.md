## Introduction

岬奈奈美 is a former Japanese AV actress who debuted as an IdeaPocket exclusive in November 2017. Known for a petite 150cm figure and a healing-type beauty image, she was called an AV actress who looks like "Hashimoto Nanami". She moved to Attackers as an exclusive in July 2022. After releasing her final title in August 2023 her activity stopped; her social-media accounts have been deleted and her profile has been removed from the agency's official site.
