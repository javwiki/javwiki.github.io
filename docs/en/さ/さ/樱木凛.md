## Introduction

樱木凛 is one of the representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry, and is also an actress and singer. She debuted as a KUKI exclusive in October 2007 and quickly drew attention with her pure looks and excellent acting.

Rin Sakuragi was a second-generation member of 恵比寿マスカッツ and was active on variety programmes. She is also a member of the singer unit P＊cherry. After retiring in April 2017 she made a limited-period comeback in July 2019 due to the retirement of 希崎ジェシカ.

## Personal notes

- Born in Hyogo Prefecture; blood type B.
- Interests: shopping and digital cameras.
- Her stage name takes the "花 (flower)" character related to spring because she was born in March.
- A second-generation member of 恵比寿マスカッツ, on good terms with 希崎ジェシカ, かすみ果穂, 初音みのり, 希志あいの and others.
- In 2014 she formed the unit P＊cherry with さくらゆら.
- In 2015 she briefly changed her name to "沼田早苗", then continued to use "桜木凛".
- Retired on 22 April 2017.
- Made a limited-period comeback in July 2019 due to 希崎ジェシカ's retirement, reporting that she was married with a son.

## Work features
