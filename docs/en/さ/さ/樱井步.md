## Introduction

樱井步 is a former Japanese AV actress and current stage actress who debuted from Prestige in March 2013. She calls herself a "ちっぱい女优 (small-busted actress)", selling her small bust as her point, and claims to have coined the word "ちっぱい" herself. During her kikaku-tantai era her output was extremely high: she passed 100 titles in January 2014 and 300 in December. She became a Million exclusive in May 2015 and retired as announced in March 2016, with over 1,300 titles across her AV career. After retiring she performs on stage under the stage name "桜井よしみ".
