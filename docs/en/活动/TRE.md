# TRE Taipei International Adult Fair

TRE (Taipei Red Expo, Taipei International Adult Fair) was the first adult fair in 2019 in Taipei, Taiwan, and was one of the largest adult exhibitions in Asia. The event brought together the AV women, the writing idol and Coser from Japan and Asia, with stage performances, signing photos, brand booths, etc., which were important occasions for the AV women's overseas activities.

The orientation was expanded from an adult exhibition to a comprehensive exhibition of human creators and Reds (Reds) who were sold through the JKFACE platform.

## Basic information

- ** Full name**: TRE International Adults Festival in Taipei (2026: TRE International Red People's Fair in Taipei)
- ** English name**: Taipei Red Expo
- ** Place of event**: Taipei (2 Southport Museum etc.)
- ** Initial**: 2019
- ** Sponsor**: Official data show that the operator is Lui Xian Tech Ltd., and that the owner is working with Taiwan JKF to sell tickets
