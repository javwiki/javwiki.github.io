# TRE Taipei International Adult Expo

TRE (Taipei Red Expo, 台北國際成人展) is an adult expo held in Taipei, Taiwan, first held in 2019 and positioned by its organisers as one of Asia's large adult exhibitions. The event invites AV actresses, gravure idols and cosplayers from Japan and across Asia, with stage shows, signing and photo sessions, brand booths and similar segments, and is an important occasion for Japanese AV actresses to appear overseas.

From 2026 the event is held under the name 「TRE 台北國際紅人展」 (Taipei Red Expo), its positioning expanded from an adult expo into a general expo gathering popular creators and influencers (紅人); tickets are sold through the JKFACE platform.

## Basic information

- **Full name**: TRE 台北國際成人展 (from 2026: TRE 台北國際紅人展)
- **English name**: Taipei Red Expo
- **Venue**: Taipei (Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center Hall 2, etc.)
- **First held**: 2019
- **Organizer**: Official materials name 雷麒科技有限公司 as the operating entity, with ticketing in cooperation with Taiwan's JKF-related platforms

## Signature segments

- **Stage shows**: several stages running performances in rotation, with actresses on stage interacting with the crowd
- **Signing and photo sessions**: close-range exchanges, autographs and photos with the actresses present
- **Booths**: booths from adult-related brands and studios, several editions with hundreds of booths
- **Ticket tiers**: single-day tickets, platinum, gold and silver cards with different benefits
