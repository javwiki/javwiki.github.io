# Activities

JAV-related exhibitions and events are mainly featured in the live exhibitions and essay fairs for adults in mainland China, Taiwan and Japan. The events have invited Japanese women, women, writing idols and others to the scene for stage performances, signing photos and shooting exchanges, and also for trades in adult health supplies.
