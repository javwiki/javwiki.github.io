# Events

Offline exhibitions and events related to JAV, mainly adult expositions and photo expos held in mainland China, Taiwan, Japan and similar places. Such events usually invite Japanese AV actresses, gravure idols and local cosplayers for stage shows, signing sessions, meet-and-greets and photo exchanges; there are also trade shows focused on adult-wellness products.
