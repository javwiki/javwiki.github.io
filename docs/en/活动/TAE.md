# TAE Taiwan Adult Expo

TAE (Taiwan Adult Expo, 台灣成人博覽會) is an adult expo held in Taipei, Taiwan, long hosted in Area C of Taipei WorldTrade Center Hall 1, one edition a year. Its main formats are photo sessions, meet-and-photo sessions and stage games, inviting gravure idols and AV actresses from Taiwan, Japan, South Korea and similar places, alongside studio booths.

The 12th edition in 2026 moved to Kaohsiung (Zuoying International Event Center) for the first time, billed as an "Asian trend carnival". The organizers trace the series back to 2011 (claiming "since 2011"); by edition count, 2025 was the 11th.

## Basic information

- **Full name**: TAE 台灣成人博覽會 (Taiwanese adult expo)
- **English name**: Taiwan Adult Expo (the organizers sometimes also write Taiwan Angel Expo)
- **Venue**: Area C, Taipei WorldTrade Center Hall 1 (over the years) → Zuoying, Kaohsiung (from 2026)
- **Organizer**: 博展国际有限公司
- **First held**: the 2010s (officially "since 2011")

## Main features

- **Photo and meet-and-photo sessions**: dedicated studios where ticket-holders can shoot and take photos with the actresses
- **Stage games**: interactive game segments on the main stage
- **A mixed line-up**: besides Japanese and Korean gravure idols and AV actresses, it also invites female-oriented AV actors, actors from gay-oriented titles and Taiwanese adult film organizations
