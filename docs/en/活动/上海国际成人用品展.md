# Shanghai International Adult Products Expo

The Shanghai International Adult Products Expo (official name: 中国国际成人保健及生殖健康展览会) is an exhibition of adult sexual-wellness and reproductive-health products held in Shanghai, China. It began in 2004, is organized by 中展集团公司, and runs on the principle 「以贸易为导向、以需求为宗旨、以发展为目标」. It is also known as 「上海国际成人展」.
