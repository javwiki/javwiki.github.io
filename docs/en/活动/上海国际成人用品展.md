# Shanghai International Adults ' Supplies Exhibition

The Shanghai International Adults' Exhibition (official name: China International Adult Health and Reproductive Health Fair) was held in Shanghai, China, from 2004 on the theme "Trade-oriented, demand-driven and development-oriented " . The project is being conducted by the Shanghai International Adults' Fair.
