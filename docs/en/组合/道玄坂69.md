# 道玄坂69

## Introduction

道玄坂69 is a Japanese idol group whose members include adult film actresses. According to the unofficial member page, its first generation was announced on 6 June 2019 on the BS Sky PerfecTV! program 『田村淳の地上波ではダメ！絶対！』.
