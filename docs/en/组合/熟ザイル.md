# 熟ザイル

熟ザイル (じゅくザイル) is a Japanese mature-beauty unit (美熟女ユニット) made up of five members: 内田美奈子、加納綾子、一条綺美香、白木優子、松嶋友里恵. On 31 July 2013 it announced its "birth" through a 31-second promotional video; that is the extent of the material available, and no public record exists of its subsequent activities or releases.
