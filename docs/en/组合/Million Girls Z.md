# Million Girls Z

The Japanese women's idols group, composed of the female favorites of the female AV under the title of the Million, are in Japanese: "The Japanese Man in the Z" (MGZ). The group was formed in 2014 to mark the tenth anniversary of the Million brand, and was mainly engaged in singing, dancing, ensembles and live performances, which were dissolved in early 2017.
