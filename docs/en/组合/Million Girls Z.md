# Million Girls Z

Million Girls Z (ミリオンガールズZ, abbreviated MGZ) is a Japanese girls' idol group made up of AV actresses from the Million label. It was formed in 2014 to mark the tenth anniversary of the Million brand, and mainly engaged in singing, dance, variety programmes and live performances; it disbanded in early 2017.

## Introduction

Million Girls Z was an idol group formed to mark the tenth anniversary of the Million brand. Alongside their work as actresses, the members actively took part in live music and variety programmes. From April 2015 the group appeared regularly on TV Tokyo's 《（仮）TV》, and released the album 《I LOVE MGZ》 that same year.

The group won the best performance award at 「セクシーアイドルミュージックフェスティバル2015」, and using its first solo show in November 2015 as a starting point went on the 「MGZ Asia Tour 2015『初恋』」, appearing in Guangzhou and Macau, China and similar places.
