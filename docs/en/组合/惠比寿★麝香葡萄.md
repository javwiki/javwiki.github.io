## Introduction

Ebisu Muscats (Japanese: 恵比寿★マスカッツ, English: Ebisu Muscats) is a famous Japanese AV actress idol music group, which debuted on TV Tokyo's late-night variety show 《拜托！MUSCATS》 in 2008. The group is made up of AV actresses, gravure idols, dancers and models affiliated with various Japanese agencies, and has been called "the AKB48 of the AV world". The first generation disbanded on 7 April 2013; the second generation debuted formally with 《麝香葡萄之夜》, which began airing in 2015, and later disbanded in 2022. The group held concerts in Hong Kong and Taiwan from November to December 2011.
