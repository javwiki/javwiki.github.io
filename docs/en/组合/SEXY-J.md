# SEXY-J

SEXY-J, an iconic group of AV women in Japan, left the streets on December 7, 2014. The group went to a standstill after the final performance on December 19, 2017; member Chestnut Riley made it clear in her post-play note that it was not informally dissolved. The group is dominated by members' ** individual ** and ** group **, most of which is a replay of the 1970s and 1980s popular songs. Except for the white wood and a fragrance, the remaining members were assigned a number upon accession.
