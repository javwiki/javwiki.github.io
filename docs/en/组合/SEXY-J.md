# SEXY-J

SEXY-J is a Japanese idol group made up of AV actresses, debuting on 7 December 2014. After its final show on 19 December 2017 the group went on hiatus; member 栗林里莉 made clear in her post-show statement that this was not an official disbandment. The group focused on members' **solo singles** and **sub-unit singles**, most of them covers of pop songs from the 1970s and 1980s. Except for 白木優子 and 一条綺美香, all other members were assigned a number when they joined.
