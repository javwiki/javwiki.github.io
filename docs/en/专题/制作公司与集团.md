# Production companies and group relationships

A “manufacturer” name on a release can identify a legal company, an in-house production brand or a distribution partner. These levels should be distinguished when reading company histories. Releases sold on the same platform do not all belong to one production group.

## Understanding historical changes

Hokuto, CA and WILL are linked by business succession, but CA's absorption and the incorporation of the current company are distinct events. The 2017 sale report also means that former membership of the DMM group cannot establish present status as a “DMM subsidiary.”[WILL history](../产业/制作公司/WILL.md)

SOD separates production from sales. Soft On Demand, incorporated in 1995, handles sales and logistics, while SOD Create, incorporated in 1999, handles planning and production. Referring to all of SOD as “SOD Create” conflates the group, the production company and the sales company.[SOD history](../产业/制作公司/SOD.md)

## Distribution platforms and production groups

FANZA is a platform brand for adult services. DMM's 2018 announcement explains the business transfer and brand change. These should be recorded separately from acquisitions, mergers and ownership of production companies.[Official DMM announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000003114.000002581.html)

When evaluating a relationship, identify whether the source describes **investment or acquisition, brand operation, distribution agency, or platform availability**. One title can involve a production brand, a separate publishing company and several sales platforms. An actress's exclusive studio and her [talent agency](../产业/经纪公司/index.md) are also different relationships.
