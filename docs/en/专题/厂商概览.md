# Producer overview

Companies, brands and sales platforms are distinct records. A brand website establishes its name and catalog, but does not by itself prove ownership, founding dates or performers’ current exclusive contracts.

## Corporate sources

WILL lists the current legal entity’s founding date as July 13, 2016. The history of Hokuto and CA requires separate historical sources; a brand’s history is not the age of the current corporation. [WILL](https://will-hp.jp/company/)

SOD lists Soft On Demand (established November 1995) separately from SOD Create (March 1999). The former’s sales operations and the latter’s production work are distinct. [SOD](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/about_us/info/)

Prestige’s corporate recruiting page lists the establishment of 有限会社プレステージ as May 2002. Individual brand relationships require specific release records. [Prestige](https://arwrk.net/recruit/prestige-av/)
