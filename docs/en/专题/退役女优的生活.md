# Life After Retiring from AV

An AV actress's retirement announcement does not explain her subsequent life. Ending new AV shoots, leaving an agency and ending all public activity are separate changes to verify. Some performers keep working under their stage names, some move to other occupations, and others stop sharing updates.

## What retirement changes

An announcement first establishes that someone has declared an end to a particular professional activity. The final shoot, release and farewell event may occur on different dates. Later releases of older footage, compilations or reissues do not by themselves establish a comeback.

Biographies should distinguish retirement from AV performance from retirement from entertainment altogether. Photography, streaming and fan events can continue after new AV appearances end.

## Paths visible in public sources

| Path | Changes in work and life | What cannot be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entertainment and creator work | Social accounts, photography, events or content under a stage name | Public visibility guarantees stable earnings |
| Businesses and brands | Running products, services and operations | Revenue equals personal income or guarantees profit |
| Other occupations | Training, job seeking or work outside the fan economy | An inactive account means unemployment |
| Family and private life | Adjusting schedules and public disclosure | Everyone marries, has children or leaves employment |

This is an analytical framework, not a count of people following each path. The examples below describe individual, publicly documented histories.

[Yua Mikami](../人物/女优/ま/み/三上悠亚.md)'s official website records her August 2023 retirement from AV and presents her clothing brand and subsequent public activities. Ending AV performance can coexist with maintaining a public identity. [Mikami's official website](https://miss.co.jp/)

[Ai Uehara](../人物/女优/あ/う/上原亚衣.md) discussed her 2016 retirement, 2019 return as a YouTuber, and business and investment activities in an ORICON interview. This illustrates a move toward content and business, but the interview does not establish net profit or long-term financial security. [ORICON interview](https://contents.oricon.co.jp/special/58907/2/), [business and working arrangements](https://www.oricon.co.jp/special/58907/3/)

[Sora Aoi](../人物/女优/あ/あ/苍井空.md) publicly shared her marriage and the birth of twins in 2019 through her blog. Family changes can form part of someone's public account without representing every retiree's aspirations or justifying investigation into relatives' private details. [Marriage announcement](https://ameblo.jp/aoi-sola-official/entry-12341116024.html), [later personal account](https://ameblo.jp/aoi-sola-official/entry-12573447880.html)

## A different income structure

Financial analysis must distinguish performance fees, event payments, advertising income, product revenue and business profit. Followers and past fame cannot be converted directly into savings. Rent, staffing, production and platform costs also affect earnings. These are analytical distinctions; this article does not estimate retirees' average income or wealth.

Continued sales of older titles do not automatically establish continuing royalties for performers. Further payments depend on the contract. A title remaining on sale is not proof that its performer still earns from it.

Moving to freelance work also requires checking new contractual terms. Japan's labour ministry explains obligations concerning disclosure of terms and payment, but applicability depends on the transaction and parties, rather than the label “former actress.” [Labour ministry: freelance transactions and working conditions](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/koyoukintou/zaitaku/index_00002.html)

## Public identity, privacy and relationships

Stage names and older footage may remain searchable after filming ends. Continuing to use a name or reducing exposure does not establish how someone feels about her past. Distribution, likeness use and account management have separate conditions; a retirement announcement does not prove that all older content has been removed.

An encyclopedia should record relevant private-life information that the person voluntarily makes public. Relationships, parenting, health and family circumstances should not be inferred from account inactivity, appearance changes or anonymous rumours. Claims about employment barriers or discrimination also need interviews or research, rather than treating them as an inevitable outcome for every retiree.

## Public examples are not the whole picture

People who remain public are more likely to generate reports and searchable material. Those who choose other jobs or private lives may stop providing updates, making the visible sample skew toward celebrities and entrepreneurs.

Stories of successful transitions or financial hardship cannot substitute for a population study. The sources here provide no representative statistics on post-retirement occupations, median income or marital status. Where reliable updates are unavailable, biographies should leave the gap open.
