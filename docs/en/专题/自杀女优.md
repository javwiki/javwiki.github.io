# AV actresses reported to have died by suicide

This feature records Japanese AV actresses whose deaths have been described as suicides in published media, alongside the limits of the available evidence. **Media reports, official investigation findings and undisclosed causes of death represent different evidentiary states.** These are selected cases with traceable sources, rather than a comprehensive list or an independent determination of cause of death.

## Hana Kuraki: reported as an apparent suicide, cause omitted from the statement

Some media described Hana Kuraki's (倉木華) death as an apparent suicide. [Tokyo Reporter, 13 September 2026](https://www.tokyoreporter.com/entertainment/adult-video/kabukicho-host-confirms-death-of-av-actress-hana-kuraki-in-apparent-suicide/) reported that her body was found on 12 September, while noting that police and her agency had not issued an official cause of death at that time. The reported discovery date should not be treated as a confirmed death date.

Her agency, T-Powers, published [a statement concerning Kuraki](https://www.t-powers.co.jp/event/34368/) on 14 September 2026, acknowledging news of her death. [The full statement republished by Nikkan Sports](https://www.nikkansports.com/m/entertainment/news/amp/202609140001356.html) gives neither a cause of death nor an exact death date. This feature therefore records her as a case with **an official death announcement and media reports of apparent suicide, but no confirmation of cause in that statement**. Social media allegations about private relationships or responsibility should not be treated as investigation findings.

## Cases that should not be included as suicides

A death announcement alone is not evidence of suicide. Deaths from illness, accidents, undisclosed causes and disputed cases require separate treatment. Inactivity online, retirement, tributes or rumors cannot establish that someone has died or that their death was a suicide.

For example, [Hotaru Akane](../人物/女优/か/く/红音萤.md) died on 15 August 2016. [J-CAST, 29 August 2016](https://www.j-cast.com/2016/08/29276365.html?p=all) relayed her agency head's explanation that suicide and foul play had been ruled out; an acute asthma attack was suspected, but the cause had not yet been confirmed. She should therefore not be included among suicide deaths.

## Interpreting these records

- **Individual cases do not establish an industry suicide rate.** This page has no statistics covering the entire workforce, observation period or comparison population, and cannot establish that AV actresses have a higher suicide rate than other professions.
- **Separate cause of death from motive.** Media accounts, personal recollections and online speculation do not replace medical diagnoses or investigation findings. Occupation alone cannot establish that AV work caused a death.
- **Respect the deceased and their families.** This page excludes suicide methods, scene images and unverified allegations about private relationships. Additions should include traceable sources and preserve their qualifications.
