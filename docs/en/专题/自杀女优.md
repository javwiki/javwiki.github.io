# AV actresses reported to have died by suicide

This feature records Japanese AV actresses whose deaths have been described as suicides in published media, alongside the limits of the available evidence. **Media reports, official investigation findings and undisclosed causes of death represent different evidentiary states.** These are selected cases with traceable sources, rather than a comprehensive list or an independent determination of cause of death.

## Hana Kuraki: reported as an apparent suicide, cause omitted from the statement

Some media described Hana Kuraki's (倉木華) death as an apparent suicide. [Tokyo Reporter, 13 September 2026](https://www.tokyoreporter.com/entertainment/adult-video/kabukicho-host-confirms-death-of-av-actress-hana-kuraki-in-apparent-suicide/) reported that her body was found on 12 September, while noting that police and her agency had not issued an official cause of death at that time. The reported discovery date should not be treated as a confirmed death date.

Her agency, T-Powers, published [a statement concerning Kuraki](https://www.t-powers.co.jp/event/34368/) on 14 September 2026, acknowledging news of her death. [The full statement republished by Nikkan Sports](https://www.nikkansports.com/m/entertainment/news/amp/202609140001356.html) gives neither a cause of death nor an exact death date. This feature therefore records her as a case with **an official death announcement and media reports of apparent suicide, but no confirmation of cause in that statement**. Social media allegations about private relationships or responsibility should not be treated as investigation findings.

### Background

Her Japanese name was 倉木華 (くらき はな). [T-Powers' official profile](https://www.t-powers.co.jp/talent/%E5%80%89%E6%9C%A8%E8%8F%AF/) gives her birth date as 7 August 2001 and her place of origin as Kanagawa Prefecture. [S1's debut release page](https://s1s1s1.com/works/detail/SONE223?page_from=actress&sys_code=74767) lists the title as 『新人NO.1STYLE 倉木華 AVデビュー』 and the release date as 28 May 2024. [TVBS, 13 September 2026](https://news.tvbs.com.tw/entertainment/jk-pop/4021551) reported that she attended beauty school and worked as a hair and makeup assistant before her debut. It also reported her June 2026 hiatus announcement and her explanation at the time that a traffic accident had caused facial and jaw injuries.

### Reports about her experiences and a statement circulated online

[GamerSky, 13 September 2026](https://www.gamersky.com/news/202609/2208585.shtml) relayed online allegations of violence, threats and financial disputes involving host Shota Taira, described as her boyfriend. The allegations placed the injury incident in May, describing her being dragged by a vehicle while pursuing him after seeking a breakup. The article also relayed a purported statement alleging that her earnings were taken and that his knowledge of her family home's address left her fearful. These are reported allegations; original material sufficient to verify the statement, injury circumstances or responsibility was not obtained, so this page does not present them as established findings.

### Subsequent reporting on cause of death

[Tokyo Reporter, 23 September 2026](https://www.tokyoreporter.com/entertainment/adult-video/death-of-av-actress-hana-kuraki-casts-renewed-spotlight-on-the-dark-underbelly-of-host-club-industry/) cited Zakzak's 21 September coverage and reported that authorities were treating the death as a suspected suicide. No corresponding police announcement or final investigation findings were obtained for this feature, so the account remains qualified as media reporting of a suspected suicide.

## Meiko Nakao: death announced by a friend, cause not disclosed

Meiko Nakao's (中尾芽衣子, なかお めいこ) death received media coverage in November 2025. [NOWnews, 14 November 2025](https://www.nownews.com/amp/news/6754141) reported that her friend and fellow AV actress Hibari Hijiri (聖ひばり) had announced her death on X the previous day, describing Nakao as 28. **13 November is the reported announcement date, rather than a confirmed death date.**

[TVBS, 14 November 2025](https://news.tvbs.com.tw/entertainment/3044644) explicitly noted that Hijiri did not explain the cause of death; it attributed the suggestion of suicide to speculation by commentator 一劍浣春秋. Both articles center on the same friend's statement, so they do not provide multiple independent sources of evidence for cause of death. This feature records Nakao as **a death announcement by a friend relayed through media, with an undisclosed cause and an unconfirmed exact death date**. Expressions of guilt or distress in a tribute do not establish suicide.

## Cases that should not be included as suicides

A death announcement alone is not evidence of suicide. Deaths from illness, accidents, undisclosed causes and disputed cases require separate treatment. Inactivity online, retirement, tributes or rumors cannot establish that someone has died or that their death was a suicide.

For example, [Hotaru Akane](../人物/女优/か/く/红音萤.md) died on 15 August 2016. [J-CAST, 29 August 2016](https://www.j-cast.com/2016/08/29276365.html?p=all) relayed her agency head's explanation that suicide and foul play had been ruled out; an acute asthma attack was suspected, but the cause had not yet been confirmed. She should therefore not be included among suicide deaths.

## Interpreting these records

- **Individual cases do not establish an industry suicide rate.** This page has no statistics covering the entire workforce, observation period or comparison population, and cannot establish that AV actresses have a higher suicide rate than other professions.
- **Separate cause of death from motive.** Media accounts, personal recollections and online speculation do not replace medical diagnoses or investigation findings. Occupation alone cannot establish that AV work caused a death.
- **Respect the deceased and their families.** This page excludes suicide methods and scene images, and does not present unverified allegations about private relationships as facts. Additions should include traceable sources and preserve their qualifications.
