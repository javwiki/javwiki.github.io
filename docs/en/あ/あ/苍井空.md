## Introduction

苍井空 is one of Japan's most influential former AV actresses. She debuted in 2002 and quickly became a star with her unique "child face, big breasts" appeal, holding not only enormous popularity in Japan but also a huge fan base across Asia. She is a pioneer of AV actresses crossing into mainstream entertainment, having appeared in several films, TV dramas and variety programmes. She has more than 19 million Weibo followers in mainland China and is affectionately called "苍老师 (Teacher Cang)" by fans. She was a first-generation member of the Japanese girls' group 惠比壽麝香葡萄.

## Personal notes

- 苍井空 is one of the few actresses in the AV industry to have successfully crossed into mainstream entertainment.
- She is extremely well known on the Chinese internet, where fans affectionately call her "苍老师".
- In 2010 she called on the Japanese public via Twitter to donate to the Yushu earthquake relief, and was praised by Chinese netizens for being "德艺双馨 (virtuous in both art and character)".
- "The Diaoyu Islands belong to China; Sola Aoi belongs to the world" became a famous slogan in 2012.
- She announced her marriage on 1 January 2018; her spouse is NON, a DJ based in Tokyo.
- On 1 May 2019 she gave birth to twin sons by caesarean section.
- As a child she wanted to become a childcare worker; after graduating from high school she attended a junior college to study childcare and qualified.

## Work features

- Early in her career she centred on an innocent, idol-like image, meeting an enormous response with her "child face, big breasts".
- In her middle period she gradually tried more varied themes, including story-driven and kikaku titles.
- A large catalogue covering many types.
