## Introduction

葵伊吹 is one of the most representative new-generation actresses in the Japanese AV industry. Debuting as an S1 exclusive newcomer in 2021, she quickly rose to the top of the industry with her pure looks and cute air. Her "innocent" image struck deep, and her releases are known for their high quality and much loved by fans. Ibuki Aoi is one of the few actresses to reach enormous popularity in a short time; her career continues to this day with a large catalogue.
