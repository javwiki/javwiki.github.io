## Introduction

Tsubasa Amami is one of the most representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She debuted at IdeaPocket in 2009, where the studio used "a beauty who could give idols a run for their money" as its slogan. The gap between her cute looks and her E-cup figure gave her enormous popularity, and she is celebrated as the "最高の美女". She was a member of the 惠比壽麝香葡萄 and BRW108 groups. Amami is one of the few active actresses who have kept high popularity over a long period, and her career continues to this day.
