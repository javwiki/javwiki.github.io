## Introduction

AIKA is a Japanese AV actress affiliated with T-POWERS. She worked as a beautician before her debut: scouted in Shibuya, she made her AV debut in 2011 and continued working part-time as a beautician for four years afterwards. She became a star with a black-gyaru style and is known as the "national 黒ギャル (black gyaru)". In 2016 she won the DMM.R18 Award of Excellence, and in 2017 the Best Actress award (top honor) at the SkyperfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards. Known for her teasing (chijo) acting, she calls herself "the gentle chijo" and has stayed at the front line for years; in 2024 she was still praised in Weekly Playboy as "天下無双のドS (peerless super-S)".

## Personal notes

- Before her debut she was a beautician; after being scouted in Shibuya she entered the industry, and worked both jobs for four years before quitting the salon.
- Although she took a gyaru route at her debut she had fair skin and was packaged as an innocent type, which she found "a bit forced"; she says her own awareness of being a "gyaru representative" is thin — she simply chased the makeup, hair and clothes she liked and was called a gyaru without noticing. As a student she read Zipper and was into the Ura-Harajuku style.
- Her distinctive hoarse voice often makes people think she can drink, but she is actually bad with alcohol and passes any alcohol she receives on to her friends (she does like bar snacks).
- Known fans include celebrities: 極楽蜻蜓's Kōji Katō and 鬼越トマホーク's Kin-chan; when she won at the SkyperfecTV! ceremony, presenter 中尾彬 commented, "A fine actress in a long while — she could carry a big production film."
- She learned her teasing acting by watching films; she is not actually dominant, and says her creed is "a gentle chijo" and "you only tease when there is affection": praise must follow any harsh word, she keeps the balance of candy and whip, and is called "a gentle queen".
- Interests: fashion and travel.
