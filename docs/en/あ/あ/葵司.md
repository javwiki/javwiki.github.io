## Introduction

葵司 is one of the most representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She debuted at Alice Japan in 2010 and quickly rose to the top of the industry with her pure looks and perfect figure. Her "classic beauty" image struck deep, and her releases are known for their high quality and much loved by fans. She moved to S1 in 2015 and her career continued until 2025. Tsukasa Aoi was a member of 惠比寿麝香葡萄 1.5; in 2013 she guest-starred in the Taiwanese TV drama 《求爱365》, becoming the first AV actress to appear in a Taiwanese TV drama. She retired on 17 August 2025.
