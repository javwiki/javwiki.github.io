## Introduction

彩美旬果 is a former Japanese AV actress and entertainer from Toyama Prefecture. She debuted in 2012 as a PRESTIGE exclusive and quickly became the studio's leading exclusive actress. In 2014 she won the gold award for Best Actress at the DMM Adult Awards and was one of PRESTIGE's most representative actresses of her era. She moved to S1 in 2018 and retired in March 2019. She was also a member of the idol group PINKEY and of the second-generation 惠比寿麝香葡萄.
