## Introduction

新有菜 is one of the most representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry. Debuting as an S1 exclusive newcomer in 2016, she quickly rose to the top of the industry with her pure looks and perfect figure. Her "innocent" image is deeply rooted, and she holds the title 「最強美腿」 (strongest legs) in the Japanese AV world. Her interests are shopping and watching videos; her specialities are singing and handstands. She entered the industry because she admired Kirara Asuka. In 2018 she won Best Actress and the Media Award at the DMM Adult Awards. She moved to MOODYZ in 2022 and changed her name to 「新有菜」. She ended her AV and public entertainment activities in December 2025.
