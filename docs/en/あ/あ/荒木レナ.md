## Introduction

荒木レナ is a former Japanese AV actress and a new-half (transgender) actress. She debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive in October 2013 and drew attention for her outstanding looks and tall figure, being called a "绝世美女ニューハーフ (stunningly beautiful new half)". In September 2017 she underwent gender reassignment surgery and completed her registry change; in December of the same year she released her retirement title and retired.

## Personal notes

- At 168cm she has model-like proportions.
- Called a "绝世美女ニューハーフ (stunningly beautiful new half)".
- She underwent gender reassignment surgery in September 2017 and changed her legal registry from male to female; her stated reason for retiring was completing her gender and registry transition.
- She also appeared under the name Rena Araki in three TGirlJapan overseas titles (The Nightingale / Beauty In Lights / Strokes It).
- After retiring she was on the roster of a high-end NH health club in the capital (recorded by 07zaru as a past affiliation); in recent years, per her own social posts, she works in live streaming (17LIVE) and runs a company.
- Interests: cooking and yoga; the 07zaru profile additionally lists beauty care, makeup and café hopping.
