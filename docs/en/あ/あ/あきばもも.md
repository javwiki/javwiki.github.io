## Introduction

あきばもも (former names: 加藤ももか, 佐藤ののか) is a Japanese AV actress who debuted in March 2017 as an "SOD女子社員". She joined SOD as a new graduate in April 2016 and was assigned to the publicity department; she made her formal AV debut in March 2017. She left SOD in March 2018 to become a non-exclusive (kikaku) actress. In February 2021 she changed her name to 「佐藤ののか」 and moved to バンビプロモーション. In April 2025 she changed her name to 「あきばもも」, moved to アルシェ, and in November of the same year became an exclusive actress of 溜池ゴロー.

## Personal notes

- Born in Tokyo. Interests: cooking, reading; speciality: ballet.
- While at a beautician's vocational school she saw an AV actress on a late-night variety show, was encouraged by it, and conceived the thought "I want to become an AV actress", which led her into SOD's publicity department.
- She first volunteered to appear in an AV herself in response to enthusiastic user feedback; later finding she could not balance office work with being an AV actress, she left the company in 2018 to focus on her AV career.
- Her first sexual experience was during her time as a vocational student, with a working-adult boyfriend; at the time that was her only partner.
- She placed 2nd in the user-voted grand prize at 「SODアワード2018」.
- In 2021 she left T-Powers over "differences in direction", changed her name to 「佐藤ののか」 and joined バンビプロモーション.
- In 2021 she appeared in the drama 「救星戦隊ワクセイバー」 as ピンクアース / 仁科おとは, which became a topic of conversation.

## Work features

- Her signature traits are a petite 153cm frame paired with an H cup — "small but big-breasted".
- Early work sold the image of an SOD女子社員 secretary and publicity-department staffer.
- After adopting her current name in 2025 she shifted to a mature direction.
