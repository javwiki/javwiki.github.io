## Introduction

Kirara Asuka is one of the most influential actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She debuted at h.m.p in 2007 and was hailed as "the last and strongest newcomer of 2007". Her "gyaru" image struck deep and she enjoyed enormous popularity among fans. Beyond her success in AV she also crossed over into mainstream entertainment, appearing in several films, TV dramas and variety programmes. She was captain of 惠比寿麝香葡萄 1.5 and is also a singer. She formally graduated from the AV world in February 2020.
