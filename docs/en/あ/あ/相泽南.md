## Introduction

相泽南 is one of the most representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry. Debuting as an IdeaPocket exclusive actress in 2016, she quickly rose to the top of the industry with her pure looks and perfect figure. Her "innocent" image struck deep, and her releases are known for their high quality and much loved by fans. In June 2023 she announced her retirement from AV.
