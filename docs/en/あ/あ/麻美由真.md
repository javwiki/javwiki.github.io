## Introduction

Yuma Asami is one of the most representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She debuted in 2005 and in 2006 won the Best Actress award jointly presented by 18 AV production companies. Her titles topped Japan's disc-rental chart five times, making her one of the most popular AV actresses in recent years. She collects perfumes, speaks English and plays the piano. She was a member of 惠比寿麝香葡萄. In 2013 she retired after being diagnosed with an ovarian tumour; she recovered after treatment and in 2014 published an autobiography sharing her experience of fighting cancer.

## Personal notes
