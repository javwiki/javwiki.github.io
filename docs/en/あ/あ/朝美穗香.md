## Introduction

朝美穗香 (みひろ) is a former Japanese AV actress and entertainer whose real name is 山濑广美. She moved from print modelling into AV in 2005, signing a two-year, 24-title deal with Alice Japan and MAX-A that set sales records at both companies that year. After retiring in 2010 she focused on her entertainment career, publishing the semi-autobiography 《nude》, which was adapted into a film. In 2021 she changed her name to "金子美广".

## Personal notes

- Her 2005 move into AV caused quite a stir; she was the largest contract by volume since 高树玛丽亚.
- After retiring she moved into entertainment, appearing in dramas and variety shows, and released a solo album in 2006.
- In 2008 she went to Hong Kong to shoot the ATV variety show 《AV事务所》.
- In 2009 she published the semi-autobiography 《nude》; it was adapted into a film released in 2010.
- In 2015 she married stunt performer 下川真矢; they divorced in 2019.
- In 2021 she changed her name to "金子美广".
- In 2023 she appeared in the Hong Kong fashion sketch comedy 《东京爱情动作故事》.
- She was once rumoured to be involved with comedy king 志村健.

## Work features

- An innocent, cute idol image; one of the most representative AV actresses of the mid-2000s.
- Her catalogue covers uniforms, chijo, creampies and many other genres.
- After retiring she reinvented herself as an all-round entertainer, active on TV, film and other fields.
