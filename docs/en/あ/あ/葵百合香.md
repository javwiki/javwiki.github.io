## Introduction

葵百合香 is a Japanese AV actress. She debuted at ジュエルズプロダクション in June 2016 as "一松愛梨", working as a mature-woman (jukujo) actress. In July 2017 she moved to カプセルエージェンシー and changed her name to "八田愛梨"; in March 2018 she moved again to LINX and took her current name "葵百合香". Known for her tall 171cm figure and G-cup bust, her astonishing squirring volume earned her the nickname "鯨奥様 (whale ojōsan)". On 22 December 2025 she announced via X that from January 2026 she would become an exclusive actress of MONROE, a label under Madonna (Madonna announced the title the same day).
