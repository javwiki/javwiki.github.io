## Introduction

石原希望 is a Japanese AV actress and YouTuber. In June 2020 she debuted simultaneously at five studios, and her debut title topped the FANZA daily chart. Known for her Kansai dialect, cheerful personality and outstanding oral skill, she is celebrated as "the No. 1 newcomer". Half a year after her debut she ranked 2nd on FANZA's first-half actress sales chart, and in 2021 she won the Miss iD new-generation porn star award.
