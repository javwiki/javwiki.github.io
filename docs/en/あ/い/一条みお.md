## Introduction

一条みお is a Japanese AV actress who made her debut as an Idea Pocket exclusive in April 2018. Known for her petite 147cm figure and pure looks with C cups, she is celebrated as the "札幌精灵 (Sapporo spirit)". Before her debut she worked at a gym; she lives a two-base life between Tokyo and Hokkaido and has a deep love of and support for agriculture and dairy farming.
