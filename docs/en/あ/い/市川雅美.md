## Introduction

市川雅美 is a Japanese AV actress, actress and singer. She joined SOD as a publicity-department employee in 2014 and made her AV debut in January 2015 as "宣伝部入社1年目市川まさみ", becoming the first case of an SOD female employee turning into an exclusive actress.

She was the fifth-generation leader of 恵比寿マスカッツ and was known for her "神対応 (god-tier treatment of fans)". In April 2022 she announced her retirement from AV, but continues activities such as 恵比寿マスカッツ.

## Personal notes

- Born in Tokyo; blood type A.
- She was in the handball club for three years in high school.
- At 17 she suffered a herniated disc and at one point lost sensation in her lower body, living in a wheelchair.
- The actress she admires is 川上奈々美.
- As a former publicity staffer her treatment of fans is called "神対応 (god-tier)".
- In August 2018 she became the fifth-generation leader of 恵比寿マスカッツ.

## Work features

- Active as an SODstar exclusive actress, with a bishoujo / innocent image.
- Her debut title took the theme 「SOD宣伝部入社1年目」, drawing on her real experience as a former SOD publicity employee and creating plenty of buzz.
- As the fifth-generation leader of 恵比寿マスカッツ she combines idol activity with AV activity.
