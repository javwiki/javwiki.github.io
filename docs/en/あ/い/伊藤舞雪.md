## Introduction

伊藤舞雪 is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as a Kawaii* exclusive in March 2018 with the selling point of an "overwhelmingly slim waist". Known for an extreme 54cm waist paired with an F-cup bust in perfect proportion, fans call her the "Reiwa VR Queen". She is also an exclusive model for the fashion magazine 小恶魔ageha and enjoys enormous popularity in Japan and overseas (Taiwan, South Korea, Thailand and similar places).
