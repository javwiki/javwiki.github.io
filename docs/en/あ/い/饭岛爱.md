## Introduction

饭岛爱 is one of the most legendary actresses in the Japanese AV industry and a pioneer of AV actresses crossing into mainstream entertainment. After debuting in 1992 she became famous for lifting her skirt to show her T-back on the late-night programme 《ギルガメッシュないと》 and was called the "T-backの女王 (queen of the T-back)". After retiring she reinvented herself as a TV personality and writer; her semi-autobiographical novel 《柏拉图性爱》, published in 2000, became a bestseller and was adapted into a film and a TV drama. "ワタシ（私）的には…" made the top 10 of the 2000 buzzword grand prix. She retired due to kidney disease in 2007 and died alone at her home in Shibuya, Tokyo in December 2008 of pneumonia.

## Personal notes

- Born and raised in Kameido, Koto, Tokyo. She did well in middle school (top 10 in her year), but after her grandfather's death she lost her balance and began going down the wrong path.
- After dropping out of high school she was scouted while working as a hostess in Roppongi and entered the AV industry. Her stage name "爱" was given by the mama-san of the club, meaning "to be loved by everyone".
- At the start of her AV career she sold as a black-haired beauty; she only became a star after switching to a tanned gyaru look with brown hair.
- Around 1994 she retired from AV and turned fully into a TV personality.
- In 2000 she published 《柏拉图性爱》, revealing experiences of being lynched, AV appearances, STIs, an abortion and plastic surgery; it became a bestseller.
- She retired in March 2007 due to kidney disease (pyelonephritis). Her final 《サンデージャポン》 episode aired for only 15 minutes because of the Noto Peninsula earthquake.
- She died around 17 December 2008 and was found by a relative on 24 December. A farewell gathering was held at the Prince Hotel in Tokyo in March 2009, attended by about 1,500 people.
