## Introduction

冲田杏梨 is a former Japanese AV actress, entertainer, singer and creator. Born in Birmingham, United Kingdom, she began her debut promotion in November 2010 with the gimmick of "the strongest human body", and formally debuted as an S1 exclusive actress in February 2011, insuring her breasts for 100 million yen at her debut. Her largest trait is an L-cup (later developing to P-cup) bust. After retiring from AV in April 2016 she reinvented herself as a creator and singer; in 2023 she formed the dance and vocal unit BLACK DIAMOND and graduated in November 2025. Her total social-media following is close to 7 million. In 2021 she used the five-year clause to take all her titles off the various AV official sites.
