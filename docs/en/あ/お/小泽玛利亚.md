## Introduction

小泽玛利亚 is one of the most internationally known mixed-race actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She is of mixed Japanese and French descent — her mother is Japanese and her father French-Canadian — and has a distinctive, almost European face. After debuting at S1 in 2005 she quickly rose to the top of the industry, her "mixed-race beauty" image striking deep. Beyond her enormous popularity in Japan she built a huge fan base across Asia, and is one of the few AV actresses with international influence. After retiring in 2010 she moved to the Philippines to continue her entertainment career.
