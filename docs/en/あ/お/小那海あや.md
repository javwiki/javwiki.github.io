## Introduction

小那海あや is a Japanese AV actress, talent and gravure idol. She debuted as an アリスJAPAN exclusive in December 2016 under the name 佐々波綾 and retired in June 2018. Afterwards she worked as an internet personality as "おなみちゃん", and in 2022 restarted her entertainment activity as a gravure idol. In October 2023 she announced her return to AV, joined ACT ENTERTAINMENT and became a million exclusive actress. Even during her retirement she remained a high-profile figure, and her comeback title entered the FANZA mail-order floor ranking at #2 on its first week.

## Personal notes

- From Nara Prefecture. A fan of the Yokohama DeNA BayStars; when watching games she always wears underwear in the team colours as a talisman.
- She loves ramen and often shares ramen photos on SNS with the hashtag 「#ラーメン大好き小那海さん」.
- After becoming a gravure idol she clearly marked 「元AV女優」 (former AV actress) in her Twitter profile, but does not push it as a selling point.
- Before her debut she worked part-time as a manga editor at a large publisher.
- After retiring she worked as a contract employee at a telecommunications company, responsible for training and customer-satisfaction surveys.
- During her stage name 「佐々波綾」 her first-person pronoun was 「おなみ」, which later became the origin of her nickname 「おなみちゃん」 and her stage name 「小那海」.
- The actress she admires is 天使もえ.

## Work features

- Active as an アリスJAPAN exclusive from 2016 to 2018, drawing attention for a lolicon look and VR titles.
- Her VR titles before retirement held the record of three consecutive months at #1 on the DMM monthly AV actress chart.
- After her 2023 comeback, a DVD and a VR title were released in the same month (rare among exclusive actresses).
- From July 2024 she has been active as a kikaku-tantai performer across labels such as h.m.p, マキシング and グローリークエスト.
