## Introduction

大槻响 is a Japanese AV actress who debuted in 2008; with her distinctive personality and excellent acting she has become one of the longest-serving active actresses in the industry. She is a member of the anime / Vocaloid music idol unit T♡Project and is much loved by fans under the nickname "ひびやん".

大槻响 has won a number of major awards, including the DMM.R18 Adult Award 2016 Best Actress and the Best Actress awards at the 25th and 26th Pink Grand Prix. In November 2025 she became a dual exclusive of ワンズファクトリー and ダスッ — a rare case of becoming studio-exclusive for the first time 17–18 years after debut.
