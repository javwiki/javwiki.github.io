## Introduction

乙都咲乃 is a former Japanese AV actress who debuted as a Prestige exclusive in October 2017. Known for her petite 143cm figure and cute looks, fans affectionately call her "おとさき". She announced her retirement in April 2020, ending an AV career of about three years.
