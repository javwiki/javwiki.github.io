## Introduction

折原ほのか is a Japanese AV actress from Tokyo. In 2012 she worked as a gravure idol under the name 「赤井美月」, and made her AV debut that December as an E-BODY exclusive. From mid-2014 new releases paused; in May 2015 she moved to オーブエンターテイメント and made her comeback as 「折原ほのか」. From February 2020 she changed her name to 「神無月れな」 and moved to アルヴィロッソ. Her traits are an H-cup bust and large areolae; her gravure-idol work before her debut is often used as a title setting.

## Personal notes
