## Introduction

千乃あずみ is a Japanese AV actress born on 4 August 1993 in Hiroshima Prefecture, affiliated with the LIGHT agency. She debuted as a teamZERO exclusive in October 2013, positioned as a big-breast actress. From October 2014 she began appearing for other studios and became a kikaku-tantai performer. She retired after releasing her final title in February 2016. Her hobbies are playing with dogs and cooking.

- In her early career (October 2013 – September 2014) she was a teamZERO exclusive, releasing titles regularly on the 13th of each month

- A rich catalogue over her active years, with studios including E-BODY, MOODYZ, マドンナ, ワンズファクトリー, プレステージ, Fitch, アタッカーズ, h.m.p, クリスタル映像, アイエナジー, ヒビノ, ROCKET, セレブの友, ダスッ! and others
