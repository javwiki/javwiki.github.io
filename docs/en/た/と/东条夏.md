## Introduction

东条夏 is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as a FALENO exclusive in February 2020, later became a kikaku-tantai actress, and from 2023 has been a dual exclusive of 本中 and ダスッ. Known for a refreshing ojō-sama air, a slender figure and extremely high sensitivity, she was chosen as one of the "次世代清純派AV女優10人 (10 next-generation innocent AV actresses)". In August 2023 she announced a hiatus, but her popularity did not wane and her older titles kept charting.
