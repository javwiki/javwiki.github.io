## Introduction

友田彩也香 is one of the representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry, and is also a singer and actress. She debuted as a Kawaii* exclusive in October 2009 and quickly drew attention with her lively, cheerful personality and excellent acting.

Ayaka Tomoda was a member of 恵比寿マスカッツ1.5, and also of the idol units PINKEY and ミリオンガールズZ. In 2015 she won the Best Actress award at the 27th Pink Grand Prix, with her acting highly evaluated. She is known as a "プロレスオタク (pro-wrestling otaku)" and is in particular a huge fan of DRAGON GATE.
