## Introduction

She debuted as an Alice Japan exclusive in January 2017 (debut title 《【初見】鷹宮ゆい 職業はAV女優です。》, 13 January), featuring a petite figure (148cm) paired with an F-cup bust. From December 2017 she became a kikaku-tantai actress and has been active at many studios. On 4 October 2019 she announced via Twitter her move to Forty-Four Management and name change to 「来まえび」; on 10 July 2021 she announced her departure and name change to 「眠り姫」. On Fantia she runs the fan club 「ねむりーず♡」 (5,832 members, currently suspended). She has intermittent records of being on the roster of fūzoku shops in Kawasaki soapland, 虎の穴 (Tokyo / Osaka), Hiroshima and elsewhere over many years (per eronb, 2020—2026).

- Known for the contrast between a petite figure and a voluptuous bust.
