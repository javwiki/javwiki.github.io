## Introduction

高桥圣子 is a former Japanese gravure idol and AV actress. From 2012 she worked as an idol and gravure performer as 高崎聖子; she left the agency in January 2016, changed her name to 高橋しょう子 in April, and made her AV debut in May from MUTEKI with 《グラビア四天王たかしょーMUTEKI Debut》, winning seven crowns at AV OPEN 2016 with a MOODYZ title the same year. In 2017 she won the DMM.R18 Best New Actress award. She announced her retirement in April 2022, with a May title as her retirement work. After retiring she is active as a guitarist and model; in September 2025 she returned to gravure photography in FLASH after three years.

## Personal notes

- After seeing an SKE48 show she set her heart on becoming an idol and failed the SKE48 second-generation audition; in 2012 she debuted live as a member of the maid-idol unit 「もえりぃすたぁ」, and from October of that year was the guitarist of the idol unit CAMOUFLAGE, doing live idol work in Nagoya while working part-time at an idol café.
- Her gravure debut came in June 2013; she won the newcomer award at the "プロが選ぶアイドルDVD賞" (2013) and best newcomer at アサ芸Secret (2014); selected for 「日テレジェニック2015」 in 2015 but withdrew in October — she said on a TV programme that a leak incident damaged her image and made it hard to keep working, so she turned to AV (2016 日刊Sports interview).
- Her nickname 「たかしょー」 was coined by comedy duo Top Lead's 悠太 Asuma... — by comedy group Top Lead member; the comedian あいなぷぅ of パーパー was her middle-school classmate.
- She has played guitar constantly from her idol days through AV: in 2015 she formed the girls' rock band HöLDERLINS with キラ☆アン (left in March 2018 to focus on AV); she has a YouTube guitar channel 「たかしょーの69'n'Roll」.
- Her 2022 autobiography says she came from a relatively well-off family; her parents divorced in her first year of high school over religious beliefs on her mother's side, and transferring to an evening high school and working part-time was the trigger for her idol path.
- For her AV debut she referenced the works of 星野ナミ and aimed for Ai Uehara; her traits are a G cup and inverted nipples.
- On her retirement she told NEWS ポストセブン: "I gave it my all for six years; in this day and age I want to take care of my body." Her retirement title took two consecutive FANZA mail-order weekly crowns and finished 1st on the first-half title chart of 2022.

## Work features
