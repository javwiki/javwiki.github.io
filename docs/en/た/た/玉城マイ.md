## Introduction

玉城マイ is a Japanese AV actress, pachinko writer and gravure idol. She made her AV debut as an Idea Pocket exclusive in January 2014, and at the same time works on pachinko column serials and gravure activity, active in many fields. A Nogizaka46 fan, her oshi ( favourite member ) are Erika Ikuta and Maia Shirashi.
