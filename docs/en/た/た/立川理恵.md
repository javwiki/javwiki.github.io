## Introduction

立川理恵 is a former Japanese AV actress. She made her AV debut from 本中 in 2013 with the hook "Gカップ現役女子大生", drawing attention for a G-cup 90cm bust. In her debut year, as a kikaku-tantai performer, she densely appeared in titles from ムーディーズ, SOD, OPPAI, E-BODY, クリスタル映像 and many other labels. On 25 February 2014 本中 released the full-retirement best 《完全引退！立川理恵コンプリートBEST「私、卒業します。」》, after which she formally retired.
