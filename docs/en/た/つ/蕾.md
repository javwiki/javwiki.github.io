## Introduction

蕾 is a Japanese AV singer, manager and AV producer who debuted as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive in April 2006. Her debut title was 《新人×ギリギリモザイク》. She suspended activity due to poor health in March 2008 and made her comeback in May. In September 2013 she became a dual exclusive of MOODYZ and Wanz Factory. In February 2022 she announced her retirement at the end of April that year. Her interests are shopping, walking the dog and watching films.
