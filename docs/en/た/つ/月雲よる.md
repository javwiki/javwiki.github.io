## Introduction

月雲よる is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive in May 2023. She is known for the charm of the contrast between a voluptuous G-cup figure and a petite 153cm frame, and is called 「奇跡の美少女」. Before her debut she worked as a web livestreamer (ライバー). In December 2025 she announced her name change to 神木彩 (かみき あや) and her move to Attackers to continue exclusive activity.
