## Introduction

九十九メイ is a Japanese AV actress born on 20 May 1999 in Gunma Prefecture, affiliated with the ACT agency. She has debuted as an exclusive under several different names at different studios: in 2018 she debuted at E-BODY as 「滝沢ななお」, in 2020 at Fitch as 「長谷川りさ」 and at マドンナ as 「芦永れい」, and re-debuted as 「九十九メイ」 in 2022. From 2019 she became a kikaku-tantai performer; her titles feature a tall height, G cups, long legs and teasing roles. Her interest is fashion checking (ファッションチェック); her speciality is dance. Her father once worked in Korea; she spent from the second year of middle school to the third year of high school there and is a returnee child.
