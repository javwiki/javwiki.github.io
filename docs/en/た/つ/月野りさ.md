## Introduction

月野りさ is a former Japanese AV actress and stripper. She debuted as a kawaii* newcomer in July 2008 and was known for a baby face and roles as a schoolgirl and gyaru type. In 2010 she won the Nikkan Gendai Award at the SkyperfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards. She retired at the end of March 2012, and at her retirement 週刊現代 called her 「AV界のエンジェル (the angel of the AV world)」.
