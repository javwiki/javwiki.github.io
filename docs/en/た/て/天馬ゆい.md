## Introduction

天馬ゆい is a Japanese AV actress who debuted in April 2018 as 「上川星空」 and re-debuted after changing her name to 「天馬ゆい」 in March 2021. Known for a balanced 162cm figure and pure looks with D cups, she is active at Prestige, Fitch and many other labels.

## Personal notes

- From Aomori Prefecture. Interests: anime, games, cosplay; likes watching B-grade horror films.
- Calls herself "the talking type of socially anxious person".
- Her first time was at 18, with her 28-year-old part-time manager (a married man).
- Her motive for an AV debut: her boyfriend at the time cheated on her; feeling abandoned and reckless, she searched agency websites herself and applied.
