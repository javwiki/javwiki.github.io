## Introduction

天使萌 is one of the most representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry. Debuting as an S1 exclusive newcomer in July 2014, she quickly rose to the top of the industry with her pure looks and perfect figure. She is also a singer and YouTuber, made music as "AMATSUKA", and was a member of the idol unit 惠比寿★麝香葡萄. She moved to FALENO in April 2020, announced in July 2024 that she would retire in June 2025, and formally ended her actress activity in June 2025.

## Personal notes

- Born in Tokyo; at debut affiliated with Bambi Promotion, later moving to Bstar in the same group.
- Interest: cooking; speciality: massage.
- Often plays schoolgirl roles, but in private calls herself a fujoshi and often wears tracksuits.
- From the softball club, with an athletic temperament.
- Called a "chameleon actress": she prefers titles with complete scripts where she can go deep into the character, and her acting is excellent.
- She publicly opposed the 《AV出演被害防止・救済法》 established in 2022, arguing that industry workers should take part in the discussion together.
