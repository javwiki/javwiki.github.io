## Introduction

ティア is a former AV actress of Japanese and American mixed heritage active in Japan, also working as a gravure idol, talent, actress and YouTuber. She debuted in 2010 as 「黒川メイサ」, re-debuted as an E-BODY exclusive under the name 「ティア」 in 2012, and became an S1 exclusive from 2013. She joined 恵比寿★マスカッツ in 2015; after announcing her retirement in 2016 she retracted it and moved to Idea Pocket, formally ending her AV activity in 2018.

## Personal notes

## Work features

- Debuted as an E-BODY exclusive under the name 「ティア」 in June 2012 (debut title 『E-BODY 専属デビュー 南の島の美少女』).
- An S1 exclusive from May 2013 to September 2015, appearing in story-driven titles such as 『秘密捜査官の女』.
- Moved to Idea Pocket in 2016,辗转 アタッカーズ, ダスッ!, Rookie, マドンナ and other labels in 2017, and ended her AV activity after her retirement title in January 2018.
