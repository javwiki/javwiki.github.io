## Introduction

A teasing series under Fitch (フィッチ); the core concept is an actress who tempts with dirty talk and stops right before ejaculation (寸止め) to keep her partner edging (deliberately drawing it out). The series is characterised by the actress toying with the male performer through "tsundome", playing up the dirty-talk lines and first-person (POV) immersion.

## Features

- The whole series runs on "dirty talk plus tsundome edging"
- Mostly takes the form of solo work (単体作品)
- Heavy use of first-person POV to deepen immersion
- The actresses are mostly big-breasted, tease-type
- Occupational settings are common (news reporter, tutor, flight attendant, married woman etc.)
