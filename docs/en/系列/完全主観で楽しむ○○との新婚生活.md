## Introduction

The New Marriage Series under the umbrella of Planet Plus was launched in February 2019 with EMT-001. The core concept is to experience daily interaction with the wife (female) in the context of the marriage, including cooking, bathing, sleeping, etc., from a first person ' s subjective perspective.

The title of the series is "The Total Male Marriages" and the female is given a female name in the zero. Some of the works are made up of several groups, the BOX collection, which is published under the PPPX symbol.
