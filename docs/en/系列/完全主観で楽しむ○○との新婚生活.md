## Introduction

A first-person newlywed series under プラネットプラス (Planet Plus), started in February 2019 with EMOT-001. The core concept is experiencing everyday newlywed life with your wife (the actress) from a first-person viewpoint, including intimate moments in cooking, bathing, sleeping and similar scenes.

The titles uniformly read 「完全主観で楽しむ○○との新婚生活」, with the actress's name filled into the ○○. Some works have multi-disc BOX compilations released under PPBX codes.
