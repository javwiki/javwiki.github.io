## Introduction

A teasing-nurse series under IdeaPocket (アイデアポケット), started in May 2016 with IPZ-746 (桐嶋りの). The core concept is an actress playing a seductive nurse (痴女ナース) with the oral creampie as the main feature. The series name translates literally as "the naughty-teasing nurse loves creampies in the mouth".

The series started with IPZ codes (2016–2017), switched to IPX codes in 2017, and still had follow-up works in 2020 and after. The latest work in 2023 is IPZZ-158 (朝日りお).

## Features

- Core play: the teasing nurse performs oral sex to a finish in the mouth
- The whole series is solo work
- Released under IPZ codes in 2016–2017
- Switched to IPX codes from 2017
- Switched to IPZZ codes from 2023
- Uniform cover style: nurse uniform plus teasing poses
