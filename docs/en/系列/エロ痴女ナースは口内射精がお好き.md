## Introduction

The IdeaPocket (アイデアポケット)-flavored nurse series was launched in May 2016 with IPZ-746 (桐嶋りの). The core concept is that of a female-pretentious-playing tempting nurse (smuggle-smuggled nurse), with the main play being the oral ejaculation. The series is translated as "The Good-Skinned Nurse likes to ejaculate."

The series started with the IPZ sign (2016-2017), changed to the IPX sign in 2017 and continues in 2020 and beyond. The latest version of the year 2023 is IPZ-158 (Adamiya).
