## Introduction

An extra-long-running popular series under PRESTIGE (プレステージ), started in November 2014 with ABP-210 (鈴村あいり). The core concept is a female student who serves as her sports club's manager but ends up as the male members' 「性处理ペット」 (sexual-servicing pet), forced into group sex.

The titles uniformly read 「女子マネージャーは、僕達の性処理ペット。XXX 女優名」, each title starring one actress. Early on, some first-installment titles read 「部活の先生は、僕達の性処理ペット。」 and are filed under this series as well. Sports bloomers (ブルマ), sweat and shame scenes are the series' signature elements.

Numbering ran from 001 to 041 over more than a decade, with BEST compilations as well. ABP codes were used from 2014 to 2022, switching to the ABW prefix from 2022.

## Features

- Core play: the manager is sexually serviced by the male members as a group
- The whole series is solo work, each title starring one actress
- Signature outfits: sports bloomers (ブルマ), gym clothes, tracksuits
- Early subtitles included 「部活の先生は、僕達の性処理ペット。」
- Uniform cover style: sports scenes plus expressions of shame
- Runtime: about 170 minutes for ABP, about 195 minutes for ABW
- Some works have leaked uncensored versions
