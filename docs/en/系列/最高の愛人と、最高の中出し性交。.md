## Introduction

An extra-long-running affair series under PRESTIGE (プレステージ), started in July 2016 with SGA-060; by now 85 works have been released (BEST compilations included). The core concept is the male performer having the highest-quality creampie sex with the finest possible mistress (lover).

The series sells itself on the affair / cheating (愛人 / 不倫) and the sense of moral transgression, and is one of PRESTIGE's most representative long-running series.
