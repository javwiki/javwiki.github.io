## Introduction

A big-breast training series led by Fitch (フィッチ) with h.m.p taking part, started in April 2018 with JUFD-893. The core setting is a "家出娘" (runaway girl) brought home by the male lead and put through breeding training (種付け調教) from morning to night, becoming his "爆乳生ハメ奴隷" (big-breast raw-sex slave).

The titles uniformly follow the format 「家出娘を自宅に連れ込み朝から晩まで種付け調教 俺専用！爆乳生ハメ奴隷 女優名」. Output stabilised from the JUFE era (2019 onwards), and a BEST compilation appeared in 2020.

## Features

- Core play: training a big-breast runaway girl into a raw-creampie slave
- The whole series is solo work
- 2018 used JUFD codes (Fitch's old code system)
- Switched to JUFE codes from 2019 (Fitch's new code system)
- h.m.p issued two titles under HODV codes (2021)
- Titles uniformly carry the 「家出娘を自宅に連れ込み朝から晩まで種付け調教」 prefix
- Uniform cover style: the actress's bust played up in room / entrance-hall scenes
