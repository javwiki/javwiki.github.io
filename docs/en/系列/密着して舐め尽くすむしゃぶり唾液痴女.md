## Introduction

A saliva-tease series under Fitch (フィッチ), started in February 2018 with JUFD-865 (美谷朱里). The core concept is an actress licking the male performer all over while pressed right against him (密着), playing up the intimacy and aggressive teasing of saliva exchange, kissing and licking.

The titles uniformly read 「密着して舐め尽くす むしゃぶり唾液痴女」 — "the saliva tease who presses close and licks you all over". Codes moved from JUFD to JUFE, and there is one 8-hour and one 12-hour BEST compilation each.

## Features

- Core play: a tease whose attack centres on saliva and licking
- The whole series is solo work
- Uniform title format: 「密着して舐め尽くす むしゃぶり唾液痴女 女優名」
- Early works (2018) used JUFD codes
- Switched to JUFE codes from 2019
- Emphasises three elements: 密着 (pressed close), 舐め尽くす (licked all over), 唾液痴女 (saliva tease)
- Director is often 五右衛門
