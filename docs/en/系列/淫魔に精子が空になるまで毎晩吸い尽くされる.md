## Introduction

A succubus-themed series under h.m.p, started in March 2017 with HOMA-011. The core concept is a "淫魔 (succubus)" who appears every night to feed on a man's semen until it is completely drained. Set against a fantasy backdrop, the series emphasises the succubus actress milking the male performer of his ejaculation again and again.

The name translates literally as "sucked dry by a succubus every night until the semen runs out", and it is one of h.m.p's more representative fantasy erotic series. A BEST compilation, HOMA-066, appeared in 2020.

## Features

- Core play: a succubus actress milking ejaculation repeatedly
- The whole series is solo work
- A fantasy worldview
- Some works come with Chinese subtitles
