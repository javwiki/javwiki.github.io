## Introduction

An extra-long-running married-woman secretary series under Madonna (マドンナ), started in November 2019 with JUL-026; by now 43 works have been released (compilations included). The core setting is a mature, beautiful married-woman secretary in the president's office, having sweat-and-kiss-filled creampie sex with the president (the male performer).

The series uses the forbidden workplace situation of "secretary × president's office" as its stage, playing up mature feminine charm and the intimacy of kissing and creampies. The codes started at JUL; later some works moved to JUQ (from 2022) and JUR (from 2024, FANZA-limited editions with a signed photo and panties).

## Features

- Core setting: a married-woman secretary has forbidden creampies with the president in his office
- The whole series is produced by Madonna
- Released under JUL codes 2019–2021
- Switched to JUQ codes from 2022
- Some works moved to JUR codes from 2024 (FANZA-limited signed-photo + panty sets)
- Some works come with a Blu-ray edition
- A compilation appeared in 2021: JUSD-944
- Directors include ながえ, 千葉慎太郎 and others
