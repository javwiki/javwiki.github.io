## Introduction

The Madonna ( Ma) series of super-long-life wife and secretary, launched in November 2019 as JUL-026, has been published 43 works (including the compilation). The core is set to have a well-developed wife and secretary in the office of the President, who has a sweaty and kissy mid-term sexual intercourse with the President.

The series is staged by the Secretary x President's Office, which emphasizes the charm of mature women and the intimateness of kissing/intimation. The numbering starts with JUL, and later parts are converted to JUQ (2022) and JUR (2024, FANZA restricted editions with real writing + underwear).
