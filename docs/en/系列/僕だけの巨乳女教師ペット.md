## Introduction

The Tameike Goro training series, which is entitled "Magic Teachers", was launched in January 2012 with the MDYD-659. The core concept is to train the titty teacher as an exclusive sex pet.

The series is translated directly into "The Breast Teacher's Pets" and is one of the early representative series of the Slurping Pool.
