## Introduction

The Madonna (single)-flavored-life wife series, launched in October 2018 with the Juy-642 (single-chick) and published 45 works to date. The core is a romantic story of male employees travelling with their long-time enthusiasts in business hotels, who accidentally "hospitals" (singing a room together) because of room stress or other reasons.

The series is known for its emotional development in the workplace and at a slow pace, emphasizing the charm and taboo of women superiors as wives/women throughout the year. The JUY has been replaced by the JUL and continued to be released since 2020.

## Series Characteristics

- Core set: occasional rooming with male and female supervisors on mission
- A single work for Madonna.
- 2018-2019 Issued in the JUY sign
- Turned into a JUL sign in 2020
- The early title is "The first-born man to see the top lady of the house of the first generation."
- The title of the post-opsis piece is "A first-class photo house... for the female boss."
- Multiple masters (JUSD-866,JUSD-914,JUSD-965) and BEST SELETECION (JUMS-047)
