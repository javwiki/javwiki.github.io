## Introduction

A big-breast training series under Fitch (フィッチ), started in 2011 and one of Fitch's classic long-running series. The core concept is an actress playing all kinds of professional women (nurse, teacher, manager, widow etc.) who are trained as a "爆乳ペット (big-breast pet)". The titles uniformly read 「僕のペットは爆乳○○ (occupation)」, with the subtitle 「敏感な乳房が咽び泣く○○調教」.

## Features

- Title format unified as 「僕のペットは○○（occupation）爆乳○○」
- Core theme: training of sensitive breasts (敏感な乳房が咽び泣く)
- Each work sets a different occupational background for variety
- The whole series is solo work (単体作品), each title starring a single actress
- Uniform cover style, playing up the actress's bust and occupational outfit
- Directors are often TAKE-D, つぶやきジロー and similar
