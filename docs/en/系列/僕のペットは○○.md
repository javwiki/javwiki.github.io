## Introduction

Fitch (フィッチ) is a classic long-lived series of Fitch, launched in 2011. The central concept is that women perform various professional roles (nurses, teachers, administrators, widows, etc.) and are taught as "blowing milk (maids). The series is entitled "The Spectrum of the Flying Milk O'Oil" and is entitled "The Sensitious Breast Sobbing O'Oil".

## Series Characteristics

- The title is in a single format called "Faces of the Nation".
- Core theme: Sensitive breast-breathing (Sensitious breast-sobbing)
- The work sets different occupational contexts, adding to the scenario changes
- The whole series is a single piece (show), each one being led by a woman.
- Cover styles are uniform, with emphasis on female-favourable breast-breathing and professional dress
- The director is TAKE-D, the director is a small-scale director.
