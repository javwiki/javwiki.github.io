## Introduction

A popular series under PREMIUM (プレミアム), started in September 2019 under the name 「強制中出し 射精執行官」. The core concept is an actress playing a "射精執行官" (ejaculation officer) who punishes "impure sperm" with forced creampies. The series' signature trait is the actress milking the male performer in a **爆速騎乗位 (high-speed cowgirl)**, emphasising the doS (super-sadistic) actress's attacking posture and the forced-creampie setup.

The early titles were 「強制中出し 射精執行官」 (ABP-894, ABP-906); from the third work (ABP-935) the name changed to 「中出し 射精執行官」. In 2021 a BEST compilation 「中出し執行官BEST 8時間 vol.01」 (FIR-004) appeared. The numbering moved up from ABP to ABW and then ABF (from 2023).

## Features

- Core play: a doS ejaculation officer performs forced creampies in a high-speed cowgirl position
- The whole series is solo work (単体作品), each title starring a single actress
- Early titles contained 「強制中出し」; later unified as 「中出し 射精執行官」
- Uniform cover style: the actress in a black or dark tight uniform, in a riding or teasing pose
- From 2022 (after ABW-297) an MGS bonus video was added
