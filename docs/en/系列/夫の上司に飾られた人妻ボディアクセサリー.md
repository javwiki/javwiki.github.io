## Introduction

The series of NTRs under Madonna (the " ナ " ) was launched in January 2019 with the name of Juy-734. The central concept is that the husband's superiors use their authority to decorate and play off the forbidden stories by using their wives as "body ornaments."

The series is translated as "The Man's wife's body ornaments decorated by her husband's boss", emphasizing that the wife is a plaything for her superiors under duress/involved circumstances, filled with shame and NTR elements.
