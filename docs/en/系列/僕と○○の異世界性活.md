## Introduction

The series of different world role-playing under the umbrella of the PRESTIGE ( P) was launched in July 2019 with the ABC-874 (the "Zoos of the Gardens"). The core concept is that the male protagonists and the female masters take risks in different worlds, and the female masters are sexually active in the "most powerful outfits" - the various cosplay costumes (fantasty costumes such as magicians, knights, elves, etc.).

The title of the series is "Father's Aberrant World Sex Activity", and the female name is added to the section "Female Sex." Numbering starts with BP and changes to ABW later.
