## Introduction

The ultra-long life series, which spans two manufacturers, was launched in May 2011 by **SOD Create (SODクリエイト)** with SACE-006. Transferred to **PRESTAGE(プレステージ)** in 2020, continued to be issued under the name ABW/ABF.

The central concept is that women perform the role of "who are naked domestic workers" and perform housework such as cleaning, washing and other things in a state of total nakedness, and are subjected to aggression or solicitation by the male owner during the process. The series highlights the visual deviations of the "black apron" or "black-nose housework".

## Series Characteristics

- Core play: domestic service and sexual activity in full naked
- Pre-production by SOD Create (2011-2017, SACE Quote)
- later produced by PRESTIGE (202023, ABW/ABF)
- It's a single piece.
- Some of the works are unsolved.
