## Introduction

An extra-long-running married-woman series under VENUS (ヴィーナス), started in October 2012 with VAGU-029 and one of VENUS's most representative series. The core setting is a beautiful married woman who, for various reasons, plays a "マネキン" (mannequin / shop dummy) — becoming a doll in her husband's fantasy, willingly becoming one for her husband, or being forced into it at a customer's request.

The series has a number of sub-themes, including 「人形に恋した男の妄想セックス」「帰ってきた麗しのマネキン夫人」「最愛の夫のため…マネキンになった私」「クレーム処理のため自らマネキンになった女上司」「恥辱のマネキン夫人」, each work built around a different situation.

## Features

- Core setting: a married woman / actress plays a mannequin (マネキン)
- The whole series is a VENUS exclusive production
- Released under VAGU codes (from early VAGU-029 onwards)
- Contains several sub-theme branches
- Still being issued in compilation form in 2024
- Compilation naming: 麗しのマネキン夫人 総集編 Vol.1–4

## Releases

By 2024, 45 works had been released (compilations included), covering many mannequin situations; it is one of VENUS's longest-lived manga / fantasy adaptation series.
