## Introduction

An isekai cosplay series under PRESTIGE (プレステージ), started in July 2019 with ABP-874 (園田みおん). The core concept is the male lead and the actress adventuring in another world, with the actress wearing the "最強セクシー装備" (strongest sexy equipment) — that is, all kinds of cosplay outfits (mage, knight, elf and other fantasy costumes) — while having sex.

The titles uniformly read 「僕と○○の異世界性活」, with the actress's name filled into the ○○. Codes started at ABP and later moved to ABW.
