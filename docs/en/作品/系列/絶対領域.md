## Introduction

A small series by エスワン ナンバーワンスタイル (S1) built on the theme 「絶対領域」, started in January 2019, with 5 releases in total.

"絶対領域 (Zettai Ryouiki)" is the concept of the thigh area shown when a woman wears a short skirt with knee-high socks, and a classic moe trait of ACG culture. The series features S1's top actresses in knee-high socks (ニーハイ), school or work uniforms, playing up the thigh line and the appeal of zettai ryouiki. Every title follows the same pattern: 「絶対領域 + description + 小悪魔ニーハイ美少女 + actress name」.

Although it was short-lived (released only in 2019), the line-up was impressive, gathering several of S1's top actresses.

## Features

- Core concept: zettai ryouiki (exposed thigh) + knee-high sock seduction
- The whole series is solo work, 5 titles in total
- All released under SSNI codes
- Titles uniformly carry the 「小悪魔ニーハイ美少女」 subtitle
- Uniform cover style: school/work uniform plus close-ups of the legs in knee-high socks
- Finished after a single year of release in 2019
