## Introduction

A classic long-running series under MARRION (マリオン), started in January 2013 with MKZ-008; by now 70 works have been released over more than 13 years. The core concept is 「可愛い顔してデカ尻！！」 ("cute face, huge butt!!"), playing up an actress with a pretty, small face and a disproportionately huge rear.

The series is directed by 宮本小次郎, treating the actress's big butt as its main selling point, with all kinds of butt close-ups, doggy-style viewpoints and butt-related play. From 2020 MMKZ codes took over from MKZ codes.

## Features
