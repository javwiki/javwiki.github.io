## Introduction

An extra-long-running married-woman series under Madonna (マドンナ), started in October 2018 with JUY-642 (黒川すみれ); by now 45 works have been released. The core setting is a male employee on a business trip with the female boss he has long admired: at the business hotel, tight rooms or other circumstances leave them unexpectedly "相部屋" (sharing one room), from which a romantic erotic story unfolds.

The series is known for its workplace settings and slow emotional development, playing up the charm and taboo air of the female boss as a married / older woman. From 2020 JUL codes took over from JUY codes.

## Features

- Core setting: bosses and subordinates of different sexes end up sharing a room on a trip
- The whole series is solo work produced by Madonna
- Released under JUY codes 2018–2019
- Switched to JUL codes from 2020
- Early titles were 「出張先のビジネスホテルでずっと憧れていた女上司とまさかまさかの相部屋宿泊」
- Later some titles were shortened to 「出張先相部屋… 女上司とまさかの～」
- Several compilations (JUSD-866, JUSD-914, JUSD-965) and a BEST SELECTION (JUMS-047)
