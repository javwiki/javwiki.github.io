## Introduction

A series under Madonna (マドンナ) built on NTR training, started in January 2019 with JUY-734. The core concept is a forbidden story in which the husband's boss uses his position to deck out and play with a married woman as a "body accessory (ボディアクセサリー)".

The name translates literally as "the married woman decorated as a body accessory by her husband's boss", emphasising the married woman becoming the boss's plaything against her will, full of shame and NTR elements.
