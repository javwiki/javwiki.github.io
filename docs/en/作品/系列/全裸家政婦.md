## Introduction

An extra-long-lived series spanning two studios: started in May 2011 by **SOD Create (SODクリエイト)** with SACE-006, then continued from 2020 under **PRESTIGE (プレステージ)** with ABW/ABF codes.

The core concept is an actress playing a "全裸家政婦" (naked housekeeper), doing chores such as cleaning and laundry completely naked, during which she is assaulted by the male employer or entices him herself. The series emphasises the visual contrast of a "naked apron" or "doing housework in the buff".

## Features

- Core play: housekeeping in the nude, with sex
- Early period produced by SOD Create (2011–2017, SACE codes)
- Later period produced by PRESTIGE (2020–2023, ABW/ABF codes)
- The whole series is solo work
- Some works have leaked uncensored versions
