## Introduction

A training series on the theme of the "big-breast female teacher" under 溜池ゴロー (Tameike Goro), started in January 2012 with MDYD-659. The core concept is training a big-breast female teacher into an exclusive sex pet.

The name translates literally as "my one and only big-breast teacher pet", and it is one of Tameike Goro's representative early series.
