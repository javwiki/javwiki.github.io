## Introduction

A long-running kikaku series under ROCKET (ロケット), started in September 2012 with the first work RCT-436. The core concept is an actress playing a female TV announcer (女子アナ) who talks dirty, masturbates and has sex while reading the news. The series' greatest trait is "dirty talk to the camera", emphasising viewer immersion and its use as masturbation aid.

Early on the series was named 「淫語女子アナ」 + number; from the sixth work it used subtitles (「下半身丸出し淫語STATION」「街角淫語突撃レポート」 etc.). From the eleventh work it switched to the RCTD product-code prefix, with cover and production quality upgraded further. The latest work in 2026 is the 38th (RCTD-710), and the series is still running.

## Features

- Core play: the actress, as an announcer, talks dirty to the camera, masturbates and has sex
- The whole series is non-exclusive kikaku solo work
- Early codes were RCT (up to the 10th work); from the 11th work they became RCTD
- Some works have a smartphone-recommended (スマホ推奨) version (TRCT prefix)
- Individual subtitles began with the sixth work
- Derivative works: RCT-546 (facial-finish SP), RCT-977 (a マジックミラー号 tie-in), RCTD-389 (mature-women pro wrestling)
- Compilation: RCTD-319 (a 10-hour, 2-disc set)
