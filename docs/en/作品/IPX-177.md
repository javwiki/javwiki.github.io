## Storyline

The tsundere little sister played by Minami Aizawa is forced by her brother to put on knee-high socks, creating an "absolute territory" that belongs to him alone. Originally cocky, the sister gradually awakens her teasing nature under her brother's training and finally remakes herself entirely into the shape he likes.

## Features

- Knee-highs × zettai ryouiki theme
- Tsundere little-sister training theme
