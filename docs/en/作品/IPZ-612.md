## Storyline

The beauty played by 木南日菜 appears poised and elegant on the surface and is adored by her students, yet in private she uses her impressive figure to seduce innocent male students. Under the pretext of after-school tutoring she wields her bust as a weapon, breaking down her students' defences step by step and running a series of forbidden "extra lessons". From the office to an empty classroom, she controls every dangerous game herself.
