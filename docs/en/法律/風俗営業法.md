## Summary

The customary industry law regulates the conduct of business in Japan, including licensing of the production and distribution of AVs, restrictions on the place of business, advertising regulations, etc.

## Main elements

### Licence

- The permission of the local public security committee is required for the production and sale of AV
- Need to meet certain standards of equipment and sites
- Allow annual updates

### Business restrictions

- Business time limits
- Restrictions on the place of business
- Advertising restrictions

### Advertising regulations

- No exaggerating, false advertising.
- Need to indicate necessary information
- Prohibition of propaganda for minors

## Linkage to the AV industry

The production and sale of AVs is part of the industry and is subject to the licensing, operation, advertising and so on.
