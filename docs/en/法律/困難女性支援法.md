# The Women in Hardship Support Act

## Summary

The Act provides comprehensive support to women facing various difficulties, including financial assistance, employment support, psychological counselling, legal assistance, etc. The Act provides for support for AV performers who have suffered from breakdowns in social relations and mental health problems as a result of their performance experience.

## Main elements

### Supporting objects

- Women facing difficulties such as violence, abuse, poverty, etc.
- Women in need of protection and support
- Including women who have experienced social discrimination and cyber violence since the AV

### Support measures

- Economic assistance
- Employment support
- Psychological counselling
- Legal aid
- Temporary asylum

## Linkage to the AV industry

The Act provides for comprehensive government support for the AV performers who may face cyberviolence, social discrimination, trauma and so forth as a result of the dissemination of their work.
