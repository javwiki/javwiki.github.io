# 困難女性支援法

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Official name | 困難な問題を抱える女性への支援に関する法律 |
| Common name | 困難女性支援法 |
| Statute number | 令和3年法律第46号 |
| In force | 1 October 2021 |
| Administered by | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare |

## Overview

The law provides comprehensive support for women facing various hardships, including financial aid, employment support, counselling and legal aid. When AV performers suffer broken social relationships, mental-health problems and similar consequences because of their performances, they can receive support under this law.

## Main provisions

### Who is supported

- Women facing violence, abuse, poverty and similar hardships
- Women in need of protection and support
- Including women who face social discrimination and online harassment after AV performances

### Support measures

- Financial aid
- Employment support
- Counselling
- Legal aid
- Temporary shelter

## Relevance to the AV industry

Because their work spreads online, AV performers may face online harassment, social discrimination, psychological trauma and similar problems, and can receive comprehensive government support through this law.
