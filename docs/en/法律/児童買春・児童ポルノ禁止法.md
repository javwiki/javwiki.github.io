## Overview

This is the law protecting children from sexual exploitation; it bans child prostitution and the production, possession, distribution and display of child pornography.

## Main provisions

### Prohibited acts

- Child prostitution (sexual acts with a child for reward)
- Producing, possessing, distributing or displaying child pornography
- Brokering or mediating child prostitution
- Inducement for the purpose of child prostitution

### Penalties

- Producing child pornography: imprisonment for 3 years or more
- Possessing child pornography: imprisonment for 1 year or more
- Distributing child pornography: imprisonment for 3 years or more
- Child prostitution: imprisonment for 5 years or more, or a fine

## Relevance to the AV industry

The law matters to the AV industry through the verification of performers' ages. AV producers must strictly verify performers' ages to avoid violating the law. Minors appearing in AV may constitute crimes related to child pornography.
