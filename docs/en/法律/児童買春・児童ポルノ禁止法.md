# The kid who buys the baby is forbidden.

## Summary

The Act is a law to protect children from sexual exploitation and prohibits the production, possession and dissemination of child prostitution and child pornography.

## Main elements

### Prohibition of conduct

- Child prostitution (for paid sex with the child)
- Children's hobbies make, hold, disseminate, show
- Media, good offices for children
- Inducement for the purpose of selling children for sex

### Penalties

- Child-making: imprisonment for more than 3 years
- Holds a child in a male-noodle: 1 year or more
- Dissemination of children ' s hobbies: imprisonment for more than 3 years
- Child prostitution: imprisonment for more than five years or a fine

## Linkage to the AV industry

The effect of the Act on the AV industry is to ensure the trueness of the age of the performers. The AV producer must strictly verify the age of the actor and avoid violating the Act. The performance of an AV by a minor may constitute a related offence for a child.
