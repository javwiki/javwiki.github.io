## Summary

Civil law is the basic law of Japanese civil law and provides for legal relations between private persons, including the basic legal system of contract, tort, kinship, inheritance, etc. The amendment introduced in April 2022, which reduced the age of majority from 20 to 18 years, had a significant impact on the ability of the AV performers to sign.

## Main relevant articles

### Age of majority

- Before 1 April 2022: 20 years old, age of majority
- 1 April 2022: 18 years of age as of age
- Persons aged 18-19 may conclude a contract independently without the consent of their guardian

### Contractual capacity

- The consent of the guardian is required for the minor (under 18 years of age) to enter into a contract
- Adults can make independent contracts.

### Violations

- Liability for damage to persons who have violated the rights of others by wilful or negligent action
- Applicable to violations in the AV performance (e.g., unconscioned video transmission)

### Avoidance

- Contracts concluded as a result of fraud or coercion may be avoided
- For the wrongful contract in the AV performance
