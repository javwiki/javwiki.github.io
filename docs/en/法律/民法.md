# 民法

## Overview

The Civil Code is the basic statute of Japanese private law: it sets out legal relations between private parties, covering contracts, torts, family, inheritance and other basic institutions. The revision in force from April 2022 lowered the age of majority from 20 to 18, with an important effect on performers' capacity to contract.

## Key provisions

### Age of majority

- Before 1 April 2022: 20 was the age of majority
- From 1 April 2022: 18 is the age of majority
- Persons aged 18–19 may conclude contracts independently, without a guardian's consent

### Capacity to contract

- Minors (under 18) need a guardian's consent to contract
- Adults may contract independently

### Torts

- A person who intentionally or negligently infringes another's rights bears liability for damages
- Applies to torts in AV performances (such as non-consensual distribution of images)

### Right of rescission

- A contract concluded through fraud or coercion may be rescinded
- Applies to improper contracting in AV performances

## Relevance to the AV industry

| Point | Notes |
| --- | --- |
