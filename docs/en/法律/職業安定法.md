## Summary

The Employment Stability Act regulates employment services such as employment exchanges, labour assignments and the like, and protects the legitimate rights and interests of workers.

## Main elements

### Employment

- Employment offers require permission from the Minister of Health, Labour or the Prefectural Governor
- Prohibition of unfair employment practices
- Protection of the privacy of job seekers

### Protection of workers

- Prohibition of forced labour
- Prohibition of improper dismissal
- Protection of workers ' legal rights and interests

## Linkage to the AV industry

AV productions involve the protection of workers ' rights and interests (actors, workers). Actors, as workers, enjoy the protections provided for in the Act, including the prohibition of forced labour and the protection of privacy. The actor may defend his rights in violation of the Act by the producers of the AV.
