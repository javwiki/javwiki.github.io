## Summary

The law on the dispatch of workers regulates the dispatch of labour, protects the legal rights and interests of the sending workers and establishes the obligations of the sending and employing units.

## Main elements

### Dispatch authorization

- The Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare is required to grant permission to work in the labour force.
- A certain qualification requirement is met

### Protection of workers

- Prohibition of improper deployment
- Protection of wages, benefits for sending workers
- Prohibition of differential treatment

### Obligations of employers

- Health obligations for sending workers
- Prohibition of forced labour
- Protection of workers ' legal rights and interests

## Linkage to the AV industry

AV actors are often involved in production as models, actors, etc., and relationships with producers may involve labour assignments. The Act provides protection for AV performers as workers and prohibits inappropriate dispatch and forced labour.
