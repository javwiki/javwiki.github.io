## Summary

The Act is aimed at preventing the victim from being harmed by vengeful pornography and prohibits the dissemination of intimate images of others without consent.

## Main elements

### Prohibition of conduct

- Provision of personal life images to third parties without consent
- Dissemination of personal life images on the Internet
- Images provided for the purpose of harming others

### Penalties

- Criminal penalties (imprisonment or fine) for violating the law
- The communicator can be held criminally liable

### Victims ' rights

- The distributor can be asked to stop spreading.
- Could request the deletion of the relevant image
- Compensation for damages may be claimed

## Linkage to the AV industry

The Act provides legal protection to the AV performers who encounter the dissemination of images without consent (e.g., outflows, forwarding, etc.). Complementarity with the AV Act on the Prevention of Scripts provides a full range of legal remedies for the performers.
