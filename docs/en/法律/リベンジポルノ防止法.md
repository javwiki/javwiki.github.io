# リベンジポルノ防止法

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Official name | 私事性的画像記録の提供等による被害の防止に関する法律 |
| Common name | リベンジポルノ防止法, revenge-porn prevention law |
| Statute number | 平成26年法律第128号 |
| In force | 1 January 2015 |
| Administered by | Ministry of Justice; National Police Agency |

## Overview

The law aims to prevent the harm of revenge porn and bans the non-consensual distribution of another person's intimate images.

## Main provisions

### Prohibited acts

- Providing another person's images of private life to a third party without consent
- Distributing another person's images of private life over the network
- Providing images with the intent to harm another person

### Penalties

- Offenders face criminal penalties (imprisonment or a fine)
- Distributors may be held criminally liable

### Rights of victims

- May demand that the distributor stop distributing
- May demand deletion of the relevant images
- May claim damages

## Relevance to the AV industry

If an AV performer suffers non-consensual distribution of images (leaks, re-uploads and similar), the law offers legal protection. Together with the AV performance harm prevention law it is complementary, giving performers comprehensive legal relief.
