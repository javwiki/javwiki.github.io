## Summary

The international treaty aims to prohibit trafficking and sexual exploitation and to protect victims. Japan, as a State party, is obliged to take measures to prevent trafficking and protect victims.

## Main elements

### Prohibition of conduct

- Trafficking in persons (recruitment, transport, transfer for the purpose of exploitation)
- Sexual exploitation
- Forced labour
- Slavery

### State party obligations

- Criminalization of trafficking in persons
- Protection of victims
- Prevention of trafficking in persons
- International cooperation

## Linkage to the AV industry

The presence of deception and coercion in the AV industry may constitute trafficking in persons. The treaty provides an international legal basis for preventing exploitation in the AV industry.
