# Laws

This directory collects the Japanese laws and regulations related to the AV industry, providing legal background for the encyclopedia's entries.

## Legal entries

| Law | Common name | In force since | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [AV出演被害防止・救済法](AV出演被害防止・救済法.md) | AV New Law | 23 June 2022 | The first law dedicated to preventing and relieving harm from AV performances |
| [民法](民法.md) | Civil Code | 16 July 1898 | The 2022 revision lowered the age of majority to 18 |
| [売春防止法](売春防止法.md) | Anti-Prostitution Act | 1 April 1957 | Bans prostitution and related conduct |
| [児童買春・児童ポルノ禁止法](児童買春・児童ポルノ禁止法.md) | Anti-Child-Prostitution Act | 1 May 1999 | Protects children from sexual exploitation |
| [困難女性支援法](困難女性支援法.md) | Support Act for Women in Difficulty | 1 October 2021 | Comprehensive support for women facing various hardships |
| [風俗営業法](風俗営業法.md) | Adult Entertainment Control Act | 1 July 1948 | Regulates the adult-entertainment trade |
| [職業安定法](職業安定法.md) | Employment Security Act | 15 October 1947 | Regulates job placement and employment services |
| [労働者派遣法](労働者派遣法.md) | Worker Dispatch Act | 1 July 1986 | Regulates the dispatching of workers |
| [人身売買禁止条約](人身売買禁止条約.md) | Convention against human trafficking | 25 January 2004 | An international convention banning the trade in persons |
| [リベンジポルノ防止法](リベンジポルノ防止法.md) | Revenge-porn prevention law | 1 January 2015 | Bans non-consensual distribution of intimate images |
