# 売春防止法

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |

| In force | 1 April 1957 |
| Administered by | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare |

## Overview

The Act on the Prevention of Prostitution is the Japanese law enacted to prevent prostitution. It does not apply directly to the AV industry, but as a law related to the sex industry it has reference value for regulating the AV industry.

## Main provisions

### Prohibited acts

- Prostitution (conduct aimed at sexual intercourse for reward)
- Brokerage of prostitution
- Forcing someone into prostitution
- Organising prostitution

### Penalties

- Offenders may face criminal penalties
- Brokering prostitution is punished as well

## Relevance to the AV industry

An AV performance and prostitution are legally different acts, but both involve the commercial use of sex. The law clarifies the boundary of illegality in sex transactions for reward, providing a legal basis for distinguishing legitimate AV performances from illegal sex transactions.
