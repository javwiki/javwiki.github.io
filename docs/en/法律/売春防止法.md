## Summary

The Law on Prevention of the Shang Chun is the law of Japan that prevents prostitution. Although the Act does not directly apply to the AV industry, it is of some interest to the regulation of the AV industry as a law relating to the sex industry.

## Main elements

### Prohibition of conduct

- Prostitution (acts for the purpose of paid sexual intercourse)
- Prostitution
- Forced prostitution
- Organization of prostitution

### Penalties

- Perpetrators may face criminal penalties
- Prostitution is also punishable.

## Linkage to the AV industry

The AV act is not legal in nature as prostitution, but both involve commercial sexual exploitation. The Act defines the unlawful borders of paid sex transactions and provides a legal basis for distinguishing between legitimate AV performances and illegal sex transactions.
