## Introduction

Jun Suehiro (末広純, born 10 January 2000) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Yamanashi Prefecture affiliated with LIGHT, and also registered at the Roppongi venue Red Dragon. She debuted as a Madonna exclusive on 12 April 2022; on 9 August that year she left Madonna and began working as a freelance "kikatan" performer.

2023 raised her profile: on 3 April her title "人妻自宅サロン 底辺クズ隣人の汚らわしいデカマラに堕ちた若妻エステティシャン" topped the weekly FANZA rental chart, and she placed first in that month's rental-floor monthly actress ranking, 19th for the half-year, fifth for November and eleventh for the year. At the MOODYZ year-end party she received the Best M's Video Group Award.

She continued to appear in FANZA weekly and monthly charts in 2024, and in December placed 25th in FLASH's independently compiled 2024 sexy-actress ranking. She stopped updating social media in January 2025, and on 5 May the Taiwan-based China Times reported her return; on 3 November a title released in April 2025 re-entered the weekly FANZA video chart at ninth. In the week of 16 March 2026 a KMPVR tenth-anniversary title she appeared in debuted tenth.

## Personal notes

- She is from Yamanashi Prefecture. Before debuting she worked as a cook, but the pay did not cover living alone and she wanted savings, so she applied to an agency casually, thinking she would "try just one".
- Her stage name came from being told she looked like Ryoko Hirosue; variety programmes also introduced her as a Hirosue lookalike.
- Almost all of her first five titles cast her as a married woman in dramas, and she was surprised that "adult video needs acting". The grounding she gained at the start has continued to serve her in freelance work.
- She says fans mostly praise her rear, and believes years of basketball in middle and high school gave her a solid lower body; because she often appears in rear-focused titles she trains daily to keep it from sagging.
- On underwear she says about ten per cent are full-back, thirty per cent thongs and the rest none, choosing breathability and fit and preferring to go without.
- An Asahi Geinō writer described her off-screen manner as "cute and sunny enough that every sentence ending seems to carry a musical note or a heart".
- On 22 January 2025 Weekly Bunshun reported a relationship with DeNA BayStars pitcher Katsuki Higashi. She is also known as a BayStars fan, frequently attending Yokohama Stadium in the team jersey and posting about it.

## Work features

- She debuted as a Madonna exclusive in April 2022 and moved to freelance work that August, thereafter appearing across several studios.
- Her early work was mainly married-woman dramas — nearly all of her first five titles — and that early acting training underpins her later freelance output.
- Many titles are built around her rear, which she also cites as a defining physical feature.
- In 2023 titles such as "人妻自宅サロン 底辺クズ隣人の汚らわしいデカマラに堕ちた若妻エステティシャン" took first place on FANZA's rental weekly and monthly charts.
- Beyond adult video she has appeared on YouTube channels and ABEMA streaming shows, released the digital photobook "はだかの家政婦" and given an interview to Monthly FANZA.
