## Introduction

Mayu Suzuki, born 16 March 1994, is a Japanese AV actress and former gravure idol from Chiba Prefecture, affiliated with ML. The opening of her Wikipedia article gives 1994 as her year of birth, while her own tweet of 13 September 2017 gives 16 March 1993; the two accounts coexist. During her gravure idol period she belonged to the now-dissolved agency "RUSH".

In October 2011 she made her gravure idol debut with 《純粋少女〜17歳、初めてのTバック〜》. The trigger for her starting chaku-ero photography was being told she "could get ○ yen" (the amount is undisclosed). In November 2014 she showed pubic hair (hair nude) for the first time in Weekly Playboy; in May 2015 E-BODY released 《現役グラビアアイドルAV解禁》, marking her formal AV debut.

In 2025 she marked ten years since her AV debut and gave a commemorative interview to Weekly Playboy, the magazine that ran her first hair nude. In November of the same year she made her film debut in 《この恋は拡張現実?》 (director: 堂ノ本敬太), playing ミコト, with a stage greeting at Shinjuku Theatre on 22 November; the role came about through a recommendation from the director's wife, 生田みく. From 27 to 29 March 2026 she attended the ADULTOPIA adult expo at Messe Taoyuan in Taoyuan, Taiwan, and received BEST POPULAR ACTRESS (the XCITY Most Popular Actress Award).

## Personal notes

- Her hobbies are shopping and building plastic models, and her favourite work is 《機動戦士ガンダムSEED》; her specialities are the piano and cooking.
- For about five years after her AV debut she did not disclose her agency; in 2020 she announced that she belonged to ML Inc.
- She makes a selling point of her thick body hair and asks the staff to leave it even at a hair-removal salon, so she is often met with puzzled reactions.
- During her gravure days she removed hair all over so as not to show anything beyond a swimsuit; she surmises that it grew thicker after her debut as a rebound from that.
- The Weekly Playboy editorial department described her appeal as "an innocent face and an unbalanced bush".
- As for how her gravure debut came about, she says she was won over by the words "you can get ○ yen"; the amount is undisclosed.

## Work features

- Her gravure videos run from 2011 to 2022 and include 《純粋少女〜17歳、初めてのTバック〜》, 《ALL NUDE 鈴木真夕》 and 《無修正 ～沖縄編～ 鈴木真夕》.
- Her AV debut was E-BODY's 《現役グラビアアイドルAV解禁》 (May 2015); since then she has worked mainly on solo and kikaku titles.
- Her photobooks are 《ハダカのまゆ》 (2014) and 《もずくの天ぷら 鈴木真夕写真集》 (2022); digital photo collections include the 2021 《密旅》 series and 2023's 《密旅 Another Edition.》.
- In the week of 13 July 2026, her title 《悩める巨根男性をナマ膣で解決!鈴木真夕のデカチン真正中出し番人》 (グローリークエスト) entered the FANZA video floor weekly chart at 9th.
- She played ミコト in the film 《この恋は拡張現実?》, released on 22 November 2025.
