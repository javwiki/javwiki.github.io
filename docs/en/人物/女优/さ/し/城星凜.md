## Introduction

Seri Kizuki (also 星セリ) is a Japanese transgender AV actress born on 12 December 1994. Her father is Japanese and her mother is of Korean and Russian descent. She signed with DAS in September 2016 and debuted as a "天然美少女ニューハーフ". She planned gender-affirming surgery before turning 25, and wanted to record "herself with a penis" on video beforehand, so she limited her AV work to one year; she retired on 1 September 2017 with 《天然美少女ニューハーフ 城星凜 引退》. On 25 August 2018 she returned in 《復活。天然美少女ニューハーフ 人生で一度きりのレズセックス 星セリ 椎名そら》, changed her name to 星セリ and retired again within the same year.

## Personal notes

- Of mixed heritage — Japanese father, Korean-Russian mother — and 173cm tall.
- She felt discomfort with her assigned gender from third grade and became certain she was female in fifth grade when she fell for a male classmate; from middle school she wore a sailor uniform with those around her understanding.
- Her reason for appearing was to preserve "herself with a penis" before surgery by 25, so she limited AV work to one year.
- The return came about after 椎名そら approached her at the club where she worked; that title was her first sexual experience with a woman.

## Work features

- The exclusive studio was ダスッ! (DAS), with "天然美少女ニューハーフ" as the central concept.
- A 173cm height and C cup are the selling points; she appeared in about ten titles while active.
- Both the September 2017 retirement title and the August 2018 return title carry the "天然美少女ニューハーフ" series name.
- The return title co-starred 椎名そら and was her first sexual experience with a woman; the November 2018 retirement title co-starred 月島アンナ.
