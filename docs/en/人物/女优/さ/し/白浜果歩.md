## Introduction

Kaho Shirahama is a Japanese AV actress represented by Diaz Group. She was born in Tokyo on 7 July 2000. Her debut was announced on 19 August 2025, and on 16 September 2025 she made her AV debut as a MOODYZ exclusive in キレイパトラ 新人 きれいなお姉さんは好きですか？ 専属 白浜果歩 AV Debut！ (MIKR-038). In 2026 she moved to kikaku-tantai work for studios including SOD, h.m.p, Gain Corporation and Muku, and has also appeared in VR titles.
