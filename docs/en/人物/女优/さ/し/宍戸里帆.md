## Introduction

Riho Shishido was born on 14 October 2001 in Tokyo and belongs to Mains. She debuted in March 2022 as a MOODYZ exclusive and appeared in Weekly Post's 1 April 2022 issue for her first gravure shoot. Her first starring film, Motel 303, opened on 29 November 2023. She reported graduating from university on 30 March 2024 and announced on 17 December 2024 that she was leaving MOODYZ exclusivity to work freelance.

## Work features

- Her MOODYZ period centred on relationships—girlfriend, wife, mistress, NTR—and also included demanding projects such as the Dream Woman series.
- After going freelance in late 2024 her labels and subject matter widened to married-woman affairs, nursing, groping and NTR.
- Her 86 cm bust and soft breasts are a physical signature; she once saw them as a complex but came to regard them as part of her identity. Her agency profile lists her height as 153 cm.
- Beyond adult video she has written columns for FANZA News, Dela Beppin R and Bezzy, and appeared in films, stage productions and games. AV writer Kazu Shibata was surprised by her move to freelance work at that point; Kurogane Arei said leaving exclusivity revealed new range and broadened her performances.
- In May 2022 she placed 41st in Asahi Geino's active AV actress poll and first among newcomers in Monthly FANZA's summer 2022 ranking.
