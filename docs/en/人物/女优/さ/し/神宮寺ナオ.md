# Nao Jinguji

A Japanese adult-film performer.

## Basic information

- **Japanese name**: 神宮寺ナオ
- **English name**: Nao Jinguji
- **Date of birth (official or publisher source)**: 1997-02-15
- **Height (official or publisher source)**: 160 cm

## Introduction

Nao Jinguji was born on 15 February 1997 in Kanagawa Prefecture. She was formerly with the Cruise Group and is now represented by ARM Promotion. She debuted in October 2017 with a title presented as her first experience. She became a Madonna exclusive in June 2019, added Honnaka exclusivity in November, and in May 2020 moved from Honnaka to Moodyz, becoming exclusive to both Madonna and Moodyz.

## Work features

- Her debut was framed around a 20-year-old with no experience; she has since worked mainly as a Madonna and Moodyz exclusive, in titles about married women, relationships with fathers-in-law, NTR and dominant women.
- She starred in her first film, 『バージン協奏曲 それゆけ純白パンツ!』 ("Virgin Concerto"), in 2019; in 2020 she appeared in 『悪女の色仕掛け カモって快感!』 and has also worked on stage and in game voice roles.
- Her photobooks include "Muse" (2019), "Gimme!" (2022) and "Stilla" (2025), alongside many image DVDs and digital photobooks. In June 2026 she received her first newly shot Weekly Playboy feature in her tenth year.
- Actor Yoichi Matsumoto said "she can act with her eyes"; director Saomari described an "unruly eroticism" and recalled improvisations beyond the brief, while producer Tomoko noted unpredictable touches such as biting actors off-camera.
