## Introduction

Emika Shirakami is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive in April 2024 and was called an "ultra-large newcomer". Her debut title reached the FANZA chart, and in July 2025 she ranked 3rd on the FANZA rental monthly AV actress chart.

## Personal notes

- In student life she was in the volleyball club (captain), with a cropped かりあげ haircut.
- Her first time was at 18, after leaving the volleyball club, with her first boyfriend — she was so nervous she stiffened. Three partners before her debut.
- Before her debut she was a fan of Yua Mikami and had only watched Mika's titles. Seeing Mika's poster during her S1 interview strengthened her resolve to join S1.
- Her stage name "白上咲花" was proposed by a producer; out of admiration for Mika the character "上" was put into her surname.
- Described by her S1 senior Minami Kojima as having a constitution that easily squirts.
