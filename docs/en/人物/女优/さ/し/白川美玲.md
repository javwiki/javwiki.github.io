## Introduction

Mirei Shirakawa is a Japanese AV actress and model standing 175cm tall, noted for her slender nine-head proportions and tall silhouette. Before entering the mainstream Japanese AV industry, she worked as a gravure and fashion model (with reports noting a runway appearance at Paris Fashion Week); Chinese entertainment media and online observers have also linked her to earlier activities under names such as 高梨遥香 and 香月怜 (also written 香月伶) across overseas/Chinese-language online video projects, social media and subscription platforms. In July 2026 she announced her AV debut via a handwritten letter on social media, and on 18 August 2026 she debuted as a flagship MOODYZ 25th-anniversary exclusive under the *きれいなお姉さんは好きですか* line with `MIKR-117`. Initially represented by GirlulePro (`ガールールプロ`), she announced her move to T-POWERS in late November 2026.
