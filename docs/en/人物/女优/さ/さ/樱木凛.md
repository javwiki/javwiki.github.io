## Introduction

Rin Sakuragi is a former Japanese AV actress, entertainer and singer born on 3 March 1989 in Hyogo Prefecture, represented by プライムエージェンシー. She made her AV debut in October 2007 as a KUKI exclusive and began television work in 2008. From 6 April 2009 she was a regular on 《おねだり!!マスカット》 and gained recognition as a second-generation 恵比寿マスカッツ member; that year she also appeared in the drama 《嬢王 Virgin》 as 春名美羽. She took a break for ill health in July 2011 and returned that November, took another break on 19 February 2012 and announced her return on 30 August. In April 2013 she returned to AV as a 「美」 exclusive and graduated from 恵比寿マスカッツ at its dissolution concert on 7 April; on 30 October she made her film lead debut in 《体温》. In 2014 she formed P＊cherry with さくらゆら and published the debut single 《さくらCircle》 on 1 May. She retired on 22 April 2017 and made a limited return on 20 July 2019 after 希崎ジェシカ's retirement, reporting that she was married with a son.

## Personal notes

- From Hyogo Prefecture, blood type B; interests and specialities are shopping and digital cameras.
- The stage name comes from flowers that bloom in spring, as she was born in March; the 《おねだり!!マスカット》 tagline also used flower-related words.
- As a second-generation 恵比寿マスカッツ member she appeared repeatedly with 希崎ジェシカ, かすみ果穂, 初音みのり and 希志あいの, and hosted the title show 《凛とジェシカのファイト一発》 with 希崎ジェシカ.
- She sang a solo part on the group's third single 《チヨコレイト》; after graduating she formed P＊cherry with agency colleague さくらゆら in 2014 and also pursued a solo singing career.
- In March 2015 a variety show announced a temporary change of name to 「沼田早苗」, after which she said on Twitter that she would continue as 桜木凛.
- On the 20 July 2019 YouTube Live 《希崎ジェシカ重大発表！引退カウントダウンで何かするってよ!》 she reported that she was married and had a son.

## Work features

- An innocent image early on, with a wider range of roles later; she was known for nymphomania and story-driven titles.
- KUKI exclusive in 2007–2008, PREMIUM in 2009–2012, a return as a 「美」 exclusive in 2013, IdeaPocket exclusive from December 2015 and Attackers titles in 2016.
- The film 《体温》 (director: 緒方貴臣), released on 30 October 2013, was her first lead; it had been shot about two years earlier and its release was delayed for various reasons.
- Beyond AV she appeared in television dramas, variety, films and stage events, and released music.
