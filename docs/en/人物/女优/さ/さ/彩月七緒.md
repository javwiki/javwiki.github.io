## Introduction

Nao Satsuki was born on 16 June 2002 in Tokyo and is represented by STAR NEXT. In high school she was told she resembled 上原亜衣 and was nicknamed "AV actress"; initially confused, she searched and agreed with the comparison, then became interested after watching Uehara's work as an adult and set her sights on the industry. She debuted from SOD on 25 January 2024. Her debut title 《超大物新人 彩月七緒 AV DEBUT 大学生からスターへ いま始まるシンデレラストーリー》 entered at No. 10 on the FANZA mail-order ranking in the week of 8 January 2024, and the 《アサヒ芸能》 editorial team called her "a terrifying newcomer who never stops screaming". Her December 2025 release became her SOD graduation title, unlocking orgy, bukkake and swallowing scenes, after which she became a kikaku-tantai performer. In September 2026 《アサヒ芸能》 named her a "NEXTキカタン美女", and in October 2026 critics in 《週刊プレイボーイ》 named her a "genius of the next generation of cowgirl".
