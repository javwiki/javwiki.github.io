## Introduction

Sachiko (佐知子, born 8 August 1999) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Miyazaki Prefecture. In May 2019 she made her adult-video debut as an exclusive for SOD Create's Kimi Hore label; in August that year she left exclusivity and became a freelance solo actress, appearing for a range of studios afterwards. Her debut role wore glasses, so about ninety per cent of her early work featured her in glasses; by 2021 she said she still wore them in "about sixty per cent" of her titles.

She is known for a J-cup, 105 cm bust, though she has said she once felt self-conscious about her large breasts. In August 2020 she placed tenth in Monthly FANZA's ranking of AV actresses for the first half of 2020, and in February 2021 she placed second in a Geo TV survey of actresses people would like chocolate from on Valentine's Day. Weekly Playboy introduced her as "plain, yet an overwhelming first when you search the adult mail-order site".
