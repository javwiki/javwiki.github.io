## Introduction

Ai Sayama is a Japanese AV actress, and also a singer and entertainer. In April 2007 she debuted as an Alice Japan exclusive with the slogan "平成生まれのHカップ".

Ai Sayama was a first-generation member of Ebisu Muscats and was active on variety programmes. In 2023 she formed the dance unit BLACK DIAMOND with Anri Okita and others. She announced her retirement in January 2025 and ended her AV actress activity with a title released that September.
