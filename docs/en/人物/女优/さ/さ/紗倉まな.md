## Introduction

Mana Sakura (紗倉まな, born 23 March 1993) is a Japanese adult-film actress, actress, television personality, singer, novelist and YouTuber affiliated with Mine'S. She studied in the environmental urban engineering department of Kisarazu National College of Technology but has not disclosed whether she graduated. In November 2011 she debuted in image video with "工場萌え美少女 紗倉まな 18歳" and also worked as a gravure model; in February 2012 she made her adult-video debut with "紗倉まな AVDebut". In recent years she has called herself an "ero-ya" in response to media replacing "adult-video actress" with "sexy actress".

She quickly became SOD's flagship performer: in the DMM.com ranking compiled on 16 April 2012 she was first in both the work and actress categories, and at the SOD Awards 2012 she took six prizes including best actress. In 2013 she won the newcomer and FLASH awards at the Sky PerfecTV Adult Broadcasting Awards 2013, and in 2015 she added the actress award and a second FLASH award, becoming the first person to complete a triple crown with her 2013 newcomer prize.

Her writing has attracted comparable attention. Her first essay, "高専生だった私が出会った世界でたった一つの天職", published on 15 January 2015, became a bestseller and reached first place in Amazon.co.jp's talent-book category (overall) and Rakuten's film category (Japan). Her first novel, "最低。", appeared on 12 February 2016, and its 2017 film adaptation was selected for the competition section, the top programme of the Tokyo International Film Festival. In October 2020 her third novel, "春、死なん", was shortlisted for the 42nd Noma Literary Newcomer Prize, and in October 2025 "うつせみ" was shortlisted for the 47th.

On 3 August 2022 she announced a temporary pause in activity because of poor health, and on 29 September she announced her return. In May 2023 she regained first place in Asahi Geinō's "2023 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election"; in February 2024 she placed first in the weekly Asahi Geinō feature "Japan's 'Beautiful Breasts' Best 50" and was called the "completely invincible erotic-breast queen".
