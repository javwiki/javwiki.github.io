## Introduction

Mei Satsuki (さつき芽衣, born 16 August 2000) is a Japanese adult-film actress affiliated with Mines and registered at the Roppongi club Red Dragon. On 7 July 2020 she debuted in adult video with Deep's title "あどけなさとおとなっぽさのちょうど間 19歳新人 さつき芽衣 AVデビュードキュメント". She held an exclusive contract with Deep's until 31 March 2021 and has since worked as a planning-only actress across several studios.

In the week of 12 December 2022 her co-starring title with Rina Takase and Ranran, "ぷにもちオッパイでご主人様をイカせまくる全裸メイドの神奉仕ハーレム" (KMP), topped FANZA's video-floor weekly ranking. She placed seventh in FANZA's 2022 annual actress ranking and eighteenth in the first half of 2023. On 8 and 9 July 2023 she held a third-anniversary photo exhibition, "君はノンフィクション", in Koenji, and on 29 September she appeared at the twelfth Ani-kura TMA anime-song event "復活響宴" at Shinjuku Loft, held for the first time in about four years. In April 2024 she placed thirty-eighth overall in Asahi Geinō's "2024 Active AV Actress Sexy General Election".

On 5 June 2024, marking her fourth anniversary, she posted on social media that she was pausing adult-video shooting while continuing public activities; that August she placed seventh in FANZA's monthly video-floor actress ranking. On 26 and 27 July 2025 she held a fifth-anniversary photo exhibition, "美女 mei nu", at DESIGN FESTA GALLERY in Harajuku. On 16 September she announced that she would resume adult-video work in October as a Muku exclusive, and on 23 September that she would also work as a Madonna exclusive. The adult-video writer Kazu Shibata called it a "feat" to serve simultaneously as the exclusive of two studios of opposite character — Muku, a pretty-girl label with many school-uniform roles, and Madonna, a mature label with many married-woman roles — even though dual exclusives are no longer rare.
