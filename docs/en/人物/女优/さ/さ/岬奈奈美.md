## Introduction

Nanami Misaki is a former Japanese AV actress born on 9 June 1996 in Shizuoka Prefecture, represented by ティーパワーズ and also working at 六本木レッドドラゴン. She debuted in November 2017 as an IdeaPocket exclusive. Known for a 150cm frame and a healing beautiful-girl image, she was said to resemble 乃木坂46's 橋本奈々未. In 2020 she appeared in three Attackers titles; in May 2022 she placed 31st in 《アサヒ芸能》's 「2022現役AV女優SEXY総選挙」, and in July she became an Attackers exclusive. After the title released in August 2023 she stopped activity, her X and Instagram accounts were deleted, and by September 2024 her profile had been removed from the agency website.
