## Introduction

Momo Sakura is a Japanese AV actress and former gravure idol, born in Akita on 3 December 1996. She moved to Tokyo at 17 aiming to be a singer, later became a gravure idol, and in May 2017 made her AV debut with the IdeaPocket exclusive "FIRST IMPRESSION 115 誕生 現役GカップグラビアアイドルAVデビュー". In May 2018 she won Best New Actress at the DMM.R18 Adult Awards 2018, and in 2021 "中出しSEX解禁" became IdeaPocket's best-selling title of the year. In December 2023 she was nominated by TC Candler for the "100 most beautiful faces in the world". In July 2026 she performed the theme song "未来コントレール" for the TV anime "同じゼミの染谷さんがセクシー女優だった話。".
