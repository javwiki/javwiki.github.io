## Introduction

Riho Fujimori (藤森里穂, born 3 December 1996) is a Japanese adult-film actress affiliated with NAX Promotion (New Actor eXperience). On 30 November 2021 she received the Sky PerfecTV Adult Broadcasting Award's "Valuable Actress of AV King" on the recommendation of "AV King". On 2 May 2022 she became an exclusive actress for SOD Create's SOD Rei label.

On 1 September 2023 she moved to NAX Promotion and simultaneously became exclusive to Honnaka and OPPAI. In December 2023 she won the Best kira☆kira Award at the MOODYZ year-end party. On 12 March 2024 her agency announced that from April she would move from OPPAI to Das!, while continuing a double exclusivity with Honnaka; on 24 December 2024 it was announced that from January 2025 she would move from Honnaka to Madonna, keeping the double exclusivity with Das!. From August 2025 she left the exclusivity system and became a freelance project actress for the first time in three years.

In her other activities, on 24 March 2024 she began her own programme, "藤森里穂のねほりはほりほラジオ", on the web radio station Radio Chronicle. On 17 November 2024 she held a fifth-anniversary fan meeting, and from 17 to 22 December she held her first photo exhibition. Her X following passed one million on 3 May 2025, and her total Instagram following reached 400,000 on 3 May 2026. The radio programme was relaunched on 27 September 2026.
