## Introduction

Yua Fukuda (福田ゆあ, born 20 February 2005) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Osaka Prefecture affiliated with Bambi Promotion. After graduating from high school she moved to Tokyo and began working as a gravure idol in 2023, then with Gold Circle Entertainment. On 3 November 2025 she announced on X that she had renamed herself Yua Fukuda and would debut as a MOODYZ exclusive, and her X account changed from her Asagiri name. The debut was also reported in Taiwan, where her stage name was translated as 福田悠亞.

Her adult-video debut entered the FANZA mail-order weekly chart at first place in the week of 27 October 2025; in the week of 3 November the Blu-ray edition placed sixth and the DVD edition fourth. She was sixth in the FANZA mail-order monthly actress ranking for November 2025 and sixth again for December.

On 23 January 2026 she opened a YouTube channel. In June that year, while travelling in New Zealand, she was held in custody for having "no detailed activity schedule", refused entry and deported, which was reported in the media.
