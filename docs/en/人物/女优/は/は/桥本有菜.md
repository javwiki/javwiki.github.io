- **Status**: Retired (ended AV and public entertainment activities in December 2025)

## Introduction

Arina Hashimoto (born 15 December 1996) is a former Japanese AV actress from Tokyo. She debuted in an image video in December 2015 and became an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive AV actress in March 2016, then with the agency Arrows. In July 2016 she joined the AV-actress idol group SEXY-J; in 2017 she served as an AV OPEN image girl alongside Marina Shiraishi and Shoko Takahashi, and in May 2018 she won Best Actress at the DMM.R18 Adult Awards 2018. She moved to FALENO as an exclusive in May 2020, then changed her name to 新ありな and became a MOODYZ exclusive on 16 August 2022.

## Work features

- She had image-video experience before AV (《ハックツ美少女 Revolution 橋本ありな》, December 2015) and continued to release image videos until 2022.
- From 2016 to 2020 she was an S1 exclusive, with releases chiefly on the S1 label.
- In 2018 she formed un panopticon girl with 君色華奈 and 桐谷まつり and made her CD debut on MILKY POP GENERATION in October, running group activity alongside video work.
- Her move to FALENO in 2020 and to MOODYZ in 2022 changed her labels and release lines.
- For the complete body of work and awards around the 2022 name change, see the 「新有菜」 entry.
