## Introduction

Mao Hamasaki (浜崎真緒, born 20 October 1993) is a Japanese DJ and former adult-film actress. She debuted in June 2012 through a video-distribution site in the PRESTIGE group and remained an exclusive actress until March 2013. From July that year she appeared for Soft On Demand on a three-month contract, and from October she worked as a project-based solo actress until her retirement in 2024. In 2016 she was nominated for both the actress and the work awards at the Sky PerfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards, and from 2017 she began working as a music DJ under the name DJ MAO, appearing in Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore, Malaysia and elsewhere.

Between 2020 and 2021 she appeared repeatedly in reader polls and sales charts: 38th in the FLASH "2020 Best 100 Active Sexy Actresses" reader vote in December 2020, and 25th in Asahi Geinō's "2021 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election" in April 2021. On 30 November 2021, on Cherry Bomb's recommendation, she was named Valuable Actress of Cherry Bomb at the Sky PerfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards. From 19 January to 16 February 2024 she was a campaign girl, alongside Matsumoto Ichika, Nanase Alice, miru, Mitani Akane, Adachi Yuri and Yamagishi Ayaka, for FANZA's "I LOVE AV" campaign.

On 9 August 2024 she announced at the TAE venue in Taiwan that she was ending a 13-year career, and that afternoon she confirmed the retirement to Japanese fans through social media. On 18 September she announced that her contract with her agency Fanstar Promotion had ended. Her retirement work, "ちんしゃぶ彼女紹介します。 浜崎真緒 Final", entered the FANZA mail-order weekly chart at first place in February 2025. In March 2026 it was reported that she had based herself in Hong Kong; on 5 July 2026 she served as one-day manager of the Hong Kong currency-exchange shop Win Win Exchange, and on 31 October 2026 she was taken to hospital after a drink was spiked at an event there, with Hong Kong police arresting a suspect on 3 November.
