# Moka Haruhi

A Japanese adult-film performer.

## Basic information

- **Japanese name**: 春陽モカ
- **English name**: Moka Haruhi
- **Date of birth (official or publisher source)**: 2002-05-27

## Introduction

Moka Haruhi was born on 27 May 2002 and is represented by ACT. She debuted from MOODYZ in March 2024 (Japanese Wikipedia gives 5 March, ACT's profile 2 April and a database 29 March — see Scope). In June 2025 she became an exclusive performer for three labels, MOODYZ, Wanz Factory and Honnaka. Her Honnaka exclusivity ended at the end of September, and OPPAI joined as the third exclusive label from November releases. In July 2025 she published the photo book 令和最強GAL降臨. Her work centres on creampie, large-breast and gyaru scenarios, and she also appears in VR titles and large-cast projects.

## Work features

- Her 2025 titles commonly use creampie, large-breast and gyaru settings, and she is often classified as a gyaru performer (Japanese Wikipedia's summary of her work at that time).
- AV writer Katsunori Tōkō is quoted as saying she has 「スタイル抜群で目鼻立ちもクッキリしており、コスプレが非常に似合う」 — a comment on her figure, features and fit for cosplay.
- From 2025 she has been exclusive to several labels at once, including MOODYZ, Wanz Factory and Honnaka (later OPPAI), and has appeared in 痴女ヘブン and OPPAI projects.
- She appears in VR works and in both the 2025 and 2026 editions of MOODYZ's large-cast バコバコバスツアー projects.
