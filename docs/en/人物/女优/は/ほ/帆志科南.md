## Introduction

Hoshikana (ほしかな) was born on 14 November 1997, is 168cm tall and has a G cup. She worked mainly in amateur-style, documentary-like projects and also appeared under the stage names 富井美帆, 美浦あや and 星野あすな. Her titles and setups often use temporary names such as みほ, あや and かな. In December 2024 the fan-compiled reference site "このAV女優の名前教えてwiki" updated its former 富井美帆 entry to record a name change to 帆志科南; the site now treats 帆志科南 as the main entry and lists her former names and works there. Besides amateur works, she appeared in tokusatsu-heroine and giant-heroine drama titles under the name 富井美帆.
