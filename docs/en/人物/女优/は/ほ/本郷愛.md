## Introduction

Ai Hongou (本郷愛, born 28 October 1999) is a former Japanese adult-film actress from Tokyo, affiliated with Bstar after DINO, and formerly known as Nikaidou Yume. On 6 March 2020, while still at university, she joined DINO and debuted as an exclusive of the streaming-oriented studio FALENO; her debut became the studio's biggest hit as of 2020.

In April 2021 she placed 21st in Asahi Geinō's "2021 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election", and her first-half FANZA sales ranked third. In 2021 she was cast in her first lead role in the Toei title "はだか拳Ω", billed as an action actress. On 28 November 2021 she announced on Twitter that she was moving to Bstar and changing her name to Ai Hongou; on 30 November she was named Valuable Actress of VENUS at the Sky PerfecTV Adult Broadcasting Awards, and on 6 March 2022 she took both the MVA (Most Valuable Actress) and MTA (Most Technical Actress) awards.

In June 2023 she announced a surprise exclusive move to S1, and her title "エスワンファン感謝祭" entered the FANZA mail-order weekly chart at first place. From November that year she worked as a VTuber, a project paused the following January; from March 2024 she streamed games and talk on Twitch, gathering about 90,000 followers in under two months and being called "最強電玩女神" in Taiwan. In June 2024 she passed the second screening of the "Minato Ward Women's Audition" for the latest "龍が如く" game, and her casting was confirmed at a press conference on 16 July.

On 15 September 2024 she addressed rumours about streamer Junichi Kato on her YouTube channel and acknowledged the relationship; on 19 September she apologised on X and announced a pause in activity, while already-shot works continued to be released. On 27 November 2025 S1 announced "本郷愛 引退 S1全19作コンプリート 15時間BOX", confirming that she had retired without returning.
