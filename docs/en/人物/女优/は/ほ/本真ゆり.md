## Introduction

Yuri Honma (本真ゆり, born 28 January 1993) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Tokyo affiliated with Venus Promotion. She is 162 cm tall, weighs 55 kg and measures B100 (H)-W64-H100, placing her in the large-bust category. She debuted in 2011 and has worked mainly in solo and streaming titles since.

In March 2013 she changed her name to Aiuchi Tsukasa and became an exclusive performer for the studio Fitch, appearing in seven of its titles. New releases then paused for a time, but resumed in 2014, when she returned to the name Yuri Honma. Between 2013 and 2015 she left work with Fitch, V&R Products and others under both names, plus a solo compilation.

In the week of 3 November 2025 her image video "Pure Beauty" (Fine Pictures) debuted ninth on FANZA's mail-order weekly chart. This is the most recent chart record given in the Wikipedia article.
