## Introduction

Maria Hoshi (星まりあ, born 16 July 1999) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Tokyo affiliated with Eightman. In June 2020 she debuted as an S1 exclusive under the name Mahina Amane. On 31 July 2022 she announced on X, then still known as Twitter, that she had left Forty-Four Management, was changing her name to Maria Hoshi and was joining Eightman.

At 145 cm with a G cup, she is promoted for the contrast between a large bust and a youthful face; she has said that in high school she was the shortest in her class and had the largest bust. The writer Health Samurai called hers "a perfect balance of size, shape, firmness, elasticity and areola proportion, one of the finest busts in the industry", while the adult-video critic Joji Shibue described the appeal of "a large bust with a baby face" and the strikingly alluring, mature expression she shows once in erotic mode.

Her debut work placed 15th in the 2020 annual AV sales ranking compiled by Asahi Geinō with FANZA's cooperation. In 2021 six of her works passed 10,000 favourites on FANZA, producing a run of hits. In print she released her first photobook "あま恋" in June 2022 and, under her new name, the digital photobook "天使の143" in October that year.
