## Introduction

Beatrix (aliases: べあこ, たなかはる) is a new-half / otokonoko performer born in Yamaguchi Prefecture on 1 October 1998. She studied asari clams at a technical college in Yamaguchi and began cross-dressing for fun in her fifth year. In summer 2019 a production company invited her to appear in an amateur title; she then left college three months before graduating and moved to Tokyo. There she lived in an internet café and worked in the sex industry before quitting that work at the end of March 2020. By the time of an April 2020 interview her Twitter followers exceeded 10,000, and in under a year since her debut she had appeared in more than 16 titles.

- After moving to Tokyo she was jobless and income-less and lived in net cafes for a while
