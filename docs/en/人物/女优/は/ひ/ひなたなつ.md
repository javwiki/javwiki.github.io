## Introduction

Natsu Hinata (ひなたなつ, born 4 May 2001) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Tokyo. She was with Forty Four Management and has been affiliated with T-POWERS since November 2024; her former name was Hinata Natsu, with the same reading. In June 2021 she made her adult-video debut as a kawaii* exclusive. She has said the opportunity came from curiosity about how professional male performers have sex.

In July 2022 she released her first best-of collection. Around that time she often complained on X about her then agency's haplessness, and because she found the manager unusable she was allowed to arrange staff and negotiate for events herself. On 6 November 2024 she announced on X that she was moving to T-POWERS and changing her stage name to ひなたなつ.

For the week of 9 February 2026, her appearance in "MOODYZファン感謝祭バコバコバスツアー2026 25周年専属大集合!大乱交!大感謝スペシャル!" debuted fourth on the FANZA mail-order chart. The release was later postponed to 19 May, but the work reached first on the same chart for the week of 16 February and third on the FANZA video chart for the week of 13 April. She also released the image DVD "Natsu なつのかけら" in June 2025 and has worked on photobooks including "プレミアムヌードポーズブック 日向なつ" in November 2023.
