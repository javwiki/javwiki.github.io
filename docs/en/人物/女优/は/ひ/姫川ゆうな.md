## Introduction

Yuuna Himekawa is a Japanese AV actress born on 3 August 1996 in Tokyo, represented by アトラクティブ. She debuted in 2016 with a loli image; by her own account kawaii* was her first shoot, while her debut title under the name 姫川ゆうな was released by プレステージ. She was first with ギルフィー and later moved to ディクレア (now シエロ). On moving to アトラクティブ in 2020 she renamed herself 月城らん and restarted, then announced in March 2021 that she was returning to 姫川ゆうな. Titles featuring her topped the weekly FANZA video floor ranking in June 2022 and January 2026.

## Personal notes

- From Tokyo; her interest is making sweets.
- Her speciality is ballet, studied for 16 years from age three; a damaged left knee makes cowgirl scenes difficult.
- The change to 月城らん was for an image change: she felt she had reached the limit of maintaining the black-haired long-haired loli stereotype. On set she was still called by the old nickname "姫", and she says either was fine.
- She says the roles closest to her unadorned self are gyaru or yankee.
- 160cm tall with a D cup; an innocent loli look is the main selling point.

## Work features

- Centred on a 160cm height and a D-cup innocent loli look, with schoolgirl, JK and cosplay subjects.
- The 2020 月城らん period switched to short hair and a different style; she returned to 姫川ゆうな in 2021.
- In the week of 27 June 2022, her appearance in 《ホイホイぱんち 12》 (released 26 June 2021) reached No. 1 on the FANZA video floor ranking.
- In the week of 5 January 2026, the November 2024 compilation 《MOON FORCE CHEERS ぱこぱこしろうとコレクション。 ベリーベストオブベスツッ！》 (DOC), which includes her work, rose to No. 1 on the same ranking.
- An interview appeared in 《月刊DMM》's February 2017 issue.
