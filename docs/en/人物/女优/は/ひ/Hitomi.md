## Introduction

Hitomi (real name: 田中瞳, born 18 July 1986) is a former Japanese AV actress and gravure star. From Kumamoto Prefecture, her trademarks are a height of 156cm, measurements of 116-59-84 and a Q-cup bust. She debuted as a gravure star in 2007, joined SOD in 2008 and moved into the AV world, changing her stage name from 田中瞳 to Hitomi. She announced her retirement in February 2022 and released her retirement title in April.

## Personal notes

- Interests: watching films, cooking, baseball.
- Specialities: karate (Wado-ryu first dan), singing.
- As of September 2015 her measurements were 116-59-84 with a Q cup.
- In 2014 her cup size grew to O, and she published CT photos to prove it was real.
- In May 2018 she revealed her relationship with Palmeri, vocalist of the metalcore band Emmure; they broke up in April 2019.
- At her debut in 2008 she was 156cm with measurements 97-59-83 and a J cup; these gradually grew to an O cup and later a Q cup.
- In November 2007 she debuted in gravure with the image video 《爆乳の衝撃》 (teardrop J cup); on 31 May 2008 she announced her departure (later deleted) and briefly deleted her blog, reopening it in August, and on 6 October of the same year announced via her blog her SOD AV debut on 6 November and name change to Hitomi.
- 6th on DMM's mail-order chart in the second half of 2008; ended her exclusivity with SOD's graduation title 《激イキ激吹きポルチオ》 (30-shot squirting) in July 2009.
- From 4 April 2010 she hosted the titular programme 《Jのぷるるん伝説》 on エンタ!371 ("おっぱい女王"); a regular on Nippon Cultural Broadcasting's 《がちっ娘》 from April 2009.
- Overseas work: the first Japanese cover of the big-breast magazine バチェラー in 31 years; a SCORELAND member (Model of the Year three years running plus the 2019 decade award, Model of the Decade); two consecutive years as an American magazine's model of the year; her breasts weigh 7kg; the model for the character 富江カタナ in the French comic 《Lastman》.
- Announced via Twitter on 28 February 2022 that she would retire with a title released in April.

## Work features

- Her debut, 《爆乳の衝撃》, put her bust in the title, and the J → O → Q cup progression became the through-line of her promotion.
- Her SOD graduation title, 《激イキ激吹きポルチオ》, recorded 30 squirts and marked the end of her SOD exclusivity.
- From 2016 she was an OPPAI exclusive, releasing mainly large-bust material.
- She began VR releases in October 2018 (MOODYZ VR and others), with further VR titles in 2020 and 2022.
- After her April 2022 retirement title she stopped appearing in AV.
