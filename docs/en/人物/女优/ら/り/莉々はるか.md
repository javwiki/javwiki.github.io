## Introduction

Haruka Riri (莉々はるか, born 8 January 2000) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Tokyo affiliated with Listar Pro. In March 2019 she debuted as an E-BODY exclusive under the name Ruka Inaba, then with C-more Entertainment. On 13 May that year she left studio exclusivity to work as a project-based solo actress, and in March 2020 she moved to All Pro.

In 2020 she placed second in the actress overall category of Monthly FANZA's "This AV Actress Is Amazing! Winter 2020", and in December she placed 31st in the reader vote for FLASH's "2020 Best 100 Active Sexy Actresses". In February 2021 she placed seventh in a GEO TV Valentine's survey; on 10 July she held "Baaruka Yose", her first comedy live show as curator; and in September she became one of the campaign girls for FANZA and the HHH Group's "Triple Happy Campaign 2021".

On 7 January 2022 she announced an indefinite hiatus from February, and on 3 October she announced on note that she was ending work under the name Ruka Inaba. On 1 December 2023 she announced her return as Haruka Riri with Listar Pro, again as an E-BODY exclusive. Her comeback title entered the FANZA mail-order weekly chart at third in May 2024. That year she placed first, tied with Ichika Matsumoto, in Monthly FANZA's "This AV Actress Is Amazing! Summer 2024", and in December received the Best E-BODY Award at the MOODYZ year-end party. Through 2025 and 2026 she continued to appear in titles, attend events in Taiwan, publish photobooks and appear on magazine covers.
