## Introduction

Ameri Ichinose is a former Japanese AV actress born on 16 September 1987 in Kanagawa Prefecture, 167cm tall and formerly with T-Powers. She performed under several names: in October 2006 she debuted as Erika Kurisu at h.m.p, in November 2008 she was renamed Ayaka Misora and debuted at kira☆kira, and in August 2009 she took the name Ameri Ichinose and released ROOKIE'S at Max-A. She went on to appear in Max-A, IdeaPocket and Prestige titles, and from 2011 shifted to planning and co-starring work for SOD, Attackers and Marx Brothers as well as uncensored streaming titles for Tokyo Hot, Caribbeancom and 1pondo. She was still releasing titles in 2013; her official blog opened in 2009 and was last updated on 16 November 2012. In July 2012 British tabloids reported a relationship with the footballer Shinji Kagawa, which neither she nor her agency confirmed.

## Work features

- The three names correspond to three studio periods: Erika Kurisu (h.m.p, 2006–2007), Ayaka Misora (kira☆kira and Attackers, 2008–2009) and Ameri Ichinose (Max-A, 2009; IdeaPocket, 2010; Prestige, 2010).
- From 2011 she worked mainly as a planning-only actress, appearing in humiliation, nymphomaniac, leg-fetish and ensemble titles for SOD, Attackers, Marx Brothers, U&K, MAD and Fetish Box; many were co-starring or compilation releases.
- Uncensored titles include 《Sky Angel Vol.73》 (October 2008), 《Sky Angel Blue Vol.10》 (January 2009) and 《SKY-115》 as Erika Kurisu, and 《Catwalk Poison 46》 and 《KIRARI 03》 (2011), Tokyo Hot (n0646, n0659), Caribbeancom, 1pondo and x1x streaming titles (2011–2012) as Ameri Ichinose.
- In 2013 she released compilations and solo titles including 《一ノ瀬アメリ BEST 4時間 The free bitch is back》 and 《スーパー美脚deタイトスカート》.
