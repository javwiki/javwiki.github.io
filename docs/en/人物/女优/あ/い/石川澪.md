## Introduction

Mio Ishikawa was born on 20 March 2002 and belongs to Production ALIVE; she has not made her birthplace public. In October 2021 she debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive billed as "an ordinary female university student", with the catchphrase 「遂に見つけた! 僕の欲しかったAV女優」. Her debut title topped FANZA's weekly chart for the week of 10 October 2021 and its monthly chart for October, and Weekly Playboy described it as MOODYZ's best-selling AV title of 2021. In December 2022 she won the grand prize for actresses at the Asahi Geino AV Awards 2022, and she placed 5th on FANZA's annual actress ranking for 2022 and 3rd for 2023. From 2024 photobooks, photo exhibitions and magazine modelling increased; in September 2025 she was a fashion model in Kobunsha's bis autumn 2025 issue, her first time in a women's magazine, and in January 2026 she formed the music unit Dopamine with 香水じゅん.

## Work features

- Studio: MOODYZ exclusive since her October 2021 debut; on 2 October 2026 she said on X that releases would stop for about half a year while she takes a break from shooting, that this was not a retirement, and that the next title is planned for spring 2027.
- Subject matter: after the "ordinary female university student" set-up of her debut, many titles portray everyday relationships — a childhood friend, a wife's younger sister, a senior colleague, a school reunion — such as 《帰省先のド田舎で僕の東京カノジョに嫉妬した幼馴染の澪に汗だくジェラシー淫語で精子ぶっこ抜かれた… 夏の思い出。石川澪》 and 《終電逃して僕の部屋にお泊り 飲み足りない澪先輩と飲み会ゲームで盛り上がって… 石川澪》.
- In the 2026 《カラミざかり同窓会編》 she played Iida Riho, a woman in her thirties older than herself; original author 桂あいり visited the set and praised the performance to the point of tears. AV writer 東風克智 wrote that he had worried a thirty-something role would not suit her youthful face, but judged that she carried it off.
- Grand-prize actress at the Asahi Geino AV Awards 2022 in December 2022; 6th in Asahi Geino's 2023 active AV actress SEXY general election in May 2023, the fastest second-year debutante to enter the top ten in that survey's history.
