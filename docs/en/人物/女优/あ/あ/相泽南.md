## Introduction

Minami Aizawa was born on 14 June 1996 in Hong Kong and lived in the United States until the early years of primary school. A former AV actress, she now works as a DJ. She debuted as an IdeaPocket exclusive in 2016, and at first publicity was banned so that her family would not find out. In May 2019 she won the Best Actress award at the FANZA Adult Awards 2019. On 2 June 2023 she announced her retirement at an event in Taiwan. In August 2024 she ended activity under the name 相沢みなみ and in November renamed herself pixeL; on 11 January 2025 she made her music-streaming debut and now works as a DJ based in Hawaii.

## Work features

- Studio: an IdeaPocket exclusive from her 2016 debut until her retirement in 2023.
- In May 2019 she won the Best Actress award at the FANZA Adult Awards 2019 (nominated on 1 March).
- The 2023 《三上悠亜と新ありなと相沢みなみ》 topped FANZA's weekly mail-order chart for two weeks running and was the No. 1 DVD of the first half of 2023; her retirement title 《相沢みなみ 引退 COMPLETE BEST 48時間BOX》 topped the mail-order chart in the week of 25 September 2023.
- Signed comments: AV writer 芝田カズ said her forte was 「清楚な女の子が実はめちゃくちゃエロかった」; actor 渋江譲二 described 「乱れまくってもけして下品にならないエレガントな淫靡さ」.
