## Introduction

Kaoru Yasui is a Japanese AV actress and former gravure idol, born in 1999 in Gunma Prefecture. She debuted as a gravure idol in 2017 and, thanks to her H-cup figure, was celebrated as the east yokozuna of the "次世代グラドル恵体番付". She made her formal AV debut from MUTEKI in February 2021, subsequently became an S1 exclusive, then moved on to OPPAI, アタッカーズ and other studios, and remains active. Alongside her acting she also works as a DJ.

## Personal notes
