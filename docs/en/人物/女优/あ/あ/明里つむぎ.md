## Introduction

Tsumugi Akari (明里つむぎ, born 31 March 1998) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Kanagawa Prefecture affiliated with C-more Entertainment. After releasing the image video "ハックツ美少女 Revolution 明里つむぎ" in January 2017, she debuted in adult video that March as an Idea Pocket exclusive with "新人 FIRST IMPRESSION 113 奇跡 明里つむぎ".

In May 2018 she won the Takeshi Tsuyoshi Award, presented as a special presenter's prize, at the DMM R-18 Adult Awards 2018, and in October that year she moved to Attackers as an exclusive. From the title released on 13 May 2019, "中年好きな小悪魔制服美少女が声の出せない状況でしたり顔でこっそりチ○ポを痴女ってくる", she returned to an Idea Pocket exclusive and held dual exclusivity with Attackers. In July 2020 she became a fourth-generation Ibert Girl alongside Eimi Fukada and Shoko Takahashi; in December she placed third in the social-media section (21st overall) of the reader vote for FLASH's "2020 Best 100 Active Sexy Actresses". In August 2021 she placed 31st in the reader vote for "FLASH 2021 Sexy Actress Ranking, chosen by 300 readers".

In January 2024 she was eighth in FANZA's monthly mail-order actress ranking, and in April "2024 Active AV Actress Sexy General Election" in Asahi Geinō placed her 48th overall (36th in Osaka, outside the Tokyo area). From May 2025 she held dual exclusivity with Idea Pocket and PREMIUM, and in November she announced a move to FALENOstar; in the week of 24 November "衝撃移籍 明里つむぎ" entered the mail-order weekly chart at eighth. In the week of 12 January 2026 "明里つむぎ 専属8年間の奇跡、軌跡 IDEAPOCKET COMPLETE BEST 全87作品 46時間 12枚組", summing up eight years of exclusivity, entered the mail-order weekly chart at second and held second the following week; she was ninth in the monthly actress ranking for January and sixth for February, and later titles continued to place high on the mail-order and video weekly charts.
