# Kokoro Asano

A Japanese adult-film performer.

## Basic information

- **Japanese name**: 浅野こころ
- **English name**: Kokoro Asano
- **Former stage name**: 歌野こころ

## Introduction

Kokoro Asano (浅野こころ, born 11 June 2002) is a Japanese adult-film performer and television personality from Sendai, Miyagi Prefecture, affiliated with Bstar. She debuted in March 2023 as a S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive under the name 歌野こころ, and announced the change to her current name on 22 August 2023. In July 2024 she topped a FANZA rental weekly chart for the first time, and she ranked No. 1 in that floor's monthly actress ranking in both July and November 2024. In January 2025 she held a release event for her second photobook and gave her first solo concert. Alongside filmed work she appears at music events, in photobooks and on television.

## Work features

- She debuted as a S1 exclusive, and her early titles largely cast her as "kire-kawa seiso" schoolgirls, younger sisters and library-committee members.
- From July 2024 her titles repeatedly took first place on the FANZA rental weekly chart, and she ranked No. 1 in the monthly actress ranking that month.
- She names "kui-uchi piston" as her speciality. AV writer Kazu Shibata called her "well established in the little-devil nymphomaniac vein", while writer Saori described her bust as "semicircular breasts with plenty of elasticity, firm enough to keep their shape even lying down".
- In 2025 she released the photobook "喉の奥まで触られたい" (GOT) and an image video of the same title (Aircontrol) produced by AV producer Nikuson.
- She works in music in parallel, appearing at events such as "Tsuki de Aimashō" and "Gakuen-sai" from 2023; at her March 2026 solo concert she performed with guitar for the first time.
