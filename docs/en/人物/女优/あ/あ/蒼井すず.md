## Introduction

Suzu Aoi (蒼井すず, born 1 May 2006) is a Japanese adult-film actress and gravure idol from Nagano Prefecture affiliated with Bstar. Her public profile described her as a current university student in Tokyo; she is 158 cm tall with measurements of B100 (I)-W59-H90. On 7 July 2026 she made her gravure debut in the 21 July issue of FLASH (Kobunsha), appearing in an I-cup swimsuit; her gravure also ran in the 10 August issue of Weekly Taishū (Futabasha), released on 27 July.

In the 22 September issue of FLASH, released on 8 September 2026, she appeared in her first hair-nude shoot and said she wanted "to express with this body the figure people want to see". She was the cover and opening gravure for the November issue of Monthly FANZA (Geo-Oh), released on 24 September, where her adult-video debut was announced with the wish "to become an actress whose existence can support someone". She also appeared as an I-cup university student in the 1 September special issue of Asahi Geinō (Tokuma Shoten), released on 18 August.

On 13 October 2026 she made her adult-video debut in the S1 title "新人NO.1STYLE 国宝級清純フェイスに最強ボディとんでもない原石が遂に、脱ぐ 蒼井すずAVデビュー". On 10 November her second adult video, "3本番 初めてのエッチ体験で原石劇変 蒼井すずセカンドAV", and the image DVD "清純は才能 蒼井すず" (FAIR&WAY) were released on the same day; on 11 November she appeared at "KNOCKIN' ON THE DOORS #13" at The DOORS in Hatsudai, Tokyo.
