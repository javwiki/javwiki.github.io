## Introduction

Azumi Ren is a Japanese AV actress from Chiba Prefecture. She made her solo debut in January 2010 under the name 蓮美恋, changed her name to あずみ恋 in July and appeared in the image video 初裸 virgin nude あずみ恋, then made her AV debut with h.m.p's 現役予備校生 4時間初めてのデビュー in August. She worked mainly with censored labels such as h.m.p, appeared in several Ōkura Eiga films between 2011 and 2014, and had a role in Shochiku's 黄金を抱いて翔べ in 2012.
