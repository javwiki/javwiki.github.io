## Introduction

Ryou Ayumi (愛弓りょう, born 27 October 1982) is a Japanese adult-film actress affiliated with ACT. In January 2019 she debuted under the name Miura Ayumi as an exclusive for SOD Create's "Honmono Hitozuma" label, then represented by ARROWS; in July that year she became a Madonna exclusive. In November 2019, marking "Good Mature Woman Day" on 19 November, she was chosen as an image girl for the "Adult Video Wife and Mature Woman Campaign".

On 7 May 2021 she announced on Twitter that she had changed her name to Ryou Ayumi, with ACT as her agency. In the week of 22 August 2022 "汗ほとばしる人妻の圧倒的な腰振りで、僕は一度も腰を動かさずに中出ししてしまった。愛弓りょう" placed first in FANZA's rental weekly chart. In the "FANZA 2024 First-Half Actress Ranking" published in the October issue of Monthly FANZA, released in August 2024, she placed 12th. In the week of 17 February 2025 the Madonna ensemble title "マドンナALL専属バスツアー2025 ハーレムを掴み取った男たちが極上の美女を独占!! 酒池肉林のお祭りはまだまだ続く!! 夢の大共演 & 大乱交FESTIVAL!! 後編" entered the mail-order weekly chart at eighth.

Beyond video work she released the photobook "清澄～せいちょう～" on 23 June 2023, and between 2019 and 2022 appeared in gravure features for Weekly Asahi Geinō, Weekly Post and Weekly Jitsuwa among others. In May 2025 she took part in "TREND GIRLS Photo Session 2025" at Shirakobato Water Park in Saitama Prefecture. From 4 January 2026 she has hosted the web radio programme "愛弓りょうのLovely Time♡".
