# Rima Arai

A Japanese adult video actress.

- **Name**: 新井リマ
- **English name**: Rima Arai

- **Place of birth**: Hyogo Prefecture, Japan
- **Height (official / published source)**: 157 cm
- **Agency**: LIGHT

## Introduction

Rima Arai (born 3 October 2000) is a Japanese adult video actress from Hyogo Prefecture, represented by LIGHT. In June 2021 she debuted as an exclusive for KMHR, a label of SODクリエイト, and in November that year she left the exclusivity and became a kikaku-tantai performer. In May 2022 《週刊プレイボーイ》 introduced her as "the pioneer who lit the ちょいギャル boom"; in the week of 10 July 2023 a title of hers reached No. 1 on the FANZA rental weekly ranking, and she also took No. 1 in that month's rental monthly actress ranking. On 3 December 2024 she made her film debut in 《ネトラレ、純愛。》 (director: 堂ノ本敬太). From June 2025 she has been exclusive to MOODYZ, kira☆kira and ワンズファクトリー, and in December 2025 at the MOODYZ year-end party she won the 最優秀NewGenius賞 and 最優秀kira☆kira賞. In January 2026 she placed 50th in 《FLASH》's 「FLASHセクシー女優ランキング2025」.

## Personal notes

- From Hyogo Prefecture; her nickname is 「リマち」.
- Her interest is saving money, and she likes watching the balance grow. Her goal is to "live independently"; she funded her move to Tokyo with savings from a high-school part-time job and kept parental support to a minimum.
- She cooks at home as much as possible using her mother's recipes and does not eat out often.
- Her first experience was at 19 with a boyfriend at a hotel; she had not been saving herself and afterwards thought "so that's all it is".
- She had hoped to work in television but was thwarted. Thinking about what to do, she remembered AV shown to her by a male classmate and called an AV agency to find out "how AV is made". She only wanted to hear about the industry as if at a job fair and had no intention of becoming an actress, but curiosity led her to debut.
- Her debut was shot in February for a June release, so she walked around Shibuya in summer clothes in winter, drawing looks from schoolchildren and passers-by.

## Work features

- Her debut persona was 「ちょいギャル」, and gyaru roles have been common since.
- After turning kikaku-tantai in November 2021 she appeared at MAXING, MOODYZ, h.m.p, ワンズファクトリー and others, with subjects including in-laws, sex services and revenge.
- She takes part in co-star projects such as MOODYZ's 「バコバコバスツアー」 series (2024, 2025); in the week of 3 November 2025 the series' VR title entered at No. 1 on the FANZA video weekly ranking.
- She has VR titles at labels such as KMPVR-彩-, and in the week of 17 February 2025 one entered at No. 9 on the FANZA video weekly ranking.
- She began appearing in films in 2024, and since June 2025 her work has centred on the three exclusive labels.
