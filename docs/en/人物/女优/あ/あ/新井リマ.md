A Japanese adult-film performer.

## Introduction

Rima Arai (新井リマ, born 3 October 2000) is a Japanese adult-film performer from Hyōgo Prefecture, affiliated with LIGHT. She debuted in June 2021 as an exclusive for SOD Create's KMHR label, left label exclusivity that November and became a kikaku-tantai performer. Her image DVD "My Girl" followed in June 2022. In December 2024 she made her film debut in "Netorare, Jun'ai." From June 2025 she has been exclusive to three labels: MOODYZ, kira☆kira and Wanz Factory.

## Work features

- She debuted with a "choi-gyaru" persona and has often been cast in gyaru roles.
- After turning kikaku-tantai in November 2021 she appeared for labels including MAXING, MOODYZ, h.m.p and Wanz Factory, with subjects ranging from stepfather and sex-industry roles to revenge scenarios.
- She has taken part in large ensemble projects such as MOODYZ's "Bakobako Bus Tour" series (2024 and 2025).
- Her VR work includes titles for KMPVR-彩-; in the week of 17 February 2025 a VR title featuring her entered the FANZA video weekly chart at No. 9.
- She began appearing in films in 2024, and since June 2025 her output has centred on exclusive releases for three labels.
