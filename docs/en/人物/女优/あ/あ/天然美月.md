## Introduction

Mizuki Amane (天然美月, born 22 December 1999) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Akita Prefecture affiliated with Wish. In August 2020 she debuted for SOD Create's Kimihore label, at the time holding part-time jobs at a ramen shop and an izakaya, and was marketed as an "ero-kawaii" performer. On 28 May 2021 she moved from Prime Agency to Wish and changed her stage name to Mizuki Amane.

Her stage name was chosen by the producer of her debut work from the phrase "tennen de azatoi", meaning naturally coy or calculated. She describes herself as a lover of "kimo-men", and many of her works are built on that theme. During her Kimihore period the packaging was uniformly orange; she calls orange her image colour and continued to name it as a favourite after leaving the label, referring to her exclusive Kimihore years as her "orange era".

After moving to project work her range widened. In the week of 7 November 2022 a title she appeared in topped FANZA's weekly video-floor AV ranking, and in the week of 24 July 2023 a VR title of hers took first place on the same chart. In 2021 she made her film debut in "共振" (Resonance), which was selected for the Kanazawa Film Festival and the Yubari International Fantastic Film Festival.

## Personal notes

- Her first sexual experience was at 16; before debuting she had been with eight people, three of them partners.
- She has said she began admiring adult-film actresses after seeing the Rui Sakuragi video her father watched. Her sexual awakening came in the early years of primary school, one trigger being "おくさまは女子高生", then serialised in Young Jump, which her father read.
- Her family worked in the nightlife trade, and her grandmother and mother raised her to "stay a woman no matter how old you get".
- She holds a manual-transmission driving licence.
- She often plays coy, calculating women, and after turning to project work has played roles from schoolgirls to young wives.
- Her pet dog's stage name is Kuroko-chan.

## Work features

- She worked for SOD Create's Kimihore label from August 2020, and its packaging was uniformly orange during that period.
- Many of her titles are built on her self-described liking for "kimo-men".
- After turning to project work in 2021 her roles expanded from schoolgirls to young wives, and the range of subject matter widened noticeably.
- Beyond adult video she has released the image DVDs "Kanon 清楚系びっち" and "くちびるツンととがらせて", the photobook "僕の元カノ", digital photobooks and pose books.
- In 2021 she appeared in the film "共振" and on the television programme "ケンコバのバコバコテレビ".
