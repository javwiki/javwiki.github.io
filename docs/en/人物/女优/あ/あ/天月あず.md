## Introduction

In February 2021 she placed third in the newcomer category of Monthly FANZA's "このAV女優がすごい! 2021 Winter". After a change of producer she switched to a bob hairstyle from March 2021. In October 2021 she retired with "電撃引退記念 星なこ COMPLETE BEST 5時間", later saying that she had not left because she disliked adult video.

In August 2022 she opened an X account under the name Azu Amatsuki and announced her return with LINX, explaining that she had asked herself whether appearing in adult video should simply become a digital tattoo. From March 2023 she was a double exclusive for E-BODY and Fitch. In May 2024 she left studio exclusivity and became a planning-only actress, and in July she appeared with Shizuki Ena, an actress she admired, in a lesbian work, marking her lesbian debut. In 2025 she cut back on work to balance her studies and planned to start employment in 2026; on 20 December she announced on X that her activity would end at the end of December 2025, with works continuing to be released until around summer 2026.
