## Introduction

Miyu Aizawa (逢沢みゆ, born 25 January 2001) is a Japanese adult-film performer and former idol. In 2013 she joined the idol group Flap Girls' School under the name 坂本マリア, and after the group's graduation in 2018 she obtained a nursery-teacher licence and worked in childcare for two years. She debuted on 12 December 2023 as a S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive; in November 2024 she announced a move to kikaku-tantai status, and in December her agency became NAX. She ranked 5th in FLASH's "Sexy Actress Ranking 2025" in December 2025. In January 2026, with her agency's permission, she disclosed that her actual date of birth is 25 January 2001.

## Work features

- Her S1 debut was titled "新人NO.1STYLE 逢沢みゆ AVデビュー 本物アイドルのAV転身、その全記録ー", marketing her former idol career as the hook.
- She remained a S1 exclusive until November 2024. After turning kikaku-tantai she appeared for SOD Create, Royal, Premium, h.m.p, Glory Quest and others, covering ensemble, step-parent, caregiving and sex-industry themes.
- Between 2024 and 2026 she released four photobooks: "相逢 sou-ai" (Futabasha), "me=you" (Tokuma Shoten), "Premium Nude Pose Book" (GOT) and "Miyutopia" (Saibunkan).
- She names high-speed cowgirl as her speciality. AV writer Katsunori Tōkō called her "good at playing virgins", and director Remiremi New World described her as "highly professional, a genius of self-production and effort".
- In September 2026 she held a photo exhibition, "未完成。", at Gallery Le Deco in Shibuya, Tokyo.
