On 10 February 2026 she announced on social media that she would return as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive with works released from March that year, about two years after retiring. After the comeback her VR title placed eighth on FANZA's weekly video-floor chart in the week of 23 March 2026 and her return work eighth in the week of 20 April; earlier titles also climbed back to tenth in the weeks of 18 May and 8 June.
