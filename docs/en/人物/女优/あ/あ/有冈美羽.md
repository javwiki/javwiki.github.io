## Introduction

Miu Arioka is a Japanese AV actress born on 17 August 1995 in Tokyo. In February 2018 she debuted as a 椎葉みくる OPPAI exclusive under the tagline "OPPAI史上最高の乳首新人－衝撃解禁。", then represented by ARROWS. On 1 October 2019 she announced on her official Twitter that she would retire that month. On 4 September 2020 she reported joining T-POWERS and returning as 有岡みう. She is known for a full bust and sensitive nipples, and is also an anime and cosplay enthusiast.
