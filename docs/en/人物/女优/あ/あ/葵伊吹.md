## Introduction

Ibuki Aoi was born on 18 May 2001 and belongs to Attractive. She made her AV debut as a MOODYZ exclusive in July 2020; her debut title placed 10th on FANZA's monthly actress chart for July and 11th in the 2020 annual sales ranking compiled by Asahi Geino with FANZA. On 26 January 2021 she moved to Bambi Promotion and renamed herself 翼あおい (Tsubasa Aoi), then on 1 October returned to the name Ibuki Aoi and to Attractive. On 25 October 2024, at the stage version of 月ともぐら, she and 石原希望 were announced as new members of Mi LUNA from お月ちゃんのうた. On 21 October 2025 (22 October Japan time) she appeared behind LeBron James in the NBA season-opener broadcast; she identified herself on X, and the post drew about 99 million impressions.

## Work features

- Studio: MOODYZ exclusive from her 2020 debut; while she used the name 翼あおい in 2021, the exclusive contract continued at the studio's request.
- Roles: university student, a girlfriend's older sister, a female boss, gyaru (GAL) characters, as in 《ノーブラで誘惑してくる彼女のお姉さんに興奮して性欲モンスター化!》, 《会社の飲みで終電過ぎて酔うとキス魔になる気さくな女上司の家に泊まったら…》 and 《隣に引っ越してきたギャルを助けた日から…》.
- From the week of 22 May 2023, 《担任教師の僕は生徒の誘惑に負けて放課後ラブホで何度も、何度も、セックスしてしまった… 葵いぶき》 topped FANZA's rental chart for two weeks running; she called it "the first title I could call a real hit".
- From 2024 she has also worked as a member of Mi LUNA with 石原希望, appearing in group titles such as 《アイドルハーレム超SUPER》 (10th on FANZA's mail-order chart in the week of 25 August 2025).
