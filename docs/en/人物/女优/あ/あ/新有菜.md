## Introduction

Arina Arata is a Japanese AV actress. She debuted as an S1 exclusive newcomer in 2016. She holds the title 「最強美腿」 (strongest legs) in the Japanese AV world. Her interests are shopping and watching videos; her specialities are singing and handstands. She entered the industry because she admired Kirara Asuka. In 2018 she won Best Actress and the Media Award at the DMM Adult Awards. She moved to MOODYZ in 2022 and changed her name to 「新有菜」. She ended her AV and public entertainment activities in December 2025.
