## Introduction

Hikari Aozora (青空ひかり, born 8 January 1999) is a Japanese adult-film actress and YouTuber from Yamagata Prefecture, affiliated with C-more Entertainment. In October 2019 she made her adult-video debut as an exclusive actress for SOD Create's SODstar label; a jacket-design contest was held for the debut work.

On 27 November 2020 she took the stage as a singer for the first time at "LADY MADONNA - Expanded Edition" at Club Citta in Kawasaki. In December of that year she placed 38th in the reader vote for FLASH's "2020 Best 100 Active Sexy Actresses". On 23 February 2021 her regular event "青空ひかりのぴかキングダム vol.1" began at LEFKADA in Shinjuku, where she was known as "Pika-hime". In May she became a campaign girl for "THE TIP" as a BASARA Girl alongside Hibiki Natsume and Mei Miyajima, first in charge of MUZU-MUZU and from October of KYUN-KYUN; on 30 November she was selected as "Valuable Actress of Midnight Blue" at the Sky PerfecTV Adult Broadcasting Awards on MIDNIGHT BLUE's recommendation, and she placed 14th in FLASH's 2021 sexy-actress ranking.

On 13 and 14 September 2022 she appeared as a singer at "Milgene 10th Anniversary Live Sweet Memories" at RUIDO in Harajuku, Tokyo. In April 2024 she modelled every page of "カメラマン リターンズEX 青空ひかり NUDE × PORTRAIT", making her nude-modelling work explicit. On 4 May 2025 she took part in the TREND GIRLS photo session 2025, appearing in a girly, classical bikini style. In January 2026 she placed 28th in "FLASH Sexy Actress Ranking 2025", published in FLASH's 6/13 January 2026 combined issue.
