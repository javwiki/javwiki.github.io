## Introduction

Tsubasa Amami is a Japanese AV actress born on 8 March 1988, from Hiroshima Prefecture (some media say Tokyo), represented by T-POWERS and working with an E cup. In October 2009 she debuted at IdeaPocket with 《FIRST IMPRESSION 44》. On 7 March 2011 she won the アサヒ芸能賞 at the スカパー!アダルト放送大賞 2011; that June she appeared in MOODYZ's 10th-anniversary collaboration series 《女教師レイプ輪姦》, and in December she made her CD debut as a member of VITCH VITCH, later also singing solo. On 16 November 2017 her title 《LEGEND IMPRESSION 史上最強コンテンツで贈る奇跡の共演!240分》 won the BEST PACKAGE award in the fan-vote category of AV OPEN 2017. She has held exclusive contracts for more than ten years since debuting, a rarity, and on 1 October 2024 she marked her 15th anniversary and announced a break from November that year.

## Personal notes

- From Hiroshima Prefecture (some media say Tokyo); her interest is playing with pets.
- Her looks often suggest a dominatrix, but the writer 久留米あぽろ calls her a "friendly older-sister type"; she describes herself as a shy "陰キャ".
- She falls in love by feeling, has no particular ideal type, and dislikes unclean people and litterers.
- She calls herself unathletic but curious, the type to try anything once.
- She was formerly with DINO and moved to T-POWERS in April 2018. She announced retirement in October 2016 and again in October 2017, but ultimately did not retire.

## Work features
