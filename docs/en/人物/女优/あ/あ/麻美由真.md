## Introduction

Yuma Asami is a Japanese TV personality, singer and actress, and a former AV actress. She began gravure work under the name 麻生由真 in March 2005, then made her AV debut with two companies at once: Alice Japan's "純情ハードコア" on 28 October 2005 and S1's "新人×ギリギリモザイク" on 7 November, and in 2006 won the Best Actress award at MOODYZ's year-end thank-you festival. In April 2008 she joined Ebisu Muscats as a first-generation member and served as its second leader from April 2010 to March 2012. In February 2013 a borderline malignant ovarian tumour was found; she underwent removal of her uterus and both ovaries plus chemotherapy, published the autobiography "Re Start～どんな時も自分を信じて～" in 2014, and announced her retirement from AV on 20 May 2015.

## Personal notes

- She collects perfumes, speaks English and plays the piano.
- In June 2013 she was diagnosed with an ovarian tumour; after removal of her uterus and ovaries followed by chemotherapy, she was left with quite a few after-effects.
- In July 2013 false reports of her death circulated; in August she appeared on a live TV programme to confirm she was well.
- In May 2014 she published her autobiography 《Re Start～无论何时都要相信自己～》.

## Work features

- Debuted with two companies at once: Alice Japan's "純情ハードコア" on 28 October 2005 and S1's "新人×ギリギリモザイク" on 7 November 2005 — a simultaneous major-label debut that was extremely unusual at the time.
- In the six months after her debut her titles took first place five times in the POS data of CDVJ member stores (November and December 2005; February, March and April 2006).
- She won Best Actress at MOODYZ's 2006 year-end thank-you festival; her title "新人 ギリギリモザイク" placed second in the festival's title award.
- In 2012 she placed second in the AV30 all-time best actress poll.
