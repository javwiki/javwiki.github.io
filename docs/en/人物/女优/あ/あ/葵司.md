## Introduction

Tsukasa Aoi is a former Japanese AV actress and gravure idol born on 14 August 1990 in Osaka Prefecture, formerly with エイトマン. She debuted as an idol in 2008 at 18 and won a regular television slot; gravure did not pay well, and when her agency told her to "fight a fight you can win" she made her AV debut in October 2010 with 《絶対少女 葵つかさ》, even though she disliked the idea. The concept was "the girl who suits a school uniform best in Japan", and uniform titles dominate. In February 2011 she became the first active AV actress to appear on the cover of 《サイゾー》 (March 2011 issue). In February 2012 she won the FLASH award at the スカパー!アダルト放送大賞; in August her first lead film 《女囚701号 さそり外伝 第41雑居房》 opened; and in December she was a ring girl at the WBA world flyweight title fight. In May 2015 she ended her アリスJAPAN contract and moved to S1, joining 恵比寿マスカッツ in September. On 20 July 2025 she announced retirement on 17 August, with her final activity the photo exhibition 「葵つかさ 生きる。」 at ギャラリー・ルデコ from 5 to 17 August.

## Personal notes

- Her 2008 idol debut brought a regular television slot. Gravure income did not grow, and when her agency said "fight a fight you can win" she debuted in AV in October 2010 despite disliking it.
- In December 2012 she was a ring girl at the WBA world flyweight title fight (井岡一翔 vs ホセ・ロドリゲス).
- In August 2013 she placed 5th in 「ラブメイト10」 on 日本テレビ's 《FNS27時間テレビ》 segment 「さんま・中居の今夜も眠れない」.
- She joined 恵比寿マスカッツ in September 2015 and took 1st in the in-show 「第4回 何か？選抜総選挙」 「マジで抱きたい女総選挙」. Nicknamed 「宇宙戦士」, she stayed about two years and was often given centre positions, though she says she is poor at dancing and some members were harsh with her.
- Her specialities are piano and saxophone.
- After shaving for the July 2017 title 《完全緊縛されて無理やり犯●れた無毛若妻 葵つかさ》 she has kept the lower abdomen hairless.

## Work features

- The concept "the girl who suits a school uniform best in Japan" gives many uniform titles.
- An アリスJAPAN exclusive from 2010 to 2015, then S1 from May 2015 until her 2025 retirement.
- In the week of 19 June 2023 the BD edition of 《エスワンファン感謝祭》 entered at No. 1 on the FANZA mail-order weekly ranking (DVD 4th), held No. 1 in the week of 26 June (DVD 5th) and reached No. 1 on the FANZA video ranking in the week of 17 July.
- 8th in the monthly FANZA video actress ranking for February 2024; 4th overall in 《アサヒ芸能》's 「2024現役AV女優セクシー総選挙」 in April 2024; 7th in the monthly FANZA video actress ranking for June 2025.
- In the week of 14 July 2025 the 2022 release 《葵つかさ 12周年 最新12タイトル全コーナー収録12時間スペシャル》 rose to No. 7 on the FANZA video weekly ranking.
