## Introduction

Unpai (うんぱい, born 1 August) is a Japanese adult-film actress, live streamer, influencer and gravure idol. She began piano lessons at four and studied classical piano on the piano course at Senzoku Gakuen College of Music. Scouted at eighteen, she started entertainment work but was defrauded of three to four million yen by an agency (a different company from the agencies mentioned below), and gave up her dream of becoming a celebrity. A friend suggested she fill her spare time with streaming; as a 17LIVE streamer she reached second place worldwide in the platform's ranking, under the name 伊藤彩 at the time, and realised she was suited to influencing. In 2018 she was a finalist in Miss iD 2019.

She made her gravure debut in 2019, using her H-cup figure as her selling point, and released the image video "うんぱいしか勝たん!" (Liverpool) on 19 January 2021. Her TikTok following passed 3.4 million in December 2021. On 25 January 2022 she announced that she would debut in adult video under the name Unpai that February, after which her adult-video work came under Mines. Her debut work, "うんぱい", became S1 NO.1 STYLE's biggest hit of the year; the 8 and 22 February 2022 issues of Kobunsha's FLASH carried semi-nude and full-nude gravure respectively. In March 2022 she signed a large contract with S1, with a second release scheduled for streaming on the 18th.

In December 2024 she announced on her own social media that she was moving to MOODYZ as an exclusive. In September 2025 she made her film debut and first lead role in "むっちり未亡人 暴かれた秘め事" (later retitled "あかねの一週間", directed by Yujiro Koseki). Alongside video work she continues to hold free birthday events and has taken part in photo exhibitions, pool photo sessions and custom-car events.
