## Introduction

Sara Uruki (now 宇流木さらら) is a Japanese AV actress born on 18 August 2000 in Chiba Prefecture, represented by LINX. She debuted in June 2020 under the name 宇流木さら, entering the industry with the title of university dance-club captain; varied technique and physical sharpness are her trademarks. On 3 December 2022 she formed the idol group mumk with agency colleagues 美園和花, 柏木こなつ and 宝田もなみ and held a debut live. In 2023 she moved from ビーダッシュプロモーション to LINX and changed her name to 宇流木さらら. On 10 March 2026 she announced an exclusive Madonna contract from April — her first exclusive deal in her fifth year (in fact sixth) since debuting — and returned to kikaku-tantai with the titles released from July that year.
