## Introduction

宇流木さらら is a Japanese AV actress who debuted in June 2020 as 「宇流木さら」, entering the industry as a dance-team leader and bringing varied technique and bodily flexibility. In 2023 she moved from ビーダッシュプロモーション to the LINX agency and changed her name to 「宇流木さらら」. From April 2026 she is a Madonna exclusive — her first exclusive contract in the five years since her debut.
