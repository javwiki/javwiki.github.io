## Introduction

Ai Uehara is a Japanese YouTuber, businesswoman and NFT creator, and a former AV actress. In April 2011 she made her AV debut in an uncredited omnibus title and then worked as a kikaku-tantai (キカタン) actress. She took 1st place on DMM's 2013 annual AV actress ranking, in April 2014 won the platinum Best Actress award at the DMM Awards 2014, and in July 2014 placed 3rd in Sega's poll for character appearances in the Like a Dragon series, earning the right to appear in "Yakuza 0". She retired on 20 May 2016, returned as a YouTuber in 2019, and in 2023 started the "AI Uehara Project" and released an AI photobook. In July 2025 she debuted in Hong Kong as the AI artist "AI_Uehara".

## Personal notes

- Born in Fukuoka, raised in Tokyo. Scouted into the AV industry while at university. Her stage name comes from the resemblance between 上原多香子 and 加藤あい.
- During her active AV years she hardly ever said no to a shoot, filming up to 25 days a month when busy. Together with Kokomi Naruse, 大槻ひびき and 波多野结衣 she was one of the figures who ignited the キカタン boom.
- Holds a kindergarten teacher's licence, childcare worker qualification, food-life adviser certification, diet certification and various other qualifications.
- In elementary school she won an award at the swimming Junior Olympics and was nicknamed "fish".
- During her active AV years she was defrauded of a total of 25 million yen, and began studying investment because of it.
- After retiring in 2016 she lost 13 kilograms and returned as an influencer in 2019.
- In 2023 she started the "AI Uehara Project" and released the world's first AI photobook.
- In July 2025 she debuted in Hong Kong as the AI artist "AI_Uehara".

## Work features

- She debuted in April 2011 in an uncredited omnibus title and then moved to kikaku-tantai (キカタン) work, becoming one of the figures — with Kokomi Naruse, 大槻ひびき and 波多野结衣 — who drove the kikaku-tantai boom.
- Took 1st place on DMM's 2013 annual AV actress ranking and in April 2014 won the platinum Best Actress award at the DMM Awards 2014.
- In July 2014 she placed 3rd in Sega's poll for character appearances in the Like a Dragon series, earning the right to appear in "Yakuza 0".
- In her final year she deliberately took on more documentary-style work; she said she wanted to show "a human side, not a machine".
