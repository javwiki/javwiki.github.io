## Introduction

Rikka Ono was born on 14 February 2002 in Shiga Prefecture and raised in Osaka; she is 148cm tall and, after All Pro and arrows, has belonged to Dias Group since 1 April 2024. On 13 May 2020 she debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive with the slogan 「おっぱいもエッチも発展途上」; her debut title was 《18歳 小野六花 新人デビュー》 (MIDE-770). She made her singing debut with 《青春JUMP!》 on 6 July 2022. In 2023 she played the heroine Iida Riho in the live-action 《カラミざかり》 series. On 25 October 2024 she was announced as a member of ねこまんまっ!! from お月ちゃんのうた, a group spun off from the TV show 月ともぐら.

## Work features

- Studio: MOODYZ exclusive from her debut; the debut title topped FANZA's daily chart for three days running, and she was 4th among actresses by sales in the first half of 2020 and 5th in the 2020 annual sales ranking.
- Subject matter: many titles use growing-up set-ups — a teenager, a younger sister, a childhood friend — such as 《まだ10代少女で発育途中! 性の知識の薄い思春期の彼女の妹とドキドキこっそりエッチッチ》 and 《両親が旅行で不在中に幼馴染がやってきて…》. The first live-action 《カラミざかり》 title in 2023 entered FANZA's weekly video chart at No. 1.
- Language: she normally speaks in Kansai dialect, and drama-style titles often cast her as a Kansai-dialect character, while many of her titles use standard Japanese; she says the Kansai dialect is easier for her.
- Signed comments: AV writer 本末ひさお described the pre-debut response as 「かわいさにユーザーが騒然となった」; 沢木毅彦 called her 「洗練された美貌でダイヤの原石そのもの」; 浜野きよぞう called the debut title 「爆発的大ヒット」.
