## Introduction

Orihara Honoka is a Japanese AV actress from Tokyo. In 2012 she worked as a gravure idol under the name 「赤井美月」, and made her AV debut that December as an E-BODY exclusive. From mid-2014 new releases paused; in May 2015 she moved to オーブエンターテイメント and made her comeback as 「折原ほのか」. From February 2020 she changed her name to 「神無月れな」 and moved to アルヴィロッソ. In October 2026 she returned at Madonna under the name 「松咲那奈」; her debut title was 《はっとくる美貌、ぐっとくるIカップ、ぱっと目覚める不倫心 松咲那奈 34歳 AV DEBUT》 (JUR-841), with a stated age of 34 and an I-cup. She is known for a large bust and large areolae, and her gravure-idol work before her debut is often used as a title setting.
