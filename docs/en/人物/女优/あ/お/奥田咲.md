## Introduction

Saki Okuda was born on 15 June 1992 in Chiba Prefecture and belongs to Office Old Crew. She released an adult image video (AIV) in January 2011 and made her AV debut as an Alice Japan exclusive in June that year, ending that exclusivity in January 2013. She moved to E-BODY in February 2013 and to S1 as an exclusive in August. She made her pink-film debut in December 2014 and her first solo stage appearance in December 2015, and in June 2018 founded the theatre troupe Dondon Cherry. In August 2023 she reached ten years as an S1 exclusive, the third actress to do so after Akiho Yoshizawa and Aika Yumeno. In January 2025 her total AV releases, including co-starring titles and personal best-of compilations, reached 200.

## Work features

- Studio phases: Alice Japan (June 2011–January 2013), E-BODY (February 2013), then an S1 exclusive (August 2013 to date); her total AV releases reached 100 in August 2018 and 200 in January 2025.
- Roles: from late 2024 mother and wife roles increased at S1, a studio centred on younger and newer actresses; she was a campaign girl for FANZA's mature-women/married-women campaign in November 2020 and again in November 2023.
- Her output spans AV, pink films and the stage: she starred in the pink film 《妹の匂い よろめきの爆乳》 in December 2014, and from November 2016 appeared in a stage production every month for a year.
- As of 2025 she names her signature technique as the "military paizuri", performed in a prone crawling posture; because only one hand holds the breasts, the other stays free.
