## Introduction

Yuika Onosaka (小野坂ゆいか, born 9 February 2002) is a Japanese adult-film actress affiliated with T-POWERS, formerly with Bambi Promotion; Chinese-language sources write her name as 小野坂唯花. In June 2024 she debuted with "FIRST IMPRESSION 174" for IdeaPocket, billed as a "fleshy heroine" (肉感ヒロイン). She has said she began reading adult manga in high school, preferred explicit men's titles, and moved from there to watching adult video; having never seen a solo-actress title, she found her first shoot different from the adult video she knew.

She finished her exclusive contract with the studio at the end of 2024 and became a planning solo actress from 2025. After a review of one of her works said her stomach bothered the viewer, she lost about four kilograms in the run-up to going solo, and in a September 2025 interview said she had dropped about seven kilograms since her debut. On 4 May 2025 she took part in the TREND GIRLS photo session 2025, where her black swimsuit was reported by WWS Channel; she was also announced for a session at Oiso Long Beach in September. In the week of 13 October 2025 her title "私たちは、イカれたファンから媚薬漬けにされてどっちにも入れ放題のアヘ顔姉妹オナホに成り下がりました…。" entered FANZA's mail-order weekly chart at fifth, and she was tenth in the January 2026 monthly mail-order actress ranking.

Her chart appearances increased through 2026. In the week of 9 February the MOODYZ 25th-anniversary title "MOODYZファン感謝祭バコバコバスツアー2026 25周年専属大集合!大乱交!大感謝スペシャル!" entered at fourth; after its release was pushed back to 19 May it topped the mail-order weekly chart in the week of 16 February and placed third on the video weekly chart in the week of 13 April. Between March and June other titles of hers entered the top ten of the mail-order, video and rental charts, including "夢?現実?仮想空間? AIでエロ動画を生成していたら、画面から飛び出してエロいプレイと中出しSEXまでできちゃった!", which debuted sixth. In April 2026 her name disappeared from Bambi Promotion's site and appeared on T-POWERS' without any statement from her; on 3 May she announced the move to T-POWERS on X. She placed fourth in Monthly FANZA's "FANZA 2026 first-half actress ranking".

- Her date of birth is given two ways: the combined 20/27 August 2024 issue of Weekly FLASH says 30 December 2001, while Monthly FANZA's July 2025 issue says 9 February 2002; Weekly Jitsuwa called her age undisclosed. This entry uses the Monthly FANZA date and records the discrepancy.
- She likes rice balls and uses a triangular "rice-ball pose" as her signature pose.
- She was in the art club in high school and at one point drew adult manga in pencil.
- Her first sexual experience was in her second year of high school with a man in his thirties she met through a game, in a manga café; she has said the tension of almost being caught became a taste for a time.
- After going solo she shoots 10 to 15 titles a month, which a Monthly FANZA editor called a high pace for a planning actress. She says her stamina means shoots never tire her and told Weekly Playboy that "sleeping resets it"; she also welcomes the wider range of roles, including unglamorous older women.
- She has little desire for fame and would rather be an actress of whom people say "this one works" than simply become famous.
- In Taiwan she is called "dark Xiao Zhennai" because she resembles the cheerleader Xiao Zhennai.
- Actor Kensuke Samejima called her tongue one of the industry's finest, reaching every corner of the mouth "as if it had a will of its own", and critic Rio Yasuda said she seems to enjoy the work of adult video itself rather than the stardom, which he called rare.
- In "TVの前のユーザー皆様に究極のオナニーを約束します!淫語女子アナ38 小野坂ゆいかアナSP", released in February 2026, she plays a female announcer; all 17 FANZA reviews as of May 2026 gave five stars, and writer Michio Sueyoshi called it a masterpiece of dirty-talk adult video.
