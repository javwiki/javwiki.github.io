## Introduction

Sakino Oto is a former Japanese AV actress born on 2 December 1997 in Kagawa Prefecture, represented by マインズ. She debuted in October 2017 as a プレステージ exclusive and became known for a 143cm frame and cute looks, called "おとさき" by fans. On 7 April 2020 she announced her retirement on her official Twitter, released a retirement title that May and ended about three years in AV. Her interests and specialities are rock baths and calligraphy.
