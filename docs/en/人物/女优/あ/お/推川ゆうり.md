## Introduction

After high school she spent two years as a pastry-chef trainee in Osaka; at the end of August 2011 she was scouted while working with a gal unit that enlivened club events, signed with Jewel Promotion and turned to gravure modelling. She passed the audition for おねだりマスカットSP! and from October 2011 appeared on the TV Tokyo programme as a seventh-generation member of Ebisu Muscats.

From 25 March 2012 she was a member of the Peace Motorsports race-queen unit Love&Peace in the Super Taikyu series; in April she passed the April round of Ketai Young Jump's ぷるるんサバイバル2012 in first place and took part again in June. She showed her first semi-nude in the mid-magazine gravure of FLASH dated 4 June 2013, released on 21 May, and debuted in adult video with MUTEKI on 1 July. She gave as her reason the sight of the Muscats members who worked as adult-film actresses looking radiant.

In 2016 she moved to planning-only work. In 2017 "淫語" (Wap Entertainment), in which she delivered verbal abuse in a gentle tone, earned her a dominatrix image and a breakthrough. She later learned of the American porn star Marika, studied abroad for about nine months, met Marika, attended an awards ceremony and met other porn stars; the stay ended with the COVID-19 pandemic. She became a Madonna exclusive in February 2024, and on 15 September 2024 walked the runway in the TREND GIRLS fashion show at TREND GIRLS 撮影会 2024.
