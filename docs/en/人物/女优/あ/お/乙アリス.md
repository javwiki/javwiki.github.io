## Introduction

Arisu Otsu (乙アリス, born 19 December 1993) is a Japanese adult-film performer from Chiba Prefecture, affiliated with L Promotion. She debuted in 2013 as 聖菜アリサ, took a break to work at an insurance company and later returned. In July 2016 she changed her name to 水嶋アリス and moved to DINO, joining the idol group Marshmallow 3d+ that October; she became its leader in 2019. In September 2020 she moved to L Promotion and took her current name. From 2021 she has worked with blonde hair, and from August 2025 she has been exclusive to IROKE, a new label within million.

## Work features

- Her early work centred on "loli" roles, often masochistic; after the change to 乙アリス her dominant-nymphomaniac roles increased, as she has described herself.
- From 2013 to 2020 she worked under the names 聖菜アリサ and 水嶋アリス, with filmed works and image DVDs released separately under each name.
- After switching to blonde hair in 2021, she says she took part in filming for around 200 or more titles in 2022 alone. That year she placed 7th in "Kono AV Joyū ga Sugoi! 2022 Summer" and 2nd in the "2022 Winter" edition.
- She worked alongside her idol activities: she belonged to Marshmallow 3d+ from 2016 to 2023, formed BLACK DIAMOND in March 2023 (as ARIS) and left it in February 2024.
- She obtained a medium motorcycle licence in December 2021 and has since appeared in media as a motorcycle enthusiast.
