## Introduction

Maria Ozawa is a Japanese businesswoman and former AV actress and stripper, born on 8 January 1986 in Hokkaido. Her father is Canadian and her mother Japanese. After high school she worked part-time while looking for a direction and entered the AV industry through an acquaintance. In October 2005 she debuted at S1 with 《新人×ギリギリモザイク ナンバーワンスタイル》 and won S1's best newcomer award at the MOODYZ year-end event that same year. In April 2007 she moved to 北都's new label ダスッ! and in July made her strip debut at 新宿ニューアート. In January 2009 she moved from ビー・オープン to T-POWERS. On 24 March 2010 she won the ライブドア賞 at the スカパー!アダルト放送大賞 and retired from AV that year to concentrate on stripping. In October 2012 she announced a full move into the Chinese entertainment industry. On 23 June 2018 a television programme ranked her third among the most famous Japanese in the Philippines and reported that she ran a bar there; she has since run lounges and restaurants based in the Philippines.
