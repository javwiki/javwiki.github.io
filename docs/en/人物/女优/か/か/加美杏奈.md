## Introduction

Anna Kami is a former Japanese AV actress and media personality born on 1 September 1996 in Tokyo, 161cm tall with measurements B89/W56/H95 (D cup) and formerly represented by Mine's. Previously working in corporate public relations, she debuted on 13 January 2020 as a flagship IdeaPocket exclusive in *FIRST IMPRESSION 138*. Alongside her studio releases, she hosted the CS television show *カミマイ!〜KamiDance!〜* on Enta!959 and the web program *はだかいっかん!*. She retired from AV at the end of August 2023 with *-The FINAL LOVE BOMB- 加美杏奈*. After retirement she married former AV actor and poker bar owner Tetsuya Shito (志戸哲也) on 28 February 2025 and participated in his Shinbashi poker room *化猫ポーカー倶楽部*. Following Shito's cerebral infarction shortly after their wedding, the marriage deteriorated into divorce mediation; in 2026 Shito posted a lengthy unilateral complaint on social media that drew online commentary, to which Kami had made no public response.

## Personal notes

- Nicknamed "Kami-chan" (`加美ちゃん`), her hobbies include reading and cooking, and her special skills are dance and rhythmic gymnastics (practiced from kindergarten through high school).
- In December 2022 she stepped back from hosting the web show *はだかいっかん!* due to a throat condition, with MINAMO stepping in as substitute MC and later taking over as regular host.
- Following her August 2023 retirement, she married former AV actor and producer Tetsuya Shito on 28 February 2025, and appeared as a special ambassador and mascot at his Shinbashi poker bar *化猫ポーカー倶楽部*.
- According to Shito's social media posts and media reports, about two months after their marriage Shito (then 46) suffered a cerebral infarction that left him with higher brain dysfunction and visual field loss (homonymous hemianopia), leading to disability and nursing-care certification and rehabilitation. Although the couple initially shared supportive updates, by late 2025 Kami shifted away from bar operations toward other activities (including appearing on a YouTube dating show), and the bar closed soon after.
- In 2026 the couple entered divorce mediation. Shito published a lengthy post on social media describing a recurring stroke and severe dizziness (including collapsing in the kitchen and injuring his front teeth), alleging that pay stubs submitted in mediation had figures blacked out to conceal income, and accusing her of nightclub spending, cutting off household electricity and publishing videos of them, which he claimed worsened his condition. The post received roughly two thousand views without sparking wider mainstream controversy; as of November 2026 Kami had not responded publicly, and the claims remain Shito's unverified unilateral account.
