## Introduction

Yura Kano (架乃ゆら, born 28 December 1998) is a Japanese television personality, actress, YouTuber and former adult-film actress from Okayama Prefecture. She worked as a model from high school and began nude modelling at 18. On 19 November 2017 she made her adult-video debut as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive under the tagline "infinite transparency"; before the debut she had appeared in a nude gravure feature in Weekly Taishū Venus.

On 29 June 2018 she joined Ebisu Muscats 1.5 as a trainee and was promoted to full member on 24 August that year. On 10 March 2020 she formed the unit SCANTY4 with Maity, Yua Mikami and Tomoki Yoshizawa, releasing the song "Panchira Rhythm"; Ichikawa Masami and Yamagishi Aika joined on 3 February 2021 to form SCANTY6. On 6 December 2021 she announced her graduation from Ebisu Muscats at the group's online concert.

In August 2021 she placed 31st in the reader vote for FLASH's "2021 Sexy Actress Ranking Chosen by 300 Readers" and fifth in Monthly FANZA's "This AV Actress Is Amazing! Summer 2021". She placed 25th in Asahi Geinō's "2023 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election" in May 2023 and 36th overall in the same election in April 2024.

On 7 May 2024 she announced that she would retire as an adult-film actress at the end of July while continuing non-AV work; on 16 May she explained on YouTube that the retirement was aimed at becoming a mainstream actress, that illness and marriage were not the reasons, and that she did not intend to return to adult video. Her retirement work had been shot that February. In October she appeared in the stage play "Back to the Un-chan: The Day the Drivers Disappear", her first stage role after leaving adult video.

On 1 May 2025 she left LINX and continued the relationship as a business partnership; on 25 September she suspended activities because of poor health and resumed in November. She has since worked as an actress and television personality in stage, web drama and television.
