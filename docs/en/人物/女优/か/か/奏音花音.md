## Introduction

Kanon Kanade is a former Japanese AV actress and entertainer born on 9 May 2000 in Fukuoka Prefecture. She debuted in July 2019, with titles from three labels released the same month, which drew attention. Her innocent, cute looks and acting won support, and the AV critic 東風克智 called her "a next-generation ace with overwhelming pitching". She placed 21st in the reader poll of 《FLASH 2020年現役最強セクシー女優BEST100》 in December 2020; in January 2021 she moved from C-more to T-POWERS, and in August she placed 50th in the FLASH reader poll. From the title released on 6 September 2022 she was a ワンズファクトリー exclusive, moved to NAX on 28 October, and from November 2023 was a 無垢 exclusive. On 26 April 2024 she announced her retirement and ended her AV work with a birthday event on 9 May.
