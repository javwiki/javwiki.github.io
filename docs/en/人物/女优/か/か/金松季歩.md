A Japanese adult-film actress, former gravure model and former AKB48 trainee. The information below follows Japanese Wikipedia and the sources listed; rankings are not used as biographical evidence.

## Introduction

Kiho Kanematsu (金松季歩, born 22 December 1989) is a Japanese adult-film actress and former gravure model from Toyohashi, Aichi Prefecture, and a former AKB48 trainee (fourth generation). She previously worked as Satomi Kaneko and Tomomi Kaneko, both read "Kaneko Satomi". In 2007 she passed the first AKB48 trainee audition, but withdrew before 15 November that year and never appeared on stage as a trainee. Under the name Satomi Kaneko she starred in the 2010 V-cinema "マン・ハンティング", her first lead role and first nude appearance, and in 2014 she moved into gravure modelling.

In gravure she won the Miss Action Grand Prix 2017 on 18 July 2017 and took first place in the first "腐りグラドルグランプリ" on TV Tokyo's "ゴッドタン" on 24 February 2018. Her agency history runs through PIGROOM (until 31 July 2020) and Lip (1 September 2020 to 24 June 2023). Her photobook "美しい人", released in October 2021, was reprinted within a week. On 24 June 2023 she announced that she would leave Lip at the end of that month and retire from show business, and held a retirement ceremony on 25 June.

On 27 February 2024 she changed her name to Kiho Kanematsu and returned in a gravure feature and interview in Kobunsha's FLASH. On 5 March she announced that MUTEKI would release her adult-video debut "18Gold" on 2 April. On 11 June she announced that she had become an S1 exclusive, with her S1 debut released on 9 July; she also opened the YouTube channel "KIHO Channel" and confirmed her agency Bstar and a first nude photobook for 18 July. On 24 December 2024 she placed fourth in FLASH's independently compiled "FLASH Sexy Actress Ranking 2024", and she placed fifth in the first-half 2025 actress ranking published in the October 2025 issue of Monthly FANZA.

## Personal notes

- She says she is poor at ad-libbing.
- Her first sexual experience was at 19, with someone she met at a part-time job.
- The media often call her a former AKB48 member, but she says she is "not someone worthy of that label" and stopped using it early.
- She never appeared on stage as a trainee; her first stage as a singer came on 26 May 2025, after she had taken the Kanematsu name, and she first sang AKB48's "夕陽を見ているか?" at a solo concert on 8 April 2026.
- During her gravure years she felt the limits of what image videos could show and watched adult videos as study material to learn which situations and settings were popular.
- At 21 she was taken to an adult-video agency by someone who had looked after her since her AKB days and stopped at the last moment; she later said she had been deceived and somewhat brainwashed.
- On retiring she said the word "retire" was the one that felt right; in another interview she said the "18-rated gravure idol" image had become so widespread that ordinary gravure offers dried up.
- When she returned in 2024 she hesitated because of her age of 34, but said she would "trust the self who worked hard at gravure for eight years" and that she had "found her calling".
- She chose her stage name herself: she kept the character 金 from her old name and combined 金銀銅 with 松竹梅, both series that put their first element first, to form 金松; 季歩 came from a fondness for two-character given names and from name fortune-telling.
- Her father worked at an ironworks and her mother was a homemaker; she has an older sister and an older brother, and her brother is a dancer.
- The adult-video writer and producer Ingoma says her appeal lies not only in being "beautiful, smiling and well proportioned" but in the boldness with which she charges at impossible requests.

## Work features

- Her adult-video debut "18Gold" (2 April 2024, MUTEKI) was framed as an extension of an image video, deliberately without interview segments.
- She has been an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive since July 2024; her titles centre on the "グラビア芸能人" series and cover adult scenarios such as boss, affair, teacher and train molesting.
- In 2024 and 2025 she joined S1's twentieth-anniversary ensemble productions with Yuka Kashiwa, Ai Hongo, Riri Nanatsumori and others.
- In 2026 she appeared in cross-studio collaborations: an Idea Pocket title with Mitsuri Nagahama and a MOODYZ twenty-fifth-anniversary production with Hinano Kuno, Momo Sakuragi and Mayuki Ito.
- Beyond adult video she continues to release photobooks and digital photobooks, and has appeared in television drama, variety, radio and the Netflix title "デスキスゲーム".
- Her VR titles have been released from 2024 onward, including the "VR NO.1 STYLE" series.
