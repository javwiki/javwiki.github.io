## Introduction

Mai Kagari, born 20 November 2000, is a Japanese AV actress and illustrator affiliated with ライフプロモーション, from Aomori Prefecture. She made her AV debut in April 2021, and her second release, 《まだAV2本目だけど中出し解禁!! おマ○コから溢れ出る精子ドロリを描きたくて… はじめてのナマ中出し》, was a long-running hit and became a talking point. Since then she has worked as a kikaku-tantai (project-only) actress, covering roles from schoolgirls to married women and mothers.

She also works as an illustrator, taking commissions through ココナラ. A lover of pachinko, pachislot and mahjong, she opened the YouTube channel 「花狩まいのギャンブルTV」 in July 2021. In the winter of 2021 she placed 4th in the actress category of Monthly FANZA's "This AV Actress Is Amazing!".

In the week of 21 March 2022, the co-starring work 《女子会してるお宅にM男くんが突撃参加! お泊り女子会で夕方から朝がくるまで一日中犯●れて小悪魔中出しされる! 白桃はな 花狩まい》 ranked 1st on the FANZA video floor weekly chart; in the week of 18 April, 《マザコンおやじに来る日も来る日も乳首をもてあそばれ開発され乳首イキを覚えてしまったワタシ。花狩まい》 ranked 1st on the rental floor weekly chart. In May of the same year she placed 49th in アサヒ芸能's "2022 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election"; in May 2023 she placed 31st in the same poll, and the rise was attributed to the number of titles she appeared in and the breadth of her roles.

From 27 to 29 March 2026 she took part in the ADULTOPIA adult expo in Taoyuan, Taiwan (メッセ桃園) and received BEST POPULAR ACTRESS (the XCITY Most Popular Actress Award); on 23 April she appeared at the music event 「KNOCKIN'ON THE DOORS #7」 at The DOORS in Hatsudai, Tokyo.
