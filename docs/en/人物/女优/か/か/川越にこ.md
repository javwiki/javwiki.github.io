## Introduction

Niko Kawagoe (川越にこ, born 23 April 2003) is a Japanese adult-film actress, chef, idol and gravure model from Hachijō, Tokyo, affiliated with GG. She is 150 cm tall. She moved to Tokyo at 18 to become a cook, trained at a culinary school and then entered the restaurant trade, specialising in Western cuisine. On 13 February 2024 she debuted as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive with "快楽で瞳トロける天性のモテSEX 新人NO.1STYLE 川越にこ AVデビュー".

Her debut work placed second in FANZA's mail-order weekly AV chart for the week of 8 January 2024 and first in the video weekly chart for the week of 5 February. She kept appearing in the charts thereafter: in the week of 6 April 2024 "照れて、惚れて、イキ止まない。ホテルでじっくりねっとり何度も何度も求め合う丸一日中ヤリまくり濃密デート 川越にこ" placed fourth in the single-title weekly chart, and her VR title that week topped the VR weekly chart. From the week of 20 May her debut work spent three consecutive weeks at first in the rental weekly chart. On 24 December 2024 she placed 19th in FLASH's independently compiled "FLASH Sexy Actress Ranking 2024".

In 2024 she released her first photobook "瞳に恋をして" and held a launch event, took part in the 10th Taiwan Adult Expo (TAE), became a campaign girl for FANZA's "Year-End New-Year Super Campaign 2024–2025", and began music work as a member of "ねこまんまっ!! from お月ちゃんのうた". In 2025 she continued mainly with S1 titles, became a campaign girl for FANZA's "Otona Campaign 2024–2025", released her second photobook "おとなにこ 川越にこ写真集", and on 26 August announced that she would also work as a gravure model under the name "nico". In June 2026 she held the photo exhibition "TRITON" at AL Gallery in Ebisu, Tokyo.

## Personal notes

- At debut she disclosed only that she was from an "island"; about two months later she revealed she is from Hachijō Island. Her family home is a shop selling household goods, and she was raised by her grandmother, to whom she explained her work during a return-home shoot for Weekly Playboy in November 2024.
- The name "Niko" expresses her wish to keep smiling while working as an actress. "Kawagoe" comes from the surname she held only briefly after birth (her father's family name, written 河越 in kanji). In some Chinese-character regions she is written as 川越鈴子.
- She majored in Western cuisine at culinary school and her specialities are carpaccio, acqua pazza and hamburger steak. At debut she worked roughly eight parts as a chef to two as an actress, and her long-term goal is to open an auberge.
- Her interests are travel, yoga, weight training and spa visits; she climbs Mount Fuji and visits Ise Shrine every year and often goes boat fishing. In June 2026 she built a gaming PC during a livestream and streamed games for the first time.
- She holds qualifications including vegetable sommelier, vegetable specialist, food coordinator and sanitation manager. On 16 June 2025 she announced her first-class small-craft operator licence, on 26 July she reported passing the special small-craft (jet ski) exam, and her next target is the maritime special radio operator licence.
- She travels privately with senior studio actresses Hime Hayasaka and Marin Mita, and is also close to Karin Tōno and Fuyumo Shiroha, who debuted in the same 2024 cohort.
- She has said her first love was a junior-high PE teacher and that she is confident about fellatio.
