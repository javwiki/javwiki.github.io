## Introduction

楓ふうあ is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive in August 2021. On 20 April 2023 she moved from Attractive to c-more ENTERTAINMENT while remaining an S1 exclusive. In July 2023 GOT published her photobook "Premium Nude Pose Book: Kaede Fua" (photography: Hiroaki Tamura). In November 2024 she appeared in S1's 20th-anniversary title "S1 PRECIOUS GIRLS 2024 All-star 24-member Harem Island Special", whose Blu-ray edition topped FANZA's weekly mail-order chart for two consecutive weeks. She has also appeared on TV Tokyo's "Tsuki to Mogura" and TBS Radio's "Nemuchiki".
