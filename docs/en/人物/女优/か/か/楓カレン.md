## Introduction

Karen Kaede (楓カレン, born 25 August 1999) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Tokyo and a former gravure idol, affiliated with Life Promotion. She debuted as a gravure idol in September 2018 and in December of that year made her adult-video debut as an exclusive performer for IdeaPocket, chosen to mark the studio's twentieth anniversary.

In April 2021 she placed ninth in Weekly Asahi Geinō's "2021 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election". In August she placed 23rd in the reader vote for FLASH's 2021 sexy-actress ranking, and in both August and September she topped FANZA's monthly rental-floor actress ranking. She took no part in the industry's award season, yet came to be regarded as a pillar of IdeaPocket.

On 28 March 2022 she announced her retirement on Twitter. On 20 April 2023 she re-debuted as "Tanaka Remon", exclusive to FALENO and represented by Eightman, releasing five works; outlets in Taiwan and elsewhere reported that this was the same person. On 27 June she announced a full comeback under the name Karen Kaede and rejoined Life Promotion; she released her comeback work on 14 November and returned to IdeaPocket exclusivity, winning the Comeback Award at the 2023 Asagei AV Awards that December. In April 2024 she placed third overall in Weekly Asahi Geinō's "2024 Active AV Actress Sexy General Election".

## Personal notes

- She is from Tokyo. Her speciality is cooking, especially nikujaga, and her staple food is cheap sweets.
- She names Arina Hashimoto as the actress she admires, and is often said to resemble the actress Haruna Kawaguchi; as a gravure idol she admired Shizuka Nakamura.
- She keeps a Chin dog named Ushi and an Exotic Shorthair cat named Kani.
- Despite her appearance she is an anime fan who used to visit Animate; encountering boys' love titles there led her to make reading BL manga a hobby.
- She wore her hair long for years but cut it short for her 2023 re-debut.
- In Japan she carries the nickname "the female Yoshimura Taku"; Chinese-language media write her name as 楓花戀, and Taiwan reports called her the "dark-side Haruna Kawaguchi" for the resemblance.
- The comedian Takaya Aoyagi describes her as "looking demure, yet the type to calculate little gags and slip them in".
- Kiryūin Shō of Golden Bomber is a declared fan and said her comeback was "like a disbanded band suddenly reuniting — Japan still has dreams".

## Work features

- She debuted as an IdeaPocket exclusive, chosen for the studio's twentieth anniversary, and remained exclusive to it after her 2023 return.
- Her output includes solo and co-starring titles; Wikipedia lists numerous VR works, among them an 8K VR feature in 2024.
- In the week of 13 February 2023, "楓カレン-純美の終焉- COMPLETE BEST 48時間BOX 豪華37作品12枚組" topped FANZA's mail-order weekly chart.
- In the week of 13 January 2025 her co-starring title with Arina Shin "楓カレンと新ありな。最強のふたり。女神ハーレム 今世紀最も美しいお姉さんに挟まれ痴女られSpecial" entered the FANZA mail-order weekly chart at third and placed 18th for the year in Asahi Geinō's "best-selling AV of 2025" ranking.
- Beyond adult video she has released image videos and six photobooks between 2019 and 2026, and has appeared in TV variety shows and a video-game role.
