## Introduction

Konatsu Kashiwagi (柏木こなつ, born 13 July 2003) is a Japanese adult-film performer from Yamanashi Prefecture, affiliated with B Dash Promotion. She debuted in November 2021 as an exclusive for SOD Create's Seishun Jidai label and moved to kikaku-tantai status in April 2022. In December 2022 she formed the four-member idol group mumk with Waka Misono, Monami Takarada and Sara Uruki. In March 2026 she appeared at ADULTOPIA in Taiwan and received the Best Creative Works award for "密着ベロチュー女子校生と先生の生中出し性交" (MAX-A).

- She has appeared at live events as a member of mumk since 2022; in December 2025 she appeared in the film "妄想ファンファーレ".
