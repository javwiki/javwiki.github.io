## Introduction

Saika Kawakita is a Japanese AV actress and singer, born in Chiba on 24 April 1999. She debuted in April 2018 with the S1 exclusive "新人NO.1STYLE 河北彩花AVデビュー". In May 2019 that title won both the streaming and rental title awards at the FANZA Adult Awards 2019, and that year she stopped releasing new titles and retired. She announced her comeback on 18 June 2021 and released the comeback title "河北彩花 Re:start!" on 19 July; that year she won Best Actress at Weekly Playboy's "2021 Adult Awards". On 6 March 2024 she changed her name to 河北彩伽 and moved to Mine's, and on 6 February 2026 she reverted to 河北彩花 and moved to T-POWERS; from February 2025 she has also worked in music as 「萌名」.

## Work features

- Her debut title "新人NO.1STYLE 河北彩花AVデビュー" took 1st place in FANZA's 2018 annual video ranking, and three further titles made the annual top 10.
- In 2019 that title won both the streaming and rental title awards at the FANZA Adult Awards 2019.
- Her comeback title "河北彩花 Re:start!" was S1's best-selling title of 2021; "シン・交わる体液、濃厚セックス" took 1st place in Weekly Asahi Geinō's "2022 AV annual sales ranking".
- Her 2023 title "出張先で軽蔑している中年セクハラ上司と〜" was the year's third best-selling title.
