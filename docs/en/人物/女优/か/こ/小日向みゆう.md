## Introduction

Miyu Kohinata was born on 10 July 2002 in Tokyo and is represented by T-POWERS. On 13 June 2023 she debuted from S1 NO.1 STYLE as 清原みゆう, then represented by Bstar. Her debut title was No. 1 on the FANZA video ranking in the week of 5 June 2023, and between September and October she placed 4th, 1st, 2nd and 3rd on the FANZA rental ranking. On 28 July 2023 she released her first photo book 《miracle》, in August the image video 《ALL NUDE 清原みゆう》, and in October she took the cover of 《月刊FANZA》 for the first time. In October 2024 she moved to T-POWERS and changed to her current name. On 16 July 2026 she explained that ill health had stopped her shooting and releases, and stated clearly that this was not a retirement.
