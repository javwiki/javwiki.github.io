# Miyu Kohinata

A Japanese adult-film performer.

## Basic information

- **Japanese name**: 小日向みゆう
- **English name**: Miyu Kohinata
- **Former stage name**: 清原みゆう
- **Date of birth (official or publisher source)**: 2002-07-10
- **Height (official or publisher source)**: 160 cm

## Introduction

Miyu Kohinata was born on 10 July 2002 in Tokyo and is represented by T-Powers. She debuted with S1 No.1 Style on 13 June 2023 under the name Miyu Kiyohara; her agency at the time was Bstar. In October 2024 she moved to T-Powers and took her current name. On 16 July 2026 she said on X that ill health had stopped filming and releases, and that she had not retired.

## Work features

- She has been exclusive to S1 since debut, with her 95 cm H-cup at the centre of works built around paizuri, nursing, busty office worker, younger sister, nurse and sex-worker settings.
- Alongside standard releases she has made a steady run of VR titles, and has published the photobook "miracle" (2023), several digital photobooks, calendars and trading cards.
- Her 2023 debut topped FANZA's weekly video ranking in the week of 5 June; in Asahi Geino's 2023 AV sales ranking she placed four titles in the top 100, the most of any newcomer.
- AV writer Kazu Shibata called her "a miracle bust on a childlike face"; Saori praised her bust from every angle, and Noriki Washitani described her as "the complete form of the loli big-breast type".
