## Introduction

Kokonoi Sunao is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive on 15 August 2023, belonging to HANAYA PROJECT under the SOD Group. Besides AV work she runs a Fantia fan club, takes part in offline events such as cosplay and photo sessions, and has a YouTube channel, "九井スナオの休日".
