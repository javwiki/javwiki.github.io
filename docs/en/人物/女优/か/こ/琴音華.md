## Introduction

Hana Kotone (琴音華, born 17 August 2000) is a former Japanese adult-film actress from Akita Prefecture who was affiliated with T-POWERS. On 1 March 2021 she debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive. She said she entered the industry to do something for her parents and did so with her mother's permission; she sent her mother the on-set photographs, package images and interview content after each shoot. In December 2021 she won the outstanding newcomer award at the Weekly Playboy Adult Awards 2021 and placed 22nd in FLASH's 2021 sexy-actress ranking.

In January 2023 she announced that she was leaving MOODYZ exclusivity and becoming a project-based solo actress. On 1 March 2024 she announced that she would retire at the end of that month, and on 31 May she left T-POWERS; her retirement was also reported in Taiwan. In January 2025 she released her retirement work and ended her active career, deleting her social media accounts on the release date. According to the official profile cited by Wikipedia, she is currently registered at Red Dragon in Roppongi.
