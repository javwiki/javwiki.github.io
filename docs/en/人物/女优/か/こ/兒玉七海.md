## Introduction

Nanami Kodama (兒玉七海, born 22 June 2002) is a Japanese adult-film actress. On 10 September 2021 she debuted with S1 NO.1 STYLE as 小倉七海 in "新人NO.1STYLE 小倉七海AVデビュー"; the adult-video writer Takehiko Sawaki called the debut "a newcomer who feels Showa" and "a rough diamond". That year she placed 33rd in Kobunsha's FLASH 2021 sexy-actress ranking and second in the newcomer section of Monthly FANZA's "This AV Actress Is Amazing! Winter 2021".

On 1 October 2022 she announced her retirement from adult video and her departure from Life Promotion. Wikipedia gives the reason as follows: she already had physical and mental health problems, and the periods when filming stopped after the Act on Prevention of Harm Caused by Adult Video Performance came into force — three months without income — worsened them, and her doctor told her to stop. She continued public work as ほしののの. In May 2023 she renamed herself Nanami, and on 12 September she announced her move to T-POWERS and the name Nanami Kodama. She returned to commercial gravure in the Asahi Geinō issue of 21 December 2023.

On 19 April 2024 she returned to acting with "伝説の美少女 兒玉七海 復活デビュー ～わたしが本当にヤリたかった3つのSEX～". On 28 October 2026 she announced that she was leaving her S1 exclusive contract to work as a freelance solo actress and gain experience across a wider range of genres.
