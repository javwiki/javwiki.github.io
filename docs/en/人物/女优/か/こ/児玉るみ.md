## Introduction

Rumi Kodama is a Japanese AV actress born on 3 June 1979, represented by ティーパワーズ and working in the mature-woman genre. She debuted on 25 October 2015 in マドンナ's 《初撮りおばさんドキュメント》 and released three Madonna titles that month. From 2016 she worked as a kikaku-tantai performer for ホットエンターテイメント, セレブの友, ナチュラルハイ, V&R PRODUCE, グローリークエスト, タカラ映像, DANDY, LEO and many others, and appeared in GIGA's tokusatsu-hero title 《ヒロイン罠堕ち 〜グラマー仮面〜》. In December 2016 she appeared in the adult film 《こくまろオッパイ かきまぜられた私》 (オーピー映画) as 村岡恵子. From 2017 she also took VR and television work, and 《100万×中出し》, in which she appeared in 2019, placed second in the planning category of AV OPEN 2018.
