## Introduction

Momona Koibuchi is a Japanese AV actress, singer and lyricist. She belongs to Life Promotion and is exclusive to SOD Create. Born in Akita Prefecture on 3 March 1999, she grew up in Yamagata Prefecture. After working as a local government employee at Yamagata City Hall, she retired in 2021 and moved to Tokyo. She made her gravure debut in FLASH in February 2022 and her AV debut as a SOD Star exclusive on 21 April 2022. She attracted attention for her M-cup figure (later O-cup) and her former civil-servant background, and from 2024 has also pursued a singing career under the name Koimomo.

## Background to the career change

In an interview with [Bunshun Online](https://bunshun.jp/articles/-/59459) published on 26 December 2022, Koibuchi discussed in detail why she left the civil service. She said that in addition to her workload, she faced workplace sexual harassment; when she resigned, her mother strongly opposed the decision and, even when her physical and mental condition had visibly deteriorated, told her that "it's your own fault for quitting being a civil servant." She had almost no contact with her parents for several years and did not tell them about her current work. She said she does not seek their approval and wants to prioritise her own feelings.

In [the first part of the same series](https://bunshun.jp/articles/-/59458), she also described her work in the tax division: she was working until midnight on her second day, and data entry for light vehicle tax and counter explanations meant the work never ended. Her peers left on time, and as a high-school graduate she earned less. What decided her departure more than the workload was sexual harassment: she was routinely asked "what cup?", was nearly forcibly kissed in a taxi, and even colleagues with wives and children took part; acquaintances she had known since primary school also harassed her. She felt the local environment blamed women, saying "it's because you dress like that." She left after about a year and a half. She later said that victims are never at fault: if they can speak out, they should; if not, they should leave the environment.

The main reason for the career change was not a desire for fame but years of distress over her large breasts. She was vulnerable to sexual harassment and sexual victimisation, could not find bras or clothes that fitted, kept searching for ways to reduce her breast size and considered reduction surgery. After reading about someone abroad whose breast reduction reversed within months, she concluded that she had to accept her body. She applied through an agency website and debuted about six months after joining the agency. On the day her debut work went on sale, she saw a customer in a DVD shop pick up her title and felt for the first time that "the body I had always been ashamed of could become a weapon."

- SOD Star exclusive; debuted on 21 April 2022. Her M-to-O-cup figure is the main selling point.
- Genres include big breasts, nymphomania, hot-spring travel, NTR and creampie; she first performed a creampie in July 2023, and expanded her range further from 2025.
- In 2023 she formed "4star X sister" with Rei Kamiki, Yotsuha Kominato and Riko Hoshino, and released co-starring works.
- From 2024 she has released music as Koimomo alongside her AV work; in 2026 her starring work won BEST ADULT WORK (a work award) at ADULTOPIA.
- Besides solo titles, there are a third-anniversary best, photobooks and digital photobooks.
