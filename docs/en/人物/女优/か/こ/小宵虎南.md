## Introduction

Koyoi Konan is a former Japanese AV actress, now working as a DJ, YouTuber and influencer. She was born in Tokyo on 2 April 1999. She made her AV debut as an S1 exclusive on 19 May 2021 and drew attention for her H-cup figure and "divine bust" image. She left C-more ENTERTAINMENT in September 2024 and announced her AV retirement on YouTube on 1 February 2025. Since retiring, she has focused on YouTube, DJ work, her fan club and live events. In September 2025 she became the centre of a controversy over her marriage, an affiliated company, unauthorised merchandise and fan-club revenue; see below.

## Personal notes

- Born in Tokyo (Chinese Wikipedia gives Fukuoka Prefecture; see "Scope"). Her nicknames are "こなんちゃん" and "こなち". She lists ballet and piano as skills and going to the gym and watching AV as hobbies.
- Her personal social media was well received before her debut; an acquaintance connected her with C-more ENTERTAINMENT, and after visiting studios she entered the AV industry. She debuted as an S1 exclusive in 2021.
- She became popular for her H-cup figure and the "divine bust" image. She placed 15th in the 2023 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election and rose to 10th in the 2024 edition.
- She left C-more ENTERTAINMENT in September 2024 and was removed from the agency's website in October. On 1 February 2025 she announced her retirement; in the retirement video she cited TikTok's suspension of her account, the loss of about 400,000 followers and her agency's slow response as reasons for leaving.
- In December 2024, at a fan event in Taiwan, she took part in a drinking game without realising what it was and was hospitalised with acute alcohol poisoning. She returned to Taiwan for a fan meeting in December 2025.
- After retiring she continued with YouTube, DJ work and her fan club. In June 2025 she placed second in the monthly FANZA actress ranking, showing that her popularity continued after retirement.
- In September 2025 a dispute over money and merchandise became public. Her side denied the claims through a staff account, calling them "entirely baseless". See the feature page for details.

## Work features

- An S1 exclusive from 2021 to 2024; her H-cup figure and "divine bust" image were the main selling points.
- Common genres included big breasts, nymphomania, office-lady and teacher roles, hot-spring travel and NTR; she also appeared in VR, co-starring and compilation titles.
- A co-starring work from the 2023 S1 Fan Thanksgiving ranked first on FANZA mail order and video charts; she appeared in many FANZA weekly and monthly rankings in 2023–2024.
- She placed 10th in the April 2024 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election; after her 2025 retirement, older titles continued to rank on FANZA's monthly and weekly charts.
- Her publications include the photobooks "with you tonight" and "プレミアムヌードポーズブック 小宵こなん", image DVDs and calendars.

## Controversy

In September 2025, Takizawa Gareso, Nanase Alice and ami (formerly Ami Tokita) publicly alleged that she had concealed her marriage, that a company whose representative was her spouse had sold merchandise without authorisation, and that fan-club revenue had not been paid. Koyoi's side denied the claims through a staff account, calling them "entirely baseless". As of October 2026, no public settlement, formal apology or court ruling had been reported. For the detailed timeline, the parties' positions and the limits of the evidence, see [The Koyoi Konan controversy](../../../../专题/小宵虎南炎上事件.md).
