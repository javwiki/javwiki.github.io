## Introduction

Minami Kojima is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as an アリスJAPAN exclusive in July 2011 with 《真★美少女》. She moved to S1 NO.1 STYLE in March 2014, graduated from S1 in September 2024 and became a Madonna exclusive from November, releasing a creampie-debut title. She is also active as a singer and is a former member of the idol groups 「SEXY-J」「Ebisu Muscats」「Ebisu Muscats1.5」「おとといフライデー」 (disbanded 9 March 2024). Her YouTube channel 「こじみな放送局」 (opened 4 April 2017) has 82,200 subscribers (as of August 2026). She won the DMM.R18 Adult Award 2015 media award and the SkyperfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards 2016 Tokyo Sports Award.

## Personal notes

- From Kanagawa Prefecture; hobby: walking; specialities: tennis and swimming (per the agency and Japanese Wikipedia; the old page's coffee/shopping notes come from early material).
- Known for the contrast between a petite 150cm figure and a big bust, a high-pitched anime-like voice and improvisational acting, she often appears on variety shows; she revealed her profession to her parents at her terrestrial-TV debut.
- A dog lover: in 2024 she founded the dog-harness brand 「LOULOU -ルル-」 (French for "cute child"; the trigger was her dog Shukuru's weak throat, which led her to switch from a collar to a body harness and then to make a cute all-in-one harness herself). She handles planning through production, stock and shipping herself: six models in eleven colourways kept under ¥10,000, fully made in Japan by an Osaka manufacturer, stored at home and packed and shipped by her; exhibited at ジャパンわんこフェスタ2024 in November. In August 2020 she, 紗倉まな and Ai Uehara all became Japanese sake culture ambassadors.
- In a November 2024 interview with 日刊SPA! she discussed her work and views on marriage: the debut period was the hardest and she thought of quitting after a few titles, she had already hit her savings target by the end of her first year, and she kept going when a transfer offer arrived as her motivation was flagging. She calls being an AV actress her 「天職」 (calling) and says actress friends often remark that there is 「辞める理由がない」 (no reason to quit). On marriage she said she cannot picture marrying while active and that the urge to date is fading year by year.
- In February 2022 she was appointed the second annual ambassador of Jex's condom brand ZONE.
