## Introduction

Mao Kurata (倉多まお, born 7 March 1994) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Akita Prefecture affiliated with Newgate. She debuted with S1 in August 2012; looking back, she has said she cannot remember what prompted the debut. Her hobbies are dance and her speciality is figure skating, and she is a member of the Vanilla Girls on the CS SkyPerfect 958 channel Vanilla Sky Channel.

She has worked continuously since 2012, appearing not only in adult video but in SILK LABO's female-oriented titles, image DVDs, photobooks and digital photobooks, as well as on television and in a music video. In the week of 22 May 2023 her appearance in "BAZOOKA 冬の大感謝セール コスパ最強ノーカットベスト爆乳限定2749分" topped FANZA's weekly video-floor AV ranking; in the week of 30 October that year a title starring her placed tenth on the FANZA mail-order floor. In the week of 24 February 2025 the 2024 release "【配信専用】倉多まお 全部見せます完全収録BEST 1320分" climbed to seventh on the FANZA video floor.

## Work features

- She debuted with S1 in August 2012 and has since worked mainly in solo and co-starring titles, appearing continuously from 2012 to 2025.
- Beyond adult video she has appeared in SILK LABO's female-oriented titles (2016–2023), an image DVD (2017) and television programmes (2013, 2017).
- Her photobook work includes the 2012 Futabasha photobook "All Blue" and several digital photobooks released by Ilogus and Prestige Publishing between 2017 and 2023.
- Between 2023 and 2025 BAZOOKA compilations and her solo best-of titles entered the FANZA weekly top ten several times.
