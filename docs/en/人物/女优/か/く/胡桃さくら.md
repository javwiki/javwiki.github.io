## Introduction

Sakura Kurumi (胡桃さくら, born 29 October 2002) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Fukui Prefecture affiliated with T-POWERS. On 23 February 2022 she made her adult-video debut as an exclusive performer for SOD Create's "Mogitate" label. She had moved to Tokyo to enter a voice-acting school; because work for new voice actors mostly involves adult animation and adult games while her own experience was limited, she appeared in three titles only, for study, and later continued.

In summer 2022 she placed third in Monthly FANZA's "This AV Actress Is Amazing! Summer 2022, Newcomer Edition". In January 2023 she appeared in episodes 434 and 435 of the Pachi-Slot Site Seven TV programme "魚拓と成瀬のツキとスッポンぽん". In March 2022 she had also given an interview and posed for the March issue of Monthly Soft On Demand.

In April 2024 she placed 37th overall in Asahi Geinō's "2024 Active AV Actress Sexy General Election"; in the week of 8 April one of her titles reached 10th on FANZA's video weekly chart, and in the week of 28 October a title entered FANZA's mail-order weekly chart at ninth. In the week of 17 February 2025 a VR title she appeared in entered the FANZA video weekly chart at ninth, and in the week of 26 January 2026 a title entered the FANZA mail-order weekly chart at eighth. Prestige published three digital photobooks of her between February and August 2025, and on 1 March 2026 she took part in REAL's 20th-anniversary event in Yoyogi, Tokyo.
