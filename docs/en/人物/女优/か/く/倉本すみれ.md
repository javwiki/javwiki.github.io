# Sumire Kuramoto

A Japanese adult-film performer.

## Basic information

- **Japanese name**: 倉本すみれ
- **English name**: Sumire Kuramoto

## Introduction

Sumire Kuramoto was born on 14 September 2001 in Tokyo and is represented by Body Corporation. She made her adult-video debut with MOODYZ in October 2021 in a newcomer title set around a café worker in Shibuya. She then worked as a freelance performer across MOODYZ, MAXING and h.m.p, and from November 2024 became exclusive to three labels: Muku, Honnaka and Das!. In 2025 she formed the duo fleuЯR with Ichika Matsumoto and released the single "Suki Sugiru / Asu Kumori no Chi".

## Work features

- Her debut was a MOODYZ newcomer title; she subsequently appeared across labels as a freelance performer, playing girlfriends, younger and older sisters, wives, scheming women and more demanding scenarios.
- From winter 2021 she appeared repeatedly in FANZA and Monthly FANZA rankings: third among newcomers in winter 2021, second in the actress category in summer 2022 and eighth for 2022 overall.
- She has a slim build and was initially known in part for natural body hair; she released a shaved work in late 2024 and returned to her previous look in 2025, which Weekly Playboy described as part of her role preparation.
- AV writer Takehiko Sawaki said she could handle top students, poor students, little devils, malicious women, daughters and sisters, calling her "the Reiwa monster of the AV world"; Hisao Motosue and Joji Shibue also commented on her physique.
