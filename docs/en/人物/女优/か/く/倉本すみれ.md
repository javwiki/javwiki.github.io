# Sumire Kuramoto

A Japanese adult video actress.

## Basic information

- **Name**: 倉本すみれ
- **English name**: Sumire Kuramoto
- **Date of birth**: 14 September 2001
- **Place of birth**: Tokyo, Japan
- **Height**: 153cm
- **Agency**: ボディコーポレーション

## Introduction

Sumire Kuramoto was born on 14 September 2001 in Tokyo and is represented by ボディコーポレーション. In October 2021 she debuted with MOODYZ's newcomer title 《新人20歳 女子アナにいそうなほど可愛い! 渋谷区にあるお洒落なカフェで働く敏感スレンダー美少女 エッチが好きすぎてAVデビュー!!》 and placed 3rd in 《月刊FANZA》's 「このAV女優がすごい!2021年冬 新人編」. She then worked as a kikaku-tantai performer at MOODYZ, MAXING, h.m.p and others, placing 2nd in the monthly FANZA actress ranking for November 2022 and 8th for 2022 overall. In November 2023 she was a campaign girl for 「みんなあつまれ MOODYZキャンペーン2023」. On 15 October 2024 she announced that she was leaving kikaku-tantai work and would become exclusive to 無垢, 本中 and ダスッ! from November. In 2025 she formed the duo 「fleuЯR」 with 松本いちか and released the single 《好きすぎる / 明日くもりのち》.

## Work features

- The debut was a MOODYZ newcomer title; she then worked across labels for a long time as a kikaku-tantai performer, covering girlfriends, younger sisters, older sisters, married women, villains and hard projects.
- From winter 2021 she appeared repeatedly in FANZA and 《月刊FANZA》 rankings: 3rd in the 2021 winter newcomer list, 2nd in the 2022 summer actress list, 8th for 2022 overall, 11th in the 2023 first half and 10th (mail-order) / 2nd (rental) for 2023 overall.
- In the week of 12 February 2024 《MOODYZファン感謝祭 バコバコバスツアー2024》 entered at No. 1 on the FANZA mail-order weekly ranking; she was 3rd in the February 2024 monthly video actress ranking.
- In the week of 13 October 2025 《【MOODYZ創立25周年記念】 MOODYZ 大ハード学園》 reached No. 1 on the FANZA video weekly ranking.
- She has a slim figure and natural body hair was one of her early signatures; after a shaved title at the end of 2024 she restored it in 2025, and 《週刊プレイボーイ》 described the shaving as part of her role-building.
- The AV writer 沢木毅彦 says she can play honours students, dropouts, little devils, villains, daughters, older sisters and younger sisters, and calls her "the Reiwa monster of the AV world".
