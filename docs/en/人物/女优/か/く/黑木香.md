## Introduction

Kaoru Kuroki was born on 21 January 1965 in Yokohama, Kanagawa Prefecture, and is a former AV actress. In 1986, while studying Italian art at Yokohama National University, she debuted in Crystal Eizo's 《SMぽいの好き》 to fund a stay in Italy. In that title she provoked the director and the male performer, unlike the male-led productions of the time, and it became an exceptional hit. She went on to appear in variety shows, dramas and discussion programmes and published several books. In 1994 she fell from a second-floor balcony at a hotel where she was staying, and in 2004 she sued several publishers over privacy and portrait rights.

## Personal notes

- Born in Yokohama, Kanagawa Prefecture; graduated from a girls' junior and senior high school.
- Studied Italian art in the art department of the Faculty of Education at Yokohama National University.
- Entered the AV industry to fund her study-abroad in Italy.
- Her first-person pronoun is 「わたくし」, known for polite diction and a fast, proactive way of speaking.
- Boldly showed her armpit hair; she has never shaved since the age of 16.
- She and 村西とおる were once romantically involved, and she served as a director of a ダイヤモンド映像 group company.
- In 1994 she had a hotel accident (a fall from a building), later reported by weekly magazines as a failed suicide attempt.
- In 2004 she sued several publishers over privacy and portrait rights.
- In the 2019 Netflix series 《全裸監督》 her character was played by 森田望智.

## Work features

- Her debut 《SMぽいの好き》 had her provoking the director and the male performer, unlike the male-led productions of the time, and became an exceptional hit.
- AV appearances by university students were not publicly known at the time, and a current student at a national university who disclosed the institution's name was exceptional; prolonged absence from classes led to her being struck off the rolls at Yokohama National University.
- Her work went beyond AV: she appeared in the film 《TOKYO-POP》 (1988), dubbed 《エーゲ海に捧ぐ》 and sat on discussion programmes such as 《朝まで生テレビ!》.
- In 1987 she published 《フルーツ白書》, 《自堕落にもほどがある》 and 《女と男の間には―黒木香対談集》, and co-wrote 《性の構造》 with 伊藤比呂美; in December that year she released the mini-album 《小娘日和》.
