## Introduction

Shiori Kuraki (倉木しおり, born 10 September 1995) is a Japanese adult-film actress, now with Lister Pro after All Promotion. She debuted in adult video in February 2018. Despite a "straight-laced pretty-girl" look, she has had few limits since debut and has appeared in hard titles; on her motivation she says finishing a shoot gives her a sense of achievement, and that "even when I think I can't do it, the camera rolls and a switch flips — it turns out I can do more than I expected".

In October 2019 she was nominated in the Tiny Girl division of the GEO TV × Men's Cyzo ADULT AWARD 2019 under the tagline "orthodox but headlong girl", and in December she won the division's Grand Prix, with the ceremony held on 10 January 2020. In February 2020 she announced that she was joining the idol group Marshmallow 3d+ team Merengue; on 8 June 2021 she played her first solo concert at Grapefruit Moon in Sangenjaya, Tokyo, as part of "月で逢いましょうvol.13". In February 2023 Kyanamin and in May 2023 Hisui Matsumiya graduated in succession, leaving her the group's only member, and on 26 July 2023 the group's official account announced its disbandment.

Between March and April 2026 her older titles climbed the FANZA weekly charts: in the week of 16 March the November 2024 release "汁男優ガチ勃起の痴女お姉さん 子宮に本物ザーメン13発" rose to fourth on the streaming floor; in the week of 6 April a co-starring title released in April 2025 rose to fifth on the video floor, and in the week of 13 April the same title placed seventh.

## Work features

- She debuted in February 2018 and has appeared in adult video, VR, streaming and image video, including hard titles and co-starring projects.
- As an idol she has the single CD "empoisonne" (2022) and group work, and in 2021 she released the image video "揺れていたい".
- Her photographic work includes the 2021 G-Walk photobook "addiction" and several digital photobooks released between 2020 and 2024.
- In 2021 she appeared in the first episode of Netflix's "全裸監督2".
