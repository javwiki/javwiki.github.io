## Introduction

Sumire Kurokawa (黒川すみれ, born 25 April 1991; another source gives 30 January 1989) is a Japanese adult-film actress affiliated with Mine's and from Shiga Prefecture. Her official statistics are 167 cm, B88 (G)-W56-H88, blood type O. She has worked under several stage names, including Inagawa Natsume and Nishida Natsu, and her career has involved repeated renamings and comebacks.

She debuted in adult video in 2010, at first appearing under several stage names, and from 2011 worked as "Inagawa Natsume" while with Five Promotion. On 1 November 2013 she made her strip debut at Shinjuku New Art but stopped performing that year. In 2018 she returned as "Sumire Kurokawa" and moved to Mine's.

On 5 February 2020 she announced a move to Life Promotion and a change of name to "Nishida Natsu", leaving that agency on 7 November. She then returned to Mine's and resumed working as Sumire Kurokawa. In the week of 27 June 2022 her title "真夜中に夫の帰りを待つ兄嫁の寂しさを精子で埋める中出し性交。黒川すみれ" topped FANZA's rental weekly chart. In May 2023 she placed 39th in Asahi Geinō's "2023 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election", 16th in FANZA's first-half 2023 actress ranking and 15th for the year; in the week of 21 October 2024 her compilation "黒川すみれ The Madonna Best 3枚組 12時間" entered FANZA's video weekly chart at fourth.

## Personal notes

- She is from Shiga Prefecture.
- Her hobbies are kyūdō, gymnastics, visiting cafés and shopping.
- Before debuting she was a freelance worker; while job-hunting she applied at once to a convenience store, a video rental shop and an adult-video agency, and joined the agency (Five Promotion) because it replied first.
- She was a virgin before her adult-video debut.
- Her stage names and agencies have changed repeatedly: Five Promotion as Inagawa Natsume, Mine's at her 2018 comeback, a brief 2020 move to Life Promotion as Nishida Natsu, and then a return to Mine's.

## Work features

- Her career spans the 2010s and 2020s; she paused in 2013 and has kept appearing since her 2018 comeback.
- Her post-comeback titles are largely wife and mother-in-law material for studios such as Madonna and Tameike Goro, and her 2024 compilation is titled "黒川すみれ The Madonna Best 3枚組 12時間".
- Her June 2022 title "真夜中に夫の帰りを待つ兄嫁の寂しさを精子で埋める中出し性交。黒川すみれ" topped FANZA's rental weekly chart.
- Before pausing in 2013 she performed as a stripper at Shinjuku New Art, so her activity has not been limited to adult video.
