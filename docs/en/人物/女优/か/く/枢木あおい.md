## Introduction

Aoi Kururugi (枢木あおい, born 20 March 1998) is a Japanese singer, television personality and pachislot performer from Kyoto Prefecture, formerly an adult-film actress, affiliated with LIGHT. Before debuting she worked as a solo underground idol in the Kansai region. On 14 May 2017 she debuted with Candy in "高級料亭で働く京都出身はんなり美少女がAVデビュー 枢木あおい"; the stage name was chosen with her agency's president to be hard to read but memorable once read.

She released two exclusive titles each for Candy and Muku, briefly decided to end her activity, then changed her mind and from September 2017 worked as a planning solo actress across a wide range of genres. She first appeared in VR in November 2017 and won high praise there in particular: a May 2020 KMP best-of called her the "VR queen", and a VR title released at the end of February 2018 ranked first in DMM Video.R18's March 2018 monthly work ranking.

In October 2018 she appeared at the expanded Lady Madonna event as the head of the Lady team and began singing, continuing to appear at Lady Madonna and MilGene events. At AV OPEN 2018 in 2019 she had one title nominated in each of the amateur, hard and VR-1 Grand Prix categories; "今からこの一家全員レイプします 大○区田園○布" placed second in the hard category, and she served as a presenter at the awards ceremony on 23 May. In March that year she was named an outstanding actress at the SOD Awards. She ranked 32nd in FANZA's 2018 annual actress ranking and 7th in 2019, and topped S-cute's annual girls ranking for three consecutive years from 2017 to 2019.

In September 2020 she debuted under the separate name "くろき葵" with KUKI; on 11 November she made her CD debut with "輪舞曲" on Milky Pop Generation and appeared as a singer at "LADY MADONNA-拡大版-" at Club Citta Kawasaki on 27 November. In 2022 she released her second single "白夜に月" and moved to DESERT AIR RECORDS, and she was cast as the voice of protagonist Eve Asaka in the browser game "クリムゾン妖魔大戦", her first voice-acting lead. From January 2023 she began working as a pachislot performer, and on 14 April announced she would become an S-Cute exclusive and graduate from adult video a year later. She held her retirement live "eternally" on 25 May 2024 and continues as a singer, voice actress and pachislot performer.

## Work features

- She debuted as a Candy and Muku exclusive, moved to planning solo work in September 2017, and played a wide range from schoolgirls and younger sisters to university students and young wives.
- Her VR work is especially well regarded: a title released at the end of February 2018 ranked first in DMM Video.R18's March 2018 monthly work ranking, a May 2020 KMP best-of called her the "VR queen", and she topped that ranking's actress category for four consecutive periods from the second half of 2018.
- At AV OPEN 2018 in 2019 she had one title nominated in each of the amateur, hard and VR-1 Grand Prix categories, with "今からこの一家全員レイプします 大○区田園○布" placing second in the hard category.
- She appeared in lesbian titles from May 2018 and, from 2020, often played the partner in other actresses' first lesbian works, earning the name "genius lesbian (top) actress".
- She became an S-Cute exclusive in April 2023 and announced her retirement from adult video a year later; she has not appeared in adult video since her retirement live on 25 May 2024.
