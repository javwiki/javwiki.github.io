## Introduction

Kana Kusakabe (日下部加奈, born 11 July 1995) is a Japanese adult-film actress who has been affiliated with Prime Agency and then GG. In April 2018 she joined Soft On Demand (SOD) and was assigned to its publicity department; from 13 November that year she and Maiko Ayase jointly took over the department's Twitter account. She had no plan to appear in adult video and both women denied it when followers asked, but on her supervisor's recommendation her appearance was announced in the April 2019 issue of Monthly Soft On Demand DVD, and she debuted in March 2019 while still an SOD employee, appearing in three works as "SOD office lady Kana Kusakabe".

She left SOD in July 2019, started a new Twitter account on 13 August and announced in September that she would debut as a MOODYZ exclusive; on 13 December she was named MOODYZ's best newcomer. She became an Attackers exclusive in January 2021 and reported a move to Madonna on 9 August 2022.

In the week of 17 February 2025 her title "マドンナALL専属バスツアー2025 ハーレムを掴み取った男たちが極上の美女を独占!! 酒池肉林のお祭りはまだまだ続く!! 夢の大共演 & 大乱交FESTIVAL!! 後編" entered FANZA's mail-order weekly chart at eighth. From 5 November 2025 she has hosted the web radio show "日下部加奈のかなのお部屋でごゆっくり～♪".
