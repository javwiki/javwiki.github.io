## Introduction

Hana Kuraki (倉木華, born 7 August 2001) was a Japanese adult-film actress and television personality with T-POWERS. She was absorbed in fashion and make-up from junior high school, entered beauty school after graduating from high school and worked as a hair and make-up assistant. On 28 May 2024 she made her adult-video debut as an S1 exclusive with "新人NO.1STYLE 倉木華 AVデビュー 「私の22年間とセックス、ぜんぶを見てくださいー」".

On 14 September 2026 her agency T-POWERS announced on its website that she had died; the Wikipedia article records her death as occurring in September 2026. Neither the date nor the cause has been officially disclosed, and the agency said it would decide individually and carefully whether each kind of information needs to be released. Her death was also reported abroad, including by the New York Post in the United States and The Sun in Britain.

In her last months she appeared at TAE Taiwan Adult Expo in Kaohsiung on 10 April 2026, held her final signing and photo session in Akihabara on 24 June, and left her last post on X on 5 September.
