## Introduction

Mio Kimijima is a Japanese AV actress and stripper. She debuted in 2005 as 京本かえで, renamed herself 瞳ゆら on 11 June 2014 with サンプロ, and renamed herself 君島みお on 13 September 2017 with T-POWERS, with a completely new profile. In 2017–2018 she was called the busiest AV actress and earned the title "No. 1 in AV sales for 2018"; in the four years under the 君島 name up to 2021 she appeared in about 800 titles. From 1 to 20 May 2019 she appeared as a stripper at 浅草ロック座.

## Personal notes

- Before debuting she was a popular Nakasu soap-land girl with a one-year waiting list. That background was used as a catchphrase at her re-debut and is said to have overturned the belief that former sex workers do not sell as actresses.
- When she debuted in 2005 as 京本かえで, the beautiful-girl genre was at its peak, she did not think she would sell and planned only one title, so she refused magazine and newspaper interviews at the time; the title sold and offers continued, so she stayed in the business.
- Her profile differs by period: the 京本かえで period gives 18 May 1986, Tokyo and blood type A with the ロック座 group; the 君島みお period gives 25 April 1988.
- At first she trimmed her pubic hair because she was embarrassed to have it seen sexually, but after becoming 君島みお she went completely shaved and says sensitivity improved.
- She renamed herself 瞳ゆら on 11 June 2014 with サンプロ and 君島みお on 13 September 2017 with T-POWERS.
- 54th in the reader poll of 「読者300人が選んだFLASH 2021セクシー女優ランキング」 in August 2021.

## Work features

- Many nymphomania and married-woman roles, across OPPAI, Fitch, MOODYZ, 溜池ゴロー, Madonna and many other labels.
- A very high volume in 2017–2018, when she was called the busiest AV actress; about 800 titles in the four years under the 君島 name.
- She appeared as a stripper at 浅草ロック座 in May 2019, extending her work to the stage.
- On 15 September 2021 she walked the runway in the fashion show within 「TREND GIRLS 撮影会 2024」 (per Japanese Wikipedia; the event year and the article year do not match, so the original wording is kept).
