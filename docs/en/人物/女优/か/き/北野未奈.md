## Introduction

Mina Kitano (北野未奈, born 20 May 2000) is a Japanese adult-film performer affiliated with LIGHT. She debuted in April 2021 with the E-BODY release "弱冠20歳! 銀座NO.1（某有名高級クラブ）Hカップホステスと大型契約 AVデビュー", billed as "the finest beauty and figure, capable of captivating men". In December 2021 she won the Best New Actress award at the Weekly Playboy Adult Awards 2021. From May 2022 she was a double exclusive for Honnaka and Fitch, adding MOODYZ in December 2023 and Wanz Factory in January 2024. In June 2025, with her contracts at those labels completed, she became a kikaku-tantai performer.

## Work features

- Her debut was marketed on her background as "Ginza's number-one hostess", with her H-cup (B92) figure central to the promotion.
- Her titles centre on dominant-nymphomaniac, sex-industry, caregiving and training themes; after turning kikaku-tantai in 2025 she appeared for a wider range of labels.
- She names the "spider cowgirl" position as her speciality, and in 2025 described a trick of playing a recorder with vaginal pressure, which she calls "oma●ko recorder".
- Male performer Genjin Moribayashi described her as "a woman full of pheromones who nonetheless has an innocent girlish side", and writer Kazu Shibata called her physique "a body like a walking weapon".
- Between 2021 and 2024 she served as a campaign girl for FANZA/HHH's Triple Happy Campaign 2021, Sexy Sweets Campaign 2022 and GW Thanksgiving 2024.
