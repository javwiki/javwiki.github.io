## Introduction

Himari Kinoshita (木下ひまり, born 21 May 1996) is a Japanese adult-film performer from Tokyo, affiliated with ACT. She debuted with Prestige on 17 April 2020. In May 2021 she was selected for Weekly Playboy's "Next Generation Beautiful Big-Bottomed AV Actress Best 10", and that year she placed 9th in Monthly FANZA's first-half actress ranking and 5th in the second-half ranking. She began performing in girl-girl scenes in October 2020 and has since appeared in many lesbian-themed titles. In January 2026 she became exclusive to three labels — Das!, Fitch and ROYAL — her first exclusive contract in six years of work.
