## Introduction

Yuzu Kitagawa is a Japanese AV actress born on 15 March 1997. She debuted in February 2016 as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive; the debut title 《新人 NO.1STYLE キュッッと細腰"W53"北川ゆずAVデビュー》 sold a "W53" waist. From June to November of that year she also held a 本中 exclusive contract, then worked as a kikaku-tantai performer at S1, 本中 and other labels. On 31 March 2018 she announced her retirement on her official Twitter. She re-debuted on 17 May 2023 under the name 上田紗奈, represented by アクト.

- Several digital photo books from 2018 to 2021 (グラッツ, PAD), plus magazine gravure such as 《ズバ王》's September 2016 issue.
