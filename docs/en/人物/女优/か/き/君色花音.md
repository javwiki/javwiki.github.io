## Introduction

Kanon Kimiiro is a Japanese AV actress born on 13 September 1998 in Kanagawa Prefecture. She debuted in February 2017 as an IdeaPocket exclusive. In February 2018 she moved to ARROWS and renamed herself 君色華奈; on 10 August that year the formation of the three-member idol group un panopticon girl with 橋本ありな and 桐谷まつり was announced, and she made her CD debut on MILKY POP GENERATION on 3 October, only to leave the group on 16 November and suspend AV work. In May 2019 she renamed herself 涼美ほのか, moved to バンビプロモーション and resumed activity. Her Twitter has not been updated since 18 December 2021, and by March 2022 she no longer appeared in the agency's model list.

## Personal notes

- From Kanagawa Prefecture; her interest is saunas and her speciality is swimming.
- Her first experience was at 16, in her first year of high school, with a local senior two years older in a supermarket men's toilet cubicle; she had four partners before debuting.
- She became an AV actress after seeing 明日花キララ on Twitter, finding her cute and wanting to do the same, and then being scouted.
- In 2018 she was a member of un panopticon girl and left shortly after the CD debut.
- She returned under the name 涼美ほのか in 2019 and stopped updating social media at the end of 2021.

## Work features

- The debut was promoted as a "超美少女AVデビュー" and positioned as an innocent beautiful-girl performer.
- Her IdeaPocket exclusive period centred on solo titles across various setups, with VR work as well.
- After the renaming and agency change the innocent, beautiful-girl direction largely continued.
- She released many titles while active, across solo, co-star and compilation works.
