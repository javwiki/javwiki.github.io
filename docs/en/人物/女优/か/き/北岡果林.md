## Introduction

Karin Kitaoka (北岡果林, born 19 September 2003) is a Japanese adult-film performer from Tokyo, affiliated with Mine's. She debuted in April 2024 as an IdeaPocket exclusive, billed in her debut work as "a current university student at a top university who has done modelling". She moved to kikaku-tantai status in September that year. In May 2026 she topped the FANZA video monthly actress ranking and released her first photobook, "Juice".

## Work features

- She debuted as an IdeaPocket exclusive and became kikaku-tantai in September 2024, working mainly in schoolgirl, younger-sister and date-themed titles.
- As of August 2025 about half her work was in schoolgirl roles, by her own account; because her high school had no uniform, she has worn one more often since turning 20 than she did as a student.
- Her September 2024 release "清楚系地下アイドルと裏営業で繋がるオフパコ乱交撮影会。" (Muku) sold without any exposed-breast scene, and she says this fixed her cosplay image.
- She has made many kiss-focused titles, and male performer Kensuke Samejima called her "an amazing actress who has mastered the way of the kiss". In 2026 Weekly Playboy, noting her growing number of date-themed works, dubbed her "the new queen of date AV".
- From 2026 she released image DVDs including "Lapis Lazuli ～瑠璃色の天使～", and in May brought out her first photobook, "Juice" (Saibunkan).
