## Introduction

She debuted as a gravure idol in 2015 on the strength of a 100 cm bust, then with e2dive entertainment. In July 2016 she appeared in Weekly SPA!'s "Gravure Soul" (2 August 2016 issue), and at the end of that year won the Miura Jun individual prize at the 2016 Gravure Soul Awards.

In March 2017 she made her adult-video debut with the studio MUTEKI and moved to Bstar; from June that year she was an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive, and on 1 November she reported on her official Twitter that she had moved to Mins. In May 2018 she received a special prize at the DMM R-18 Adult Awards 2018. In January 2019 she left S1 exclusivity and became a freelance performer; that November she formed "make♡" with agency colleagues (Nanami Matsumoto, Ayu Kumano and Miu Akemi) and appeared at Mins Fest.

In April 2020 she won the Momokuma newcomer prize in the pink-film top ten, and in December she placed fourth in the large-bust category and 18th overall in the reader vote for FLASH's "2020 Best 100 Active Sexy Actresses". In June 2021 she announced a stripper debut at Asakusa Rockza for 1 July; in August she placed 54th in FLASH's 2021 sexy-actress reader ranking, and on 1 October she announced a move to All Pro with a business tie-up with Mins.

In March 2022 a title she appeared in won the feature-animation prize at Shueisha's Weekly Playboy 2022 Ero-Demy Awards. On 21 March 2024 she reported becoming a Materiall exclusive and moved to NAX in April; from the work released on 17 January 2025 she became a Fitch exclusive. On 5 January 2026 she reported leaving her agency to go freelance, and on 12 January announced her graduation from mainstream adult video while continuing stripping and other work. In a July interview she said the choice followed reflection on whether to keep the status quo, and that she hopes eventually to support other actresses behind the scenes.
