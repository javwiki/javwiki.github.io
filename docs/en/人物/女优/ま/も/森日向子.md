## Introduction

Hinako Mori was born on 9 September 2000 in Mie Prefecture and is represented by NAX Promotion. She debuted in June 2020 as an exclusive performer for SOD Create's KMHR label, moving to planning-only work after three titles, and that same month was chosen as a semi-finalist in Kodansha's Miss iD 2021. In April 2021 she published the photo book 僕のわがまま; in September 2024 she became a campaign girl for FANZA and HHH Group's Triple Happy Campaign 2024, and in December she took her first lead role in the film みゆきの結婚. From April 2025 she has been exclusive to MOODYZ's きれいなお姉さんは好きですか label. She has also appeared in image videos under the name 朝比奈えみり.

## Work features

- Her debut was an exclusive SOD Create KMHR title; after three works she moved to planning-only appearances, and she has since appeared across studios, including compilations and VR projects.
- Settings in her titles include the 地雷系 (jirai-kei) girlfriend of 僕の可愛い彼女は地雷系女子◆□, saliva-heavy kissing and edging/ejaculation control; since 2025 she has appeared on MOODYZ's きれいなお姉さんは好きですか label.
- Under the name 朝比奈えみり she has appeared in image videos such as ミス○教大学でモデル衝撃のデビュー (29 January 2021).
- From April to July 2024 she placed in the top ten of FANZA's monthly video actress ranking four months running (5th, 9th, 4th and 7th).
