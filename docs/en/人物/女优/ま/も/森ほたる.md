## Introduction

Hotaru Mori is a Japanese AV actress from Nagano Prefecture, born 3 October 1987 and represented by ジールグループ. She debuted on 7 February 2018 as a mega-exclusive newcomer at Madonna; the debut title 《独占!! 超大型専属 美巨乳くびれHカップ!! 新人 森ほたる AVデビュー!!》 sold her "美巨乳くびれHカップ" figure as the hook, and she released seven Madonna exclusive titles by July of that year. She left the exclusive roster in July 2018 and became a kikaku-tantai actress, appearing from September at Fitch, OPPAI, ダスッ!, グローリークエスト, ヒビノ, タカラ映像, VENUS and other labels, mainly in big-breast married-woman and mature-woman themes. In 2019 her range widened to プラム, 桃太郎映像出版, JET映像, アタッカーズ and ながえスタイル, and she also appeared in VR and compilation works.

## Personal notes

## Work features

- The main selling point is a beautiful H-cup bust with a tight-waisted "くびれ" figure, which also supplied the debut title.

- VR works from December 2018, and multi-label compilations and long-form projects from 2019.
