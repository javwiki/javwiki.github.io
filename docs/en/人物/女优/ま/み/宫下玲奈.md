## Introduction

Reina Miyashita is a Japanese AV actress born in Miyagi Prefecture on 15 July 2002. She debuted on 5 April 2022 as a MOODYZ exclusive; her debut, 《新人 専属 宮下玲奈 19歳 AV Debut!》, reached No. 1 on FANZA's weekly solo-title chart and became MOODYZ's biggest hit of 2022. In March 2023 she released her first photobook, 《透明少女》, and that year made her first Taiwan photo-session appearances and stage appearance. In 2024 she was one of the campaign girls for FANZA's fifth anniversary. In May 2025 it was reported that she would pause public events until July while continuing to shoot AV. Japanese Wikipedia lists her agency as ルミナスプロモーション, which does not match the C-more given in the previous version of this entry.

## Personal notes

- From Miyagi Prefecture; 162cm with a C cup.
- Debuted on 5 April 2022 as a MOODYZ exclusive at the age of 19.
- She originally wanted to be an idol; in a Japanese Wikipedia interview she says that after hearing from a friend she weighed the money and time involved and chose AV acting.
- Specialities: track and field, skipping. She belonged to a track team at school.

## Work features

- A MOODYZ exclusive; her debut was sold on "19 years old", "exclusive" and "AV Debut", and later titles continue with first experiences, commuter-train molestation, teacher–student and lover/friend setups.
- From 2023 she widened into VR (8K), image videos and photobooks, with "natural" and "life-size" as recurring promotional words.
- In 2026 she had a creampie-unveiling title (《解禁 初めての中出し…》) and cut her hair short for the live-action adaptation of a 薺屋本舗 manga.
- She released five photobooks between 2023 and 2025 (《透明少女》, 《Cosplay Fetish Book》, 《純情》, 《無垢な季節》, 《サマー・イン・サマー》), running video and print work in parallel.
