## Introduction

Yua Mikami is a Japanese TV personality, YouTuber and former AV actress, born in Nagoya, Aichi on 16 August 1993. She joined SKE48 in 2009 under the name 鬼頭桃菜, graduated in April 2014, and on 1 June 2015 made her AV debut with the MUTEKI exclusive "Princess Peach". In August 2016 she moved to S1 NO.1 STYLE and won four awards at the DMM.R18 Adult Awards 2016, including Best New Actress and Best Title; in 2017 she won the same awards' Best Actress. In 2018 she formed HONEY POPCORN with 松田美子 and 樱萌子 and debuted in Korea, and in November that year became an S1 NO.1 STYLE ambassador; in January 2022 she set up her own agency, Miss. On 13 March 2023 she announced her retirement, releasing the retirement title "完全引退 AV女優、最後の1日。三上悠亜ラストセックス" on 15 August and holding a birthday and retirement event on 16 August.

## Work features

- She debuted in June 2015 with the MUTEKI exclusive "Princess Peach"; the title took first place in all three categories — mail-order BD, streaming and rental — at the DMM.R18 Adult Awards 2016.
- In August 2016 she moved to S1 NO.1 STYLE, and in November 2018 she became an ambassador for the label.
- The March 2021 title "アナタの五感を刺激する三上悠亜のシコシコサポートラグジュアリー 脳をエロスで満たす6つの癒され勃起シチュエーション" was, by her own account in July 2023, her best-selling work, and it won the sound award in Weekly Playboy's "2021 Ero-Demy Awards".
- Around her 2023 retirement, titles such as "引退発表 126日後にAV女優をやめる三上悠亜" and "完全引退 AV女優、最後の1日。三上悠亜ラストセックス" reached #1 on FANZA's weekly mail-order chart.
