## Introduction

Kanna Misaki (美咲かんな, born 3 July 1994) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Kanagawa Prefecture. Her official statistics are 158 cm, B85 (E)-W65-H93, blood type O. She debuted in December 2014 as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive and became a Honnaka exclusive in December 2015.

In 2016 her VR title "生中出しスペシャル!! VRセックスだから、すごくリアルでしょ?" (KMPVR) became a major hit and she began to be called the "VR Queen". The work is also described as the first adult VR shot in Japan, although several titles were released on the same day. On 21 March 2019 she won the Outstanding Actress Award at SOD AWARD 2019; in October she was nominated in the cowgirl-master category of Geo TV × Men's Cyzo ADULT AWARD 2019, under the nickname "Dosukebe VR Queen".

She rested from 2021 to 2022, announced her return after about a year in August 2022 and moved to GG. On 13 November 2023, in her ninth year since debut, she released her first photobook, "Naturale", from Tokuma Shoten. In December 2024 she made her film debut and took her first lead role in "離れられない君へ", directed by Haruhi Oguri.

In 2025 several of her titles entered FANZA's weekly charts: seventh on the mail-order chart in the week of 27 January, a co-starring title with Yotsuha Kominato entering the mail-order chart at second in the week of 9 June, third on the video chart in the week of 7 July and fourth on the video chart in the week of 18 August. She took part in Taiwan's "Top Goddess Taipei Thanksgiving Festival" in February 2025 and May 2026. From October 2026 she became a DAHLIA exclusive under the Fereno Group.

## Personal notes

- Her hobby is photography; from 2023 she has presented "美咲かめら", her first show bearing her name, on CS Sky PerfecTV. Her other interests and skills are fashion, shopping and travel.
- She entered the industry to pay for full-body hair removal and dental braces and to improve herself, rather than from a personal ambition to join adult video, and she says she had almost no motivation on set at first.
- The growth of VR work after 2016 became a turning point. In VR shoots, which allow little editing and respect the actress's performance and plans, she began thinking about the work herself and her attitude to adult video changed.
- In VR circles she is known as the "riding queen" and is also called the "VR Queen".
- She does not rate her own acting highly and generally does not rewatch her work, calling this "acting hit-and-run"; she does say that shooting dramas is enjoyable.
- Her 2021–2022 break came from losing sight of what was wanted from "Kanna Misaki" and of the right answer for how a work should turn out.
