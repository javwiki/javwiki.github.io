## Introduction

Sumire Mizukawa (水川スミレ, born 3 February 1994) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Sakai, Osaka Prefecture, affiliated with LINX. She grew up surrounded by delinquents and comedians and left home at 17. After working as a hostess in Osaka's Minami district, she made her adult-video debut in April 2016 as "Mizuki Miri", exclusive to Prestige; her profile at the time gave 5 May 1996 and Kyoto Prefecture.

On 15 November 2017 she opened a new Twitter account as "Mizuki Rei" and reported that she had changed agencies and name to broaden her work, initially with Lock-On and later with Karos Entertainment. On 10 January 2018 she opened an account as "Sumire Mizukawa", having moved to LINX and renamed herself; that profile gave 3 February 1995 and Osaka Prefecture. From that year she also worked overseas.

In August 2020 she began the podcast "MI SO HORNY", which reached the overall top 100 and was covered by Bunshun Online as sex-education content. From 2021 she lived in the United States and married an American man she had met on social media and dated long-distance for several years. After the marriage she found that he was, in her words, "the worst psychopath in history"; she fled back to Japan but has not divorced. In November 2023 she became a Madonna exclusive with "Madonna電撃専属 真正痴女 水川スミレ 深く濃厚な接吻と寸止め中出しでM男クンを身も心もトロけさせる無限射精オーガズム".

On 6 January 2026 Taiwan's Liberty Times reported that she would graduate from proper Japanese adult-video work and shift her base overseas, and on 9 March she posted about filming her retirement work on her own X account. From 27 to 29 March she appeared at ADULTOPIA at Messe Taoyuan in Taiwan and won BEST POPULAR ACTRESS (the XCITY most popular actress award). On 8 July she announced her marriage and retirement on Instagram, phrasing it as "Sorry…officially sold out." and "完売" (sold out).
