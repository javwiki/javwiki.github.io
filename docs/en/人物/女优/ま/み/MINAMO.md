## Introduction

MINAMO (born 2000 in Kyoto Prefecture) is a Japanese model, adult-film actress and gravure idol affiliated with T-POWERS. On 24 September 2019 she made her gravure debut under the name Taiyō Shizuku, appearing on the cover and in the opening spread of Young King issue 20; the rapper and gravure critic Ryobu Karuma called her "a sign of great talent". She took the cover and a fifteen-page opening and closing spread in issue 22 as well. She then changed her name to MINAMO and worked as a still model, photographed by Ichirō Fujisato, Shūji Kobayashi, Hideki Kōno and Kazuyuki Hagiwara, and drew attention mainly on social media. In the 30 April 2021 issue of Shūkan Post she appeared in a magazine for the first time as MINAMO, and two digital photobooks, "MINAMO 煌めきの波紋" and "MINAMO 艶めきの波紋", were announced for simultaneous release.

On 10 May 2021 a press conference at Syain Bar in Shin-Nakano, Tokyo, announced her debut as an adult-film actress. She continued to work as a still model and held the photo exhibition "MINAMO×Fujisato Ichiro写真展「Velvet」" at the gallery ロッコール in Meguro, Tokyo, from 10 to 20 June. Her debut work, "MINAMO 超大型新人 AV DEBUT", was released by SOD Create on 10 June 2021 and ranked first on the FANZA video chart for the week of 31 May. In August she placed first in the newcomer division of Monthly FANZA's "This AV Actress Is Amazing! Summer 2021", and in December she shared the W Newcomer Award at the Asahi Geinō AV Awards with 安位カヲル. In autumn 2021 she spent a total of fifty hours modelling for two works by the painter Tetsuya Mishima.

In December 2022, after Anna Kami's throat problems, she began presenting the web show Hadaka Ikkan as a substitute host and became a regular host in 2023. On 9 June 2023 she released her first photo essay, "惑溺", and on 17 June held a second-anniversary event at Mandaray in Nakano, where about seventy per cent of visitors were women. In February 2024 one of her works placed fourth on the FANZA rental chart; in April she placed 28th overall in Asahi Geinō's "2024 Active AV Actress Sexy General Election"; and in December she placed ninth in FLASH's 2024 sexy-actress ranking. In 2025 she appeared on the variety show "珠代とMINAMOの令和爆モテ男子教育計画" and in the Netflix production "デスキスゲーム", and was featured in Young Champion Retsu.

On 4 June 2026 she released a fifth-anniversary work marketed as having the world's longest title. On 14 June, at a fan event and press conference at SOD's Tokyo headquarters, she announced that she would retire from adult video on 31 December that year, continuing afterwards as a television personality and model; in September she released her first novel, "母の泥舟". In June 2026 she ranked first in the FANZA rental monthly actress ranking and in July eighth.
