## Introduction

Rei Mizuna is a former Japanese AV actress born on 20 May 1984 in Tokyo. She debuted on 25 July 2008 with アリスJAPAN's 《みづなれいDEBUT》, moved to MAXING on 16 March 2009 with 《みづなれい 解禁》, and left exclusivity for kikaku-tantai work that July. Known as a "little-devil beauty", her interests are reading (Haruki Murakami), rock music, perfume and games, and her speciality is knitting. She was the first AV actress to appear at all four major Chinese-language adult expos: Guangzhou in 2013, Macau in 2013, Taiwan in 2015 and Shanghai in 2016. A titular streaming show began on 18 February 2016; on 17 March she announced she would finish shooting at the end of May and retire, and the eighth アニクラTMA 「みづなれいLAST GIGS」 on 3 September 2016 was her final active appearance.

## Personal notes

- The name: 「れい」 comes from Rei Ayanami in 《Neon Genesis Evangelion》; 「みづな」 from mizuna greens appearing when she ate with agency colleagues and from the president liking mizuna. It uses 「づ」 rather than 「ず」 simply because she preferred the look of the character.
- Interests: reading (preferring Haruki Murakami), music (mainly rock) and games such as Monster Hunter. She also collects perfume, mainly Guerlain's Shalimar and Après l'Ondée, including vintage bottles.
- Her speciality is knitting; she belongs to the industry cycling club 「業界人自転車部 Team BUKKAKE」.
- She wrote the column 「みづなれいin無修正」 for スポーツニッポン, and from 4 April 2016 the Tuesday column 「みづなれいAVのアレとかソレとか」.
- After retiring she renamed herself スズナミナツ and took up cycling.
- She took part in Taiwan's 「潘朵啦夏日女神祭」 and the Macau adult expo in 2013, and the Taiwan adult expo in 2015.

## Work features

- Debuted at アリスJAPAN, moved to MAXING in 2009 and to kikaku-tantai the same year, releasing many titles over eight years.
- A wide range from innocent and nymphomania roles to drama and kikaku-tantai material.
- From 2013 to 2016 she appeared at all four major Chinese-language adult expos in succession, the first to do so.
- On 18 February 2016 the ニコ生 titular show 《トイズハートプレゼンツ みづなれいの「れい様のいうとおり」》 began.
