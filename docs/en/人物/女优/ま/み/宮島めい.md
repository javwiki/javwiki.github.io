## Introduction

Mei Miyajima is a Japanese AV actress born on 17 October 2000 in Matsuyama, Ehime Prefecture. She was a second-year university student in early-childhood education when she applied to SODクリエイト to earn money for studying abroad; from about 1,000 applicants the vice-president 高岡哲也 took an immediate liking to her and hired her on the spot. In July 2020 she debuted at SODstar under the banner of "an AV actress for 100 days only"; her debut title 《宮島めい AV DEBUTします。でも、次で引退です。》 entered at No. 4 on the FANZA mail-order weekly AV ranking in the week of 3 June, and the second title, her retirement work 《宮島めい これで、引退します。思いっきり絶頂して、完全燃焼しました。》, entered at No. 1 in the week of 22 July. The pandemic then cancelled her study abroad and left overseas travel uncertain, and the response to her work exceeded expectations, so she returned in October that year; her comeback title 《奇跡の復活 女優としての本気を見せる覚醒SEX4本番 宮島めい》 entered at No. 1 on the daily mail-order floor on 18 September.

## Personal notes

- From Matsuyama, Ehime Prefecture; interests are making sweets and reading, and her specialities are dance and speed-reading manga.
- Her first experience was in her third year of high school on a hot-spring trip with her then boyfriend.
- In a 2022 interview she said she weighed 48kg at debut and 43kg at the time; to maintain her figure she trains and also uses breathing methods and inner care, running or walking twice a day.
- In May 2021 she became a 「THE TIP」 campaign girl, a 「BASARAガール」, alongside 青空ひかり and 夏目響, in charge of UZU-UZU; from October she was in charge of PIKU-PIKU.
- At the end of March 2021, finding it hard to balance acting and study and questioning her path as the pandemic pushed nursery-course classes and practice online, she left university at the end of her second year.
- On drama titles she says that after realising "I don't need to act specially; if I show what I genuinely feel, people enjoy it" she began to enjoy them, and because pre-planning makes her stiff she decides her movements from what happens on set.

## Work features

- The contrast between a 155cm frame and an F cup is the selling point.
- A SODstar exclusive who also appears in SOD kikaku-tantai titles.
- Her "100 days only" debut caused a stir, and she returned fully after the study-abroad cancellation; her drama work is well regarded, with the writer 末吉みちお saying she has "outstanding acting ability and a string of drama hits" and 芝田カズ saying she is "unrivalled at playing women with a sense of transgression".
- In the week of 4 December 2023 《隠れエロボディの牛丼チェーン店で働くパート主婦が学生バイトと避妊具なしでネチョベロ不倫中出し 宮島めい》 entered at No. 5 on the FANZA video ranking.
- On 12 June 2025 she made her first music-event appearance at 「月で逢いましょう vol.121 ニューカマースペシャル」 at 三軒茶屋グレープフルーツムーン; on 30 April 2026 she held her first solo live there, singing eight songs including 《DESIRE -情熱-》 and 《にじいろ》 with the encore.
