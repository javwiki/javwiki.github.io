# Hikaru Minazuki

A Japanese adult-film performer.

## Basic information

- **Japanese name**: 皆月ひかる
- **English name**: Hikaru Minazuki
- **Date of birth (official or publisher source)**: 2000-01-11
- **Height (official or publisher source)**: 148 cm

## Introduction

Hikaru Minazuki was born on 11 January 2000 in Akita Prefecture and is represented by Bambi Promotion. She debuted in July 2018 as DEEP'S exclusive amateur performer 「ひかる」, having already appeared in a title released the previous month, which Japanese Wikipedia treats as the trigger for her formal debut. In September 2018 she left exclusivity and began planning-only work. She suspended activities in November 2019 because of ill health and announced her return on 24 March 2020. In December 2022 she entered DMM GAMES' open voice audition for a new character in クリムゾン妖魔大戦, and since 2024 several of her titles have entered FANZA's weekly top ten.

## Work features

- Her debut was in DEEP'S exclusive amateur-performer series, credited as 「ひかる」 on the package while retailers also used 「皆月ひかる」; she has also appeared in image videos under several names.
- After moving to planning-only work her titles often use schoolgirl, uniform, NTR, aphrodisiac and つるぺた (flat-chested) settings, and include VR works and compilations.
- Her small 148 cm frame and musical background are often written into the set-up: DEEP'S introduced her as a current music-college student, and she played trumpet in her debut title.
- Between 2024 and 2026 titles such as オジサンに没頭する発情コスプレイヤー…, 女子校生孕ませ民宿… and つるぺた女子の男水着チャレンジ… entered FANZA's weekly top ten; つるぺた女子… reached 1st on the video chart.
