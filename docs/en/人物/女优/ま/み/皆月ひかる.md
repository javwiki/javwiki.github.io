## Introduction

Hikaru Minazuki was born on 11 January 2000 in Akita Prefecture and is represented by バンビプロモーション. In July 2018 she debuted as DEEP'S exclusive amateur girl 「ひかる」; she had also appeared in a title released the previous month, and Wikipedia treats that appearance as the trigger for her formal debut. She left the exclusivity in September 2018 and became a kikaku-tantai performer. She took a break for ill health in November 2019 and announced her return on 24 March 2020. On 1 December 2022 she entered the DMM GAMES 「クリムゾン妖魔大戦」 new-character voice audition. Since 2024 her titles have repeatedly entered the FANZA weekly top ten, and in the week of 7 July 2025 《つるぺた女子の男水着チャレンジ…》 reached No. 1 on the video weekly ranking.
