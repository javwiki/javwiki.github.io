## Introduction

Marin Mita (三田真鈴, born 28 June 2002) is a Japanese adult-film actress affiliated with LIGHT. The second of five sisters in a family of two parents and five daughters, she lists magic as her hobby and describes herself as "easy-going and extremely natural". On 14 November 2023 she debuted as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive with "新人NO.1STYLE 三田真鈴 AVデビュー", disclosing in the debut work that she was a university student. Her stage-name surname was taken from its resemblance to the announcer Yurika Mita.

In 2024 her titles appeared repeatedly in FANZA's rankings. In the week of 12 June, "激イキ186回!痙攣4602回!イキ潮3200cc!エロめっちゃ可愛い三田真鈴 エロス覚醒 はじめての大大大・痙・攣スペシャル" entered the rental-floor weekly ranking at tenth. In the week of 26 August, "アナタの最高オナニーのために三田真鈴が可愛く見つめておち●こを刺激、そして何度も射精を笑顔で受け止める…大量顔射スマイルオナサポ" rose to eighth on the video-floor weekly ranking. In the week of 4 November her title "S1 PRECIOUS GIRLS 2024 オールスター24名大集合ハーレムアイランドSpecial" entered the mail-order weekly ranking at first, and in the week of 11 November the Blu-ray held first with the DVD third. She opened a YouTube channel on 15 November and placed eleventh in Kobunsha FLASH's own "FLASH Sexy Actress Ranking 2024" on 24 December.

In March 2025 she became a campaign girl, alongside Mayuki Ito and Mitsuri Nagahama, for FANZA's "Spring Pants Festival 2025". On 6 December she held a launch event for her first photobook, "ring-ring", at Shosen Book Tower in Akihabara, Tokyo. In January 2026 she placed twenty-first in the "FLASH Sexy Actress Ranking 2025" published in FLASH's combined 6 and 13 January issue, and in the week of 16 March her first best-of, "癒しの笑顔で実は超エッチ 三田真鈴 初ベスト デビューから1年分 全12タイトル&全コーナー収録 12時間スペシャル", entered FANZA's streaming-floor weekly ranking at ninth.
