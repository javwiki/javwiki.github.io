## Introduction

Akiho Yoshizawa is a Japanese actress, TV personality and YouTuber, and a former AV actress. After working as a gravure idol she made her AV debut on 28 March 2003 with Alice Japan's "天使の蕾", becoming exclusive to both Alice Japan and MAX-A. In October 2006 she made her sell-video debut with MAXING and later became a dual exclusive of S1 and MAXING, releasing two titles a month. In 2013 she won the title award at the 2013 Adult Broadcasting Awards for "吉沢明歩 ドM自縛痴女". She retired at the end of March 2019, having appeared in 351 titles — then the record for a solo actress — and published 10 photobooks; from 2020 she has worked mainly on YouTube, and from 2021 has run cooking classes aimed mainly at men.
