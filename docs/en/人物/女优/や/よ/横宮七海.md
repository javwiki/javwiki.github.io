# Nanami Yokomiya

## Introduction

Nanami Yokomiya (横宮七海, born 17 February 2002) was a Japanese adult-film actress affiliated with Krone. She debuted on 10 April 2021 and became known for a "loli" persona.

In February 2022, in the "Kono Ero VR ga Sugoi!" awards, her title "【VR】天井特化アングルVR ～何もない休日に彼女とヤリまくる～ 横宮七海" took first place in the ranking of works from the second half of 2021.

On 10 December 2023 a friend announced her death on social media. There was almost no coverage in Japan, while outlets in Taiwan and elsewhere in Asia reported it at length. Her exact date of death has not been publicly confirmed: Wikipedia records 10 December as the date of death, but reporting only establishes that the announcement was published that day, and this entry preserves the discrepancy.

## Personal notes

- Her interests and specialities are eating out and cooking; she also listed drawing with a ballpoint pen and water pistols.
- Her favourite flowers are moss phlox, forget-me-nots and sunflowers; she was a fan of Yutaka Ozaki.
- She said she had had more than 100 sexual partners.
- The director Migiwa Ōta, discussing actresses whose experience of abuse looks fragile, named Yokomiya and argued that "with a partner like that you can become dependent, and that is extraordinarily erotic".
- She also released image DVDs under the names "Nao Majima", "Mashiro Usa" and "Moe Shiraishi", though Wikipedia does not list these as aliases.
