## Introduction

Ria Yamate is a former Japanese AV actress and nude model born on 27 June 1999 in Fukuoka Prefecture. She was working as a nude model in Fukuoka when 光文社's 《FLASH》 editorial team found her, and she made her gravure debut in issue 1622 on 19 October 2021. On 23 November that year she debuted at S1 NO.1 STYLE. Known as "九州No.1 Body" for a 170cm height and I cup, her debut title 《新人NO.1STYLE 九州No.1新人グラドル山手梨愛AVデビュー》 placed 9th in 《アサヒ芸能》's 2022 AV annual sales ranking. She stopped working after autumn 2023, and an X post on 15 October 2024 saying "it's almost a year since I became an ordinary person" (since deleted) suggests she retired.
