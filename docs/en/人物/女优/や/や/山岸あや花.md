## Introduction

Ayaka Yamagishi (山岸あや花, born 30 November 1992) is a Japanese actress, former adult-film actress and former announcer at a local television station. In July 2017 she debuted as a PREMIUM exclusive under the name Aika Yamagishi (山岸逢花, やまぎし あいか), then represented by the Cruise Group. On 15 May 2023, as she turned 30, she announced a move to Listar Pro and the name Ayaka Yamagishi. She explained that the surname "Yamagishi" was already strongly associated with her among fans, so she kept it, and of the two given-name options "あや花" and "あやめ" she chose the former because she wanted to retain the character for "flower".

On 19 May 2018 she was nominated for the newcomer award at the DMM Adult Awards 2018. On 11 February 2020 she appeared as a final candidate for new members of Ebisu Muscats and announced her joining on 31 March. In May her title "綺麗なお姉さんがチ○ポバカになるまでヌイてくれる種搾りメンズエステ 山岸逢花" topped FANZA's video weekly chart for two weeks, she placed ninth in the first-half 2020 actress ranking, and in December she placed 31st in the reader vote for FLASH's "2020 Best 100 Active Sexy Actresses". On 1 January 2021 the industry's first Ultra HD Blu-ray titles were released simultaneously by IdeaPocket, PREMIUM and Das!, and she starred in the PREMIUM release; on 17 June she won both the newcomer actress award and the Momokuma newcomer award at the 2020 Pink Film Top Ten.

In January 2022 she formed the unit "Gishitani" with label-mate Akari Mitani and opened the YouTube channel "山岸と美谷。", starting a talk column in Weekly Asahi Geinō in May. In August she held a solo photo exhibition, "縁-circle-", and graduated from Ebisu Muscats when the group disbanded after its graduation show. She placed 45th in Asahi Geinō's "2023 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election" in May 2023, and from October to November the title "お義父さん、そんなに強く抱かれたら…若妻が絶倫義父との中出しセックスに溺れた日々。山岸逢花" topped FANZA's rental weekly chart for four consecutive weeks and the November monthly actress ranking. In 2024 she served twice as a FANZA campaign girl, and in December she and Akane Mitani shared the co-starring award at the Asagei Sexy Awards 2024. In February 2025 she attended Taiwan's "TOP Goddess Taipei Thanksgiving Festival"; on 10 September her agency announced on X that she would leave Listar Pro at the end of September and end her adult-video career, and she has worked freelance since October.

## Work features

- From 2017 she appeared for a long stretch as a PREMIUM exclusive, covering married-woman, father-in-law and co-starring projects; her 2023 father-in-law title topped the rental weekly chart for four consecutive weeks.
- In 2021 she starred in one of the industry's first Ultra HD Blu-ray titles, making her an early participant in the format.
- Her photobooks are "花と逢" (2020), "Reflection" (2021), "duende" (2022), "Cosplay Fetish Book 山岸あや花" (2024) and "斐色" (2024), plus the pose book "プレミアムヌードポーズブック Model 山岸あや花" (2023).
- Beyond adult video she has appeared in pink films, original videos, stage plays and web dramas, including episode seven of Netflix's "全裸監督" and several fempass Cinema titles.
- She has continued as an actress since retiring from adult video at the end of September 2025, and an earlier VR co-starring title still topped FANZA's video weekly chart in November 2025.
