## Introduction

Marina Yuzuki is a Japanese AV actress born on 25 August 1994 and represented by エスフラート. She debuted in 2017 as a Fitch exclusive, promoted as "this year's strongest big-breast AV debut". Selling the contrast between a K cup (110cm bust) and a slim figure, she went on to appear at E-BODY, OPPAI, ROCKET, ナチュラルハイ, KMP, SODクリエイト, CASANOVA, ブイワンVR, Hunter and others, with DVD releases of doujin-circle works and numerous VR titles. Her interests are wide: games, animals, models, cosplay, cosplay prop-making, cooking, travel, fishing, photography, films, manga and steampunk.
