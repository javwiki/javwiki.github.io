## Introduction

Maina Yuuri (優梨まいな, born 31 May 1995) is a Japanese adult-film actress and model from Saitama Prefecture affiliated with LINX. In October 2017 she debuted with MOODYZ under the tagline "an ordinary woman working at an advertising agency who cannot hide her perverted submissive streak". She worked as a freelance solo actress and began appearing in films the following month, playing Fujino Noa in "未来の足音", a title also mentioned on the programme "めざましどようび".

In December 2020 she received the "2020 Annual Nukilva Award" chosen by the adult-video comedian Revolver Head. On 25 October 2021 she announced on X that she was retiring from adult video and would work freelance as "Yuuri Maina", taking advertising and photo-session modelling. On 25 October 2022, the fifth anniversary of her debut, she announced her return to adult video and restored the name Maina Yuuri.

In the week of 31 July 2023 her appearance in "あの、プロ野球中継カメラに抜かれたガチ日●ハ●ファン美少女がAV出演! 球場に行けば80%の確率で抜かれるという美巨乳Gカップ少女が自前のきつねコスダンス衣装着て乳揺れ抜き抜き中出しエッチ" topped the FANZA weekly video floor; the title rose to second in the week of 29 January 2024, placed third on the rental floor in the week of 5 February and seventh on the video floor in the week of 1 July. In 2025 she appeared on the cover of the June issue of Monthly VIBES. A VR title from SOD Create was released on 6 July 2026 and a 2D title on 6 August, after which she retired.
