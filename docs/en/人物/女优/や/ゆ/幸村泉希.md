## Introduction

Itsuki Yukimura (幸村泉希, born 28 December 2000) is a Japanese adult-film actress affiliated with Mine'S. She was previously an office worker and changed careers after doubting a routine of arriving at the same time every day and working at a computer. On 2 September 2025 she debuted with "新人 きれいなお姉さんは好きですか?FIRST CLASS エレベーターガール専属 幸村泉希AV Debut!" (MOODYZ), a title built around an elevator-girl persona.

From 2026 she became a planning-only actress working across studios, and her number of shoots rose sharply. In the week of 5 January 2026 her debut work placed first in FANZA's rental weekly AV chart and stayed first the following week. In the week of 26 January "いつも強気な年下の女上司は激ピスされたがりのドMマ●コでした。…" rose to first, and she placed third in the January FANZA rental monthly actress ranking. She was second in February; in the week of 2 March "きれいなお姉さんと過ごす休日って、きっとこんなかんじ。…" topped the rental weekly chart and she placed tenth for March.

Her run continued to the end of the first half of the year. In the week of 18 May "家賃滞納奴● 裸エプロンでチャーハン作りに風呂奉仕！弱みを握られ大家に好き放題ヤラれる女" placed seventh in the rental weekly chart, sixth the following week and tenth in each of the next two weeks. She was seventh in the June 2026 rental monthly ranking and 14th in Monthly FANZA's "FANZA 2026 First-Half Actress Ranking". In September 2026 Tokuma Shoten's Asahi Geinō named her a "NEXT planning-only beauty", and Weekly Playboy's feature on actresses who moved to planning-only work also mentioned her.
