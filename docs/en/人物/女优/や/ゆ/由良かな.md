## Introduction

Kana Yura is a Japanese AV actress born on 30 November 2003, represented by HANAYA PROJECT. She debuted in April 2022 as a 本中 exclusive, and her 143cm frame and cute looks drew attention from the start; the AV writer さおり called her "a supernova who descended on the loli world". After three titles she left the exclusivity, became a kikaku-tantai performer and worked at Fitch and other labels, her roles ranging from a cute younger sister to a cheeky メスガキ and a beautiful girl subjected to hard scenes. In May 2025 she took part in TREND GIRLS撮影会 2025, and in March 2026 she won BEST UNIFORM ACTRESS at ADULTOPIA in Taiwan.
