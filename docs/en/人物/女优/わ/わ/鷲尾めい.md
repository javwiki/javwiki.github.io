# Mei Washio

A Japanese adult-film performer.

## Basic information

- **Japanese name**: 鷲尾めい
- **English name**: Mei Washio
- **Former stage name**: 筧ジュン
- **Height (official or publisher source)**: 164 cm

## Introduction

Mei Washio was born on 20 October 1997 in Kanagawa Prefecture and is represented by Eightman. She debuted in April 2019 as 筧ジュン, an exclusive performer for S1 NO.1 STYLE, then represented by Arrows. In July 2019 she appeared in her first specially shot gravure for Weekly Playboy (22 July 2019 issue). In March 2020 she moved to Eightman and changed her name to 鷲尾めい; Weekly Post's 10 April 2020 issue ran a nude gravure, and Kodansha released the digital photo books sugar and milk at the same time. She placed 50th in the reader poll of the FLASH 2021 sexy actress ranking, 36th in Asahi Geinō's 2023 active AV actress sexy general election and 11th overall in the 2024 edition, and 32nd in the FLASH sexy actress ranking 2025.
