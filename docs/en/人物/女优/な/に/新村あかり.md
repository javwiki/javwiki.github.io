## Introduction

Akari Niimura (新村あかり, born 7 July 1994) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Kyoto Prefecture affiliated with Body Corporation. She made her adult-video debut in September 2016 with a clean-cut, dark-haired image. By her own account she began to feel the strain during her second shoot, and afterwards took on more M-oriented hard titles; when she lightened her hair, offers for dominatrix-style (chijo) roles increased, and she came to feel that "there is no limit to chijo".

In March 2019 she formed the punk band Tachimachi with Reno Aihara, Mizuki Sanada, Kurumi Tamaki and Tomotaka Ishigaki, playing bass. In May 2022 she placed 17th in Asahi Geinō's "2022 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election". On 31 May 2024 she became one of the EX Girls, the successor unit to the Sky PerfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards, alongside Yū Kawakami, Kana Morisawa and Yuria Yoshine, with a presentation event on 26 June; from the same June she began a talk event with Kurumi Tamaki. In September 2024 she became an exclusive Fitch actress.

In the week of 10 February 2025 her title "MOODYZファン感謝祭 バコバコバスツアー2025" entered FANZA's mail-order weekly chart at third during pre-orders. On 30 March 2025 her unit contract ended with the "EX Girls Graduation Event FunFan Thanksgiving" (she missed the event through ill health), and in September she returned to working as a freelance solo actress. In the week of 13 October 2025 her title "【MOODYZ創立25周年記念】MOODYZ 大ハード学園" reached first on FANZA's video weekly chart, and it was still ninth in the week of 2 February 2026. On 24 and 25 October 2026 she held the two days of her tenth-anniversary event, "Akari Niimura Festival".
