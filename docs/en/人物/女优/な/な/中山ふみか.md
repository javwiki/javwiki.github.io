## Introduction

Fumika Nakayama (中山ふみか, born 5 September 1997) is a Japanese adult-film actress and singer affiliated with LIGHT, reported in Chinese-speaking regions as 中山文香. She worked as a child actress, mainly on stage, for a short period from kindergarten to the early years of primary school. In December 2019 she debuted in adult video as a MOODYZ exclusive. At the time she also held a part-time job in the restaurant business, shut down her private social media and turned down all media exposure; the day after her debut, however, a colleague at work said she "looked like" the new actress. She sensed that those around her knew, but as no one confronted her directly she kept the job, sometimes going straight to work after a shoot.

She had initially been contracted for five titles and left the decision on continuing unanswered, so she paused her activity. The pause coincided with the impact of COVID-19 and prompted her to reconsider her work, and she decided to resume as an adult-film actress, which left a gap in her releases from April to August 2020. She regards the work with which she returned as her "debut as an adult-film actress" and names it as the shoot that made her most nervous.

As a singer she first appeared live on 28 September 2021 in "月で逢いましょう＃22 ニューカマースペシャル", and on 20 December 2021 she held her first solo concert, "月で逢いましょう＃30", at Grapefruit Moon in Sangenjaya, Tokyo; she has continued regular live activity since. On 1 December 2022 she entered DMM GAMES' public audition for a new character voice in "クリムゾン妖魔大戦" and reached the final round. On 20 May 2025 she announced on her social media that she was leaving MOODYZ, where she had spent many years, and moving to OPPAI as an exclusive.
