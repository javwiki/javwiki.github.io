## Introduction

Hikari Nagisa was born on 14 February 1997, comes from Tokyo, is represented by Bstar and has blood type A. In April 2017 she debuted as an IdeaPocket exclusive, promoted as "アニヲタ天然Fカップ". The official description sells her "ぴちぴちボディ", "たわわに実ったおっぱい", cute smile and vocal reactions. Her interests are anime, music and basketball, which also explain the "アニヲタ" in the tagline. In May and June 2017 she appeared with Arina Hashimoto on エンタメ〜テレ's 《W女優おんせん》 #23 and #24. According to a fan reference site, her debut title was 《新人 FIRST IMPRESSION 114 秋葉原でスカウト！バスト90cmアニヲタ天然Fカップ桃色乳房の素人娘AVデビュー》, meaning she was scouted in Akihabara before debuting.
