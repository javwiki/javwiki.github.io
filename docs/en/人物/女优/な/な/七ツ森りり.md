## Introduction

Riri Nanatsumori (七ツ森りり, born 11 November 1995) is a Japanese adult-film actress and former fashion model affiliated with T-POWERS. Her father is Japanese and her mother Filipino. She worked as an exclusive model for CHOKi CHOKi girls and hosted the TV Asahi series musicる TV as a reader model. On 13 July 2020 she moved to T-POWERS and changed her stage name to Riri Nanatsumori; on 17 July she announced her adult-video debut for 19 August.

Her debut work ranked first in the 2020 annual AV sales ranking compiled by Asahi Geinō with FANZA's cooperation, and her second work placed 18th for the year. In FANZA's 2020 annual adult-video sales ranking, which counts DVD and Blu-ray separately, three of her works appeared in the top four. In December 2020 she placed 25th in the reader vote for FLASH's "2020 Best 100 Active Sexy Actresses".

In February 2024 she became a campaign girl for FANZA's fifth-anniversary campaign. In March she took part in the "WINK of LIFE" photo exhibition at MAGNET by SHIBUYA109, and in August she appeared at TRE Taipei Adult Expo in Taiwan. On 19 August 2026, her sixth debut anniversary, she announced on X that she would take an indefinite break after the work released on 22 September that year.
