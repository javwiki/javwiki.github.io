## Introduction

Kokomi Naruse is a former Japanese AV actress, TV personality and singer. She debuted on 19 December 2008 in a lolita-style kikaku amateur title, then worked as a kikaku-tantai (キカタン) actress under the name 成瀬心美. In February 2012 she won both the Actress Award and the Nikkan Gendai Award at the 2012 Adult Broadcasting Awards, the first kikaku-tantai actress to take that Actress Award, and from September 2012 she was exclusive to KMP (宇宙企画). She went on hiatus from April 2013 for health reasons; after a comeback event in January 2014 she mainly worked as a TV personality and musician, and AV appearances effectively ended. In January 2024 she released the photobook 《coco》 marking her 15th anniversary.
