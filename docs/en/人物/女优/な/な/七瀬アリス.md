## Introduction

Alice Nanase (七瀬アリス, born 25 February 1997) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Saitama Prefecture who has been with MINE'S since December 2025. She once worked under the name Ichishima Ami, but her official debut date is 2020, under the name Alice Nanase. On 13 September 2020 she re-debuted as an Idea Pocket exclusive; after five titles she moved to freelance solo work. She has said that the freelance period "trained my understanding of a work".

In June 2022 she became head of KMP's president's office, effectively in charge of public relations, and at the same time an exclusive for the company's Million label. On 9 November 2022 she took part as a Cyzo representative in the official programme of the poker app m HOLD'EM, facing gravure idols. On 13 November she failed to appear at a photo session without notice and could not be reached, and Taiwan's CTi News reported it as a disappearance. On 31 December she wrote on Twitter that the reason for her break had been ill health. In 2022 she also joined the metaverse and crypto-asset publicity project Meta Girls with Amakawa Sora, Hirose Narumi and Sakura Moko, and its first real-world event was held on 18 January 2023.

On 3 August 2023 she appeared as a singer at LADY MADONNA ANNEX at GOTANDA G6 in Shinagawa, performing idol songs with backing dancers. That year, while AI-generated models dominated Amazon's adult-photobook category, she was the only real model to reach the upper ranks and was called the "queen of e-books" (she had no paper photobook at the time). From December 2023 she became a triple exclusive for Honnaka, DAS! and E-BODY, releasing new titles from each studio after about half a year away and returning to adult video. From 19 January to 16 February 2024 she was a campaign girl for FANZA's "I Love AV" campaign and discussed production with the producer Tomoko. In May she reported on X that she had moved to ACT; from 9 to 11 August she appeared at TRE Taipei Adult Expo at TaiNEX in Taipei and was reported as a "Taiwanese-Japanese sexy-actress DJ"; in July she covered issue 369 of the Harley-Davidson magazine VIBES.

For the week of 10 February 2025 her appearance in "MOODYZファン感謝祭 バコバコバスツアー2025" debuted third on the FANZA mail-order chart at the pre-order stage. On 13 April she announced on X that she intended to leave her agency, and on 15 April ACT's official account posted an apology and notice. In September she became a MOODYZ exclusive; for the week of 3 November a VR work she appeared in debuted first on the FANZA video chart, and on 30 November she announced she was leaving her agency, with MINE'S announced in December. On 5 March 2026 she announced an indefinite break from adult video, continuing other television and modelling work, and said filming was complete through releases in October that year. From 22 to 27 September she held a sixth-anniversary photo exhibition at Gallery Le Deco in Shibuya.
