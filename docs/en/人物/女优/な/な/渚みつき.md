## Introduction

Mitsuki Nagisa (渚みつき, born 7 February 1998) is a Japanese former adult-film actress from Shizuoka Prefecture who was affiliated with Bambi Promotion at the time of her retirement. She debuted in January 2019 with the tagline "the school beauty everyone admired". According to an interview in the July 2023 issue of Monthly FANZA, she had appeared anonymously before her official debut in kira☆kira's "大嫌いなオヤジ相手なのに大量潮吹き!". She was still blonde when she joined the agency, but was told to go black if she was going to debut, so she changed her hair before shooting her debut work and image video.

By April 2019 she had successively taken on lesbian and swallow scenes, and she went on to tackle hard projects such as mass facials, group violation and suspension bondage, becoming one of the leading actresses in the planning-single field. In August 2020 she placed fifth in Monthly FANZA's first-half AV actress ranking, and in December she placed fourth in the "neat" category of the reader vote for FLASH's "2020 Best 100 Active Sexy Actresses". She was eighth in FANZA's 2020 annual AV actress ranking. In September 2021 and September 2022 she served as a campaign girl for the Triple HAPPY Campaign.

On 18 January 2023 she announced that she would retire on 13 January 2024, the fifth anniversary of her debut, in order to look for a marriage partner; she said the wedding date given by fortune-tellers kept slipping back each year and she felt she was missing her chance. She ended her adult-video career with a fan event on 13 January 2024 but stayed with her agency, continuing model and acting work, and joined the girls' action project "Action KICK Girls", produced by action director Shigeki Hayase. On 31 January 2025 she announced that she would leave the agency at the end of August that year and retire completely.
