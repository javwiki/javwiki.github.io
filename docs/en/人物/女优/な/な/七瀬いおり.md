## Introduction

Iori Nanase is a Japanese AV actress born on 23 November 1993, from Miyagi Prefecture and represented by JETSTREAM. In January 2020 she debuted as an exclusive for million, a label of ケイ・エム・プロデュース (KMP). She announced in October and November 2020 that she would leave the exclusive roster, moved to マドンナ (Madonna) as an exclusive in January 2021, and in October 2021 announced that she had ended the Madonna exclusivity with the title released that September and become a kikaku-tantai performer. She is 164cm with an F cup and worked as a nursery teacher before debuting. Beyond AV she has appeared in image videos, photo books, digital photo books, magazines and radio.

## Personal notes

- From Miyagi Prefecture; interests are collecting lingerie, drinking and horse racing.
- She was a nursery teacher before debuting and moved into AV because she wanted to try a range of things (per Japanese Wikipedia).
- Japanese Wikipedia records that she had watched AV since high school and that her first experience was with a boyfriend in her second year of high school, with four partners in total.
- From 1 October 2020 she appeared on RADIOワンダースレージ's 《盛合でぇすけのMOTTO SHIN-SAPPORO》, later 《でぇすけ・ユーキ・いおりのMOTTO SHIN-SAPPORO》.
- In 2021 she appeared in 舞ワイフ No.1175 under the name 七瀬香 (per the Japanese Wikipedia work list).

## Work features

- The million exclusive period centred on innocent and busty roles; the Madonna period moved to married-woman and affair subjects, and the title released in September 2021 was her last exclusive work.
- Image videos in 2020–2021: 《Iori 桃色えちえちおねえさん》 (REbecca, 2020-12-03) and 《明日はきっと晴れるよ!》 (Superlative, 2021-12-23).
- On 23 December 2021 she published the photo book 《七色 七瀬いおり写真集》 (ジーウォーク, photography by 篠原潔).
- Digital photo books from KMP, REbecca, プレステージ出版 and ジーウォーク (2020–2022).
- Magazine appearances: 《月刊サイゾー》 February 2020, an interview in 《月刊FANZA》 April 2020, and a bound insert in 《週刊実話》 25 February 2021.
- VR: KMPVR's 《10日間不在の間に欲望を抑えまくった彼女と狂ったようにSEXした夜》 (2020-08-31) and Madonna's first VR title (2021-09-03).
