## Introduction

Non Nonoura is a Japanese AV actress born on 10 February 1999 in Tokyo, represented by T-POWERS. After appearing on 「シロウトTV」 in January 2019, she debuted as a プレステージ exclusive that February under the tagline "1億人が恋する美少女". She says she watched a great deal of AV from junior high school as "something interesting" rather than as erotica, and had no negative image of the industry; she entered through an acquaintance in the business, with AIKA and 桐嶋りの actually making the connection, and AIKA's line 「どうせだったら大きいことしてみなよ」 helped her decide. She placed 80th in the reader poll of 「読者300人が選んだFLASH 2021セクシー女優ランキング」 in August 2021 and appeared at the TRE Taipei adult expo at the Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center in August 2024.
