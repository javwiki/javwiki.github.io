## Introduction

Rora Misaki (formerly 滝澤ローラ) is a former Japanese AV actress born on 1 June 1992, of Russian and Japanese heritage. She debuted as a PRESTIGE exclusive in July 2012, renamed herself 水咲ローラ in April 2013 and made a second "AV debut" as an IDEA POCKET exclusive in November 2013. Her mixed-heritage looks and 172cm height have given her high recognition in Japan and the Chinese-speaking world, especially mainland China. She also starred in the Chinese films 《天神伝》 (2013) and 《流氓来了》 (2013). From 21 September 2022 she has been a brand ambassador for SOHIMIJAPAN (株式会社ソヒミジャパン).

## Personal notes

- Of Russian and Japanese heritage, with her mixed looks as the defining feature.
- At 172cm she is tall for an AV actress.
- Highly recognisable in the Chinese-speaking world; the name 滝澤ローラ is widely known in mainland China.
- Her AV career spanned the two major labels PRESTIGE and IDEA POCKET.
- After retiring she opened the YouTube channel 「芳沢ローラ」.

## Work features

- Mixed-heritage, exotic looks and a tall figure are the selling points.
- The PRESTIGE period centred on series such as 「絶対的美少女」 and 「一泊二日、美少女完全予約制。」; after moving to IDEA POCKET in 2013 she released solo titles such as 《FIRST IDEAPOCKET》.
- Work ranges from innocent to nymphomania, with many compilations and best-of volumes.
- She was one of the AV actresses who became popular early in the Chinese market.
