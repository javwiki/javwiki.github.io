## Introduction

水咲萝拉 (old name: 泷泽萝拉) is a former Japanese AV actress of Russian and Japanese descent. She debuted as a Prestige exclusive in July 2012 and moved to IDEA POCKET in November 2013. With her distinctive mixed-race looks and a tall 172cm figure she is extremely well known in Japan and Chinese-speaking markets (especially mainland China). She also starred in the Chinese films 《天神传》 (2013) and 《流氓来了》 (2013).
