## Introduction

Mai Tamaki is a Japanese pachinko writer, gravure idol and AV actress born on 11 February 1992 in Gunma Prefecture. She began activity in 2013, writing a pachinko column for サンケイスポーツ and entering ZAK THE QUEEN 2013. In January 2014 she made her AV debut as an IdeaPocket exclusive. She has since combined pachinko-related television, gravure and acting, appearing in the adult film 《美人妻覚醒 破られた貞操》 (オーピー映画, as 前川さやか) in 2016 and the V-cinema 《セクシャルダイナマイトヒロイン23 バトル・オブ・デス 国際犯罪捜査官メイファー編》 (ZENピクチャーズ, as メイファー) in 2017.
