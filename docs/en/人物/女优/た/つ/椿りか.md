# Rika Tsubaki

A Japanese adult-film performer. Debut information below follows the cited sources; rankings are not used as biographical evidence.

## Basic information

- **Japanese name**: 椿りか
- **English name**: Rika Tsubaki

## Introduction

Rika Tsubaki was born on 5 June 1999 (per Japanese Wikipedia; a database gives 1996-06-05, see Scope). She debuted on 1 April 2017 as 大島ひな, appearing in only two titles before moving to a soapland in Kawasaki. In 2019 she placed first in the Kanagawa soap category and fifth in the national soap category of ヘブンネット's 全国ミスヘブン総選挙2019. On 13 June 2020 she changed her name to 椿りか and redebuted; by 2022 she had appeared in more than 1,000 titles, including uncredited ones. From around 2021 she also worked pachinko parlour appearances as りかしゃふちゃん, and since 2024 she has appeared in films.

## Work features

- She works as a planning-only (kikaku-tantai) performer across studios. Her ROOKIE official work list includes the 美女と野獣 ギャルとキモオタ series, compilations of 痴女 material, urination and squirting compilations, and creampie best-of volumes.
- The February 2024 release サンバでエロすぎギャルをナンパ！… (DOC) is built around street pick-ups, large breasts and creampies; it reached 10th on FANZA's weekly video chart in June and again in August 2024.
- From 2024 she has appeared in titles from h.m.p, マドンナ, MOODYZ and KMPVR, including VR works and large-cast projects.
- Screen appearances include the OP Eiga films 密着巨乳姉妹 おっぱいは知っている (29 November 2024, as Saya) and さあやとこはる 最終章 (21 November 2025, as 高橋桃花).
