## Introduction

Risa Tsukino is a former Japanese AV actress and stripper born on 1 October 1989 in Tokyo. She debuted on 25 July 2008 in kawaii*'s 《kawaii*新人デビュ→キラキラ★カワイイ♥ 月野りさ》 and was known for a baby face and schoolgirl or gyaru roles. On 24 March 2010 she won the 日刊現代賞 at the スカパー!アダルト放送大賞. She retired at the end of March 2012, when 《週刊現代》 called her the "angel of the AV world"; a retirement event ran from 29 to 31 March that year at 東洋ショー劇場.

## Work features

- Centred on a baby face and schoolgirl or gyaru roles; in the early period she worked at kawaii*, kira☆kira, プレステージ and other labels.
- From 2009 she appeared in SODクリエイト titles as 月野理紗, and that year also did many kikaku-tantai works at ワンズファクトリー, MOODYZ, クリスタル映像 and others.
- Her retirement title 《月チャンネル!》 (マキシング) came out in April 2011, and the photo book 《Juicy Moon》 followed in May.
- Compilations and reissues have circulated since her retirement.
