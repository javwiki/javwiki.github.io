## Introduction

Tsubomi is a Japanese columnist and former AV actress born on 25 December 1987 in Yamaguchi Prefecture, represented by Excelia Promotion. The stage name comes from her December birth, when flowers are still buds. In April 2006 (aged 18 years and about four months) she signed an S1 exclusive contract and debuted, appearing in six S1 titles. In 2007 she signed with 笠倉出版社, whose first release 《Re-Birth》 came out on 23 February, followed by twelve titles. She became a kikaku-tantai performer in 2008, took a break for ill health that March and returned in May. On 19 February 2009 at AVグランプリ2009, 《S-Cute ex Special》 won the best amateur work award and 《蕾に滴る野蛮な汁 つぼみ》 the best fetish work award. She placed first in the 2012 AV30 popularity poll and by June 2012 had appeared in more than 1,000 titles including compilations. From September 2013 she was exclusive to both MOODYZ and Wanz Factory. On 9 February 2022 she announced retirement at the end of April, held her last large event 「つぼみ祭り…卒業」 on 16 April and in December received the アサ芸AV大賞2022功労賞.

## Personal notes

- From Yamaguchi Prefecture; interests are collecting fancy goods, reading, playing the piano and playing with dogs. Her dog is called 楓介.
- A loli-genre performer who long played girls and younger sisters, she still played high-schoolers several times in her thirties; the lack of a major change in roles across her career is extremely unusual for an AV actress.
- For her longevity and popularity, co-stars called her "the god of the loli world"; her nickname is "終身名誉処女".
- She studied piano from age three to sixteen and in 2020 bought a soundproof room and a grand piano to take it up again.
- She has little desire to marry and says she is neither determined to stay single nor interested enough to change.
- She continued writing after retiring and has described starting a rural life three to four hours from Tokyo.

## Work features

- An S1 exclusive at first, then 笠倉出版社 in 2007, a high-volume kikaku-tantai career from 2008, and two AVグランプリ awards in 2009.
- In 2011 she was chosen as a ケイ・エム・プロデュース 「レアル・ガールズ」 campaign girl and in 2012 as a 「宇宙企画ガールズ」; at the end of 2011 she won 《週刊プレイボーイ》's 「2011 AV オブ・ザ・イヤー」.
- From April 2012, for six months, she formed the スカパーアダルト応援隊 「AIDL5」 with さとう遥希, 加藤リナ, 小倉奈々 and 成瀬心美.
- From September 2013 she was exclusive to both MOODYZ and Wanz Factory, with harder material in the later period.
- Music and brand: on 1 December 2010 she released the download single 《夢花火》 as TSUBOMI; on 13 February 2016 she opened the web store of her official apparel brand 「TSUBOMIN」.
