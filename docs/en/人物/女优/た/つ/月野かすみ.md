## Introduction

Kasumi Tsukino was born on 26 January 2002 in Kamakura, Kanagawa Prefecture. After periods with All Pro and Arrows, she is now represented by Restar Pro. She debuted in October 2020 as a DEEP'S exclusive; her debut work, DVDMS-585, was released physically on 7 October, while FANZA lists the streaming start as 3 October. She left exclusivity in May 2021 to work freelance.

## Work features

- She describes herself as fond of taking the lead, and many titles cast her as the one who initiates, whispers and dominates. Around 2024 she publicly identified as bisexual and has appeared in lesbian titles.
- In 2026 she described her direction as "the soft, downer older sister".
- Writer Chiyoko Mochizuki called her "a far more formidable figure than expected" and noted her body hair.
