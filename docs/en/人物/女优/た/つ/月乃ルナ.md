## Introduction

Luna Tsukino (月乃ルナ, born 1 October 1995) is a Japanese adult-film actress and singer from Tokyo. She worked as a gravure model for Weekly Playboy and other magazines. In 2018 she formed the music unit JC + The Mechanics (ジュラシック・カッパーフィールド・アンド・ザ・メカニックス), sang lead and distributed original songs, and contributed to the photobook omnibus "天使集"; she published the photobook "月の魔法" in 2019 and was with Eightman at the time. In February 2019 she made her adult-video debut with "新人 月乃ルナ ～最上●が激似のアノ子が衝撃のAVデビュー～" and broke through on the strength of that title.

Around 2021 she moved to Bambi Promotion. In August 2021 she placed 30th in the reader vote for "FLASH 2021 Sexy Actress Ranking", as chosen by 300 readers, and in the same month placed sixth in Monthly FANZA's "This AV Actress Is Amazing! Summer 2021" and fifth in the winter actress edition. In April 2022 alone she released 13 works, among them "凄テクを我慢できれば生中だしSEX", which was highly praised; in May she placed 24th in Asahi Geinō's "2022 Active AV Actress SEXY General Election". From August 2022 she was exclusive to two studios, Attackers and Honnaka.

In August 2023 she moved to NAX Promotion and opened an X account. In March and April 2024 a title scheduled by SOD Create, "SNSで大炎上!? ファンにおっぱいを揉まれた金髪美人DJのセックス動画がヤバすぎて問題になっています。 月乃ルナ", was cancelled; SOD cited "various circumstances". According to Josei Jishin, the work was seen as parodying the 2023 sexual-assault incident involving the Korean DJ SODA at a music festival, and drew criticism online. In April 2024 she placed 27th overall in Asahi Geinō's "2024 Active AV Actress Sexy General Election" (46th in Tokyo, 19th in Osaka). In August 2025 she moved back to Eightman (later renamed SO MODEL AGENT), and from March 2026 (dated April in the magazine) she became a DAHLIA exclusive.

## Personal notes

- As a girl she was shy and stayed at home, later entering a girls' high school. A senior from her part-time job at a family restaurant asked her out; when she refused he began stalking her, and she gave in and dated him, taking about half a year to reach her first sexual experience. When she broke up because he was too controlling he stalked her again, and later boyfriends showed the same tendency, earning her the nickname "menhera maker"; she had dated about seven people before her AV debut.
- She gives her reason for appearing in adult video as "I was interested, and I wanted to try all sorts of things, so I gave it a go". In a 2024 interview she also cited befriending an adult-film actress by chance and consulting her about a sexless relationship with her boyfriend.
- She expected her boyfriend to stop her, but he took it as "if you want to do it, you should", so she went through the interview and the shoot while thinking, "I'm really going to go film an AV, you know?"
- During her first year as an exclusive she rarely appeared in the media and built a deliberately mysterious persona; after going independent she stopped fixing her image and played a range of roles.
- Her admired actress is Akiho Yoshizawa.
- Around 2019 she was marketed for her resemblance to Moga Mogami, of whom she is a fan and from whom she once got a cheki photo; in 2021 she stopped dyeing her hair blonde and was repositioned as a slender, slightly shadowed innocent type.
- She had also appeared in hard titles and is described as "the passive side who takes deep penetration during sex". The adult-media researcher Rio Yasuda wrote in December 2022 that she had "broken free of the celebrity-lookalike image and brilliantly established her own line of being erotic with a cool expression".

## Work features

- Before adult video she was a gravure model and the vocalist of a music unit; she moved into AV in February 2019 and released 13 titles in April 2022 alone, a high-output independent phase.
- From August 2022 she was exclusive to Attackers and Honnaka, and from 2026 her work is mainly DAHLIA exclusives.
- Her output spans innocent drama, hard titles and independent co-starring works; early on she was sold on the "celebrity lookalike" angle and from 2021 as a slender, shadowed innocent type.
- Her photobooks are "月の魔法" (2019) and "月に満ちて。" (2026), and she contributed to the 2018 omnibus "天使集".
- In music she released "Night Breeze" in 2018 and worked as a member of JC + The Mechanics; on 16 April 2024 she covered songs by Ringo Sheena, B'z and BiSH at "月で逢いましょう vol.91", and on 8 May 2025 she held a solo concert.
- She was the cover model for VIBES Vol.370, the August 2024 issue.
