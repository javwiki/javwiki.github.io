## Introduction

Honoka Tsujii (辻井ほのか, born 15 November 1995) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Tokyo affiliated with T-POWERS. She is 172 cm tall. After working as a hairdresser for about six years she quit because of the low pay; she also had debts at the time and chose adult video in order to earn. She debuted from PRESTIGE in March 2019 and was marketed as a tall, busty actress.

In October 2020 she placed second in FANZA's monthly mail-order actress ranking, and she also placed second in the actress ranking compiled by Monthly FANZA on 25 November 2020. On 30 November 2021, on the recommendation of the Playboy Channel, she was named "Valuable Actress of Playboy Channel" at the Sky PerfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards.

On 2 February 2023 it was reported that a traffic accident had left her with a broken nose, a fracture of the outer skull and 15 stitches above the eyelid; because the injuries were to her face, they took about three months to heal. In the week of 22 May that year, the studio compilation "BAZOOKA 冬の大感謝セール コスパ最強ノーカットベスト爆乳限定2749分", which includes scenes of her, topped FANZA's weekly video-floor ranking. From 27 to 29 March 2026 she appeared at ADULTOPIA (大人國成人展) at Messe Taoyuan in Taiwan and won BEST ADULT ACTRESS. On 3 May 2026 she injured her left knee while playing basketball and was diagnosed with a complete medial collateral ligament tear, a complete anterior cruciate ligament tear, a fracture and a meniscus injury; in July it was reported that she planned to return in October.

## Personal notes

- Her hobbies are watching films and her speciality is basketball. She started playing in the fifth grade as a guard, became a candidate for the under-15 Japan national team and was named MVP at the National Sports Festival; she also played power forward and centre.
- She was recruited by a basketball powerhouse high school and could have aimed for a professional career, but back pain and a relationship distracted her, the gap with the top group widened and her playing career ended in high school.
- Her bust continued to grow and measured 118 cm as of 2024.
- After leaving hairdressing she was effectively cut off from her family, but the traffic accident became the occasion for them to support her.
