## Introduction

Mei Tsukumo is a Japanese AV actress born on 20 May 1999 in Gunma Prefecture, represented by ACT. She debuted at different labels under several stage names. On 13 December 2018 she debuted at E-BODY as 滝沢ななお with a single exclusive title before becoming a kikaku-tantai performer; on 13 November 2020 she debuted as a Fitch exclusive under the name 長谷川りさ, and on 25 November 2020 as a マドンナ exclusive under the name 芦永れい. On 19 July 2022 she re-debuted as 九十九メイ. Tall stature, a full bust, long legs and nymphomania roles characterise her work. Because of her father's job she lived in South Korea from the second year of middle school through the third year of high school, making her a returnee. On 18 December 2025 a fan reference site updated its page to record a name change to 津久葉メイ, listing the former name 九十九メイ and the aliases 新田みのり and 後藤綾.
