## Introduction

Miho Toono (通野未帆, born 21 January 1991) is a Japanese adult-film actress from Tokyo, now affiliated with ACT and formerly with GREEN. She is 160 cm tall with measurements of B87 (E)-W59-H85, as stated in 2013. She calls herself an actress with "no selling point" and "an eternal amateur", a phrase that refers both to her range from mature-woman roles to magical-girl parts and to not forgetting her beginnings. The writer Kurogane Arei has said that although she claims to have no selling point, she has a lean, trim figure and good legs, and that many of her works use pantyhose and high-cut costumes to emphasise them.

She debuted in 2013, and her early work was mainly amateur and pick-up-style projects. In July 2014 she entered Sega's popularity vote for sexy actresses in the Ryū ga Gotoku (Yakuza) series; the result was announced on a live stream on 24 August, when she placed 30th with 47,561 votes and won the right to appear in Ryū ga Gotoku 0: Chikai no Basho. In September 2024 she became a Madonna exclusive actress, and in August 2025 she reported on social media that she had returned to project-by-project work after about a year.
