## Introduction

Makoto Toda was born on 9 October 1996 and is a writer and film director, formerly an AV actress. In June 2016 she debuted from SOD's new Seishun Jidai label with 《「私、Hがしてみたいんです」 戸田真琴 19歳 処女 SOD専属AVデビュー》, and in November that year she moved to SOD Star. In May 2018 she received the Buzz Award at the DMM R-18 Adult Awards 2018 and the newcomer prize at the 30th Pink Film Awards, and in March 2019 the actress award at the Sky PerfecTV Adult Broadcasting Awards 2019. In 2019 she made her directing debut with the short-film collection 《永遠が通り過ぎていく》, funded by crowdfunding. In December 2021 she graduated from about five years as a SOD exclusive and became a FALENO exclusive, retiring on 31 January 2023; she has continued writing and filmmaking, publishing her first autobiographical novel 《そっちにいかないで》 in May 2023.

## Personal notes

## Work features

- Studio: about five years as a SOD exclusive (Seishun Jidai, then SOD Star from November 2016), followed by a FALENO exclusive from December 2021 until her retirement in January 2023.
- Idol and contest work: joined the group 原宿バンビーナ in October 2016 and graduated in August 2017; won the first 大森靖子賞 in June 2017 and was selected for ミスiD2018 in November.
- Film-making: in 2019 she made 《永遠が通り過ぎていく》 (a MOOSIC LAB 2019 special invitation) with about ¥6 million raised from 450 backers; her retirement title 《戸田真琴、引退。私が最後にやりたかったこと》 began streaming on 5 December 2022 and entered FANZA's weekly mail-order chart at No. 2.
- From April 2021 she ran the project "I'm a Lover, not a Fighter." with photographer 飯田エリカ, handling direction, styling and copywriting.
