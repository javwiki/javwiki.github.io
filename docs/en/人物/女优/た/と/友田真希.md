# Maki Tomoda

A Japanese adult-film actress. The information below follows Japanese Wikipedia and the sources listed; rankings are not used as biographical evidence.

- **Active**: 2002–2010; 2016–present

## Introduction

Maki Tomoda (友田真希, born 20 August 1972) is a Japanese adult-film actress. She made her adult-video debut in 2002 while at Star Craft, and went on to appear in a very large number of titles centred on the mature-woman genre for studios including Madonna, Dogma, Crystal Eizō and Big Morkal. She announced her retirement in March 2010 and returned on 25 August 2016 as a Madonna exclusive, remaining active into the 2020s.

She has said that she entered adult video because a partner at the time had run up debt in her name; she ended the relationship and chose the work as a source of income to escape the situation. Her early output was extremely dense, and her original titles from 2002 to 2010 cover roles such as married women, mothers-in-law, widows and female teachers.

In 2006 she appeared in the Dogma-organised D-1 CLIMAX title "レズフィスト・ドラッグ〜少女の夢はいつだってフィストファック〜", directed by TOHJIRO. It ranked first in sales, and she and Chihiro Hasegawa shared the second D-1 CLIMAX "Ero Cinderella" award; she also joined TOHJIRO's "TOHJIRO・M女軍団" group. That year a documentary about her, "影の光", was produced. In 2008 "人間酷包 友田真希", released by Mill, won the SM and humiliation category award at AV Grand Prix 2008.

She announced her retirement in March 2010, citing pregnancy and a wish to try raising a child; retirement and commemorative titles were still released in June and August that year. She began using Twitter on 29 July 2014. On 25 August 2016 she returned as a Madonna exclusive with "専属復活 友田真希", and her subsequent titles have been almost entirely for that studio. In May 2019 she released her first VR work.

In July 2022 she became one of the co-initiators of the petition campaign "女優・男優・スタッフが働きやすい『AV新法』にしてください", which calls for revision of the law on preventing and relieving harm from adult-video appearances. In the FANZA first-half 2024 actress ranking reported in the October issue of Monthly FANZA, released in August 2024, she placed 14th. On 26 November 2025 the photobook "真希" was published by G-Walk.

## Source coverage

This entry draws mainly on Japanese Wikipedia's "友田真希" (accessed 23 November 2026) and her FANZA performer page. The secondary sources cited by Wikipedia have not been traced individually. Height, measurements, blood type, birthplace and other fields not stated in the source are not filled in. The source's list of works is extremely large; this entry selects representative titles and turning points by year rather than listing every title, and does not count the total. The source's list of original titles ends in May 2020, but its career summary separately records the 2024 ranking and the 2025 photobook, so the end of the list is not treated as evidence of retirement. The source also lists a new Madonna tenth-anniversary title and adult goods in 2013, after the 2010 retirement, without explaining why; they are recorded as given and no inference is added. The debut is dated only to the year 2002, so the specific date is taken from the earliest listed title. Rankings and awards are kept with the source's counting basis and are not treated as independent biographical facts.
