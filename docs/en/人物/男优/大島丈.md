## Introduction

Joe Oshima is a Japanese AV actor and a reader at the Omotesando Bungaku events. Organizer Brick Corporation dates his AV debut to 1991 in its crowdfunding profile. [Organizer’s account](https://camp-fire.jp/projects/632414/view)

## Career and activities

The organizer describes literary readings using a dummy-head microphone and audience headphones, beginning in July 2017, with regular online performances from May 2020. A December 2022–January 2023 campaign proposed a five-city tour.

These details come from the organizer’s account. The existing entry titled only “大島” does not contain enough information to establish that it refers to the same person.
