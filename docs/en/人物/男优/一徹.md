## Introduction

Ittetsu is a Japanese actor in AV made for women, presented by SILK LABO as an “eromen.” His official profile lists a height of 175 cm and a birthday of 21 February. [SILK LABO profile](https://www.silklabo.com/eromen/ittetsu/)

## Career and activities

A 2013 profile states that he was born in 1979, entered the industry around 2004 through an application for extra work, and became exclusive to SILK LABO in 2012. [Numero TOKYO report](https://numero.jp/news-20131101-eromen/)

The official blog records the end of his exclusive arrangement in January 2017 and the release of 卒業-graduation- 一徹 on 6 April. Ending exclusivity should be distinguished from retirement from the industry. [Official blog (March 2017)](https://silklabomaking.doorblog.jp/archives/2017-03.html)
