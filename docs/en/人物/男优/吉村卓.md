## Introduction

Taku Yoshimura is a Japanese AV actor. In 2019, Rooftop interviewed him and the leader of the fan group Yoshimura Taku wo Moriagetai about acting and live events. [Rooftop interview](https://rooftop1976.com/interview/190706200000.php)

## Career and activities

He discusses how demand for roles changes with age, alongside changes in filming and the industry. The fan-group leader describes gratitude for Yoshimura’s support as the starting point of the group.

This career summary uses the 2019 first-person interview. That report does not establish a verified birth date, exact debut year, or complete filmography.
