## Introduction

Taito Tsukino is a Japanese AV actor associated with SILK LABO’s works and events for women. A 2013 profile identifies him as born in 1979 and from Kanagawa Prefecture. [Numero TOKYO report](https://numero.jp/news-20131101-eromen/)

## Career and activities

The report dates his SILK LABO exclusivity to 2012. The official blog later records that he and Ittetsu ended their exclusive arrangements in January 2017. [Official blog (March 2017)](https://silklabomaking.doorblog.jp/archives/2017-03.html)

SILK LABO’s event archive confirms his appearance alongside Ittetsu and Shota Kitano at SILK EROMEN SHOWCASE Vol.10 2016 on 13 August 2016. [Official event archive](https://www.silklabo.com/event_detail/2244/)
