## Introduction

Genjin Moribayashi is a Japanese AV actor, author, and speaker. His official profile states that he was born in 1979 and grew up in Yokohama. [Official profile](https://moribayashigenjin.jp/about)

## Career and activities

In his official account, he recalls applying to an actor recruitment notice while at university and later becoming an apprentice of 桜井ちんたろう. The site also describes writing, lectures, and organizing and appearing at events.

The National Diet Library catalogs the June 2016 Kodansha paperback edition of his book 偏差値78のAV男優が考えるセックス幸福論, providing an independent bibliographic record of authorship and publication. [National Diet Library record](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000002-I027329434)
