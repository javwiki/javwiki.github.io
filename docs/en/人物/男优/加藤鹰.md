## Introduction

Taka Kato is a former Japanese AV actor. A 2024 TV Tokyo report dates his AV debut to 1988 and retirement to 2013. [TV Tokyo report](https://www.tv-tokyo.co.jp/plus/entertainment/entry/202404/14946.html)

## Career and activities

On 14 August 2013, he announced that his AV acting career would end on 31 December that year. At the press conference he corrected his previously published birth year from 1962 to 1959. [Josei Jishin press-conference report](https://jisin.jp/entertainment/entertainment-news/1609555/)

The 2024 TV Tokyo report also describes advisory work for the Taiwanese video platform SWAG. This is a dated account of post-retirement work, rather than evidence of a return to AV acting.
