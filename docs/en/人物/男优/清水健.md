## Introduction

Shimiken, also known as Ken Shimizu, is a former Japanese AV actor and a writer. A 2018 interview identifies him as born in 1979 and from Chiba Prefecture. [New R25 interview](https://r25.jp/articles/928885293173506050)

## Career and activities

In that interview, he recalls answering a newspaper advertisement after high school, first modelling for Badi and later moving into AV work. Magazine modelling and AV appearances are separate stages of this account.

In a 2019 interview, he names Ittetsu among his apprentices, describing their professional relationship in his own terms. [Rooftop interview (2019-11-10)](https://rooftop1976.com/interview/191110230000.php)

A 14 April 2025 report, based on his own video remarks, confirms that he retired from AV acting in 2024. Descriptions of him as active in older interviews are therefore historical. [Report on his retirement remarks](https://www.nikkansports.com/entertainment/news/202504130000455.html)
