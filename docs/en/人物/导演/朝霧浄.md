## Introduction

朝霧浄 is a Japanese AV director known for a natural shooting style and for drawing out performers' individual charm. His work covers story films, kikaku titles and many other types, and is widely appreciated by audiences.

## Features

- Good at creating a warm, romantic shooting atmosphere
- Focuses on drawing out each performer's individual character
- Strong on story: his releases put weight on narrative
