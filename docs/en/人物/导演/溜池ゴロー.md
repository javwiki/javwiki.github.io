## Introduction

溜池ゴロー is a famous Japanese AV director known for a natural shooting style and for bringing out actresses' individuality. His work excels at amateur kikaku and story-driven titles, and he is one of the most active directors in Japan today.

## Features

- Good at catching actresses' natural moments on camera
- A natural, unforced style; strong on story-driven kikaku
- Discovered and trained a number of popular actresses
- The "溜池ゴロー" series are his signature works

## Main series

- The 溜池ゴロー series (NINE, COOL, etc.)
