## Introduction

深田咏美 is one of the most representative new-generation actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She debuted at SOD as 「天海心」 in February 2017; after a hiatus and plastic surgery she re-debuted renamed 「深田咏美」 in November 2018. Her "gyaru" image struck deep, and her releases are known for high quality and much loved by fans. Extremely active on social media — with large followings on TikTok and Twitter in particular — she is a representative figure of social-media operation among the new generation of AV actresses.
