## Introduction

古川伊織 is a former Japanese AV actress from Osaka Prefecture. She debuted at SOD Create in November 2012 and formally retired on 31 December 2022. Known for excellent acting and a varied catalogue, she was a member of the second-generation 惠比寿麝香葡萄. In her 10-year AV career she appeared in over 100 titles and also ventured into film, television and the stage. Her nickname is 「こっちゃん」.

## Personal notes

- Before her AV debut she worked as an actor and model; she entered the AV industry because she wanted to widen her range as an actress.
- Interests: reading, appreciating music, fitness and watching videos.
- Speciality: getting lost.
- Keeps two dogs at home: a white Chihuahua 「ゆず」 (since 2014) and a black miniature schnauzer 「ぽんず」 (since 2017).
- In 惠比寿麝香葡萄 she is called 「チーフ・コガワ」 and is often entrusted with hosting and推进 work.
- Placed 1st in 惠比寿麝香葡萄's knowledge test two years running in 2016 and 2017.
- Her first time was at 18 while attending vocational school in Osaka.

## Work features

- Known for excellent acting and character-building ability.
- Her catalogue covers many themes, including story-driven, teasing and subjugation.
- She has also appeared in women-oriented titles, taking part in SILK LABO works.
