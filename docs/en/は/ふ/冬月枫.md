## Introduction

冬月枫 is one of the representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry, and a former race-queen and singer. She debuted as a PREMIUM exclusive in December 2007 and quickly drew attention with her pure looks and excellent acting.

Kaede Fuyutsuki was a member of the idol unit SEXY-J and released a solo single in 2014. In 2015 she was nominated for the Best Actress award at the DMM Adult Awards. After March 2017 she stopped releasing new titles; reportedly she married a professional baseball player.
