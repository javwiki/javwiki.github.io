## Introduction

羽田爱 is one of the representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry and a former gravure idol. She debuted as a gravure idol in 2008 and made her AV debut as an SOD exclusive in February 2010. With a tall 170cm figure and outstanding presence she quickly rose to the top of the industry.

Ai Haneda won the Best Actress award at the 9th SOD Awards (2010) and the Newcomer Actress Award at the SkyperfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards for 2011. After retiring in 2014 she made her comeback as an SOD exclusive in July 2018.

## Personal notes

- Born in Tokyo; blood type O.
- Debuted as a gravure idol in 2008, releasing 8 DVDs and 10 digital photobooks in two years.
- Made her AV debut as a "celebrity" in February 2010; four partners before her debut.
- Won the SkyperfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards Newcomer Actress Award in 2011.
- Placed 21st in Sega's 《龍が如く》 popularity poll in 2014.
- On good terms with Kirara Asuka, めぐり, Shiori Kamisaki and others.
- Made her comeback as an SOD exclusive in July 2018.
- Appeared in the Hong Kong TV drama 《東京愛情動作故事》 in 2023.
