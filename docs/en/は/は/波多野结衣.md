## Introduction

波多野结衣 is one of the most internationally known AV actresses in Japan. She debuted in 2008 and, with her outstanding looks and professionalism, has remained active in the AV industry ever since. Because she resembles the famous model Lin Chi-ling, fans in Taiwan call her the "AV界林志玲 (Lin Chi-ling of the AV world)", which in turn made her known in Chinese-speaking circles. From 2011 she has also pursued non-AV entertainment activity overseas, appearing in several films.

## Personal notes

- Because she resembles Lin Chi-ling she is called the "Lin Chi-ling of the AV world" in Taiwan.
- During the 3.11 earthquake in 2011 a rumour falsely reported that she had died in the tsunami; her agency later confirmed she was safe.
- In 2014 she won the Adult Broadcasting Awards actress of the year and the Fuji Yūkan award.
- Appeared in the Taiwanese film 《沙西加》... — the Taiwanese film 《沙西米》 and the Hong Kong film 《棟篤特工》.
- She plays the character Yui in the Yakuza (人中之龍) series of games.
- She has extremely high popularity across Asia, especially in Taiwan, Hong Kong and South East Asia.

## Work features

- A varied catalogue covering story-driven, kikaku, VR and many other themes.
- Early on centred on a beauty-idol image, later gradually trying more varied roles.
- With over 2,000 titles she is one of the most prolific actresses in the industry.
