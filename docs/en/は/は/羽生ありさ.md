## Introduction

羽生ありさ is a Japanese AV actress who debuted in 2008 as 「小峰ひなた」, initially a big-breast kikaku-tantai actress. She moved to MOODYZ in March 2009. She retired briefly in March 2013 and made her comeback in 2016 as 「羽生アリサ」 (羽生ありさ); in January 2020 the stage name 「羽生ありさ」 was formally unified. She is characterised by an I-cup bust and a petite figure (153cm), and her titles are mainly married-woman and mature-woman themes.
