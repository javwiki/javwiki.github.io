## Introduction

莲实克蕾儿 is a Japanese AV actress known in the industry for teasing roles and cowgirl technique, celebrated as the "strongest SEXY Monster". She debuted as a 美 exclusive in 2012 with the debut slogan 「今世紀最大の極上痴女」. She is famous for the Spider cowgirl — swinging her hips at high speed while playing with the male performer's nipples with both hands, without ever losing the rhythm. She has appeared in over 1,600 titles over her career. In September 2022 she retired due to illness (a uterine tumour) and ran a bar in Kabukichō; in May 2025, recovered, she announced her comeback, and in February 2026 she returned as a MOODYZ exclusive.

## Personal notes

- She calls herself an "environmental M"; her reason for choosing the AV actress profession was "because it looked hard".
- A master of the Spider cowgirl; AV director レモンハート中島 claims to have named the "Spider cowgirl", but Hasumi is the one who embodied it and made it stick.
- While active she could keep up a high-speed cowgirl for 30 minutes straight and swing her hips for 10 hours a day.
- A master of dirty talk with a huge vocabulary, she calls dirty talk 「淫語は報・連・相」. Because she is bad at memorising lines, she instead forged a powerful improvisational dirty-talk ability.
- Loves collecting train stamps; she once conquered 77 JR East stations' stamps. In JR East's Ultraman stamp-collecting campaign in 2016 her tweet was retweeted the most.
- In the summer of 2015 she was captured by 圣饥魔II and became a believer of 圣饥魔II thereafter.
- During her active AV years she ran a bar in Kabukichō, Shinjuku (opened 2020; her model was 加山なつこ who ran a jukujo cabaret); after retiring she served full-time as its manager, and after her comeback she does both.
- Industry people describe her bare face as "serious and artisan-like".
