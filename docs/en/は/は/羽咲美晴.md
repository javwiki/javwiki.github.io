## Introduction

羽咲美晴 is a former Japanese AV actress and a current YouTuber, Twitch streamer and singer. She debuted from MUTEKI in April 2016 and became an S1 exclusive that June. She was a third-generation member of 惠比寿★麝香葡萄. In 2022 she retired as an AV actress and transitioned to being a YouTuber and Twitch streamer; her YouTube channel "うさちゃん🐰" has about 130,000 subscribers and her Twitch channel about 50,000 followers. She is a heavy Disney fan and, when she has time, goes to Tokyo Disneyland every day — called a "true Disney fan".
