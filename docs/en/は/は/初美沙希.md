## Introduction

初美沙希 is a Japanese entertainer and former AV actress. She debuted in 2010 as "しずく" and changed her name to 初美沙希 in 2011. She is the first actress in the history of the SkyperfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards to win four crowns at a single ceremony (2016), and is called the "Sky PerfecTV! 成人之子". After retiring in 2017 she became an entertainer and made her comeback in 2019. She is a huge professional-wrestling and baseball fan and has been to the US several times to watch WWE WrestleMania and Masahiro Tanaka's games.

## Personal notes

- Born in Miyagi Prefecture; moved to Tokyo in 2009.
- Likes common foods such as ground meat and burdock root, and often drops in at ramen shops, standing soba shops, Yoshinoya and Matsuya.
- Has taken an interest in fishing.
- Her Twitter followers once exceeded 170,000 (while active); she mutually follows many pro wrestlers and baseball players.
- Her oshi idols: Kanako Momota (Momoiro Clover Z), Fuka Akimoto (Team Shachihoko).
- Her oshi wrestler: Shinsuke Nakamura.
- Her oshi baseball player: Masahiro Tanaka.
- In 2015 she went to the US to watch WrestleMania 31 and Tanaka's Yankees home opener.
- In 2016 she went to the US again to watch WrestleMania 32 and Nakamura's US debut at NXT.
- In 2017 she went to Florida to watch WrestleMania 33.
- Appointed h.m.p's publicity director in November 2015.
- During her retirement she worked at a pachinko parlor.

## Work features

- At her debut she was positioned as a lolicon big-breast actress.
- After her name change she became a kikaku-tantai actress with a varied style.
- In 2016 she became an h.m.p exclusive — taking seven years from unknown kikaku actress to studio exclusive, extremely rare.
