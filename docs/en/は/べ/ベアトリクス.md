## Introduction

ベアトリクス (aliases: べあこ、たなかはる) is a new-half / okama AV actress from Yamaguchi Prefecture. She debuted around 2019 and built her popularity mainly on TikTok and Twitter. As of 2020 her Twitter followers exceeded 10,000, and in under a year since debut she had appeared in more than 16 titles — a serious seller.
