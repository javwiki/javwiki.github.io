# WILL (Hokuto / CA)

WILL is a Japanese video production company. Hokuto, CA and Outvision are names encountered in its business history; corporate succession and brand history need to be distinguished. The current company's official website gives **July 13, 2016** as its incorporation date.[Company profile](https://will-hp.jp/company/)

## Brand relationships

[S1](S1%20NO.1%20STYLE.md), [MOODYZ](MOODYZ.md), [IdeaPocket](IdeaPocket.md) and [Attackers](Attackers.md) belong to this production lineage. An “internal manufacturer” in historical accounts can mean an in-house production brand; it does not establish that each brand is a separate legal company.[CA brand information (secondary source)](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/CA_(アダルトビデオ))

Historically, Outvision covered both directly operated brands and manufacturers whose products it distributed. Old references to the “Outvision group” therefore cannot be read as a list of wholly owned subsidiaries at a particular date.

## Relationship with DMM / FANZA

CA formerly had a group relationship with DMM, but the 2017 sale report documents a change. Historical connections, distribution cooperation and present equity ownership are separate relationships.[withnews](https://withnews.jp/article/f0170111002qq000000000000000W05s10701qq000014542A)

DMM's announcement of July 19, 2018 states that its adult business passed to Digital Commerce on March 1 that year, and that DMM.R18 would become FANZA on August 1, with DMM continuing to supply platform systems. This platform transition does not establish FANZA's ownership of production companies.[Official DMM announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000003114.000002581.html)
