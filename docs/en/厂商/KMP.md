# KMP

## Basic information

- **Short name**: KMP
- **Full name**: KMP (K.M.Produce)
- **Company**: 株式会社ケイ・エム・プロデュース
- **Founded**: April 2002
- **Profile**: A long-established studio known for turning popular non-exclusive (kikaku) actresses into exclusives; the largest player in the VR field
- **Sub-brands**: million (ミリオン), 宇宙企画, レアル・ワークス, etc.
- **Website**: <https://www.km-produce.com/>

## History

- Founded in April 2002, initially focused on kikaku titles and non-exclusive actresses; opened the exclusive-newcomer label 「ミリオンエンジェル」 in 2004 and shifted toward actress titles.
- Left the Video Software Ethics Organization (ビデ倫) in October 2005; reviews moved to the Japan Visual Software Production & Distribution Ethics Organization (制販倫) and the Japan Content Scrutiny Center (JCRC).
- Around 2010 it was issuing the 宇宙企画, レアル・ワークス and S級素人 series.
- Nearly all programmes on SkyperfecTV!'s CS channel 「KMPチャンネル」 (942ch) are supplied by the company.
- Signed an exclusive contract with Kokomi Naruse in September 2012 (exclusive to 宇宙企画).
- Launched the monthly subscription service 「Aver」 in September 2020 and ended it in April 2021.
- 2025–2026: popularising the "exclusive" treatment for big non-exclusive actresses — 乙アリス (August 2025), 弥生みづき (October 2025), 尾崎えりか (October 2025) and 沙月恵奈 (January 2026) successively became million exclusives.
