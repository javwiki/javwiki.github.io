# KMP

## Basic information

- **Abbreviation**: KMP
- **Full name**: KMP (K.M.Produce)
- **Operator**: K.M.Produce Inc. (ケイ・エム・プロデュース)
- **Founded**: April 2002
- **Features**: veteran manufacturer known for converting popular non-exclusive actresses into exclusive talent; the largest maker in the VR field
- **Sub-labels**: million (ミリオン), 宇宙企画, レアル・ワークス, etc.
- **Official site**: <https://www.km-produce.com/>

## History

- Founded in April 2002, initially focusing on planning works and non-exclusive actresses; launched the exclusive newcomer label "ミリオンエンジェル" in 2004 and shifted to actress-centred releases.
- Left the Japan Video Ethics Association (ビデ倫) in October 2005; joined the Japan Software Publish & Sales Ethics Organization (制販倫) and the Japan Content Review Center (JCRC).
- By 2010 the company also released 宇宙企画, レアル・ワークス and S级素人 works.
- Supplies nearly all programming for the CS channel "KMPチャンネル" (942ch) run by Sky PerfecTV Broadcasting.
- Signed an exclusive contract with 成瀬心美 in September 2012 (universe-plan exclusive).
- Launched the monthly streaming service "Aver" in September 2020; it ended in April 2021.
- In 2025–2026 pushed the exclusive conversion of popular non-exclusive actresses: 乙アリス (August 2025), 弥生みづき (October 2025), 尾崎えりか (October 2025) and 沙月恵奈 (January 2026) became million exclusives in turn.
