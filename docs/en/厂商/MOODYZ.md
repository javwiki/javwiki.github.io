## History and group relationships

MOODYZ began in **September 2000**. Historical accounts describe it as a general production brand reorganized from Hokuto's “Mr. President,” predating the incorporation of the current WILL entity.[CA brand history (secondary source)](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/CA_(アダルトビデオ))

MOODYZ and [S1](S1%20NO.1%20STYLE.md) are different brands in the same production lineage; MOODYZ should not be described as S1's parent company. See [WILL](WILL.md) for succession from Hokuto and CA and the historical production and distribution relationships.
