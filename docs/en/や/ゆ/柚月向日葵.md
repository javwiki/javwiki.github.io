## Introduction

柚月向日葵 (柚月ひまわり) is a former Japanese AV actress. In November 2016 she debuted as an IdeaPocket newcomer exclusive; early in her career she led with a "natural beauty" image, drawing attention for her refined looks and voluptuous figure. Her career was concentrated between 2016 and 2019, passing through three stages: an IdeaPocket exclusive, an Attackers exclusive and a セレブの友 exclusive.

In July 2018 she moved to Attackers, and that October moved again to セレブの友. In March 2019 she announced her retirement through her personal social account, ending her actress activity.
