## Introduction

八木奈奈 is an active Japanese AV actress known for her literary talent and acting. She debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive in December 2019 and was praised as "a pure, innocent beauty — a once-in-a-decade star candidate". She is famous for an astonishing volume of reading (200 books a year, 3,000 at home) and excellent acting, while also actively developing her entertainment career with several TV dramas and stage works. In 2023 she formed the idol unit Mi LUNA from お月ちゃんのうた with Nana Miho and Kaede Fuyutsuki.
