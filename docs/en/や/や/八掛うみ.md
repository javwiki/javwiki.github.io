## Introduction

八掛うみ is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as a Prestige exclusive in November 2020, known for overwhelming transparency and a sweet smile. She has a small face rare in the industry (a face length of 18.7cm) and lost about 8kg before her debut. Her surname is often misread as 「はちがけ」; the correct reading is 「やつがけ」. In 2021 she revealed in 週刊 Playboy the A-cup figure she had hidden out of a long-standing complex.
