## Introduction

弥生みづき is a Japanese AV actress who debuted in August 2019. She transformed from a slim-type actress into a voluptuous type, gaining popularity with teasing titles as her core charm. She has repeatedly reached the top of the FANZA charts and won the Best Actress award at the アサ芸AV大賞 2023. In 2025 she became a million exclusive.

## Personal notes

- From Kitakyushu, Fukuoka Prefecture. Interests: sleeping and eating; speciality: doing a handstand.
- Debuted with the identity of an "お嬢様大学生 (lady college student)", but at the time she was actually a NEET.
- From the summer of 2021 she rebuilt her body through training, transforming from a slim type into a voluptuous type.
- Her creed for teasing titles: "the most important thing is that I enjoy it myself".
- The actor he admires is Yuto Kuroda; her debut title co-starred with him.

## Work features

- Centred on teasing titles; her popularity surged after transforming from slim to voluptuous.
- After debuting in 2019 she was active across MOODYZ, 妄想族, Fitch, Kira☆kira, ダスッ!, TMA and other labels.
- Repeatedly reached the top of FANZA monthly/annual charts: 4th on the first-half AV actress ranking and 8th on the annual AV actress ranking in 2023.
- Strong at teasing and subjugation titles.
