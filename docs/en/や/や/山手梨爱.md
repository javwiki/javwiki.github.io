## Introduction

山手梨愛 is a Japanese AV actress and former nude model from Fukuoka Prefecture. Originally a nude model living in Fukuoka, she was discovered by Kodansha's FLASH editorial department and debuted in gravure in October 2021. The following November she made her AV debut as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive; with a tall 170cm figure and an I-cup bust she is called "九州No.1 Body", and her debut title reached 9th place on the annual sales chart.
