## Introduction

吉川爱美 (Japanese: 吉川あいみ, born 20 March 1994) is a former Japanese AV actress and former gravure idol known for an H-cup bust. From Kanagawa Prefecture, her breasts began developing in elementary school and had reached an H cup by graduation from middle school. She debuted with a gravure DVD in 2012 and made her AV debut in January 2013; that year she received the "優秀女優賞" and the "一万本達成賞" at the 64th SOD Awards. In November 2018 she announced that she would formally retire on 31 December of that year. In 2022 she changed careers to become a hostess.
