## Introduction

吉泽明步 is one of the most representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She debuted in 2003 and quickly rose to the top of the industry with her pure looks and excellent acting. Her "classic beauty" image struck deep, and her releases are known for high quality and much loved by fans. Akiho Yoshizawa is one of the few actresses in the industry to keep high popularity over a long period: her career spanned 16 years, with over 300 AV titles and a rough total output value estimated as high as ¥10 billion. She was a member of 惠比壽麝香葡萄. She formally retired in March 2019, but her influence is still widely discussed today.
