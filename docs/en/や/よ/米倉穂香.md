## Introduction

米倉穂香 is a Japanese AV actress. She made her AV debut in August 2018 as KANBi's first exclusive actress, at the age of 34. After three titles she took a hiatus for pregnancy and childbirth. In October 2019 she returned as a KANBi exclusive. In April 2021 she moved to a Madonna exclusive, and that December left the exclusive roster and became a kikaku-tantai actress.

## Personal notes

- From Nagano Prefecture. Her debut title used the setting of a "bride of Kobe", but she is not actually from Kobe (a title's persona does not represent real information).
- Interest: being with her cat; speciality: squirting masturbation (per Japanese Wikipedia).
- Before her debut she was a housewife and debuted in AV as a married woman.
- During her hiatus she went through pregnancy and childbirth.
- After returning she drew attention with breast-milk-themed titles.

## Work features

- Known for a distinctive married-woman / mature-woman positioning; KANBi's first exclusive actress.
