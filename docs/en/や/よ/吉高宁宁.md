## Introduction

吉高宁宁 is an active Japanese AV actress known as a "顔面国宝 (national treasure of a face)" and a former gravure idol. She debuted as a gravure idol in April 2017 and made her AV debut as an S1 exclusive that September; her debut title won three crowns at AV OPEN 2017: the overall division, the actress division and the fan-vote title award. She moved to a FALENO star exclusive in February 2020 and has remained one ever since. Her stage name comes from her resemblance to 吉高由里子 and 大塚寧々.
