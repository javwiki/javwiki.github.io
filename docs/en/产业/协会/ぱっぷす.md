## Overview

ぱっぷす (People Against Pornography and Sexual Exploitation Survivors) is a Japanese non-profit organization (NPO) founded in 2009 that provides consultation and support specifically to victims of the pornography industry, and conducts research on pornography-related issues, makes policy proposals and similar activities.

## History

### Background

In 2007 the book 「ひとはみな、ハダカになる。」 by AV actress and director バクシーシ山下 was published and drew public attention. In 2008 田口道子 and other people connected to women's protection facilities protested against the book, and came to recognise that "this is not a problem of one publisher, but society's tolerance for sexual violence against women and children". On 1 May 2009 「ポルノ被害と性暴力を考える会」 was formally founded by people from women's protection facilities, researchers on pornography issues and civic activists; at first its activities focused on awareness-raising.

### How the work grew

- **2012**: Started accepting consultations on sexual exploitation (only 1–2 cases at first)
- **October**: Opened consultations in response to appeals from victims of coerced AV performances
- **2014**: Consultation support formalised
- **2017**: Reorganized as a specified non-profit corporation (NPO)
- **2020**: Formally renamed ぱっぷす (PAPS)
- **2021**: Opened the "Center for Support of Victims of Digital Sexual Violence"
- **2022**: Paid staff increased to 25
- **2025**: Around 200 consultations per month; about 70,000 image-deletion requests

## Main activities

### Consultation support

- **Telephone consultation**: Free consultation for victims of the pornography industry
- **Lawyer referral**: Referring victims who need legal help to specialist lawyers
- **Psychological support**: Providing counselling services

### Image deletion support

- Supporting deletion for victims whose images were published without consent
- Sending deletion requests directly to site operators
- Received a social-welfare promotion grant from the Welfare Medical Care Agency (WAM) for FY2019–FY2022

### Policy proposals

- Proposing government policy on the pornography industry
- Taking part in the drafting process of the AV出演被害防止・救済法
- Pushing for protective measures to accompany the Civil Code revision (lowering the age of majority to 18)

### Academic research

- Conducting research on the pornography industry
- Holding regular academic seminars and public lectures
- Publishing survey reports and statistics

### International cooperation

- Cooperating with international anti-pornography-violence organizations
- Taking part in international human-rights activities
- Submitting reports to the United Nations and other international bodies

- Digital sexual violence is taking on an international character
- Criminal networks use social media to target teenagers in Japan and abroad
