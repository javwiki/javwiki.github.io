## History and publishing relationships

KANBi has its own brand name and official website, while its product releases are connected to [Prestige](../制作公司/Prestige.md). A retailer record for the 2019 compilation KPB-002 uses the KANBi brand and lists Prestige as the manufacturer.[KANBi official website](https://www.kanbi-av.com/), [KPB-002 product record](https://www.suruga-ya.jp/product/detail/131932638)

A brand launch, a first release and company incorporation are different events. Available sources do not establish KANBi as a separate legal company, so this article treats it as a brand. The brand launch year awaits verifiable early sources.

See [production companies and group relationships](../../专题/制作公司与集团.md) for the distinction between publishers and brand relationships.
