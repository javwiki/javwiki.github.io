## History and group relationships

- **1998**: Its history reaches back to a brand within Attackers and the “ANGEL” series.
- **From 2002**: It operated as a distinct in-house manufacturer within Hokuto. “Independent” here describes the brand and production organization, not departure from the group.
- **April 2005**: It began issuing rental-market products; that October, the Hokuto network expanded rental distribution through TIS.

These milestones follow [IdeaPocket history (secondary source)](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/アイデアポケット). IdeaPocket and [Attackers](Attackers.md) share both an early brand origin and a later place in the [WILL](../制作公司/WILL.md) production lineage. The initial origin should be distinguished from the present brand identity.
