## History and group relationships

S1 launched in **November 2004** within Hokuto's production structure, later associated with CA / WILL. This is a brand launch date, not WILL's incorporation date.[CA brand history (secondary source)](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/CA_(アダルトビデオ))

S1 shares a production lineage with [MOODYZ](MOODYZ.md), [IdeaPocket](IdeaPocket.md) and [Attackers](Attackers.md), each with its own name and editorial identity. See [WILL](../制作公司/WILL.md) for business succession and historical DMM connections. A separate “manufacturer” listing on a sales platform does not establish a separate legal company.
