## History and group relationships

Historical accounts date Attackers' establishment to **December 1996**, its first releases to **February 1997**, and the launch of IdeaPocket-lineage releases to **December 1998**. Originally an independent manufacturer, it entered Hokuto's structure in the early 2000s, after which Attackers and IdeaPocket operated as distinct in-house brands.[Attackers history (secondary source)](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/アタッカーズ)

Acquisition and the start of product handling are different milestones: the Attackers article records a 2001 acquisition, while CA's manufacturer table records handling from 2002. This article uses “early 2000s” without combining these dates into an unverified legal transaction date.[CA manufacturer information (secondary source)](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/CA_(アダルトビデオ))

[IdeaPocket](IdeaPocket.md) originated as an early Attackers brand; both later belonged to the [WILL](../制作公司/WILL.md) production lineage. This history does not establish that Attackers currently holds shares in IdeaPocket.
