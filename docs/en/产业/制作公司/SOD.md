# SOD (Soft On Demand)

SOD can refer to the group, the sales company or a production brand. **Soft On Demand and SOD Create are separate legal companies** with different incorporation dates.[Group company information](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/about_us/info/)

## History

| Date | Event | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November 1995 | Soft On Demand Co., Ltd. was incorporated. | [Company information](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/about_us/info/) |
| March 1999 | SOD Create Co., Ltd. was incorporated. The official website describes the 1999 division of work as strengthening planning and production, while Soft On Demand took responsibility for sales and logistics. | [Company information](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/about_us/info/), [Sales business](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/business/softondemand/) |
| January 2004 | SOD Artworks Co., Ltd., responsible for video editing, was incorporated. | [Company information](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/about_us/info/) |
| October 2022 | SOD Corporate Co., Ltd., providing back-office outsourcing, was incorporated. | [Company information](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/about_us/info/) |

## Companies and their roles

| Name | Level | Main role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft On Demand | Company / distribution and sales | Connects manufacturers with retailers and digital distributors; handles sales and logistics. |
| SOD Create | Company / production | Content planning, video production and brand development within the group. |
| SOD Artworks | Company / editing | Video editing. |
| SOD Corporate | Company / administration | Back-office outsourcing. |

The companies and roles above come from [company information](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/about_us/info/), [sales business](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/business/softondemand/) and [production business](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/business/sodcreate/). They describe the officially listed group companies and division of work, without inferring ownership percentages.

## Meaning of “group manufacturers”

The official business description uses “group manufacturers” when describing its distribution network. This should be distinguished from the legal companies listed on the company information page. SOD distributing a manufacturer's products does not by itself establish that manufacturer as a wholly owned subsidiary.[Group business description](https://corporate.sod.co.jp/business/)

Sources checked: 2026-10-09. Dates follow the current group website. References in older sources to a spin-off month or former company name should identify the type of event and not be confused with incorporation dates.
