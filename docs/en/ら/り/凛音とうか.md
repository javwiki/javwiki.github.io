## Introduction

凛音とうか is a former Japanese AV actress born on 23 October 1990 in Chiba Prefecture, affiliated with the LINX agency. She debuted as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive in February 2018; her debut title was 《新人 NO.1STYLE ドリームIcupボディ 凛音とうか 27歳－結婚直前－決意 AVデビュー》 (SSNI-140). That August she left the exclusive roster and became a kikaku-tantai actress, appearing in a large number of titles for ワープエンタテインメント, MOODYZ, 本中, Fitch, マドンナ, OPPAI, E-BODY and many other labels. She is characterised by a model-height 165cm figure with an I-cup bust. In June 2020 she announced that she would leave LINX on 9 July and temporarily suspend activity (not a retirement); from July 2021 her releases increased and she de facto restarted activity; on 15 March 2022 she formally retired with the retirement title 《引退 凛音とうか THE FINAL》. She was also active on ラグジュTV as 「東雲秋菜」.
