## Summary

People Alliance Polnogramy and Sexual Exploitation Survivors, a Japanese non-profit organization founded in 2009, provides counselling and support to victims of the sex industry and conducts research, policy advocacy and other activities related to pornography.

## History

### Background

In 2007, the director of the AV Women ' s Association, the author of the book " The Heart, the Heart and the Heart " , was a woman. The publication of the publication has generated social attention. In 2008, women such as Takuko protested the book, and it was recognized that "it is not a single publishing house issue, but a society-wide tolerance of sexual violence against women and children". On 1 May 2009, the Women's Association for the Protection of Women's Facilities, Researchers on Pornography, Citizens' Activists and others officially established the "Counsel of Sexual Violence against Women in the Case of the Crime of Rogue, " which was mainly an initial event.
