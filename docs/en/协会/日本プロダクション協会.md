## Overview

The 日本プロダクション協会 (below, "JPG") is an industry group founded in April 2017 by a group of volunteer AV agencies (entertainment productions). Spurred by the public debate over coerced AV performances and the 2016 「マークスジャパン事件」, it aims at the sound operation of AV agencies.

## How it was founded

In June 2016 マークスジャパン was prosecuted for violating the Worker Dispatch Act; the judgment made clear that AV production companies dispatching actresses to harmful and dangerous work (AV performances) was against the law. The case greatly raised the AV industry's attention to legal compliance.

At the same time, coerced AV performances gradually became a social problem after August 2016, and the industry urgently needed self-regulation. Against this background, 21 AV agencies jointly established the 日本プロダクション協会 in April 2017.

## Main activities

### Contract standardization

- Drafting the JPG standard contract based on the unified contract form produced by the AV人権倫理機構
- Making contract terms explicit so performers fully understand them

### Compliance

- Setting industry compliance standards
- Pushing member companies to observe laws and regulations
- Giving member companies compliance guidance

### Work-consignment structure

- Promoting a work-consignment relationship between AV agencies and actresses
- Making clear the two sides are equals, not an employment hierarchy
- Banning unfair penalty clauses and coerced appearances

### Making interviews and contracts visible

- To eradicate coercion, interviews and contract signing are in principle recorded
- Ensuring the contracting process is transparent and traceable

## Stance on the AV New Law

Regarding the AV出演被害防止・救済法 (AV New Law) in force from 2022, JPG holds that:

- The law is partly meaningful
- But there are points that need improvement
- According to a 2022 survey (441 people in the industry), 37% answered their income had fallen by more than 50%, and 7.8% reported income of zero

## Criticism and controversies

### Impact on regular AV

One view is that the AV New Law places unnecessary burdens on normally operating AV production companies, particularly that visibility requirements such as recording contracts raise running costs.

### Regulation of non-standard AV

Another view is that the AV New Law has limited effect on non-standard AV (amateur shoots, doujin AV etc.) and cannot fundamentally solve the problem of coercion.

### Doubts about the effect

Some in the industry believe the AV New Law may shrink the industry and instead increase the risk to victims.
