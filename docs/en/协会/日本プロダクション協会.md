## Summary

The Japan Association of the Japanese Confederation of the Free Trade Unions (JPG) is an industry group founded by the dedicated AV Broker (Hinnam) in April 2017. The socialization of the issue of the AV's performance and the 2016 "Silent Incident" are opportunities to promote the sound operation of the AV Brokers.

## Founded at latitude

In June 2016, Zheng was prosecuted for violating the law on the dispatch of women, and the judgement clearly states that the AV Production Company has violated the law by sending women to hazardous industries (AV shows). This case has led to a significant increase in the importance attached by the AV industry to legal compliance.

At the same time, the issue of the AV ' s performance became a social issue after August 2016, and there is an urgent need for industry to regulate itself. In this context, 21 AV Brokers co-founded the Japan Association of Trade Unions in April 2017.

## Criticism and controversy

### Impact on regular AV

The view was expressed that the new AV law placed an unnecessary burden on the functioning of the AV production company, particularly with regard to the increased operating costs of visualization requirements such as contract recording.

### Regulation of informal AVs

It was noted that the new AV Act had limited regulatory effect on informal AVs (personal filming, peer AV, etc.) and was difficult to address the root causes of the issue of strength.

### Effect challenge

Some practitioners believe that the new AV law may lead to a contraction of the industry, but instead increase the risk for victims.
