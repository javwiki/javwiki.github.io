# Adult Broadcasting Awards (スカパー!アダルト放送大賞)

The Sky PerfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards began in 2005 and recognise Japan’s adult satellite-broadcasting sector. Historical individual awards, such as Best Actress and Best Newcomer, are recorded separately from selection for EX Girls, a promotional unit for services including SPOOX EX.

## Award categories

### Individual awards (2006–2020)

| Award | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Best Actress | The year's best AV actress |
| Best Newcomer | The year's best new actress |
| Best Mature Actress | The year's best mature-genre actress |
| Most Prolific Actress | The actress with the most releases that year |
| Media Award | Awards presented by individual media outlets |
| スカパー!オンデマンドアダルト賞 | Best actress in the on-demand category |

## EX Girls (verified records for 2022–2024)

After winning MVA in 2022, Ai Hongo joined Yui Hatano and Yu Shinoda in the promotional unit. The organiser redesigned the 2023 selection as an audition, with judging and fan voting determining one centre and two members. A 2024 report identifies four third-generation members and their introduction event on 26 June. Membership is distinct from the historical Best Actress award. [2022 report](https://www.nikkansports.com/entertainment/news/202203140000428.html), [2023 official report](https://note.com/sptv_note/n/n34b625094de0), [2024 event report](https://www.menscyzo.com/2024/05/post_227377.html)
