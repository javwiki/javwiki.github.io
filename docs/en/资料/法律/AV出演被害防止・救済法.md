# AV出演被害防止・救済法

## Overview

In force since 23 June 2022, the Act regulates performance contracts for legally defined sexual-act recordings and protects performers regardless of sex or age. Applicability depends on Article 2 and the contract circumstances, rather than distribution labels.

## Producer obligations

- Provide contract and explanatory documents and explain the required matters.
- Wait at least one month after providing the documents before filming; performers may refuse filming or unwanted acts.
- Wait at least four months after all filming ends before publication and allow performers to review the recording first.

## Termination and stopping publication

Performers can also terminate unconditionally before filming and between filming and publication. Post-publication periods are listed below. This is a statutory right to terminate the contract.

| Contract date | Unconditional termination after publication |
| --- | --- |
| 2022-06-23–2024-06-22 | Within two years of publication |
| 2024-06-23–2025-06-22 | One year from publication or 2026-12-22, whichever is later |
| From 2025-06-23 | Within one year of publication |

Contracts concluded before 23 June 2022 are outside the Act’s cancellation and termination provisions, although the Cabinet Office explains that an injunction against publication remains possible. Publication without a contract or after cancellation or termination may be subject to demands to stop sale or distribution. Each remedy has its own conditions.

## Penalties

Penalties cover specified conduct, including deceptive or intimidating obstruction of termination and failures to provide required documents or truthful information. They do not impose one uniform penalty for every breach. Chapter V penalties took effect on 12 July 2022.

## Consultation and support

`#8891` is Japan’s nationwide short code for one-stop sexual-crime and sexual-violence support centres, which also handle AV-related harm. It is not an AV-only hotline. The Cabinet Office provides termination notice templates and information on stopping distribution.
