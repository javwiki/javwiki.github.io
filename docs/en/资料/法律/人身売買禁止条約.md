# 人身売買禁止条約

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Official name | 人身売買及び他人の売春からの搾取の禁止に関する条約 |
| UN General Assembly adoption | 1949-12-02 |
| International entry into force | 1951-07-25 |
| Japan’s deposit of accession | 1958-05-01 |

## Overview

The Convention addresses procuring others for prostitution, exploiting their prostitution, and maintaining or financing brothels. The UN Treaty Collection records Japan’s accession deposit on 1 May 1958. This date is distinct from international entry into force.

## Relevance to the AV industry

This is distinct from the Trafficking in Persons Protocol adopted in 2000, also called the Palermo Protocol. Japan deposited its acceptance of that Protocol on 11 July 2017, according to MOFA. Allegations of deception or coercion in AV require assessment under applicable domestic law; industry participation alone does not establish trafficking.
