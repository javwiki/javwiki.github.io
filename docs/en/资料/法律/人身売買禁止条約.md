# 人身売買禁止条約

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Official name | 人身売買及び他人の売春からの搾取の禁止に関する条約 |
| Common name | 人身売買禁止条約, パルermo条約 |
| Ratification by Japan | 25 January 2004 |

## Overview

This international convention aims to ban human trafficking and sexual exploitation and to protect victims. As a contracting state, Japan is obligated to take measures to prevent trafficking and protect victims.

## Main provisions

### Prohibited acts

- Human trafficking (recruitment, transport or transfer for the purpose of exploitation)
- Sexual exploitation
- Forced labour
- Enslavement

### Obligations of contracting states

- Criminalise human trafficking
- Protect victims
- Prevent trafficking
- International cooperation

## Relevance to the AV industry

The AV industry contains conduct that forces others to perform through deception and coercion, which may constitute human trafficking. The convention provides an basis in international law for preventing exploitative conduct in the AV industry.
