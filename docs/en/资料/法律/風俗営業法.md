## Overview

The Act Regulating Business Offering Specific Adult Entertainment regulates the adult-entertainment trade in Japan, including the licensing system for AV production and distribution, restrictions on business premises, advertising rules and more.

## Main provisions

### Licensing

- AV production and sales require a licence from the local Public Safety Commission
- Certain equipment and premises standards must be met
- The licence is renewed annually

### Business restrictions

- Restrictions on business hours
- Restrictions on business premises
- Restrictions on advertising

### Advertising rules

- Exaggerated and false advertising is prohibited
- Required information must be displayed
- Advertising to minors is prohibited

## Relevance to the AV industry

AV production and sales fall under the adult-entertainment trade and must observe the law's rules on licensing, business operations, advertising and more.
