# 風俗営業法

## Overview

The Act regulates specified entertainment and sexual-service businesses through licensing, notification, location restrictions and protections for minors. The original law took effect in 1948 and has been amended repeatedly. Article 2 distinguishes business categories, including premises-based, non-premises-based and image-transmission sexual businesses.

## Relevance to the AV industry

It does not establish a blanket entertainment-business licence for all AV production and distribution. Article 2(7)–(8), Article 31-2 and Article 31-7 address relevant definitions and notifications for adult-media sales, rentals and image transmission; applicability depends on the actual business category.
