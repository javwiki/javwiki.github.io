# 職業安定法

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Official name | 職業安定法 |
| Statute number | 昭和22年法律第141号 |
| In force | 15 October 1947 |
| Administered by | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare |

## Overview

The Employment Security Act regulates employment services such as job placement and worker dispatch, protecting workers' lawful rights and interests.

## Main provisions

### Job placement

- Job placement requires a licence from the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare or a prefectural governor
- Improper job-placement conduct is prohibited
- Job seekers' privacy is protected

### Worker protection

- Forced labour is prohibited
- Unfair dismissal is prohibited
- Workers' lawful rights and interests are protected

## Relevance to the AV industry

AV production involves protecting the rights of workers (performers and staff). As workers, performers enjoy the protections this act provides, including the ban on forced labour and privacy protection. When an AV producer violates the act, performers can assert their rights by law.
