# 職業安定法

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Official name | 職業安定法 |
| Law number | 昭和22年法律第141号 |
| Entry into force | 1947-12-01 |

## Overview

The Act regulates job placement, recruitment, recruitment information and labour supply. Job placement differs from worker dispatch, which is governed separately by the Worker Dispatching Act. Article 44 generally prohibits labour supply, Article 45 provides an exception, and Article 63 addresses forced placement and placement in harmful work.

## Relevance to the AV industry

Whether an agency’s recruitment or placement violates this Act depends on the business, recruitment process and actual work. The agency’s name or a contract label alone cannot establish legality.
