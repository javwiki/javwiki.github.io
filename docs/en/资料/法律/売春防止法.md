# 売春防止法

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |

| Law number | 昭和31年法律第118号 |
| Entry into force | 1957-04-01 |
| Full commencement | 1958-04-01 (Chapter II and related provisions) |

## Overview

Article 2 defines prostitution as intercourse with an unspecified person for payment or its promise. Article 3 prohibits prostitution and being its counterpart but does not itself impose a direct penalty for that conduct alone. Chapter II penalizes specified conduct such as solicitation, procurement, coercion and management.

## Relevance to the AV industry

Payment for sexual activity alone does not establish prostitution under this definition, and filming under an AV label does not establish legality. Assessment requires the elements in Article 2, actual conduct and other applicable laws.
