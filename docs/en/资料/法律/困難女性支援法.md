# 困難女性支援法

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Official name | 困難な問題を抱える女性への支援に関する法律 |
| Law number | 令和4年法律第52号 |
| Entry into force | 2024-04-01 |

## Overview

The Act establishes coordinated support based on women’s welfare, human rights and gender equality. Article 2 covers women facing difficulties in daily or social life because of sexual harm, family circumstances or community relationships, including those at risk of such difficulties.

## Relevance to the AV industry

Sexual harm, violence or hardship associated with performing may prompt a request for support. Women’s consultation support centres coordinate assistance according to individual needs; performing history alone does not establish entitlement to a particular benefit.
