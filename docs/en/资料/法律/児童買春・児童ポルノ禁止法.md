# 児童買春・児童ポルノ禁止法

| Entry into force | 1999-11-01 |

## Overview

Article 2 defines a child as a person under 18. The Act regulates and penalizes child prostitution and pornography involving real children, and provides protective measures. Production, provision and possession for satisfying personal sexual curiosity have distinct statutory requirements.

## Relevance to the AV industry

The Civil Code’s lower age of majority does not change the under-18 threshold here. A title, fictional character’s age or costume alone does not prove the performer’s actual age; assessment depends on the real subject and recorded content.
