# Laws

This directory collects the Japanese laws and regulations related to the AV industry, providing legal background for the encyclopedia's entries.

## Legal entries

| Law | Common name | In force since | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [AV出演被害防止・救済法](AV出演被害防止・救済法.md) | AV New Law | 23 June 2022 | The first law dedicated to preventing and relieving harm from AV performances |
| [民法](民法.md) | Civil Code | 16 July 1898 | The 2018 amendment, effective in 2022, lowered majority to 18 |
| [売春防止法](売春防止法.md) | Anti-Prostitution Act | 1 April 1957 | Bans prostitution and related conduct |
| [児童買春・児童ポルノ禁止法](児童買春・児童ポルノ禁止法.md) | Anti-Child-Prostitution Act | 1 November 1999 | Protects children from sexual exploitation |
| [困難女性支援法](困難女性支援法.md) | Support Act for Women in Difficulty | 1 April 2024 | Comprehensive support for women facing various hardships |
| [風俗営業法](風俗営業法.md) | Adult Entertainment Control Act | 1 September 1948 | Regulates the adult-entertainment trade |
| [職業安定法](職業安定法.md) | Employment Security Act | 1 December 1947 | Regulates job placement and employment services |
| [労働者派遣法](労働者派遣法.md) | Worker Dispatch Act | 1 July 1986 | Regulates the dispatching of workers |
| [人身売買禁止条約](人身売買禁止条約.md) | Convention against human trafficking | 25 July 1951 (international) | An international convention banning the trade in persons |
| [リベンジポルノ防止法](リベンジポルノ防止法.md) | Revenge-porn prevention law | 27 November 2014 | Bans non-consensual distribution of intimate images |

Dates distinguish original commencement, phased commencement and international treaty entry into force; they are not necessarily the commencement dates of every current provision. Chapter II and related provisions of the Anti-Prostitution Act took effect on 1 April 1958, and the private sexual images law also had phased commencement. Individual entries link to statutory texts and official explanations.
