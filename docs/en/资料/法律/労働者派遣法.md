# 労働者派遣法

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |

| Statute number | 昭和60年法律第88号 |
| In force | 1 July 1986 |
| Administered by | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare |

## Overview

The Worker Dispatch Act regulates the dispatching of workers, protects dispatched workers' lawful rights and interests, and sets out the obligations of dispatch agencies and receiving employers.

## Main provisions

### Dispatch licence

- Worker dispatching requires a licence from the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare
- Certain qualification requirements must be met

### Worker protection

- Improper dispatch is prohibited
- Dispatched workers' wages and benefits are protected
- Discriminatory treatment is prohibited

### Obligations of the receiving employer

- Duties on occupational safety and health towards dispatched workers
- Forced labour is prohibited
- Workers' lawful rights and interests must be safeguarded

## Relevance to the AV industry

AV performers often take part in productions as "models" or "actors", and their relationship with the production company may constitute worker dispatch. The act protects AV performers as workers, banning improper dispatch and forced labour.
