# 労働者派遣法

## Basic information

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Official name | 労働者派遣事業の適正な運営の確保及び派遣労働者の保護等に関する法律 |
| Law number | 昭和60年法律第88号 |
| Entry into force | 1986-07-01 |

## Overview

Worker dispatch generally means directing one’s employee to work under another party’s instructions while maintaining the employment relationship. The Act regulates dispatch businesses and protects dispatched workers; applicability depends on actual employment and supervision arrangements.

## Relevance to the AV industry

A performance contract, management agreement or “model” label does not automatically establish worker dispatch. Identify the employer and supervising party before assessing this Act and the Employment Security Act.
