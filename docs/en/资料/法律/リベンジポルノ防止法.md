# リベンジポルノ防止法

| Phased commencement | Article 3 penalties: 2014-12-17; Article 4: 2014-12-27 |

## Overview

The Act criminalizes public distribution or display of qualifying private sexual images in ways that identify the subject, and provision for that purpose. It also provides measures related to online removal. Its definition does not encompass every image shared without permission.

## Relevance to the AV industry

The NPA excludes images recorded with the subject’s permission and knowledge that others would view them, such as AV and glamour photography. Performers’ private images require assessment of the recording circumstances; disputes over commercial releases also require checking the AV Act and other applicable rules.
