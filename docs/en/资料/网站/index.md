# Forums and subtitle-sharing platforms

This page lists third-party forums and subtitle sites focused on JAV and explains what each one is for, what it offers and what access requires. Every site listed is run independently and has no affiliation with this encyclopedia; this site does not host their content and cannot guarantee that links or interfaces stay unchanged.

Checked on 26 November 2026. Interfaces and permissions change without notice, so the descriptions below reflect the public pages as they appeared on that date.

## JAV forums

### JAV-Forum.com

An English-language community for JAV and gravure idols; its front page describes itself as “Discuss JAV & Asian Babes”.

- Discussion: JAV Chat under General covers releases, actresses and industry topics; there are also Japanese Amateur and Sexy Girls (gravure) sections.
- Subtitles: the JAV Subtitles board covers English subtitles for JAV and adult animation and is labelled “(No AI)”, meaning machine-translated subtitles are not accepted.
- Downloads: Download Area offers direct downloads of Japanese adult and gravure videos, mostly posted by users.
- Access: discussion threads are usually readable as a guest; download links, attachments and some subtitle threads require an account, and the free-subtitle area has additional conditions.

### Akiba-Online.com

A long-running English-language otaku and adult community that calls itself “Neo Otaku Community”; besides JAV it has general chat and subculture boards.

- 18+ Discussion: adult topics and JAV-related discussion.
- Torrent Station: video sharing through BitTorrent.
- Direct Downloads: links through file-hosting services (OCHs).
- P2P, Request + Trade, Club Forums: other transfer protocols, requests and trades, and member group projects respectively.
- Access: some boards and downloads are hidden from guests and require registration; membership level and site rules determine what is visible.

## Subtitle platforms

### JAV Subtitles (javzimu.com)

A Chinese-language subtitle search site for JAV. It searches subtitles by product code and says it covers studios such as FC2, S1 and Moodyz, offering SRT and ASS downloads without registration.

- Use: enter a product code on the home page, open the matching search page and choose a subtitle entry; a CAPTCHA may appear before download.
- Scope: subtitle files only, not video; the source and proofreading status of each subtitle are not labelled.
- Funding: free access supported by third-party advertising; the page loads several ad scripts, so pop-ups or redirects can occur.

### Subtitle Cat (subtitlecat.com)

A multilingual subtitle aggregator and translation service for film and television in general, not JAV specifically; it searches by title, file name or product code.

- Library: the home page reports more than 17 million subtitles, with each entry labelled by language, size, download count and translation source.
- Translation: upload an SRT and machine-translate it into 100+ languages, or download files uploaded or translated by others.
- Account: browsing and downloading need no login; registering allows uploads and management.
- JAV coverage: results depend on whether someone uploaded a matching subtitle; one product code may return several entries, such as the original and machine-translated versions, which users must compare themselves.

## Caveats

- Rights: download boards, file-hoster links and subtitles are largely user-uploaded, and sites usually do not state their rights status. Downloading and redistributing may raise copyright issues; legality depends on the licence, local law and the site's terms.
- Law and region: Japan and the user's country differ in rules on adult content and copyright, and some networks or regions cannot reach these sites.
- Accounts and privacy: registration usually needs an email address. Use a dedicated address and a distinct password, and do not reuse credentials for important accounts.
- Safety: such sites carry many ads and redirects; downloading executables, players or purported “codecs” is risky, and subtitle files should come from a source you trust.
- Accuracy: subtitles are third-party translations, so names, terminology and tone may not match this encyclopedia; cite release pages and official material when stating facts about a work.
- Age: all of these sites involve adult content and normally require users to be 18 or the local age of majority.
