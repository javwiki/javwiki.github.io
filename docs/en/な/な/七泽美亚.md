## Introduction

七泽美亚 is an active Japanese AV actress known for a petite figure and a cute image. In November 2017 she debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive as a "current 18-year-old university student", with the debut slogan "全AVファンの妹分 (the little sister of all AV fans)". Loved by fans for her petite 145cm frame and black-haired, pure image, she is called "可爱制霸 (cuteness domination)" in Chinese-speaking markets. In 2019 she was nominated for the Best Actress award at the FANZA Adult Awards, and placed 13th in the "active AV actress SEXY general election" in 2024.
