## Introduction

Nanao Nakano (なかの なお, born 18 October 1980) is a Japanese AV actress and former gravure idol. Her former stage name was 永瀬ゆみ. Since 2008 she had worked as a big-breasted gravure idol under FRESH PROMOTION, while also serving as a photo-shoot model and as an event companion at car shows and motor shows around the Kansai region. In April 2010 she joined the idol group [恵比寿★マスカッツ](../../组合/惠比寿★麝香葡萄.md) as a 4th-generation member, the oldest in the group. She left in May 2011 and retired from gravure within the same year, afterwards working as a photo-shoot model and a make-up artist. She debuted in AV in July 2019 under 溜池ゴロー as 「中野七緒」, became an OPPAI exclusive in July 2020, and left her exclusive status in January 2021 to work as a kikaku-tantai actress.

## Work features

- Known for an H-cup (95cm) bust on a small 151cm frame, and marketed on her 「巨乳」 from her gravure days onward.
- Her subjects centre on mature and married-woman themes; her 2019 debut title was billed as 「元・芸能人 Hcupスレンダー巨乳人妻」.
- After becoming kikaku-tantai in 2021 she appeared for many labels beyond 溜池ゴロー and OPPAI, including VENUS, U&K, Global Media Entertainment and プレステージ.
- She has used many aliases: 七々緒, ななお, なおこ, あみ, なお, なな, 英子, 奈々子, 椎名寛子 and others.
- She has been featured in numerous compilation and best-of releases, such as 溜池ゴロー's 「歴代溜池ゴロー超厳選爆乳熟女BEST20人」 and OPPAI's 「中野七緒 8時間BEST」.

## Lawsuit

In 2014 she sued the production company Kingdom for 3 million yen in damages over an image work released in 2011, on the grounds that she had been filmed 「勝手にUO映され、耐え難い精神的苦痛を被った」. The company lost the case and was ordered to pay 2.2 million yen.
