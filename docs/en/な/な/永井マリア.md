## Introduction

永井マリア is a Japanese AV actress from Aichi Prefecture, of Filipino and Spanish descent. After graduating from high school she debuted in the AV world under names such as 「柏木胡桃」; after a hiatus she made her comeback at Madonna as 「永井マリア」 in 2019. Characterised by an L-cup bust (120cm) and 125cm hips in a voluptuous figure, she is active across Madonna, MOODYZ, Fitch, ダスッ! and many other labels.
