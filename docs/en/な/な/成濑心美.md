## Introduction

成濑心美 is one of the most representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She debuted in 2008 and quickly rose to the top of the industry with her lolicon looks and cute air. Her "lolicon" image struck deep, and her releases are known for high quality and much loved by fans. At only 147cm tall she is one of the most petite actresses in the industry. Her hobbies are music and karaoke; her specialities are cooking and drawing. In 2012 she won the Actress Award at the Adult Broadcasting Awards, and the same year she was invited to Taiwan with 波多野结衣 and 藤北彩香 for the second Taiwan Adult Expo.
