## Introduction

七海ひな is a popular Japanese AV actress. In April 2019 she debuted under プレステージ as 「七瀬ひな」. Before her debut she worked as a dental hygienist, then changed careers to become a hostess, and then resolved to become an AV actress because she looked so much like Kirara Asuka. In her debut year she took both the overall and newcomer championships of 「このAV女優がすごい!2019年夏」 hosted by 月刊FANZA, quickly rising as one of the year's most talked-about newcomers. She moved to ライフプロモーション and renamed herself 七海ひな in May 2020, joined T-POWERS that October, and has worked with a personal agency since August 2022.
