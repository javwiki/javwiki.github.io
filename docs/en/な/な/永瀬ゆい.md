## Introduction

永瀬ゆい is a former Japanese AV actress who debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive in May 2019. Her former-idol career (バクステ外神田一丁目) became a topic of conversation, and she gained popularity with lolicon looks and a cute air. In December 2019 she became famous as the actress of the industry's first 「天井特化アングル (ceiling-specialised angle)」 title. She announced her retirement in September 2021 and left the AV world that December. After retiring she opened the YouTube channel 「あだちる」 together with 松本いちか.

## Personal notes

- Born in Tokyo. Interest: dance; speciality: acrobatic gymnastics.
- Likes Japanese rap such as 阿修羅MIC, and likes Paris.
- In 2013 (age 13) she became an idol (バクステ外神田一丁目) admiring AKB48, but was disappointed by the gap between fantasy and reality — hierarchies among seniors, competition to keep popularity, pillow business — and retired in under a year. 桜もこ, who also retired later, was her senior, and they later co-stared in AV.
- Her motive for becoming an AV actress was high income. In her idol days she earned less than ¥100,000 a month, and after class fees it was hard to live, so she worried about money.
- At her debut she only planned to shoot about four titles, but gradually found "being watched" interesting and the AV industry cleaner than she had imagined (an agent accompanies the shoot, the content is safe to trust), so she continued; but the decision to "retire after three years" was already made then.
- Before retiring she said: besides the high income, having her once-complex-ridden face, voice and bust size accepted as "individuality" and regaining her confidence was the biggest gain of her AV career (XCITY retirement interview).
- Her first squirting was experienced in her debut title.
- Favourite positions: standing doggy, missionary, reverse cowgirl. Standing doggy was a position she often used with her boyfriend in high school; she likes it because it's "convenient".
- Early in her career, as a lolicon actress, she often co-starred with 渚みつき; the two also drank and went out together in private.

## Work features

- Core: her former-idol status, lolicon looks and cute air.
- After debuting in 2019 she was active across MOODYZ, kawaii*, Kira☆kira, ダスッ!, 本中, TMA, 宇宙企画 and other labels.
- In December 2019 she became the first ceiling-specialised-angle actress, and titles with the unique camera angle became popular.
- After retiring she is active as a YouTuber.
