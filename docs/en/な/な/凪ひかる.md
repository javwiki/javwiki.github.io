## Introduction

凪ひかる is a Japanese AV actress. From July 2020 she published nude photos in weekly gravure magazines as 「有栖花あか」; that September she entered the entertainment world with the photobook 《Big Baby》, and in October debuted as an S1 NO.1 STYLE exclusive with the slogan 「とにかく顔が美形--、とにかく乳が美形--」 ("the face is beautiful anyway — the breasts are beautiful anyway"). Her debut title ranked 6th on Blu-ray and 8th on DVD in FANZA's "2020 annual DVD chart". She later changed her name twice: to 「汐世」 in December 2021, and to her current 「凪ひかる」 in November 2022 when she moved to 8MAN, taking part in that agency's gravure project 「8woman」 at the same time. She sells a 105cm J cup and refined looks, with titjob themes as her signature; she has been an S1 exclusive throughout her career. Her agency 8MAN is now called SO MODEL AGENT (the official site 8man.jp redirects to so-agent.jp), and in 2026 her official profile is still carried under that name.
