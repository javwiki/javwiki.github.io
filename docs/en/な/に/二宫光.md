## Introduction

二宫光 is a former Japanese AV actress who debuted as a MOODYZ exclusive in September 2018 and later spent a long period as an Attackers exclusive. Known for pure, cute looks and excellent acting, she was especially strong in subjugation and story-driven titles. In October 2024 she announced her retirement, ending an AV career of about six years. She has also been active as a singer, holding several music lives.
