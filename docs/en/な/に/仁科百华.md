## Introduction

仁科百华 is a former Japanese AV actress. From Tokyo, she was an underground idol before her AV debut; she debuted in August 2010 with the Prestige title 《ワリキリ、ハミ乳娘貸し切り。3》 and quickly became a sensation with a J-cup 100cm bust, called the "一億円のバストJカップアイドル (hundred-million-yen bust J-cup idol)". By the end of 2012 she had appeared in about 500 titles, making her one of the most productive popular big-breast actresses of the time. In April 2011 she made her CD debut as a member of the idol unit OFA21, and in 2012 won the SOD Award excellence actress prize. On 28 May 2013 she announced her retirement on her blog; afterwards she renamed herself 悠木イヴ (悠木夏娃) and continued as an actress, singer and model.
