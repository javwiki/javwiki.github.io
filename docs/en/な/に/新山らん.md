## Introduction

新山らん is a former Japanese AV actress and gravure idol. In 2010 she debuted as a gravure idol with 「本当にデカップ」 (Diapress), billing herself as 「本格的大罩杯」, and became a topic of conversation with a K-cup bust of 110cm, earning the title "灵长类最强巨乳 (the strongest big breasts in the primate kingdom)" from 週刊プレイボーイ. She made her AV debut through MUTEKI in May 2013 and moved to エスワン (S1) that September as an exclusive; her S1 exclusivity lasted until July 2015 (SNIS-451).
