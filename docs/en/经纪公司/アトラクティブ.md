# アトラクティブ

## Basic information

- **Full name**: アトラクティブ (Attractive LLC)
- **History**: An agency founded by a practitioner who had worked at 名東 and managed 神纳花, specialising in AV actress management
- **Location**: Nakano, Tokyo
- **Profile**: From 2020 it has run its own programme 「アトラクティブ女学園」 on エンタ!959; two of its members were nominated at the スカパー!アダルト放送大賞 2023
- **Association**: Member of the Japan Production Association (JPG)
- **Website**: <https://attractive-llc.net/>
