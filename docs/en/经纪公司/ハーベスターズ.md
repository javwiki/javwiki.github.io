# ハーベスターズ

## Basic information

- **Full name**: HARVESTERS Co,LTD. (ハーベスターズ)
- **Former name**: ALLURE (アリュール)
- **History**: Originally the "アリュール division" of フィットワン; spun off as 有限会社アリュール in 2008 and renamed ハーベスターズ in 2018
- **Location**: Akasaka, Minato, Tokyo
- **Association**: Former member of the Japan Production Association (withdrew in 2023, rejoined in 2024, withdrew again in 2025)
