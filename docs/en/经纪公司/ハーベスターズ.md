# ハーベスターズ

## Basic information

- **Full name**: HARVESTERS Co., LTD. (ハーベスターズ)
- **Former name**: ALLURE (アリュール)
- **History**: started as the "アリュール division" of Fitt One, became independent as ALLURE LLC in 2008, and was renamed ハーベスターズ in 2018
- **Location**: Akasaka, Minato, Tokyo
- **Association**: formerly a member of Japan production guild (JPG); left in 2023, rejoined in 2024, and left again in 2025
- **Official site**: <https://harvesters.web.fc2.com/>
