|Financial management|Managing women's earnings, taxes, etc.|

### Vendor relations

Brokers are cooperative with manufacturers, who provide women-friendly resources and produce and distribute works. The parties agreed on a proportional and working conditions through a contract.

## Brokering and female superiority

### Type of contract

|Type|Annotations|
| --- | --- |
|The exclusive contract.|The woman is only a producer.|
|Non-exclusive contracts|The woman can publish in many companies.|
|Independent contract|Women run their own business independently.|

### Duration of contract

The contract is usually for a period of 1-3 years and may be renewed or terminated upon expiry. Some women opt for independent development and no longer rely on brokerage companies.

## Broker selection

### Selection criteria

|Standard|Annotations|
| --- | --- |
|Size of the company|Big companies are rich, small companies are more flexible.|
|Scope of operations|Whether business types that are desirable for women are covered|
|Oral evaluation|Industry reputation and women ' s evaluations|
|Proportional|Reasonable share of income|
|Development opportunities|Increased development opportunities|

### Attention

- Selecting formal brokerage companies to avoid black brokering
- Read the terms of the contract and know what the rights and obligations are.
- Maintain evidence to protect its rights and interests
