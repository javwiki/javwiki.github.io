# Agencies

An agency (jimusho) is the intermediary that represents an AV actress, handling her career, contract negotiations, work arrangements and more. Knowing the major agencies helps you understand an actress's professional background and career path.

## What an agency does

### Main services

| Service | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Actress management | Managing the actress's career and contract negotiations |
| Work arrangements | Booking shoots, events, promotions and similar work |
| Image management | Managing the actress's public image and social media |
| Legal affairs | Handling contracts, copyright and other legal matters |
| Financial management | Managing the actress's income, taxes, etc. |

### Relationship with studios

Agencies and studios work together: the agency supplies the actresses, the studio produces and distributes the releases. The two sides agree on the revenue split and working conditions by contract.

## Agencies and actresses

### Contract types

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Exclusive contract | The actress releases only for a specific studio |
| Non-exclusive contract | The actress may release with several studios |
| Independent contract | The actress manages her own career independently |

### Contract terms

Contracts usually run one to three years; they can be renewed or terminated on expiry. Some actresses choose to go independent and no longer rely on an agency.

## Choosing an agency

### Criteria

| Criterion | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Size | Large agencies have more resources; small agencies are more flexible |
| Scope | Whether it covers the kinds of services the actress needs |
| Reputation | Standing in the industry and reviews from actresses |
| Revenue split | Whether the income split is reasonable |
| Opportunities | Whether it can offer more room to grow |

### Cautions

- Pick a licensed agency and avoid illegal middlemen
- Read the contract terms carefully and understand your rights and obligations
- Keep the relevant evidence to protect your own interests
