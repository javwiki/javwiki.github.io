## Introduction

松本一香 is an extremely popular Japanese AV actress who debuted as an SOD 「青春时代」 exclusive in September 2019. With lolicon looks and a cute air she quickly became a star, topping all kinds of FANZA charts many times, and is one of the most representative AV actresses of 2020-2023. She is also active as a member of the idol unit FleuЯR (フルール). In 2023 she placed 7th in Asahi Entertainment's "2023 active AV actress SEXY general election".

## Personal notes

- From Sendai, Miyagi Prefecture. Interests: games, watching anime, illustration. Speciality: finishing a can of beer quickly.
- Extremely active on SNS, often doing Twitter livestreams and interacting frequently with fans, with popularity similar to a "livestream hostess".
- Her nickname is 「いっちゃん」.

## Work features

- Core charm: lolicon looks and a cute air.
- After debuting in 2019 she quickly became a regular champion of the FANZA charts: 7th in the first-half AV actress sales ranking of 2020, 1st in FANZA's annual AV actress ranking for 2020.
- 1st in first-half AV actress sales of 2021; 1st in Monthly FANZA's summer "このAV女優がすごい!" of 2021.
- Ranked 1st for multiple consecutive months on the FANZA video floor monthly AV actress chart across 2022-2023.
- Active across SOD, ダスッ!, 本中, 痴女ヘブン, MOODYZ and other labels.
