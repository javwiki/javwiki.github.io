## Introduction

真木今日子 (Japanese: 真木 今日子 / まき きょうこ, born 3 September 1990) is a Japanese AV actress from Kanagawa Prefecture, affiliated with the LINX agency. 160cm tall with an H-cup bust (99-60-88), she made her AV debut as a MAX-A exclusive in January 2011, was an OPPAI exclusive from May to August of the same year, then became a kikaku-tantai actress and has been active across many labels for a long time. A former member of the AKB48 group's 「ギンギン♂ガールズ」. In 2014 she was shortlisted for SEGA's 《龍が如く》 character poll, and in June 2026 she announced that she would become a Madonna exclusive. The contrast between her H-cup bust and cute image, plus her past work as a French-pastry chef, keep her a topic of conversation.
