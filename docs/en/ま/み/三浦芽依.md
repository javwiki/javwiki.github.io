## Introduction

三浦芽依 is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as a KUKI exclusive in August 2009 (debut title 『初花 -hatsuhana-』). In February 2011 she announced her graduation from the AV actress world, and that September changed her name to 「上条めぐ」 and made her comeback, continuing her activity. She is characterised by a 160cm figure with a G-cup bust.
