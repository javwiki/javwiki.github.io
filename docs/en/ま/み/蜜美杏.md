## Introduction

蜜美杏 (Japanese: 蜜美 杏 / みつみ あん, born 15 November 2000) is a Japanese AV actress and former gravure idol from Tokyo. 170cm tall, G cup (measurements 86-58-89). She made her AV debut as a PRESTIGE exclusive in May 2020 and quickly became a sensation with her tall figure and G-cup bust. She retired briefly in May 2022. After renaming herself 「藤井蘭々」 in March 2023 she formally returned, launched gravure activity in 週刊Post, and became a FALENO exclusive that April. Her interest is using social networks; her speciality is selfies. The senior she admires is Kirara Asuka.
