## Introduction

三上悠亚 is one of the most influential actresses in the Japanese AV industry. Transitioning into the AV industry as a former member of the idol unit SKE48, she quickly rose to the top of the industry after debuting in 2015. With outstanding looks, a perfect figure and professionalism she achieved enormous success in the AV industry while also enjoying extreme recognition in mainstream entertainment. She is one of the few actresses in the industry to succeed in both the AV and mainstream fields. On 16 August 2023 she formally retired as an AV actress.

## Personal notes
