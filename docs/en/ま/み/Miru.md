## Introduction

Miru is an active Japanese AV actress known for her technique. She debuted at S1 as "坂道みる" in August 2018 and changed her name to "miru" and moved to C-more ENTERTAINMENT in May 2021. AV critic 東風克智 bestowed on her titles such as "SEX的天才 (genius of sex)", "genius of cowgirl" and "genius of fellatio"; she is famous for her superb cowgirl technique and squirting ability. She calls herself the "co-starring queen" and appears in co-starring titles far more often than any other S1 exclusive.
