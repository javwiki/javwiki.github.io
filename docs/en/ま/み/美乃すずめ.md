## Introduction

美乃すずめ is a Japanese AV actress. In August 2019 she debuted in 週刊ポスト gravure as 「美乃」, and that October made her AV debut as a FALENO star exclusive as 「美乃すずめ」. In February 2021 she moved to DAHLIA, a new streaming-specialised label. Also continuously active as a gravure idol, she is the longest-serving exclusive actress of the FALENO group. In July 2022 there were reports of a romance with the comedy entertainer インディアンス・田渕章裕, which both denied.
