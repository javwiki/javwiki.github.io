## Introduction

水菜麗 is a famous former Japanese AV actress who debuted with an Alice Japan title in July 2008; she is called the "AV界郭采潔 (Guo Caijie of the AV world)" and the "little-devil beauty idol". Her interests are reading (such as Murakami Haruki), listening to music (mainly rock) and playing games (Monster Hunter); she is good at knitting and drinking. She retired on 3 September 2016, later renamed herself スズナミナツ and formed a cycling team with like-minded friends.
