## Introduction

宮島めい is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as an SODstar exclusive in July 2020. She was originally a second-year university student in early-childhood education, and applied to SOD to earn study-abroad tuition. Out of about 1,000 applicants she was spotted at a glance by SOD vice-president 高岡哲也 and hired. She debuted with the buzz premise of "a 100-day limited AV actress", but after her study abroad was cancelled due to the pandemic she formally returned. Her debut title took 4th on the FANZA weekly chart, her retirement title 1st, and her comeback title also took 1st.
