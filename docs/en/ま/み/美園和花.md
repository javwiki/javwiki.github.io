## Introduction

美園和花 is a Japanese AV actress from Kagawa Prefecture. She debuted as an exclusive of SOD's in-house 青春时代 project in February 2018 (her own X bio says 8 February 2018; the page's old December record is wrong); after three titles she suspended activity, and restarted on 16 March 2019 by opening an official Twitter. She became widely known for setting the "most swallowing records in AV history" in February 2020 — swallowing 242 times in a single title — breaking the 231-times record held by 美沢美加. From May 2025 she is a triple exclusive of MOODYZ, ダスッ! and アタッカーズ; across 2024–2026 she topped the FANZA video monthly chart multiple times.
