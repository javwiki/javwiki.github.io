## Introduction

みやび音羽 (Otoha Miyabi) is a former Japanese AV actress, a new-half (transgender) performer. Born 4 August in Tokyo, 166cm, blood type O. In 2015 she debuted with 僕たち男の娘's 《AV解禁！みやび音羽 アナルと巨根に媚薬をタップリ塗り込まれ絶叫SEX》 and was active as the label's exclusive new-half actress. In 2016 she retired with 《卒業 美しすぎるニューハーフ みやび音羽 ～10回射精すまで終われまてん～》. During her AV years she used the name 「みやび音羽」; afterwards she used 「あまね るか」 as a model name.
