## Introduction

未步奈奈 is a former Japanese AV actress known for her activity as a singer. She debuted as an S1 exclusive in June 2022 and had been an idol band member before her debut. Selling "cuteness" as her point, she also actively developed her singing career, holding several solo concerts. In 2023 she formed the idol unit Mi LUNA from お月ちゃんのうた with 古川ほのか and 八木奈々. She retired on 30 December 2025.
