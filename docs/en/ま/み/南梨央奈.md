## Introduction

南梨央奈 is an active Japanese AV actress known for a distinctive personality and energy. She debuted as a KUKI exclusive in September 2011 and later became a kikaku-tantai actress. She is also a member of BRW108. Early in her career she had a gyaru style; after 2012 she switched to a black-haired lolicon style. With rich offline events, close interaction with fans and a distinctive personality she stands out in the industry, with a relatively high share of female fans.
