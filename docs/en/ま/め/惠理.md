## Introduction

惠理 (Japanese name: めぐり, former stage name 藤浦惠) is a well-known Japanese AV actress, gravure star, entertainer and YouTube creator. From Ibaraki Prefecture, she debuted in gravure DVD in 2007 and made her AV debut at MUTEKI in 2009, quickly becoming a sensation with a G-cup bust and idol-level looks. She has been a member of the idol units 「恵比寿マスカッツ」「BRW108」「me-me」. She retired once in 2010, returned renamed 「惠理」 in 2011, and has remained active in the industry and on social platforms ever since. Her personal YouTube channel 「めぐりチューブ」 has over 330,000 subscribers.

## Personal notes

- In 2007 she debuted as a gravure star as 「藤浦めぐ」, a core member of the "藤军团" of the ピンキーネット agency, with extremely high DVD sales.
- AV debut at MUTEKI on 1 January 2009 (limited-talent project).
- Announced her retirement in December 2010; renamed herself 「惠理」 and joined the T-POWERS agency in May 2011 for her comeback, re-debuting in AV from SOD ACE in July.
- Announced her retirement in 2017; made her comeback again and became active in 2019.
- Went to Hong Kong again for an event in February 2026.
- Her specialities are piano and English; during her retirements she invested heavily in overseas markets.
- Secretly married in 2014 (not disclosed at the time).

## Work features

- Known for a G-cup bust and idol beauty; her catalogue covers idol, affair, story-driven, teasing and many other types.
- In her 藤浦惠 era she was known for S1's 「芸能人×ギリモザ」 series, often collaborating with mainstream entertainers.
- In her 惠理 era she was active at SOD ACE and MOODYZ, with her style gradually maturing and diversifying.
