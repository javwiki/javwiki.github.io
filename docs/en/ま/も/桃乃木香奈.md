## Introduction

桃乃木香奈 is one of the most representative actresses in the Japanese AV industry. She debuted under the stage name "松嶋真麻" in February 2015, became a member of 惠比壽★麝香葡萄 that September, and made her formal debut as an IdeaPocket exclusive in October. Her "cute" image struck deep, and her releases are known for high quality and much loved by fans. Her interest is solo karaoke, and she is also a big eater. She was in the volleyball club for all six years of middle school and served as its captain. She loves Ramen Jiro. A fan of the idol group Nogizaka46, her favourite member is Saito Asuka. She announced her retirement in September 2025 and ended her AV actress activity on 7 October of that year; afterwards she continued other public activities as 「桃乃木かな」.
