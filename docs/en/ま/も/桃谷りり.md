## Introduction

桃谷りり (Momotani Riri) is a Japanese transgender AV actress (new half). Born 29 July 1995 in Saitama Prefecture, F cup. She is also known as the Tsugaru shamisen player "KIJU" and was affiliated with the traditional-instruments performance unit 「桜men」. After leaving 桜men in August 2021 she made her AV debut as 桃谷りり in September 2021, becoming a "電撃専属 (surprise exclusive)" actress of グローリークエスト.
