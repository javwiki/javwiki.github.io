## Introduction

森ほたる is a Japanese AV actress from Nagano Prefecture, born 3 October 1987. She debuted as a mega-exclusive newcomer at Madonna in February 2018; her debut title 《独占!! 超大型専属 美巨乳くびれHカップ!! 新人 森ほたる AVデビュー!!》 sold her 「美巨乳くびれHカップ」 figure as its hook. She left the Madonna exclusive roster in July 2018 and became a kikaku-tantai actress, afterwards active at Fitch, OPPAI, ダスッ!, グローリークエスト, ヒビノ, タカラ映像, VENUS and many other labels, mainly in big-breast married-woman and beautiful mature-woman themes.
