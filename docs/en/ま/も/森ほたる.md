## Introduction

Mori Hotaru is a Japanese AV actress from Nagano Prefecture, born on 3 October 1987. She debuted in February 2018 as a major Madonna exclusive, with her debut work promoting her "big-breasted, slim-waisted H-cup" figure. She left the exclusive contract in July 2018 and has since worked as a non-exclusive actress across studios such as Fitch, OPPAI, ダスッ!, グローリークエスト, ヒビノ, タカラ映像 and VENUS, mainly in big-breasted wife and beautiful mature-woman themes.
