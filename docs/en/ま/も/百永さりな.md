## Introduction

百永さりな is a Japanese AV actress who debuted as a Prestige exclusive as 「黒川サリナ」 in September 2017. She changed her name to 「黒川さりな」 in September 2018, and in April 2021 moved to NAXプロモーション and changed her name to 「百永さりな」. She received the honorary SkyperfecTV! Adult Broadcasting Awards 2022 Valuable Actress of the Rainbow channel. She is also active as a YouTube content creator; her channel "Sarina TV" has about 19,700 subscribers.
