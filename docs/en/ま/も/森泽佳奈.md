## Introduction

森沢かな is a Japanese AV actress and YouTuber. She debuted as 「飯岡かなこ」 in July 2012 and changed her name to 「森沢かな」 in February 2016. She took 1st place on FANZA's actress chart in the first half of 2024 and 4th for the year, a veteran kikaku-tantai actress with over 13 years of activity. With refined looks and an elegant air, she is celebrated as "beauty you never tire of seeing".

## Personal notes

- She only started using Twitter in May 2019 and did no SNS before that, making her persona rather mysterious
- Her debut materials said she was from Ehime, but she denies it — she is actually from Tokyo
- Her fans are collectively called 「かなにぃず。」, decided through a TwitCasting discussion
- Actively challenges TikTok, YouTube and other fields; runs the YouTube channels 「かな先生の性教育教室」 (about 21,500 subscribers) and 「もりかなちゃんねる」
- Her ideal man is "someone whose body fits hers" and "someone with the same passion for sex"
- In the summer of 2020 she published her first photobook through crowdfunding, and held a photo exhibition in April 2021
- Appointed an EX Girl on 31 May 2024

## Work features

- Has kept up a "beautiful onee-san" route for 13 years, winning without strong personas or hard-core play
- A varied style covering many themes, much loved by viewers
- In 2024 she entered the monthly Top 10 on all three major FANZA platforms: mail order, rental and video
