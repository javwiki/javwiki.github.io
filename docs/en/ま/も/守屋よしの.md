## Introduction

守屋よしの is a Japanese AV actress who debuted under the SOD女子社員 label in May 2025. She joined SOD as a mid-career hire in autumn 2024, working in the general affairs department's secretarial section as an executive secretary for the food and beverage division. Only eight months after joining she debuted with the theme 「秘書 守屋よしの 初AV出演」, called "the fastest secretary AV debut in history". Before her debut she released amateur videos on FC2 as 「ゆあちゃん」 (around 2022-2024).
